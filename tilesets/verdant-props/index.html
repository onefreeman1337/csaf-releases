70 props. 194 tiles. 7 ground materials. 3 animated sets. A clutter pack for 16×16 top-down games — the things you put on the ground once the ground is done. Every prop ships twice, and that is the point Transparent — the prop alone, to drop on your terrain. Grounded — the same prop on ours, with a contact shadow between the two. That is 70 grounded tiles beside 70 transparent ones, and you need both.

A shadow has to darken something, so on a transparent tile it can only be an opaque dark blob — which under a bush reads as a hole in your grass. And a pack that ships only grounded tiles is useless to anyone who already has their own terrain. Look at the second screenshot: the six shadows are six different colours.

Each one is derived from the material it falls on — that material's own darkest tone, scaled down and cooled — so a shadow on sand cannot come out green.
