A candy world you can build a level from: 550 tiles at 16x16 in one 59-colour palette: a mint sugar-grass meadow under gumdrop trees, pink candyfloss thickets standing up out of it, a chocolate river that flows through its cocoa-crumb bank, a biscuit path, and frosted peaks of lavender icing with amethyst rock-candy crags.

What is in it A chocolate river that flows: 141 animated transition tiles (the river set, 3 key frames played 1, 2, 3, 2 at 450 ms a beat) plus every chocolate variant as an animated fill.

The middle frame of the flowing set is the static river set, tile for tile, so you can paint the still river and swap the moving one in with no seam.

423 transition tiles in 7 complete 47-mask sets: the candyfloss thicket and the rock-candy crags standing up off their ground (a lit face and a shadow at the foot), the cocoa bank meeting the meadow, the chocolate river in its bank, the icing climbing out of the meadow, and the biscuit path.
