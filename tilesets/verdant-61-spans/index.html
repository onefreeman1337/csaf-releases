Verdant 61 — Spans: Bridges, Fords & Stepping Stones Over Water and Chasm, Every Style in Both Directions [16x16] is a tileset pack for Unity, RPG Maker, Tiled and Godot, paid.

Bridges, fords and stepping stones for a 16x16 map: 644 tiles in one 52-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Every bridge runs both ways 9 decks: rope bridge, plank walk, lumber bridge, lumber bridge with rails, stone arch bridge, carved stone bridge, fallen log, drawbridge and pontoon bridge.

Each one comes west to east AND north to south, and the north to south pieces are drawn for the 3/4 view rather than turned on their side, so a bridge going up the screen shows its side beams and its end face on the south bank.

Every deck comes 1 or 2 tiles wide, with a start, a middle that repeats as long as you like and an end, and every piece also comes broken (162 broken pieces), so a span can be washed out or snapped. What is in it 324 bridge pieces, drawn as overlays: wherever there is no deck the tile is transparent, so one piece sits on a river, a pool, a chasm or plain ground.
