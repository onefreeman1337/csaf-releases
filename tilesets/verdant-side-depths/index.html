Verdant Side Depths is a 16x16 side-view platformer tileset for Godot 4 and Tiled: three underworld biomes in one download, the Infernal Halls (lava and brimstone), the Desert Tomb (from the dunes down into a pyramid) and the Crypt (a graveyard and the catacomb under it).

It holds 703 tiles in one 132 colour palette: 9 complete 47 shape autotile sets (6 solid, 3 back wall), 60 slope pieces at 45 and 22.5 degrees, one-way platforms, climbables, 3 animated hazards, 43 kinds of object (8 animated) including iron doors open and closed and statues, three parallax backgrounds per biome and a finished sample level for each.

Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

The Godot TileSet is ready to paint and play: collision on every solid tile and slope, one-way collision on the platforms, hazards on their own physics layer, a terrain set so Godot's own painter picks the right one of the 47 shapes, custom data (kind, climbable, hazard) and tile animations. Three sample scenes open with the level and its parallax already built.
