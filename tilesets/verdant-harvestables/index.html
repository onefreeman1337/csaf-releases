Most tile packs ship a tree. A game where the player harvests needs the tree, the chopped trunk, the stump, the regrowth, and the log that drops. Ship only the tree and you end up drawing the other four yourself, later, in a style that no longer matches. Every object in this pack ships as a state chain, and every chain ends in the item it yields. 45 props, 12 chains, 106 tile files, at 16×16.

Chain States Drops oak standing → chopped → stump → regrowth log ore rich → worked → depleted ore jar intact → cracked → shattered shards barrel intact → cracked → shattered staves bush in berry → picked berries crop sprouting → growing → ripe → reaped sheaf gourd young vine → ripe → picked gourd herb in flower → picked herb sprigs hive in honey → robbed honeycomb mushroom grown → picked mushroom caps reed standing → cut reed bundle stone whole → cracked → broken cut stone That structure is enforced when the pack is built, not merely intended: the build fails if any chain has fewer than two states or does not ship exactly one drop, and it refuses to emit a chain map whose ids do not resolve to real tiles.

Every prop ships twice 45 transparent — drop them onto your own terrain.
