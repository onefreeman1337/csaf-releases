Verdant 67 — Scarp Slopes: Diagonal Cliffs, Cliff Stairs & Ramps for Verdant 12 Scarp [16x16] is a tileset pack for Unity, RPG Maker, Tiled and Godot, paid.

An add-on for Verdant 12 — Scarp: 72 pieces at 16x16 in Scarp's own 21-colour palette that turn its cliffs, roads, bracken and scree on a 45 degree diagonal, plus stairs and ramps cut into the scarp. You need Verdant 12 Scarp: its straight edges, corners and fills are used as they are. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it 30 diagonal edges across all 7 of Scarp's sets: the scarp itself (the rock face and its shadow carried round the southern diagonals, the rim on the northern ones), the scree fan, the drove road on both levels, bracken on both levels, and Scarp's meadow meeting Verdant 01's grass.

28 shoulders, 6 caps and 2 elbows: the pieces for the elbow of each step of a diagonal run, and for where a run meets a straight scarp. 3 stairs and 3 ramps cut into the scarp face, one cell wide or the two halves of a two-cell flight. At Scarp's low face the ramp is a short taper. A 30x18 sample map, Angled Terraces, as PNG and as JSON with every cell named (Scarp's tiles by their Scarp names).
