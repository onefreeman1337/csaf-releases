Tall grass, ledges, a pond, a cave Encounter grass that sways (3 frames, played back and forth), a worn road, one way ledges you hop down and cannot climb (13 drop tiles carry "oneWay": "south" in tiles.json, with stone steps to put where the route climbs back up), a still pond, and a cave with its mouth and its rock walls. Every one is a complete 47-mask autotile set, so every corner, edge and junction is drawn.

What is in it 423 transition tiles in 5 complete 47-mask sets: tall grass and the pond animated, the road, the ledge and cave rock still. 10 fill tiles (meadow grass, cave floor, road variants) and 9 animated open-water tiles so a big pond does not repeat one cell.

19 objects, each standing on the grass with a contact shadow: route signs, a shrub and its cut stump, a boulder, a cracked rock, berry trees, a mailbox, a lamp, fences, a bench, the town sign, an item satchel, stone steps, a cave mouth, and a small route town (a slate-roofed hall and a cottage).
