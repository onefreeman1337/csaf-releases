The raid happens on your map Every one of the 510 tiles in Verdant 09 Town has a twin here with the same name, sheet and position, checked file by file against Verdant 09's shipped tiles.json, RPG Maker MZ sheets, Tiled tilesets and Godot TileSet.

So the village your players walked through burns by swapping the tileset: in RPG Maker MZ with the Change Tileset event command or a second Tileset in the Database, in Tiled by repointing the tilesets, in Godot by loading this TileSet. The positions are proven identical file by file; the swap itself was not run inside the MZ editor. The GIF above is Verdant 09's own sample map, swapped.

What is in it Structural damage, not a darker palette: thatch burned through to the rafters and battens, shingle roofs with shingles gone, rubble on the street, hedges burned to twigs, walls with charred frames and soot.
