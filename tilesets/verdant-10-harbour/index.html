581 tiles. Ten complete 47-mask autotile sets, 28 harbour objects each shipping two or three times, animated water — and a port built as ONE place rather than as four packs you have to reconcile. Verdant 01 gave you a grassland. 04 gave you the coast.

This pack is where the coast gets a town — granite setts, a tarred plank wharf out over the water, warehouses, and the working gear of a dock that people actually load ships from. A harbour is almost entirely BOUNDARIES Search "harbour tileset" and you find a ship pack, a port-town pack and an island pack — often from the same seller, sold separately, drawn at different scales, on no shared grid.

You can buy all three and still not be able to lay a quay that meets a wharf that meets the water. Stone meeting water. Plank meeting stone. Plank meeting water. Two different walls standing on both. That is why this pack spends 470 of its 581 tiles on transitions — nine of the ten 47-mask sets are load-bearing in the sample map, and a harbour pack without them is a pile of squares you cannot join.
