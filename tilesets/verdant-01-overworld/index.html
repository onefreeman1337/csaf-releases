A complete 16×16 overworld set — and the delivery is the point. Five ground materials with four variants each, four complete 47-mask blob autotile sets, and seventeen props — eleven of which also ship grounded, on their own grass with a contact shadow beneath them. 239 tiles, one 35-colour palette, one grid. Everything in the screenshots was built from this pack and nothing else.

What usually goes wrong with tile packs, and what we did about it Nearly every complaint we could find on the best-selling tilesets here is about delivery rather than art — a PNG that is secretly a JPEG, a folder present at one grid size and missing at another, no licence in the zip, an autotile set with holes in it. So: All 47 masks are present in all four autotile sets — 188 transition tiles.

They were enumerated from the 256 possible neighbourhoods rather than drawn from a list, so a missing transition is not a state this set can be in. tiles.json maps every mask number to its tile. Every tile is documented. tiles.json gives each one an id, a role, whether it self-tiles, its sheet slot and its autotile mask. Nothing ships undocumented.
