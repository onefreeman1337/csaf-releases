This is the first at 32x32, and it is not an upscale: a 2× nearest-neighbour blow-up of 16px art would be the “no anti-aliasing” claim turned into a lie about detail. Every material, every one of the 376 transition tiles and every object was drawn for this cell. Elevation, and why it took this long A cliff is not hard to draw. It is hard to make read, and the reason turns out to be arithmetic rather than art.

Whether a raised surface reads as raised depends on how far its tone sits from the ground below, measured against how much the ground’s own texture already varies. Put a step of any size on a meadow whose grass swings 110 levels of brightness inside a single tile, and the meadow swallows it.
