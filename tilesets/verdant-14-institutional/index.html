Verdant 14 Institutional is a 16x16 pixel art tileset of a modern office for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4: open plan office, lobby, corridor and studio. It holds 635 tiles, a sample map and ready importers. Commercial use in unlimited projects, no royalty and no attribution required. The free Verdant Playground lets you paint a 47 mask set in your browser before you buy.

What is in it 24 fill tiles: 6 materials, 4 variants each: contract carpet, sheet vinyl, lobby terrazzo, a power floated concrete slab, a demountable partition and a glazed screen. 11 complete 47 mask autotile sets, 517 tiles: 81% of the pack, so rooms of any shape get every edge and corner. 20 objects across 70 tiles: desks, chairs, monitors, a meeting room whiteboard, lockers, benches, plants and server racks.

Each ships cut out and grounded with its contact shadow. 3 animated objects, 4 frames each: a server rack with flickering lights, a desk monitor with a live trace and a photocopier with a sweeping scan lamp. A sample map, 34x24 cells with 44 objects, as a PNG, as JSON and as a Tiled.tmx. The partition is dark on purpose.
