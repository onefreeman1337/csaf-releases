The thing most dungeon tilesets get wrong Top-down elevation at 16 pixels is the problem that kills dungeon tilesets. Two flat areas of different colour do not read as floor and wall. They read as two floors, and no amount of detail on the wall fixes it.

We know because we tried: an earlier pack in this series had a 19-tile cliff set that was cut after six iterations, each one fixing a real defect and each one still reading as a trench. Two things fix it, and this pack does both. A separation ratio, not a colour choice. The wall's mid-tone has to sit far enough from the floor's relative to how much the floor already varies inside a single tile.
