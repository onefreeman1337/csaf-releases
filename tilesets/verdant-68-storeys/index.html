An add-on for Verdant 07 — Interiors: 255 tiles at 16x16 that give its one-storey rooms a way up and a way down. What is in it 12 stair flights set into the floor: up into a dark wall opening and down through the floor, two wide and narrow, in plank, cut stone and a carpet runner on oak. Each up flight has a cast shadow strip for the floor beside it.

6 side flights rising east or west, in the same three materials, each transparent and grounded on its floor.
