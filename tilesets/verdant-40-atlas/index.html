Verdant 40 — Atlas: Parchment & Natural Colour World Map Tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled & Godot [16x16] is a tileset pack for RPG Maker, Tiled and Godot, paid.

A world map drawn like a chart, and the same map in natural colour. 700 tiles at 16x16: every tile exists twice, once on parchment and once in natural colour, drawn once so the two line up exactly. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Two palettes, one drawing The paper palette (15 colours) is an old printed chart: parchment land, a pale blue sea, sepia ink. The natural palette (36 colours) is the same map as a JRPG world: green land, blue water, grey peaks with snow caps, red roofs.

There are 350 twin pairs, and the sheets come in twins laid out cell for cell, so in Tiled or Godot you can swap one sheet image for its twin and the whole map changes palette without moving a tile. The GIF above is exactly that swap, wiped across the map.

What is in it 470 transition tiles in 10 complete 47-mask sets, five per palette: the coast (an ink shoreline with a line of water-lining offshore), the shallow shelf, and desert, snowfield and marsh regions. Every inner corner, outer corner, edge, junction and single is there.
