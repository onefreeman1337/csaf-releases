Paint the plan, and the walls draw themselves Rooms and corridors are one complete 47 shape autotile: paint the floor plan and the ink walls, the square corners and the corridor mouths draw themselves, so a corridor runs into a room with no seam, turns, forks, crosses and dead ends. Around the plan is hatched rock, the way a cartographer draws it.

Caverns get a rough wall of their own, and water and the chasm paint on top of the plan. Two palettes, one drawing Every tile ships twice: in the parchment and ink of Verdant 40 Atlas, the world map this pack pairs with, and in the old school blue dungeon map style with solid blue rock, white rooms and a blue grid.

Each blue tile is its paper tile's twin, drawn from the same picture (380 pairs, each in the same cell of its twin sheet), so swapping one sheet for its twin restyles the whole dungeon without moving a cell.
