What is inside 2,113 tiles · 22 complete 47 shape autotile sets (4 animated) · 18 wall and roof blocks · 107 objects and 13 animated pieces · a ship · 5 sample maps The Overworld (570 tiles): animated sea and river shores, a waterfall, a bridge, mountains, forest, a castle, a lighthouse, a cave mouth and a dock.

The Castle Town (605): a castle gate, a drawbridge over an animated moat, a fountain, market stalls and three house styles built from roof and wall blocks. The Interiors (269): beds and a fireplace, bookshelves and potion shelves, weapon racks and armour, an altar and pews. The Cavern (327): rock walls, mine rails with turns and a minecart, crystals, ore, ladders and an animated pool.

The Lava Keep (342): animated lava and lavafalls, a lava bridge, a throne, gargoyles, spikes and a portcullis.
