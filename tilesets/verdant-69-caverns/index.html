Verdant 69 — Caverns: Cave Tileset with Underground Lake, Limestone & Cave Mouths for RPG Maker MZ & Godot [16x16] is a tileset pack for Unity, RPG Maker, Tiled and Godot, paid.

A natural limestone cave you can build a level from: 675 tiles at 16x16 in one 68-colour palette: dark rock walls standing up from a pale cave floor, an underground lake with turquoise shallows that swell and draw back, silt banks, flowstone terraces, and cave mouths that open in the cliffs of the Verdant packs you already own.

Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it Cave mouths that fit your cliffs: one cut into this pack's own wall, and two transparent mouths (one for a one-tile face, one for a tall cliff) drawn in the same rock colours as the cliffs of Verdant 12 — Scarp, Verdant 02 Elevation and Verdant 38 — Cascades, checked colour by colour against those packs.

A breach into the dungeon: a set where the cave floor tears up through the flagstone of Verdant 05 — Depths. Depths' four flagstone tiles are included, so it works on its own. A lake that moves: 2 animated transition sets (282 tiles, 3 key frames played 1, 2, 3, 2 at 400 ms a beat), the lake on stone and a pool on silt, plus every lake variant as an animated fill.
