Want the whole set? Verdant 63 is a 587 tile 16x16 ice palace tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity. It builds a castle made of ice on a snowfield: wall walks with a battlement of ice merlons, glacial towers, ice plate roofs, a frozen moat of black ice, a trodden ward, crystal spires and an ice throne. Commercial use is permitted in unlimited projects with no attribution required.

Do not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. What is in it 517 transition tiles in 11 complete 47 mask autotile sets: wall walks and towers in the ward, in the snow and out of the moat, the ward, a hall roof, the moat, and the snowfield meeting Verdant 01's grass. 24 ground fills: snow, ward, moat, wall walk, tower and roof, 4 variants each.

21 objects across 46 tiles: crystal spires, an ice gate with a frosted portcullis, frost braziers of cold cyan fire, banners, the ice throne, ice knights, a frozen fountain, steps up to the wall walk, cut ice blocks, a fishing hole for the moat and more. A 36x26 sample map, The Ice Palace, built only from this pack, as PNG, as JSON and as a Tiled.tmx.
