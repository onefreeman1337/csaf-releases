Verdant Ironworks is a 16x16 pixel art tileset volume for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: three machine-age places in one download, a working mine with a rail network and gold in its walls, the inside of a clock tower with turning gears and a dial that shows any hour, and a steam train as four cars (coach, sleeper, dining car and freight).

It holds 867 tiles in one 122 colour palette: 13 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 48 kinds of still object plus ten animated ones, and three finished sample maps. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler and the browser Verdant Playground.

The Mine Works Tunnels cut through dark rock, with a wall face along every north wall and a second wall set whose face carries gold veins, so a seam can run along a drift and around a stope and stop where the plain rock starts.

A rail network of ten pieces: straights both ways, four curves, a crossing, a buffer stop, and a switch drawn in both states, the points set straight or turned with a pale or red target on its stand to say which.
