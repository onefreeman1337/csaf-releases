Waterfalls you build on the 16x16 grid. A river crosses the upland, runs white through rapids, goes over the edge and falls down a cliff of any height into a plunge pool, and every piece of it is a tile that animates. 489 tiles in one 22-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it The fall column in 4 shapes (single, left, middle, right): one tile that repeats down the cliff with no seam, so a cliff is as tall as you paint it, and a river of any width gets a fall of the same width.

Lips where a river goes over the edge (over a tall cliff, or over Scarp's one-tile cliff), landings at the cliff foot, and plunge pools whose tile cuts the pool's bank so the white water runs straight in. Rapids flowing north to south and west to east, on the upland or the meadow, with their banks.

3 complete 47-mask sets (329 autotile tiles): water on the upland and water on the meadow, both animated, and the upland edge. A tall cliff face: 4 rock variants that meet on every side, plus its top edge, ends, foot and a step where it meets a one-tile cliff (22 pieces).
