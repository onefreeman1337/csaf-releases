Verdant 26 Deep Ocean is a 16x16 pixel art tileset of the sea floor for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4. It paints a lit shell sand shelf, abyssal ooze, a coral reef, a kelp forest, a drowned city's pavement, talus, columnar vent rock and a trench that drops into black. It holds 1052 tiles, a sample map and ready importers. Commercial use in unlimited projects, no attribution required.

The free Verdant 00 Series Sampler carries one complete autotile set from this pack, so you can try it first. What is in it 32 fill tiles: 8 materials, 4 variants each, on a value ladder so a deep water palette stays readable. 13 complete 47 mask autotile sets, 611 tiles: inner corners, outer corners, edges, junctions and singles, 58% of the pack.

75 objects, 51 distinct silhouettes: coral, sponges, anemones, a giant clam, kelp, a galleon's stern, an anchor, a cannon, chests, amphorae, columns, a statue, a whale skull and ribs, a diver's helmet and vent chimneys. Each ships transparent and grounded with its contact shadow. 10 animations, 72 frames: 8 caustic light overlays you lay over your own sea floor, plus a swaying anemone and a vent plume.
