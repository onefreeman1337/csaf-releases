Verdant Wonders is a 16x16 pixel art tileset volume for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: three fantasy landmarks in one download, the Poison Fen, the Sky Isles and the Great Tree. It holds 909 tiles in one 111 colour palette: 9 complete 47 shape autotile sets (four of them animated water), 28 kinds of still object plus four animated ones, and a finished 40x24 sample map for each landmark.

Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler and the browser Verdant Playground. The Poison Fen A clear stream runs out of the sedge into a lake, and a purple poison plume spreads inside it.

Poison and clear water are both animated water, and the edge where poison meets clear water is its own transition set, so the plume can take any shape. Poison pools also lie open in the bog mud and on the sedge, each with its own bank. Objects: a dead snag, bones, a skull warning post, dead reeds, toxic toadstools, a leaking cask, stepping stones, poison bubbles and a toxic vent that puffs. 654 tiles.
