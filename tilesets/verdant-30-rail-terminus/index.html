Verdant 30 is a 541 tile 16x16 train station tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4. It covers a railway terminus and the yard behind it: track in its ballast bed, raised platforms, the concourse hall, forecourt setts, weeds and cinder. Commercial use is permitted in unlimited projects with no attribution required. Do not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it 329 transition tiles in 7 complete 47 mask autotile sets: every inner corner, outer corner, edge, junction and single, checked on every build. A platform that reads as raised: a yellow edge line on its north and west shoulders, a brick face on the south and a shadow on the east.

The concourse and the setts both meet the platform, and weeds come through the setts as well as the ballast, so one floor lays over another with no false edge. 8 materials, 4 variants each, on a value ladder so the station does not turn into one grey.

48 objects across 116 tiles: a ticket kiosk, turnstiles, lockers, a newspaper stand, benches, lamps, a clock, luggage trolleys, churns, mail sacks, buffer stops, a semaphore signal, a water crane, a handcar and more.
