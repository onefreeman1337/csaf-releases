Verdant 66 Bounds: Connecting Fence Tileset with Gates on Every Side [16x16] is a tileset pack for Unity, RPG Maker, Tiled and Godot, paid.

Connecting fences for a 16x16 map: 729 tiles in one 55-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. A gate on every side Every material has a gate in a west-east run AND a gate in a north-south run, each in four states: closed, opening, opening, open.

The north-south pieces are drawn for the 3/4 view rather than turned on their side, and that gate swings open to the east so you see its face as it opens. That is 216 gate frames across the pack. Every junction 8 materials: split rail, white picket, wattle hurdle, stained plank, dry-stone wall, wrought iron, post and wire and bamboo.

Each is a complete connecting set of 16 pieces: a single post, four end caps, both straights, four corners, four T junctions and the cross, plus broken and leaning pieces for ruins and abandoned farms.

What is in it 27 connecting sets: every material as a transparent overlay that goes on a layer above any ground (216 pieces), and again drawn on the Verdant grounds it belongs on, with a contact shadow under every post (513 pieces). 54 broken and 27 leaning pieces.
