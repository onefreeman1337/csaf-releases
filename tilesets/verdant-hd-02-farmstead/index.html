A working farm on one level, in four fields and a yard: grazed pasture, a laid hawthorn hedge on an earth bank, ploughed furrow, standing corn, cut stubble, a trodden farmyard and a duck pond — with 8 complete 47-mask autotile sets, 26 objects, animated water and a scarecrow whose rags move in the wind. The second pack in the Verdant HD line.

It shares Verdant HD 01 Highland's palette and cell exactly, so the two mix without a recolour — and a complete 47-mask boundary between them ships in this download, at 32px on both sides, with no upscale and no resampling anywhere. ⛔ Read this before you buy: there is NO RPG Maker MZ importer RPG Maker MZ uses 48-pixel tiles, and 48 ÷ 32 is 1.5.

Resampling pixel art by a fraction destroys it, so this pack ships no MZ sheet at all — not a reduced one, not a blurry one, none. The importer script refuses to write one, which is the correct behaviour and is why you are reading this now instead of discovering it after paying. Every 16x16 pack in the Verdant series (48 ÷ 16 = 3, an integer) ships a full A2 autotile sheet.

If you use MZ, buy one of those instead of this one.
