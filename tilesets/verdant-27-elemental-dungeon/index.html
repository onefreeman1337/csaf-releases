Verdant 27 Elemental Dungeon is a 16x16 pixel art tileset of a dungeon with four elemental halls (fire, ice, flora and storm) off one flagstone nave, for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4.

It holds 864 tiles (40 fill tiles, 611 transition tiles in 13 complete 47-mask autotile sets, and 213 object and animation tiles), a sample map, and ready importers: an RPG Maker MZ A2 autotile sheet with B to E object pages, Tiled.tsx tilesets and a Godot 4.tres TileSet.

Commercial use is allowed in unlimited projects, including paid games, with no royalty and no attribution required; the one thing you may not do is resell or redistribute the pack itself. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler gives away one complete autotile set from each of 16 paid Verdant packs, one of them from this pack, and the Verdant Playground lets you paint a 47-mask set in your browser.

864 tiles at 16x16 for a dungeon of four elemental halls: coursed firebrick with live coals, faceted ice, a moss floor with roots, and fractured storm slate, all opening off one dark flagstone nave, with a rune channel carrying each hall's light out across the floor.
