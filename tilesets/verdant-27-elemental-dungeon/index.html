Verdant 27 Elemental Dungeon is a 16x16 pixel art tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4: four halls of fire, ice, flora and storm off one nave. A rune channel carries each hall's light across the flagstone floor. It holds 864 tiles, a sample map and ready importers. Commercial use in unlimited projects, no royalty and no attribution required.

The free Verdant 00 Series Sampler carries one complete autotile set from this pack and from 15 other paid Verdant packs, so you can try the series first. What is in it 40 fill tiles: 10 materials, 4 variants each: flagstone, coursed firebrick with live coals, faceted ice, moss with roots, fractured storm slate, a granite hall wall and four rune channels. 13 complete 47 mask autotile sets, 611 tiles: 71% of the pack.

Each hall meets the nave, and the halls meet each other in a ring (ember, rime, storm, bloom), so every corner where two halls touch has a real transition. 56 objects: 47 props and 9 animated objects, one ornament metal per hall: gates, floor sigils, statues, ice crystals, coal heaps, an anvil, root knots, lightning rods, chests, urns and bones. Each ships cut out and grounded on its own floor with a contact shadow.
