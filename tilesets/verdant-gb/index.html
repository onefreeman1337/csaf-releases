Verdant GB is a 16x16 Game Boy style tileset for GB Studio, Tiled, Godot 4, RPG Maker MZ and Unity, drawn only in the four GB Studio shades (#071821 #306850 #86c06c #e0f8cf). Three environments in one download, the Overworld, the Town and the Dungeon: 569 tiles, 10 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 37 objects and three sample scenes, each already a GB Studio background that stays under the 192 unique tile limit.

Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, Game Boy ROMs included, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler (full colour Verdant tiles). Built for the Game Boy tile budget A GB Studio background can hold at most 192 unique 8x8 tiles, and busy art runs out fast.

Every autotile set here is built from 8x8 quarters: each quarter of a tile depends only on its own three neighbours, and every texture repeats every 8 pixels, so a whole 47 shape set is drawn from thirteen distinct 8x8 tiles. The three sample scenes (320x288 each) count 140, 153 and 99 unique tiles of 192, so there is room left for your own. The gallery shows every one of those tiles.
