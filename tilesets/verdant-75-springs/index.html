Pools held by their terraces, pouring down the slope Paint a hillside of orange runoff, then two raised levels inside it: cream travertine, and a white sinter crown inside that.

Each level is a complete 47 mask autotile and each is lighter than the one below, so the hill brightens as it climbs (94 tiles in the two). A level's whole south edge is the step down, a bright lip over a shaded rock face, and it turns every corner. Lay a drop on the tile below the edge and the step is a full tile tall, with its cast shadow on the ground below.

Put a turquoise pool on a level against its south edge (each level has its own pool set, so the rim is that level's ground) and lay dams along the edge under it: the water runs right up to the lip. Wherever it should go over, use a pour in place of a dam: the water spills over the lip in an animated sheet, falls the full step and splashes into the pool you start on the row below. 7 dams, 7 pours and 16 drops in all.
