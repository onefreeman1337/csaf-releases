Verdant 02 Elevation is a 16x16 pixel art elevation tileset (raised plateaus and cliffs seen from above), for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity 6.

It holds 149 tiles: 11 fill tiles, 94 transition tiles in 2 complete 47-mask autotile sets, 39 cliff tiles and 5 props, with an RPG Maker MZ A2 autotile sheet with a B object page, Tiled.tsx tilesets, a Godot 4.tres TileSet and a Unity folder that builds Tiles and RuleTiles in your project.

Commercial use is allowed in unlimited projects, including paid games, with no royalty; attribution is welcome but never required, and the one thing you may not do is resell or redistribute the pack itself. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler gives away one complete autotile set from each of 16 paid Verdant packs and the Verdant Playground lets you paint a 47-mask set in your browser.

Height that actually reads as height, from directly above. Top-down elevation is hard for one specific reason: a grey band drawn between two identical lawns is not a cliff, it is a path. We know because Verdant 01 built a 19-tile cliff set and cut it — six iterations, each fixing a real defect, each still reading as a trench.
