Verdant 02 — Elevation Tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot & Unity [16x16] is a tileset pack for Unity, RPG Maker, Tiled and Godot, paid.

Height that actually reads as height, from directly above. Top-down elevation is hard for one specific reason: a grey band drawn between two identical lawns is not a cliff, it is a path. We know because Verdant 01 built a 19-tile cliff set and cut it — six iterations, each fixing a real defect, each still reading as a trench. This pack is what that diagnosis turned into.

Three cues carry elevation at 16px, and each one was A/B tested against a control before a single tile was drawn — ten arms over two rounds: A lighter plateau surface. Necessary and, on its own, not sufficient. A crest-to-foot falloff across four separated ramp steps down the cliff face. A cast shadow on the ground, offset away from the light and with a broken lower edge.

Two complete elevation systems The plateau step — a 47-mask autotile. Paint any region and the set supplies every edge, every outer corner and every inner corner. Unlike an ordinary blob set these edges are directional: a lit rim to the north and west, a shadowed face to the east, a real drop with a cast shadow to the south. That is what makes a plateau read as a landform instead of as a lighter rectangle of grass.
