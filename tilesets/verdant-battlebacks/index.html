Verdant Battlebacks is 40 pixel art battle backgrounds for RPG Maker MZ, with 1280x720 and VX Ace sets: 10 biomes, each at dawn, day, dusk and night. Commercial use is permitted in unlimited projects with no attribution required. Do not resell the art itself as an asset pack.

What is inside 40 scenes · 10 biomes × 4 times of day · 2 layers per scene · RPG Maker MZ 1000x740 · MZ at 1280x720 1568x853 · VX Ace 580x444 · flat PNGs for any engine No plugin and no script in MZ. Copy two folders into img/ and choose the battleback in Map Properties or with Change Battle Background, front view or side view. A battle that looks like the map it started on.

Each ground is a Verdant tileset's own shipped tiles laid out in perspective, and the props at the front are its own shipped props, at the 3x scale MZ draws a 16x16 map. You do not need the tilesets to use this pack. Two layers, the way RPG Maker draws them. battlebacks1 is the sky and ground, battlebacks2 is everything standing up. Every scene puts its horizon on the same row, so any backdrop sits on any ground.

A set sized for 1280x720.
