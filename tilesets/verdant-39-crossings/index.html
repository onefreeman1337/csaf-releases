Verdant 39 is a 592 tile 16x16 terrain transition tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4. Ground meets ground with no strip of grass in between: a dirt road runs onto a beach, a pond sits in sand, snow lies on stone. 564 of the 592 tiles are transitions. Commercial use is permitted in unlimited projects with no attribution required. Do not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it 564 transition tiles in 12 complete 47 mask autotile sets, every inner corner, outer corner, edge, junction and single in each. 6 complete Verdant 01, so every pair of its five grounds now meets; 6 cross Verdant 03's snow, dune sand and ash into Verdant 01's grounds. 28 ground fills: dirt, sand, stone, water, snow, dune sand and volcanic ash, 4 variants each.

They are Verdant 01's and Verdant 03's own tiles, pixel for pixel, so the new edges match a map already built with those packs. The pack also works on its own. A 40x28 sample map, Ground meets ground, using all 12 sets and no grass at all, as PNG, as JSON and as a Tiled.tmx. Good to know Each transition tile shows two grounds. Where three meet, nest them: the pond in the stone, the stone in the dirt.
