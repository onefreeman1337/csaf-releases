Verdant Hex 01 is a 32x32 pointy-top hex tileset in pixel art, for Godot 4 and Tiled (RPG Maker MZ cannot draw hex maps, so there is no MZ importer).

It holds 485 tiles: 24 terrain fills, 315 edge tiles (all 63 edge shapes for each of the 5 terrains laid over grass, each on its own layer, so any terrain can meet any other), 128 road and river tiles that join in any direction, and 9 features in two variants each, with a Godot 4.tres TileSet, 9 Tiled.tsx tilesets and a sample map.

Commercial use is allowed in unlimited projects, including paid games, with no royalty and no attribution required; the one thing you may not do is resell or redistribute the pack itself. There is no free hex sample; the free Verdant 00 Series Sampler covers the square 16x16 Verdant packs.
