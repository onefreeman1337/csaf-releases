492 tiles · 4 seasons · 8 complete 47-mask autotile sets · one locked 54-colour palette Most tile packs give you a place. This one gives you a year. The same meadow, the same track, the same pond and the same tree — authored four times, in spring, summer, autumn and winter, on one 16x16 grid and one palette. Paint a map once and you can show it in any month. The pond freezes.

What you get 48 terrain fills — meadow, track and pond, 4 seasons x 4 variants each 376 autotile tiles — 8 complete 47-mask blob sets, 376 mask slots, none missing 56 prop tiles — 16 distinct objects, each shipped transparent and grounded 12 animated tiles — the pond surface, 4 seasons x 3 frames 16 distinct objects and 492 tile files are different numbers and both are true: a tree is a 2x2 footprint, and every object ships twice.

The objects, in all four seasons A tree — blossom, full leaf, orange, then bare branches with snow lying on the upper side of each limb. A shrub. A rock. And one object that belongs to its season alone: a flower clump, a stand of grass gone to seed, a swept leaf pile, a wind-carved drift. The rock is deliberately the same rock in all four seasons — only what lies on it changes.
