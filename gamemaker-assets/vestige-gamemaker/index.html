Vestige: Achievement System and Medals for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Vestige is an achievement system for GameMaker that strikes a medal for every award from 42 devices, 12 blanks, 6 rims and 7 alloys, in pure GML. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. Define an award by its name, category and tier, and Vestige builds its medal from those properties: a blank, a rim, a device sunk into the field, a legend around the edge and a ribbon in the category colours.

Two awards never share a medal, and the same award strikes the same medal on every machine. What is inside 42 devices · 12 blanks · 6 rims · 7 alloys · 8 ribbons · 6 case papers · pure GML source ·.yymps package with a demo room One lamp lights every outline, so each device reads as raised, struck metal. Seven alloys from iron to adamant: a tier ladder a player can read with the name covered.

Counters and tiered chains: one shared counter can drive linked awards at 10, 100 and 1000. Prerequisites, secret awards with three states, points and completion per category. Save and load as one string that skips ids you have since renamed and never replays last session's toasts. A trophy case with category tabs, progress bars, paging and empty wells for the awards still to earn.
