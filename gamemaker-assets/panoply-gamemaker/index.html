Panoply — Loot & Gear Cards for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Panoply is a loot and gear system for GameMaker that draws its own item cards: 900 base variants, 40 affix glyphs and seeded rolls, in pure GML with no sprites. Every rolled item gets a card drawn from the roll itself: silhouette, material colours, affix glyphs on a rail and a drawn rarity treatment. Use it in unlimited commercial games, royalty free.

What is inside 30 base forms · 6 materials · 5 rarities · 900 base variants · 40 affix families · 3 example sets A card for every roll. A PNG pack cannot draw a card for an item that was just rolled; Panoply draws one each time, at 90 px on an inventory wall or 430 px as a drop reveal. It recolours from data. Add a seventh material with fourteen numbers and every base gains a variant.

Content you add gets art for free. Register a base form or an affix as a name plus a list of strokes, and it rolls, renders and titles like everything shipped. The roll is the save format. Rolls are seeded (xorshift32, never random()), so an item saves as a seed and a version and comes back identical, card included. Stats and sets. Computed stats, a loadout, and set detection with bonus tiers at piece counts.
