Cloudshadow - Drifting Cloud Shadows for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

The shadows of passing clouds, drifting across your rooms. Cloudshadow reads the room you already built (which cells are water, roof, cliff, wall or open ground) and draws the weather's shadow over it every frame, as one quad.

Nothing to author, no sprite to paint, and nothing your game draws is replaced: what is under a cloud is darkened and cooled, the way ground looks when a cloud takes the sun. // put obj_cls_shadow on a layer above your ground: that is the installation.

Then, if you like: cls_surface_layer("Tiles_Harbour", "water"); // say what a tile layer is (names like Water, Roof, Cliff are read for you) cls_set_sky("squall", 6); // crossfade to a squall line over six seconds var my_light_lost = cls_shade_at(x, y); // 0 in full sun..
