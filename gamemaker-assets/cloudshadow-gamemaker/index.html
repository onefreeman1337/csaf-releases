Cloudshadow - Drifting Cloud Shadows for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Cloudshadow is a GameMaker package that reads your room and draws 13 skies of drifting cloud shadow over it, every frame, as one quad. Use it in unlimited commercial or free games, with a painted demo room in the download. Put obj_cls_shadow on a layer above your ground and that is the installation.
