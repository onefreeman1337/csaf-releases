The visual layer above your game's relationship system. Tether draws a chart page where every character is a medallion and every bond between two of them is a CORD: its material is the kind of bond, its weight is how strong it is, and its knots are the events that changed it. Pure GML, no image files. Most relationship systems end in a list of names and numbers.

Tether keeps your system and draws what it reports: love as round red silk, friendship as laid rope, an oath as a steel chain, a rivalry as barbed wire, family as a ribbon that turns over as it runs. A strained bond frays, its chain link hangs open, its wire snaps.
