Affliction is a status effect system for GameMaker with 960 icons it draws in code: burn, chill, toxin, shock, mind and body. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. Define an affliction once as a struct. Affliction applies it, stacks it, ticks it, resists it and cleanses it, and from that same definition it draws the icon, the HUD chip and an overlay on the afflicted character.

A poison that intensifies looks worse as it gets worse, with nothing extra for you to draw. What is inside 40 status marks · 6 family frames · 4 severities · 960 icons · 10 tints · 8 overlay fields · 10 interaction chains · pure GML source ·.yymps package with a demo room Three stacking modes: every application on its own clock, refresh, or intensify, where each stack raises the severity.
