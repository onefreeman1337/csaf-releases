Beacon — Procedural Energy Renderer for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

A sprite sheet cannot connect two things that move. The moment a bolt has to start at a wand and end at whichever enemy is currently closest, pre-rendered lightning has to stretch to an arbitrary length and rotate to an arbitrary angle — and stretched lightning reads as stretched lightning.

Chain arcs that jump between four live targets, a leash that sags between two moving instances, a trail that follows an actual flight path: none of them have a fixed length or a fixed angle, so none of them can be a fixed image. Beacon generates the light instead, in pure GML.

Lightning, beams, lasers, chains, tethers and motion trails are built as geometry every frame from a handful of primitives — no atlas to re-export, no resolution to outgrow, and a colourway you change by assigning a struct field.

Six verbs, one system bolt · beam · laser · chain · tether · trail This is the part that makes it a system rather than six snippets: every effect compiles to the same intermediate — a BeaconPath — and one renderer draws them all. Retune the renderer and every effect in your game changes together. Write a new generator that emits a BeaconPath and it inherits the entire look for free.
