Armoury - Weapons That Forge Themselves is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Armoury is a GameMaker weapon system in pure GML: 37 sword, axe, mace, hammer and polearm forms, drawn from data at runtime, with no sprites in the package. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free, with no royalties.

What is inside 37 forms · 7 provenance classes · 20 materials · 8 pommels, 8 guards, 5 grips · raw GML · 1 demo room 18 hand blades and 19 axe, mace, hammer and polearm heads, built from three arguments: form, provenance and a seed. Wear that changes the shape. Notches are cut out of the outline where a blade takes damage, and a well kept blade is ground narrower over the years.

Corrosion toward a real target: iron rusts, bronze turns to verdigris, silver tarnishes, gold stays bright. A generated name, a description and a stat line, with mass and balance computed from the weapon's own dimensions. Inventory icons at any cell size, and opt in rendering to a sprite you own. The same weapon and seed over forty fights. The kept row loses width, the neglected row gains notches and rust.

Icons drawn at 32 px. Long weapons lie on the diagonal so a spear stays readable; the same four at 48 and 64 px below.
