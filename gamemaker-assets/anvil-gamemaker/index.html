Want the whole set? A forging minigame for GameMaker, drawn in code: 6 weapon families, 5 metals Anvil is a hot metal forging minigame for GameMaker crafting, written in pure GML with no sprites. One commercial licence covers unlimited games, with no attribution required.

The player works a glowing bar toward a pattern generated from the weapon itself, reads the heat by the colour of the metal, reheats it in the hearth and quenches it at cherry red. The finished piece is drawn as they shaped it, and a quality score goes back to your game.

What is inside 6 weapon families · 5 metals · 4 quality grades · 3 difficulties · 24 documented functions · a demo armoury of 12 weapons Name a weapon the way your database does and the pattern is generated from it: the type picks the family (sword, dagger, axe, spear, mace, claw), the name picks the metal (steel, iron, bronze, mithril, blacksteel), and the seed and attack set its proportions.

The bar behaves like metal. Each blow moves it toward the chalked pattern, more at white heat and finer as it cools, and a blow struck too cold cracks it.
