Anvil - A Forging Minigame Drawn in Code, for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

A hot-metal forging minigame for your GameMaker crafting. The player works a glowing bar on the anvil toward a pattern generated from the weapon itself, reads the heat by the colour of the metal, reheats it in the hearth and quenches it at cherry red. The finished piece is drawn exactly as they shaped it, and a quality score goes back to your game. Pure GML, no image files. Most crafting ends in a progress bar.

Anvil is the step where the player actually makes the thing. Name a weapon the way your database does and the pattern is generated from it: the type picks the family (sword, dagger, axe, spear, mace, claw), the name picks the metal (steel, iron, bronze, mithril, blacksteel), the seed and attack pick its proportions.
