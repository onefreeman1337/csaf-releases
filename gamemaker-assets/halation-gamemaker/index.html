Halation is a lens flare system for GameMaker 2024.14 with 80 designs: 8 lens kits by 10 light kinds, every ghost, spike and ring drawn in pure GML. A light declares its kind and colour, and the package generates its flare through the lens you choose. One commercial licence covers all your games, and a free Windows demo of the whole thing is on this page.

What is inside 80 flare designs · 8 lens kits · 10 light kinds · 5 occlusion modes · glints · a demo room · raw GML source Eight lenses: Vintage Prime, Anamorphic Cinema, Cheap Phone, Space Telescope, Fantasy Crystal, Retro Pixel, Night Vision and Dreamy Soft. Make your own from any of them.

Ten lights: sun, moon, torch, lantern, neon tube, muzzle flash, magic orb, eye glint, headlamp and explosion, each with its own colour temperature and flicker. Real optics: ghosts string along the axis through your frame's centre and take the shape of the aperture. A 7-blade lens draws 14 spikes, as a real one does.

It knows what is in the way: objects, a tile layer, a mask surface, a line of sight or your own function. A sun half behind a column gives half its flare, and it fades.
