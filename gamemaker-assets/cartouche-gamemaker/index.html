Cartouche - The Message Box Drawn as an Object, for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

The message box drawn as an object, and a different object for every speaker. Cartouche generates each speaker's dialogue box in pure GML: what its frame is made of, how it is built, the bands along its rails, its corners, a nameplate struck into the head rail, a sunk panel for a portrait, a ground under the text and its own advance caret. The king's box is not the goblin's box, and neither is the innkeeper's.

No image files. Most games draw every line of dialogue in one box, whoever is talking. A window skin pack gives you a few more boxes to choose from by hand. Cartouche reads who is speaking - their kind, their standing, their name - and builds the box from that, so a whole cast gets boxes that differ in material, construction and ornament without you drawing or assigning a single one.
