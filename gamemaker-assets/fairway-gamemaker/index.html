Fairway - Mini Golf for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Fairway is a mini golf system for GameMaker 2024.14 in pure GML, with a runnable demo room. Every hole is generated from a seed and drawn by the package: felt shaded by its own slopes, mower stripes, rails in six materials, water, sand, ramps, pipes, bumpers, a windmill with turning sails, swing gates, spinners and the scenery round the course.

Six themes, courses of 9 or 18 holes, up to four players with AI rivals, a drawn paper scorecard, and a par for every hole set by a shot planner that plays the hole first. No image files ship. One commercial licence covers all your games. Use it for a whole mini golf game, a festival or arcade minigame inside a bigger game, or a party mode.
