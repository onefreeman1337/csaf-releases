Sit your player at a money-changer's counter and give them a weighing puzzle on a brass balance: find the false coin in a hoard in the fewest weighings, or weigh out an exact order with a box of weights. The counterfeiter answers every weighing, so a lucky guess never wins. Every coin, weight and pan is drawn by the package. Pure GML.

The twelve-coin puzzle is one of the oldest puzzles there is, and in most games it is a text box. Scales makes it a counter: the hoard laid out in a numbered tray, the coins moved onto the pans by click or key, the beam released and swinging until it settles, the ledger keeping every weighing, and at the end the false coin cut open to show the base metal under its face.
