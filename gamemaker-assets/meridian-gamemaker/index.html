Meridian - Procedural Skill Trees for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Your skill tree needs forty icons. This one draws them. Every progression system you can buy hands you the graph and the points economy, and then asks you for an icon per node. A modest tree has forty nodes. Forty icons is a commission, an art pack, or forty evenings — and it is exactly why so many otherwise-finished skill trees ship with forty coloured squares in them. Meridian draws the marks itself.

Sixty hand-authored sigil motifs across eight discipline palettes — 480 distinct marks — generated from geometry and colour maths at draw time. There is not a single PNG in the package. No atlas, no texture page, nothing to disturb your project's sprite packing, and every mark is exact at 24 px and at 240 px because there is no image to scale.

New in 1.1: Armature, the authoring layer Meridian could always build a tree from a few lines of GML. Armature is the part that lets you draw one. It is a room that ships inside the package: click a node to select it, drag between two nodes to make one require the other, hold a key to add a child, and step every field with the arrow keys while the generated mark redraws beside it.
