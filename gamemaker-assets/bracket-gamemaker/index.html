Foxhole: 14 Animated PSX WW2 Soldiers, Allied and Axis is a GameMaker asset for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Foxhole is also sold on the Fort Vale page, together with the Fort Vale modern soldiers and three more PSX packs on the same skeleton. Foxhole is 14 low poly PS1-style Second World War soldiers of four armies for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get Infantry of four armies, American, British, German and Soviet, 14 soldiers: US rifleman, German rifleman, British rifleman, Soviet rifleman, US sergeant, German machine gunner, Soviet sniper, British officer, US paratrooper, German squad leader, Soviet tank crewman, British commando, US medic and Soviet field medic. Every one an adult, each a different body and uniform.

Every helmet and cap is modelled, not painted: a round dome, a flat dish, a flared steel helmet, a high Soviet dome, a padded tank helmet, a peaked service cap, a field cap and a beret, so the armies read apart by silhouette.
