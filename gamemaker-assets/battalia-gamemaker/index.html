Battalia - Army Battles for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Battalia is an army battle system for GameMaker 2024.14 in pure GML, with a runnable demo room.

You declare two armies of regiments (foot, pikes, archers, horse and mages, any number of soldiers), and Battalia draws every soldier, every regimental banner and the battlefield itself, lets the player choose a formation from four cards, then plays the clash out soldier by soldier and hands the survivors back to your code.

Inside: 5 arms in 4 poses in any army's colours, banners struck from each regiment's name (16 ordinaries, 14 charges, 4 cloths), 5 battlefields painted from a seed, 4 formations, a war record with the course of the battle charted. No image files ship; everything is drawn by the package. One commercial licence covers all your games.
