Table Service - A Restaurant Serving Minigame for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

A restaurant serving minigame for any GameMaker room. Customers walk in from your door, sit at your tables, ring for service and wait while their candle burns down; the player takes orders, carries dishes from the kitchen pass two at a time and serves the right table before patience runs out. At closing time a ledger page counts the takings and tips and gives the night up to three stars.

Pure GML; every customer, dish and table is drawn by the package. Use it as a tavern job in an RPG, a cafe shift in a life sim, a festival minigame, or the base of a whole restaurant game.
