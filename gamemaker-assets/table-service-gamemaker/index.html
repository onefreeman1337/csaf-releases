Showa Dusk: 14 Animated PSX Japanese Horror Townsfolk

A GameMaker asset from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Showa Dusk: 14 Animated PSX Japanese Horror Townsfolk is a GameMaker asset for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Showa Dusk is 14 low poly PS1-style residents of a small rural Japanese town after dark, for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get 14 townsfolk for a Japanese horror map: a salaryman, a night-shift nurse, a rice farmer with a towel headband, an office worker, a konbini night clerk in a striped uniform shirt, a village grandmother in a quilted jacket and monpe, a schoolteacher, a patrol officer, a delivery rider, a school caretaker and a shrine caretaker in samue work clothes, plus three urban-legend figures: a faceless man in a suit, a woman in a white surgical mask and a drowned woman.

Every one an adult, each a different body and painted outfit. Japanese from the clothes: the costumes carry the setting by cut and colour, with no flags, signs or text, so they drop into the Japanese town, school and shrine environments already on the shelf.
