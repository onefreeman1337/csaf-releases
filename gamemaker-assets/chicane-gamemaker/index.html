Every circuit is generated from a seed and drawn by the package, and the race layer every GameMaker racing project ends up hand-rolling is done: live positions, laps and sector times, checkpoint gates that refuse a cut corner, a computed racing line with braking points, AI drivers that follow it, overtake and defend, and a broadcast-style HUD.

Use it for a whole racing game, a kart minigame inside an RPG, or a chase level.
