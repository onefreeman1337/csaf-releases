Repast is a cooking system for GameMaker that draws every dish it makes from 46 ingredient forms, 24 substances and 12 cook states, in pure GML. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. Give it a recipe and how well it was cooked, and it hands back a plated dish: the vessel, the bed, the main ingredient in the state the heat left it in, the sides, the garnish it earned and the steam.
