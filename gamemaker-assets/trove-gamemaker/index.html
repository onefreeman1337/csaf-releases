Trove ships both halves, and the pictures are code.

An icon here is not an image. It is a short list of polygons, discs and strokes on a ten-by-ten grid, coloured by role — blade, grip, accent, shade, light — with a material palette supplying the actual colours at draw time. 75 item forms × 12 material palettes = 900 distinct icons Weapons, armour, supplies, arcane and materials. An iron sword and a gold sword are the same nine polygons.

Your thirteenth material is one line of code. Exact at any size. The same geometry is crisp at 18px in a hotbar and at 512px on a shop banner. There is nothing to resample, and no second asset anywhere in the package. Nothing on your texture page. No PNG, no atlas, no sprite.
