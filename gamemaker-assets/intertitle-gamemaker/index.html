Want the whole set? Ashfall is also sold on the Fort Vale page, together with 14 PSX modern soldiers on the same skeleton. Ashfall is 14 low poly PS1-style post-apocalyptic raiders and wastelanders for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get The people of an ash-covered wasteland years after the collapse, 14 of them: raider boss, scavenger, gas mask trooper, wasteland medic, trader, dust prophet, mechanic, hunter, scarred brute, sniper, pit fighter, nomad, drifter and settlement elder. Every one an adult, each a different body and outfit, in dusty, patched, worn clothes.

Raiders and settlers in one cast: enemies, traders, quest givers and a town elder share one skeleton and one set of clip names, so one animation script drives the whole wasteland.
