Sit your player down across a tavern table from one of your characters and play one of four old board games for a stake: Nine Men's Morris, Fox and Geese, Oware or Fanorona. The opponent thinks, at five strengths, without ever stalling a frame. Every board, every man and every seed is drawn by the package. Pure GML plus one shader. A tavern in an RPG is usually a menu.

Taproom makes it a table: the old man in the corner always sets out the same scratched slate, the captain plays Oware on tooled leather with cowrie shells, and the player's record against each of them is kept.
