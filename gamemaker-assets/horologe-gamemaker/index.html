Horologe - Mechanisms That Draw Themselves is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

A picture of a gear cannot mesh with another picture of a gear. The clockwork shelf for GameMaker is static art. Horologe is the mechanism instead: wheels, pinions, escapements, dials and hands computed from real tooth counts and drawn in pure GML. There is no sprite, no spritesheet, no atlas and no texture page in this package. sprites/ does not exist.

12 machines · 8 tooth profiles · 10 crossing patterns · 8 escapements · 6 oscillators · 10 dials · 10 hands · 8 frames · 6 drives · 14 materials A wheel is a number, and everything follows from it pitch radius r = module * N / 2 circular pitch p = pi * module <- equal on both wheels of a mesh angular pitch a = TAU / N ratio to a mate -N_this / N_that Change a wheel from 96 teeth to 60 and the teeth get coarser and the hand it drives runs faster, because the drawing and the ratio come out of the same number.
