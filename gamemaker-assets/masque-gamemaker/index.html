Masque - Every Creature Carves Its Own Mask, for GameMaker is a GameMaker asset for GameMaker, paid.

Every creature carves its own mask. Masque reads the enemy and character records you already wrote and carves each one a mask - lit, painted, worn, inlaid and broken in its own way - in pure GML. The player fills a rack with the masks of what they have met, and any mask can be shown full screen as a creature's true face, carving itself as you watch. No image files. A mask pack ships five masks.

A face pack ships a hundred faces, and your own creatures are not among them. With Masque a Bog Wight and a Cinder Drake do not just get different labels: they get a different face structure, a different crown growing out of the brow, a different pigment, a different inlay and a different amount of wear, and every one of those traces back to a field in your records.
