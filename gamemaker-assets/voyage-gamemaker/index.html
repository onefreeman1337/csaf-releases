Voyage is a GameMaker sailing system that draws its own sea chart in pure GML, in 4 styles with 6 ship rigs. The wind sets your speed against your heading, a full hold is slower, cargo spoils, storms take the hull, and what you land moves the port's price. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

What is inside 4 chart styles · 6 ship rigs · 32 point compass roses · pure GML · GameMaker 2024.14+ A sea chart generated from a seed: inked coasts, hachured high ground, woods, compass roses, rhumb lines, a border, a named cartouche and a scale of leagues. 4 chart styles: vellum, admiralty, sepia and night.

6 ships drawn by rig, cog, sloop, dhow, junk, brig and galleon, flying your colour and facing the way she heads. Points of sail, cargo that spoils, storms and hull damage, and port prices that answer what you land. Sea routes that keep off the land, and a check for whether a point is on an island. Four chart styles. Six rigs, each facing the way she heads.
