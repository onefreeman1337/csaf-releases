Connecting fences for a 16x16 map: 729 tiles in one 55-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

A gate on every side

Every material has a gate in a west-east run AND a gate in a north-south run, each in four states: closed, opening, opening, open. The north-south pieces are drawn for the 3/4 view rather than turned on their side, and that gate swings open to the east so you see its face as it opens. That is 216 gate frames across the pack.

Every junction

8 materials: split rail, white picket, wattle hurdle, stained plank, dry-stone wall, wrought iron, post and wire and bamboo. Each is a complete connecting set of 16 pieces: a single post, four end caps, both straights, four corners, four T junctions and the cross, plus broken and leaning pieces for ruins and abandoned farms.

What is in it

  • 27 connecting sets: every material as a transparent overlay that goes on a layer above any ground (216 pieces), and again drawn on the Verdant grounds it belongs on, with a contact shadow under every post (513 pieces).
  • 54 broken and 27 leaning pieces.
  • Two sample maps, a smallholding and a town square, as PNG and tile-id JSON.
  • Proof renders: a 4x4 repetition of every straight (54 images), in the download.

Made for the Verdant ground you already have

The grounded pieces stand on Verdant 01 Overworld's grass and dirt and on Verdant 09 Town's cobbles, and the sample maps are painted on them. Those ground tiles are not in this download and the maps' JSON names every one. The overlay pieces work on any ground. For doors, chests and more gates that open as RPG Maker MZ events, see Verdant Openables; for the clutter around them, Verdant Props.

Engines

  • Godot 4: one TileSet with 27 terrain sets (Match Sides). Pick a fence terrain and paint with the Connect brush; corners, T junctions and the cross choose themselves. Loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 with all 729 tiles built, and the Connect brush painted 837 cells across every set on an empty layer with 0 wrong.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per set, each with an edge Terrain Set. Draw with it in the Terrains panel. Maps built from the shipped Terrain Sets alone rendered in Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer exactly as the pack's mask table says (810 cells).
  • RPG Maker MZ: every piece on the B, C, D object pages at MZ's 48px grid, placed piece by piece (no A2 autotile: MZ builds those from quarter tiles, which would cut posts and gates in half). The gates also ship as an object event sheet in the Verdant Openables layout. In a blank MZ project with the sheet installed, MZ's own event code (run headless, not the editor) played all 16 gate events closed to open in order.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantBoundsFences/ into your project and run Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 66 Bounds > Build Unity Assets. It builds 729 pixel exact sprites, one Tile each with its collider, and 27 Tile Palettes. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project in batch mode. In Unity fences are placed piece by piece; no rule tile brush ships for them.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and junction completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 55-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and every set carries all 16 pieces. The pieces are drawn from one fence geometry, and a test stitches whole fences (rings, crosses, T junctions) out of the tiles and compares them, pixel for pixel, with one drawing of the same fence.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The store images, the cover included, are composed from the tiles themselves.
