Verdant 66 Bounds is out: 729 tiles at 16x16 in one 55 colour palette, for Godot, Tiled, RPG Maker MZ and Unity. $4.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-66-bounds

8 materials: split rail, white picket, wattle hurdle, stained plank, dry stone wall, wrought iron, post and wire, and bamboo. Each one is a full connecting set of 16 pieces, so a single post, four end caps, both straights, four corners, four T junctions and the cross, plus broken and leaning pieces.

The part we wanted most: every material has a gate in a west to east run AND a gate in a north to south run, each in four states from closed to open. The north to south pieces are drawn for the 3/4 view instead of turned on their side, and that gate swings open to the east so you can see it open. 216 gate frames in all.

In Godot the 27 sets are terrain sets, so the Connect brush picks the corners and junctions for you (it painted 837 test cells with 0 wrong). In Tiled each set has an edge Terrain Set. In RPG Maker MZ the pieces are on the B to D pages and the gates also ship as an event sheet. 216 pieces are transparent overlays for any ground; the rest are drawn on Verdant 01 grass and dirt and Verdant 09 cobbles.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
