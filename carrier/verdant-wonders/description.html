Verdant Wonders is a 16x16 pixel art tileset volume for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: three fantasy landmarks in one download, the Poison Fen, the Sky Isles and the Great Tree. It holds 909 tiles in one 111 colour palette: 9 complete 47 shape autotile sets (four of them animated water), 28 kinds of still object plus four animated ones, and a finished 40x24 sample map for each landmark. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler and the browser Verdant Playground.

The Poison Fen

A clear stream runs out of the sedge into a lake, and a purple poison plume spreads inside it. Poison and clear water are both animated water, and the edge where poison meets clear water is its own transition set, so the plume can take any shape. Poison pools also lie open in the bog mud and on the sedge, each with its own bank. Objects: a dead snag, bones, a skull warning post, dead reeds, toxic toadstools, a leaking cask, stepping stones, poison bubbles and a toxic vent that puffs. 654 tiles.

The Sky Isles

Grassy and stony isles floating in open sky, each a complete 47 shape set. The underside is a separate drop piece in six shapes, the roots and rock hanging below the island, laid on the sky under every south edge. Rope bridges run east to west (deck and posts) and a rope span runs north to south between isles. Objects: wind-bent birches, a rune waystone, an anchor chain and a waving banner. 129 tiles.

The Great Tree

Mossy bark walkways high above a dark canopy. Every south edge of a branch is a lit face, and the branch's shadow falls on the leaves below (six shadow pieces). A hollow in the trunk sinks below the bark as a room with a heartwood floor. Objects: a treehouse, a rope ladder, a hammock and a lantern that flickers. 126 tiles.

What is in it

  • 32 ground tiles (four variants of each ground) and 799 transition tiles in 9 complete 47 shape sets, 4 of them animated water (3 frames, played 0,1,2,1).
  • 28 kinds of still object, the drop and shadow pieces included, and 4 animated objects (poison bubbles, a toxic vent, a banner, a lantern).
  • Three 40x24 sample maps, one per landmark, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.
  • The grounds under the landmarks are the series' own, byte for byte (67 tiles): Verdant 08 Mire's bog mud and sedge, Verdant 12 Scarp's meadow and upland, Verdant 37 Cloud Realm's sky. So these landmarks drop into maps made with those packs on the very same ground. You do not need those packs; everything is in this download.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: the four waters on an A1 page as MZ's own animated water (672 of 672 shape frames composed back with MZ's own autotile table), all 9 sets on an A2 page (432 of 432 shapes), and the grounds, objects and animation frames on the B, C, D, E pages. MZ animates only its A1 water, so the objects are stills there. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation written in at its own timing. Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew all three sample maps identical to ours (2,949,120 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 15 instants with the animations advanced, its playback matched our own render exactly.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (909 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantWonders/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant Wonders the Poison Fen, the Sky Isles and the Great Tree [16x16] > Build Unity Assets). Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: it built 909 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, and 9 RuleTile brushes drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (2,304 of 2,304); Unity's render of the sample scene matched the map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 and every sheet an exact multiple of it, every pixel is inside the 111 colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 9 sets carry all 47 shapes, the water sets in every frame.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover and every gallery image are made from the pack's own tiles; no image model drew any of them.
