A natural limestone cave you can build a level from: 675 tiles at 16x16 in one 68-colour palette: dark rock walls standing up from a pale cave floor, an underground lake with turquoise shallows that swell and draw back, silt banks, flowstone terraces, and cave mouths that open in the cliffs of the Verdant packs you already own. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • Cave mouths that fit your cliffs: one cut into this pack's own wall, and two transparent mouths (one for a one-tile face, one for a tall cliff) drawn in the same rock colours as the cliffs of Verdant 12 — Scarp, Verdant 02 Elevation and Verdant 38 — Cascades, checked colour by colour against those packs.
  • A breach into the dungeon: a set where the cave floor tears up through the flagstone of Verdant 05 — Depths. Depths' four flagstone tiles are included, so it works on its own.
  • A lake that moves: 2 animated transition sets (282 tiles, 3 key frames played 1, 2, 3, 2 at 400 ms a beat), the lake on stone and a pool on silt, plus every lake variant as an animated fill. The middle frame of each moving set is the static set, tile for tile, so you can paint the static one and swap the moving one in with no seam.
  • 564 transition tiles in 8 complete 47-mask sets: the cave wall (a lit face on its south edge, a lit shoulder on its north and west, its shadow on the floor), the lake, silt banks, a pool on silt, raised flowstone terraces and the breach.
  • 25 objects: stalagmite, tall stalagmite, stalagmite cluster, flowstone column, snapped stalagmite, boulder, large boulder, rubble, bone pile, glowing cave mushrooms, supply crate, rope coil, bedroll, collapsed timber, cave pearls in a rimstone cup, rimstone pool, drip pool, sinkhole, rope ladder down a shaft, stepping stones and a plank walkway for crossing the water, and the cave mouths. Each ships transparent for your own ground and grounded on ours, with a contact shadow drawn from that ground's own colours.
  • 2 animated objects: a lantern post and a torch on a tripod stand, 4 frames each at 160 ms.
  • 24 fill tiles: pale limestone, wet silt, dark wall rock, cream flowstone, the lake water and the Depths flagstone, 4 variants each.
  • A finished 40x26 sample map, The Drowned Grotto, with 47 objects placed, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: the moving lake on the A1 page in MZ's own animated-water layout and order; every static set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, so you paint and the edges draw themselves, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (384 of 384 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B, C and D sheets; MZ does not animate object tiles, so the lantern and torch frames are there as tiles.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant69Caverns/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 69 Caverns > Build Unity Assets). It builds 675 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 8 RuleTile brushes (the two lake brushes animated), 8 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (2,048 of 2,048), the animated brushes handed back every frame in order (512 of 512), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation and its timing written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,064,960 px, 0 differ), and, advanced to the middle of every beat, drew exactly our frames for the lantern, the torch and both lake sets (31 checks, 2,134,016 px). Transition tiles are placed by name; every tile's mask is in tiles.json.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (675 tiles built). The TileSet carries no animation; the frames are there as tiles.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 68-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 8 sets carry all 47 masks, the moving ones in every frame.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover is a designed title card over the pack's own tiles.
