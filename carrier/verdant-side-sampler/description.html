Verdant Side Sampler is 214 free 16x16 side-view platformer tiles for Godot 4 and Tiled, cut from the three paid Verdant Side volumes. Free for commercial use, no credit needed.

What is inside

214 tiles · 3 complete autotile sets · 30 slope pieces · 12 one-way platform pieces · 6 objects · 3 sample levels · PNG, Godot 4 and Tiled files · 16x16

  • From Verdant Side (65 tiles): grass-capped forest loam with all 47 shapes and 10 slopes, a branch platform, meadow flowers.
  • From Verdant Side Depths (70 tiles): brimstone rock with an ember-lit underside with all 47 shapes and 10 slopes, an iron grate platform, animated molten lava, a heap of skulls and obsidian shards.
  • From Verdant Side Village (79 tiles): cobbled street with a dressed kerb with all 47 shapes and 10 slopes, a market awning platform, a planter box of red and gold flowers, a wooden street bench and an iron street lamp with a flickering flame.
  • Three sample levels (32 by 12 cells) as Godot scenes, Tiled maps, JSON and PNG.

Every tile in the download. Each is the paid volume's own file under the same name, so the art you test with is the art the volumes sell.

The full volumes

This is one biome's ground from each volume, at most 10% of its tiles. Each paid volume is three biomes with back walls, climbables, parallax and finished levels, on the same grid:

  • Verdant Side, $8.99: Greenwood, Keep and Undercroft. 651 tiles, 39 kinds of object.
  • Verdant Side Depths, $8.99: Infernal Halls, Desert Tomb and Crypt. 703 tiles, 43 kinds of object.
  • Verdant Side Village, $8.99: Market Street, Walk-In Homes and Farm Outskirts. 807 tiles, 69 kinds of object.

From the paid volume Verdant Side Depths: the same rock and lava, built out with the brick hall, statues and props the sampler leaves out.

Works with

  • Godot 4 (built and run on 4.7.2): copy importers/Godot into your project and paint with the terrain brush. Collision, one-way platforms and animations are on the TileSet.
  • Tiled (drawn by Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer): open sample_maps/*.tmx.
  • Any other engine: PNGs, sheets and tiles.json with every collision polygon and autotile mask.

Full setup guide online.
