Verdant Side Sampler is out: 214 free 16x16 side view platformer tiles for Godot 4 and Tiled, cut from the three paid Verdant Side volumes. Name your own price, zero is fine, commercial use is permitted and no credit is needed. csaf.itch.io/verdant-side-sampler

It is a demo cut: one biome's ground from each volume, at most 10% of its tiles, so you can paint a level in your own engine tonight and see whether the family fits your game. Every tile is the volume's own file, byte for byte, under the same name, so the art you test with is the art the volumes sell.

  • Verdant Side (65 tiles, $8.99 for the full volume): grass capped forest loam with all 47 shapes and 10 slopes, a one way platform, and meadow flowers.
  • Verdant Side Depths (70 tiles, $8.99 for the full volume): brimstone rock with an ember lit underside with all 47 shapes and 10 slopes, a one way platform, molten lava as an animated hazard, and a heap of skulls and obsidian shards.
  • Verdant Side Village (79 tiles, $8.99 for the full volume): cobbled street with a dressed kerb with all 47 shapes and 10 slopes, a one way platform, and a planter box of red and gold flowers, a wooden street bench and an iron street lamp with a flickering flame.

That comes to 3 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 30 slope pieces, 12 one way platform pieces, 6 objects and three sample levels in one 65 colour palette. You get a Godot TileSet with collision, one way platforms and a terrain brush, Tiled tilesets with collision shapes and terrain sets, every tile as its own PNG, and a tiles.json with every collision polygon and autotile mask.

Measured on the shipped files: Godot 4.7.2 built 202 of 202 atlas tiles exactly as declared, its own terrain painter placed the right tile for 141 of 141 shapes, and a CharacterBody2D made 16 runs over the slopes, the platforms and the lava. Tiled 1.12.2 drew all three sample maps identical to ours.

The full volumes add what this leaves out: three biomes each with back walls, climbables, parallax and finished levels, 2,161 tiles together on the same grid, $8.99 each. If you would rather not start from the sampler, they are linked above.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag. The cover illustration was generated with an OpenAI image model; every gallery image is made from the tiles of the sampler and the three volumes.
