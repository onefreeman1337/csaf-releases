Verdant 65 Alien Worlds is out: 296 tiles at 16x16 in one 41 colour palette, for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot and Unity. $7.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-65-alien-worlds

Three biomes on one planet: a cratered plain of dusty mauve regolith, an acid sludge marsh, and a teal xeno jungle of bulb stalks and glow fronds. 5 complete 47 mask autotile sets join them, and one of the sets paints a sunken basin of any shape, with the near wall in shade, the far wall lit and a raised rim round the outside.

The part we worked on longest was the craters. Painted on a 16 pixel grid, a pit comes out square. So the round craters are objects, in 3 sizes, each a ring wall lit on its outer north west slope, with the rim casting a crescent of shadow across the floor. They sit in the ground, so they carry no contact shadow; the 12 raised objects, from a crashed ship's nose section to an escape pod and a cave mouth with a glowing throat, all do.

The sample map, The Crash Site, is 36 by 26 cells: the wreck lies at the end of the furrow it ploughed. It ships as a PNG, as JSON and as a Tiled .tmx. In Unity one menu command builds 296 Tiles and 5 RuleTile brushes, and every brush drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements. Nothing in this pack is animated.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
