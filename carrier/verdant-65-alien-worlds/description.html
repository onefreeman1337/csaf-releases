An alien planet for your map: 296 tiles at 16x16 in one 41-colour palette: a cratered plain of dusty mauve regolith where a ship came down, an acid sludge marsh, and a teal xeno jungle of bulb stalks and glow fronds. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • Round impact craters in 3 sizes, each a ring wall with its outer slope lit on the north-west, the inner wall facing the light bright, and the rim's shadow across the floor. Placed like objects, painted into the ground.
  • A sunken basin set: one of the 5 autotile sets paints a pit of any shape, its floor below the plain, the near wall in shade and the far wall lit, with a raised rim round the outside.
  • 15 objects: impact crater, small, impact crater, medium, impact crater, large, crashed ship, nose section, cargo crate, basalt boulder, cave mouth with a glowing throat, bulb stalk, glow frond fan, glow reeds, spined xeno shrub, glowing vent, torn hull plate, escape pod, emergency beacon. Each ships transparent for your own ground and grounded on ours; the raised ones carry a contact shadow drawn from that ground's own colours.
  • 235 transition tiles in 5 complete 47-mask sets: sludge pools on the plain, the jungle floor spreading over the regolith, a sludge pool inside the jungle, pale dust drifts, and the sunken basin.
  • 20 fill tiles: regolith, dust, basin floor, xeno jungle floor and acid sludge, 4 variants each.
  • A finished 36x26 sample map, The Crash Site, with 51 objects placed, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx. The demo GIF builds it layer by layer in the order you would paint it.
  • No animated tiles. Nothing in this pack moves, and nothing here claims to.

Engines

  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant65AlienWorlds/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 65 Alien Worlds > Build Unity Assets). It builds 296 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 5 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,280 of 1,280), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (958,464 px, 0 differ).
  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (240 of 240 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B and C sheets.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (296 tiles built).

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 41-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 5 sets carry all 47 masks.
