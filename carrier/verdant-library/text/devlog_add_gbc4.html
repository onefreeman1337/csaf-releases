Verdant GBC 4: Fantasy Interiors Tileset is now in Verdant Library: 730 Game Boy Color tiles at 16x16, across four fantasy interiors, an inn, a throne hall, a chapel and a wizard's study, sharing one household kit, each in a lit and a dark palette that differ by colour alone. It has 12 terrain sets, 77 objects and 8 animated pieces, and 8 GB Studio backgrounds ready to import, lit and dark, each with its .mono.png. The tiles split as The Wayside Inn 185, The Throne Hall 184, The Village Chapel 182, and The Wizard's Study 179. It is the first Game Boy Color volume of interiors in the series.

It is new here first. Verdant GBC 4 has no page of its own yet, so this library is the first place it ships. Its folder, handheld-palette/verdant-gbc-04-interiors/, holds 1,789 files: the finished, tested package its own page will carry, copied file for file and checked against that package's manifest.

If you already own the library, download it again at no extra cost and the new folder is there. That makes 103 packs on this page, 69,088 tiles in all.

The price for new buyers stays $19.99 for now. It becomes $24.99 when the library reaches 106 packs and $29.99 at 109, and each step will be announced here with its date at least five days before it happens. If you already own the library, you never pay again.
