Verdant Iso Rooms: Tavern, Cottage and Bookshop is now in Verdant Library: 179 isometric tiles on a 64x32 grid for three interiors, a tavern, a cottage and a bookshop. Each room has its own floors, rug, walls and furniture: 36 floor and rug fills (6 floors and 3 rugs), 42 rug transitions, 55 wall tiles at 64x96 and 46 prop tiles (29 pieces in two facings), with 5 animations of 4 frames (four hearths and a candle stand), all in one locked 112 colour palette. It ships importers for Tiled and Godot 4. It is the second isometric volume in the library, on the same 64x32 grid as Verdant Iso 01.
