Verdant Library is every paid Verdant tileset in one download: 98 packs and 60,551 tiles for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4. Commercial use permitted, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles as a tile pack. New Verdant tile packs join this download on their launch day. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler.

What is inside

98 packs · 60,551 tiles · 860 autotile sets · 40 battle backgrounds · PNG + engine files · 16x16, 32x32 and 64x32 isometric

  • Top down 16x16 (87 packs): overworld biomes, towns, castles, dungeons, caves, interiors and props.
  • Side view (3), hex (2), HD 32x32 (2), Game Boy palette (2) and isometric 64x32 (1) volumes, plus 40 battle backgrounds.
  • Every pack in its own folder, exactly as its own page delivers it: tiles, sheets, engine files, sample maps, its Readme and its licence.
  • New here first: Verdant Farmstead: Orchard, Vineyard & Farmyard (707 tiles, 10 autotile sets) has no page of its own yet, so this download is the first place it ships, as the tested package its own page will carry.
  • New here first: Verdant Breeze: Animated Grass and Trees Tileset (3,780 tiles, 8 autotile sets) has no page of its own yet, so this download is the first place it ships, as the tested package its own page will carry.
  • A catalogue (CATALOGUE.md and catalogue.json) naming every pack, its grid, its tile count and its engines.

Works with

  • Godot 4: a TileSet in 97 packs, each loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 from its folder inside this download.
  • Tiled: a .tsx in 97 packs, opened and rendered by Tiled's own tmxrasterizer.
  • RPG Maker MZ: tileset images in 90 packs; the A2 pages compose against MZ's own autotile table.
  • Unity imports in 57 packs and GameMaker imports in 19; CATALOGUE.md says which.

Try it free

The Verdant 00 Series Sampler is a free cut of Verdant tiles, so you can see the style in your engine first.

Licence and how it was made

Use the tiles in commercial and free games, no credit needed. Do not resell them as a tile pack. Each pack carries its own LICENSE.txt. The tiles are generated by code written with AI tools; the cover is a mosaic cut from the packs' own preview images.
