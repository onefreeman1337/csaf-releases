Verdant GBC Platformer: Meadow, Cave, Castle Tileset is now in Verdant Library: 451 Game Boy Color tiles at 16x16 for a side view platformer, laid out as 3 sample levels, each by day and by night. It has 6 terrain sets, 32 objects and 9 animations, and collision on 324 tiles, among them 12 one way platforms, 9 climbable tiles and 6 hazards. It ships 6 GB Studio backgrounds, each with its .mono.png; run through GB Studio's palette merge, which we rebuilt from its source, they need 7 palettes at worst and at most 174 of 192 unique 8x8 tiles. The tiles split as the Meadow 155, the Cave 144, and the Castle 152. It is the first side view volume of the Game Boy Color series.

It is new here first. Verdant GBC Platformer has no page of its own yet, so this library is the first place it ships. Its folder, handheld-palette/verdant-gbc-platformer-meadow-cave-castle/, holds 1,135 files: the finished, tested package its own page will carry, copied file for file and checked against that package's manifest.

If you already own the library, download it again at no extra cost and the new folder is there. That makes 104 packs on this page, 69,539 tiles in all.
