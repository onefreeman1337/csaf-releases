Verdant GBC Farm: Fields, Ranch, Barn Tileset is now in Verdant Library: 482 Game Boy Color tiles at 16x16 for a farm, in three environments: the fields with twelve crops through their growth stages, a ranch with a barn, silo, coop and windmill, and the barn interior. Four seasons and a night palette differ by colour alone. It has 6 terrain sets, 88 objects, 6 animated objects and 5 sample scenes. It ships 25 GB Studio backgrounds, each with its .mono.png; run through GB Studio's palette merge, which we rebuilt from its source, they need 7 palettes at worst and at most 173 of 192 unique 8x8 tiles. The tiles split as the Fields 161, the Ranch 175, and the Barn 146.

It is new here first. Verdant GBC Farm has no page of its own yet, so this library is the first place it ships. Its folder, handheld-palette/verdant-gbc-farm-fields-ranch-barn/, holds 2,904 files: the finished, tested package its own page will carry, copied file for file and checked against that package's manifest.

If you already own the library, download it again at no extra cost and the new folder is there. That makes 105 packs on this page, 70,021 tiles in all.
