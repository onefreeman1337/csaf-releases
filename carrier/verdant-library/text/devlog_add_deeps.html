It has 11 terrain sets, 39 still objects, twelve animated objects and three sample maps, all in one locked 136 colour palette, so the three settings share one map. It ships importers for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4, and Unity. The tiles split as the Dwarf Hold 271, the Ossuary 204, and the Drowned Vault 246.
