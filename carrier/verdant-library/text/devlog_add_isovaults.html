Verdant Iso Vaults is now in Verdant Library: 181 isometric tiles on a 64x32 grid for three underground rooms, a cellar, a crypt and a jail. Each room has its own floors, rug, walls and furniture: 36 floor and rug fills (6 floors and 3 rugs), 42 rug transitions, 63 wall tiles at 64x96 and 40 prop tiles, with 8 animations of 4 frames (wall lanterns and torches, a lantern on a crate and an iron candelabrum), all in one locked 128 colour palette. It ships importers for Tiled and Godot 4. It is the fourth isometric volume in the library and the third of interiors, on the same 64x32 grid as Verdant Iso 01, so its three rooms join the six of Verdant Iso Rooms and Verdant Iso Halls.

It is new here first. Verdant Iso Vaults has no page of its own yet, so this library is the first place it ships. Its folder, isometric/verdant-iso-vaults-cellar-crypt-jail/, holds 252 files: the finished, tested package its own page will carry, copied file for file and checked against that package's manifest.

If you already own the library, download it again at no extra cost and the new folder is there. That makes 111 packs on this page, 73,192 tiles in all.
