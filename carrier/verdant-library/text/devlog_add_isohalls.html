Verdant Iso Halls is now in Verdant Library: 174 isometric tiles on a 64x32 grid for three interiors, a smithy, a chapel and a great hall. Each room has its own floors, rug, walls and furniture: 36 floor and rug fills (6 floors and 3 rugs), 42 rug transitions, 55 wall tiles at 64x96 and 41 prop tiles, with 6 animations of 4 frames (the forge, the fireplace, the votive rack and the brazier), all in one locked 108 colour palette. It ships importers for Tiled and Godot 4. It is the third isometric volume in the library and the second of interiors, on the same 64x32 grid as Verdant Iso 01 and Verdant Iso Rooms, whose tavern, cottage and bookshop it sits beside.

It is new here first. Verdant Iso Halls has no page of its own yet, so this library is the first place it ships. Its folder, isometric/verdant-iso-interiors-smithy-chapel-great-hall/, holds 245 files: the finished, tested package its own page will carry, copied file for file and checked against that package's manifest.

If you already own the library, download it again at no extra cost and the new folder is there. That makes 110 packs on this page, 73,011 tiles in all.
