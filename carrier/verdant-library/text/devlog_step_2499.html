With Verdant Deeps, Verdant Library now holds 106 packs. When the price rule went on this page at 103 packs, it said the price for new buyers becomes $24.99 at 106 packs and $29.99 at 109. This post announces the first step and its date.

The price stays $19.99 until 4 December 2026. From 5 December 2026 it is $24.99 for new buyers.

If you already own the library, nothing changes for you. You never pay again, and every new Verdant pack still joins your download on its launch day.

The date falls after itch's Black Friday sale is expected to end on 3 December and more than a week before its Winter sale is expected to start on 17 December, so the price does not rise in the week before a sale.

The next step, to $29.99, comes when the library reaches 109 packs. It will be announced here the same way, with its date at least five days ahead.
