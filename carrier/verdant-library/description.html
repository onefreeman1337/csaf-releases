Verdant Library is every paid Verdant tileset in one download: 94 packs and 53,711 tiles for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4. Commercial use permitted, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles as a tile pack. New Verdant tile packs join this download on their launch day. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler.

What is inside

94 packs · 53,711 tiles · 793 autotile sets · 40 battle backgrounds · PNG + engine files · 16x16 and 32x32

  • Top down 16x16 (84 packs): overworld biomes, towns, castles, dungeons, caves, interiors and props.
  • Side view (3), hex (2), HD 32x32 (2) and Game Boy palette (2) volumes, plus 40 battle backgrounds.
  • Every pack in its own folder, exactly as its own page delivers it: tiles, sheets, engine files, sample maps, its Readme and its licence.
  • A catalogue (CATALOGUE.md and catalogue.json) naming every pack, its grid, its tile count and its engines.

Works with

  • Godot 4: a TileSet in 93 packs, each loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 from its folder inside this download.
  • Tiled: a .tsx in 93 packs, opened and rendered by Tiled's own tmxrasterizer.
  • RPG Maker MZ: tileset images in 87 packs; the A2 pages compose against MZ's own autotile table.
  • Unity imports in 54 packs and GameMaker imports in 19; CATALOGUE.md says which.

Licence and how it was made

Use the tiles in commercial and free games, no credit needed. Do not resell them as a tile pack. Each pack carries its own LICENSE.txt. The tiles are generated by code written with AI tools; the cover is a mosaic cut from the packs' own preview images.
