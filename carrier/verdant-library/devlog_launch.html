Verdant Library is out: every paid Verdant tileset in one download, 94 packs and 53,711 tiles for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot 4. $19.99. New Verdant tile packs join it on their launch day. csaf.itch.io/verdant-library

What is inside. 84 top down 16x16 packs (overworld biomes, towns, castles, dungeons, caves and interiors), 3 side view volumes, 2 hex volumes, 2 HD 32x32 volumes, 2 Game Boy palette volumes and 40 battle backgrounds. 793 autotile sets in total. Bought one by one, the packs list at over $900.

Every pack in its own folder. Each folder holds exactly what that pack's own page delivers today: tiles, sheets, engine files, sample maps, its Readme and its licence. We fetched every live build with butler and staged it as is, so each folder is the same set of files a buyer of that single pack gets today. A catalogue (CATALOGUE.md and catalogue.json) names every pack, its grid, its tile count and its engines.

How it was tested. Every tile pack was proved from its library folder: grid, palette lock, the seam fence and autotile completeness, 53,711 tiles. Every pack was then installed from its nested folder into blank projects: the TileSets loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2, the .tsx files opened and rendered by Tiled's own tmxrasterizer, and the A2 pages composed against MZ's own autotile table. The battle backgrounds went through their own battle scene check instead, pixel for pixel.

Engine files: Godot 93 packs, Tiled 93, RPG Maker MZ 87, Unity 54, GameMaker 19. The free Verdant 00 Series Sampler is the place to try the style first.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag. The cover is a mosaic cut from the packs' own preview images.
