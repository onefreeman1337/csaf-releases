Verdant 8-Bit is a 16x16 NES-style tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: 2,113 tiles of overworld, castle town, interiors, cavern and lava keep. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles as a tile pack. Free to try the series first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler.

What is inside

2,113 tiles · 22 complete 47 shape autotile sets (4 animated) · 18 wall and roof blocks · 107 objects and 13 animated pieces · a ship · 5 sample maps

  • The Overworld (570 tiles): animated sea and river shores, a waterfall, a bridge, mountains, forest, a castle, a lighthouse, a cave mouth and a dock.
  • The Castle Town (605): a castle gate, a drawbridge over an animated moat, a fountain, market stalls and three house styles built from roof and wall blocks.
  • The Interiors (269): beds and a fireplace, bookshelves and potion shelves, weapon racks and armour, an altar and pews.
  • The Cavern (327): rock walls, mine rails with turns and a minecart, crystals, ore, ladders and an animated pool.
  • The Lava Keep (342): animated lava and lavafalls, a lava bridge, a throne, gargoyles, spikes and a portcullis.

Built for RPG Maker MZ

Pages A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, B at 48x48, the Database row, a ship vehicle sheet and 5 sample maps. Water, lava and waterfalls sit on the A1 page, where MZ animates them itself. We ran RPG Maker MZ 1.10.0's own tilemap code on the shipped pages: 864 A2 shapes and 696 A1 shapes byte identical to our composition against MZ's own autotile tables, and all 5 maps drawn identical. The MZ editor itself was not run.

Drawn under NES colour limits

The NES colours a background per 16x16 area: one of four 3-colour sub-palettes, plus one shared backdrop. Every tile, and every 16x16 of every sample map as composed, fits one sub-palette of its environment, and the build refuses a cell that breaks the rule. All 23 colours come from the 55-colour Lospec NES table; NES RGB varies by emulator. This is an NES look, not a ROM: the Overworld holds 262 and the Castle Town holds 307 unique 8x8 patterns, over one pattern table's 256.

Works with

  • RPG Maker MZ: the pages above.
  • Tiled: .tsx with Wang sets, opened and rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own tmxrasterizer; all 5 .tmx maps drew identical (2,432,000 px, 0 differ), and the animations play.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (2,113 tiles built). No animation in Godot.
  • Unity: copy one folder, run Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 8-Bit NES RPG Tileset for RPG Maker MZ [16x16] > Build Unity Assets. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: 28 RuleTile brushes, 7,168 neighbourhoods tested. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package; walls and roofs ship as sprites.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven.
