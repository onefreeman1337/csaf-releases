Verdant 8-Bit is out: a 16x16 NES style tileset for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity. 2,113 tiles across an overworld, a castle town, interiors, a cavern and a lava keep. $8.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-8bit

Drawn under NES colour limits. The NES colours a background per 16x16 area, using one of four 3 colour sub palettes plus one shared backdrop. Every tile, and every 16x16 of every sample map as composed, fits one sub palette of its environment, and the build refuses a cell that breaks the rule. All 23 colours come from the 55 colour Lospec NES table. It is an NES look and not a ROM: two of the maps use more 8x8 patterns than one pattern table holds, and the listing gives the counts.

Built for RPG Maker MZ. Pages A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, B at 48x48, the Database row, a ship vehicle sheet and 5 sample maps. Water, lava and waterfalls sit on the A1 page, where MZ animates them itself. We ran RPG Maker MZ's own tilemap code on the shipped pages: 864 A2 shapes and 696 A1 shapes came out byte identical to our composition, and all 5 maps drew identical. The MZ editor itself was not run.

The Overworld (570 tiles): animated sea and river shores, a waterfall, a bridge, mountains, forest, a castle, a lighthouse and a dock, with the ship to sail from it. The Castle Town (605): a gate, a drawbridge over an animated moat, a fountain, market stalls and three house styles. The Interiors (269): an inn, a shop, an armoury hall and a chapel. The Cavern (327): mine rails with a minecart, crystals, ore and an animated pool. The Lava Keep (342): animated lava and lavafalls, a lava bridge, a throne and a portcullis.

22 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 18 wall and roof blocks, 107 objects and 13 animated pieces. Tiled's own renderer drew all 5 maps identical (2,432,000 px, 0 differ). Godot 4.7.2 loaded the TileSet and built 2,113 tiles, with no animation in Godot. Unity 6000.5.7f1 drew the right tile in all 7,168 RuleTile neighbourhoods tested.

Every tile and the cover are drawn by our own procedural pixel art software, which is AI written. No image model draws any of it, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
