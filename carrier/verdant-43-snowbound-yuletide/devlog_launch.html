Yuletide is out, the Christmas dressing for Verdant 43 Snowbound at 16x16. 226 tiles in one 102 colour palette, objects and animations only: it needs Snowbound for the snow, streets, walls and roofs. $4.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-43-snowbound-yuletide

The idea is a swap, not a second town. All 37 of Snowbound's objects and its 3 animated objects are redrawn for the holiday at the same sheet position, with the same outline: lamplit windows, wreaths on the doors, coloured lights along the stalls and eaves, holly and bunting. Point a Snowbound map's object tilesets at this pack and the town turns festive; point them back for the rest of the year. We checked 106 of 106 object tiles at the same sheet, x and y.

On top of that, 27 new holiday objects: gifts in five colours, a town tree, a potted fir, two snowmen, a sleigh, a gingerbread house, two market stalls and more, each grounded on Snowbound's snow, street and walls. 6 new animations: string lights on a wall and along the eaves, a brazier, a candlelit window, a twinkling fir and a star.

Tiled plays every animation: its own renderer played all 18 animated sets at 8 points in time and matched our frames. Unity: one menu command builds 226 Tiles and 18 AnimatedTiles in your project, tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project. Godot 4 and RPG Maker MZ get every frame as its own tile.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine and hand authored pixel rows run through the same code; that software is AI written. No image model, no scraped art and no training data.
