The Christmas dressing for Verdant 43 — Snowbound, and it needs Snowbound for the ground: this pack ships objects and animations only (226 tiles at 16x16, one locked 102-colour palette); the snow, streets, hedges, walls and roofs they stand on are Snowbound's ($7.99). Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • Snowbound, dressed: all 37 of Snowbound's objects and its 3 animated objects redrawn for the holiday: lamplit windows, wreaths on the doors, coloured lights along the stalls, rails and eaves, holly on the signs and posts, red and green bunting. Each keeps its Snowbound outline and sits at the SAME sheet position, so a Snowbound map turns festive by swapping its object tileset: 106 of 106 object tiles checked at the same sheet, x and y.
  • 27 new holiday objects: gifts in five colours, a gift pile, sack and tall stack, a wreath, a garland swag, bells, stockings, a star, mistletoe, a town tree, a potted fir, a holly bush, two snowmen, a sleigh, a sled, a yule log, firewood, a garlanded lamp, a gingerbread house and two market stalls, each transparent and grounded on Snowbound's snow, street and walls.
  • 6 new animations, 4 frames each: string lights on a wall and along the eaves, a brazier, a candlelit window, a twinkling fir and a star.
  • A sample map: Snowbound's own town with every object swapped for its twin and the holiday objects placed, as PNG and JSON.

Engines

  • Tiled: 5 .tsx files. Point a Snowbound map's props and animated tilesets at this pack's and it turns festive, slot for slot. Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer rendered every tileset and played all 18 animated sets on the right frames.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantYuletide/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant Yuletide > Build Unity Assets). It builds 226 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 18 AnimatedTiles and 6 Tile Palettes; tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project. AnimatedTiles need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (226 tiles built). It carries no animation; every frame is there as a tile.
  • RPG Maker MZ: the objects on B and C sheets. There is no autotile here, so no A sheet; use Snowbound's own MZ sheets for the ground. MZ does not animate object tiles, so the frames are there as tiles.

The proofs

Grid and palette are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 102-colour palette, and alpha is strictly on or off. The dressed objects are checked against Snowbound's sheets position by position, and the sheets are compared with the loose tiles before every release.
