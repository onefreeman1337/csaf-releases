Verdant GBC Platformer: Meadow, Cave, Castle Tileset is now in the Verdant Retro Library. It is the first side view volume of the Game Boy Color series: 3 sample levels, the Meadow, the Cave and the Castle, each by day and by night, built from 451 tiles and 6 autotile sets at 16x16. Collision is marked on 324 tiles, among them 12 one way platforms, 9 climbable tiles and 6 hazards.

If you own this Library it is already yours, at no extra cost. Download again from your itch library, or let the itch app update, and its folder is there beside the others.

That makes 7 packs and 6,643 tiles on this page, still $14.99. Verdant GBC Platformer is sold only in Libraries: here and in the Verdant Library ($19.99, 104 packs).

Like every pack here it keeps its own Readme, LICENSE and tile map. It carries Tiled and Godot 4 files and 6 GB Studio backgrounds, each with its .mono.png; run through GB Studio's palette merge, which we rebuilt from its source, they need 7 palettes at worst. Being a side view pack, it has no RPG Maker MZ or Unity files.
