Verdant GBC Far Lands: Five Regions Tileset is now in the Verdant Retro Library. It is a Game Boy Color volume in five regions: the Ancient Ruins, the Desert Kingdom, the Eastern Village, the Mire and the Graveyard. The ruins have columns and braziers that burn, the desert a step pyramid and an oasis pool, the village rice paddies, a teahouse and a shrine gate, the mire plank boardwalks and wisps that glow, and the graveyard headstones and a crypt. It has 1,197 tiles and 20 autotile sets at 16x16, 65 objects, 11 animated pieces and 10 sample scenes, with day, dusk and night palettes that differ by colour alone.

If you own this Library it is already yours, at no extra cost. Download again from your itch library, or let the itch app update, and its folder is there beside the others.
