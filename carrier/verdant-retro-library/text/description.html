What every pack contains

  • Single 16x16 tiles and packed sheets, with a tile map (tiles.json) naming every tile's role.
  • A Tiled .tsx and a sample map, and a Godot 4 TileSet.
  • RPG Maker MZ tileset sheets and a Unity import layer in 6 of 7 packs: every pack except the side view Verdant GBC Platformer.
  • Its own Readme, its own LICENSE.txt and its proof renders.

Compatibility

  • GB Studio: 6 of 7 packs are built to its background rules (Verdant GB in the Game Boy's 4 shades; the GBC packs in its colour mode, every 8x8 quarter 4 colours or fewer from one of 7 palettes), counted on the shipped PNGs by the rule GB Studio's documentation states. Of those, Verdant GBC Platformer's 6 GB Studio backgrounds were also run through GB Studio's palette merge, rebuilt from its source: 7 palettes at worst.
  • Godot 4: a TileSet in 7 of 7 packs, each loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 from its folder inside this download.
  • Tiled: a .tsx in 7 of 7 packs, opened and rendered by Tiled's own tmxrasterizer.
  • RPG Maker MZ: tileset images in 6 of 7 packs; the A2 pages compose against MZ's own autotile table.
  • Unity: an import layer in 6 of 7 packs, each passed a fresh install in a blank Unity project on these same bytes.

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven; this download's 6,643 tiles were proved again from their folders here.
