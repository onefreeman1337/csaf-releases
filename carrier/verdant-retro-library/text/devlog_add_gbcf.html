Verdant GBC Farm: Fields, Ranch, Barn Tileset is now in the Verdant Retro Library. It is a Game Boy Color farm in three environments: the fields with twelve crops through their growth stages, the ranch with a red barn, a silo, a coop and a windmill, and the barn interior. It has 482 tiles and 6 autotile sets at 16x16, 88 objects (6 of them animated) and 5 sample scenes, in four seasons and a night palette.
