Verdant 29 is live. It is a crystal cavern: dark fractured cavestone, glow lichen, amethyst crystal fields, a quartz sand trail, and raised geode walls with a violet crystal lip. In it stand crystal clusters in four hues, an amethyst column and a quartz spire, geodes, stalagmites and flowstone, a rune obelisk, a crystal altar and a miners' camp.

The pack ships 498 tiles at 16x16. 329 of them are transitions, which is 66% of the download, across 7 complete 47 mask autotile sets. The floors meet each other as well as the stone: amethyst grows over lichen, and the trail crosses both the lichen and the amethyst with its own sets. There are 44 objects over 85 prop tiles, and 64 animation frames for a pulsing heart crystal, water dripping into a pool, glowing mushrooms and a miner's lantern.

A cave turns into one undifferentiated dark unless the values are planned, so the 5 floors were placed on a value ladder before any art existed. The smallest gap on that ladder is 16.6 luma, measured from the shipped pixels. The palette is locked to 79 colours and every pixel is checked against it on each build.

One thing we changed before it shipped: the cavestone showed faint vertical bands when sixteen copies were laid side by side, because its cracks started on the wrap boundary. The crack starts were moved inside the tile and the seeds chosen again, and every 4x4 repetition was rendered and looked at after the change.

The importers were run by the engines they target

  • RPG Maker MZ: an A2 autotile sheet plus B, C, D object pages. It composes against MZ's own FLOOR_AUTOTILE_TABLE, 7 of 7 sets and 336 of 336 shapes byte identical. The animation itself plays in Tiled and Godot, not in MZ.
  • Tiled: 11 tsx files, all opened and rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own tmxrasterizer and compared pixel for pixel with the shipped sheets. 616 cells, 0 differing. The sample map's tmx renders 1,146,880 pixels identical to the shipped PNG.
  • Godot 4.7.2: a tres TileSet with 11 atlas sources. Godot itself counted 498 of 498 tiles and 73 multi cell footprints.

What is proven and what is not

The grid is exact, the palette is locked, the alpha is binary with no anti aliasing, every tile carries a declared role, and every autotile set is complete. Those are exact checks run on every build.

Seamlessness is not on that list and we do not claim it. What we do is measure every tile's wrap boundary against its own interior, and render every 4x4 repetition so it can be looked at. All 20 of those renders ship with the pack so you can judge them yourself.

It fits the rest of the series

The 16x16 Verdant packs are drawn at one cell size in one style, so this cavern sits beside the others. Verdant 05 — Depths, Verdant 27 — Elemental Dungeon, Verdant 25 — Infernal.

AI disclosure

Tagged as AI assisted, and precisely: every tile is drawn by a procedural generator written for this series, and that generator's code was written with AI assistance. No diffusion model is anywhere in the tile pipeline. The store images, the cover included, are composed from the pack's own tiles and sample map.
