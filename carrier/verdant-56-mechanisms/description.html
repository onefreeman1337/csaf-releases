The puzzle layer for a top-down dungeon: 12 mechanisms in 25 states, 3 animated cycles and breakable walls, at 16x16 in one 46-colour palette, every state its own tile and wired for RPG Maker MZ events, Tiled, Godot and Unity. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Said before you buy: 54 of the 185 tiles are the flagstone floor and the ashlar wall of Verdant 05 — Depths, included unchanged so a puzzle room builds without it; the other 131 are new. The brick breakable wall is drawn for Depths' brick wall, which is not included here.

What is in it

  • Every state of 12 mechanisms: floor button (up / down), pressure plate (raised / pressed), pull switch (rest / pulled), crystal switch (blue / red), blue barrier (raised / lowered), red barrier (raised / lowered), floor spikes (retracted / rising / armed), collapsing floor (cracked / crumbling / fallen), arrow vent (idle / firing), push block, ice block, clay pot (whole / cracking / shards). Each ships transparent for your own floor and grounded on the flagstone with a contact shadow, and tiles.json names every tile's mechanism and state.
  • 3 animated cycles: a fire vent burning, a floor fan turning and a lit brazier, 4 frames each at 160 ms.
  • 6 breakable wall tiles: ashlar and brick, intact, cracked and breached. The intact tile is the wall's own edge tile, byte for byte, so it hides in a wall run until it cracks.
  • An ice floor: 4 fills and a complete 47-mask set, ice on the flagstone, plus the Depths ashlar wall set (94 transition tiles in 2 complete sets).
  • A finished 48x26 sample map, The Proving Hall: six rooms with one route through them, 93 objects placed, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ, as events: one event sheet, !VerdantMechanisms1.png, laid out like MZ's own door sheet: each object's states on the four direction rows, so an event page picks a state by its direction. The 3 cycles play as a turning move route (the Readme gives it). Checked with MZ's own Sprite_Character arithmetic run on the shipped file: 68 cells, 0 differing. Tested against the engine's code, not inside the MZ editor.
  • RPG Maker MZ, as tiles: both sets on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush (96 of 96 shapes identical when composed back with MZ's own autotile table), every tile on the B page.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation and its timing written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,277,952 px, 0 differ) and, advanced to the middle of every frame, drew exactly our frames (11 checks).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (185 tiles built). States are separate tiles; the TileSet carries no animation.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant56Mechanisms/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 56 Mechanisms > Build Unity Assets). It builds 185 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 2 RuleTile brushes, 6 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (512 of 512), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel.

The states are art: no collision and no behaviour is set, and your game decides when each one shows.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 46-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and both sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model.
