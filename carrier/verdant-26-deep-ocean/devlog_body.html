Verdant 26 is live. It is the sea floor: a lit shell sand shelf, abyssal ooze, a coral reef, a kelp forest, a drowned city's pavement, talus, columnar vent rock and a trench that drops into black, with a wreck and the ruins lying on it.

The pack ships 1052 tiles at 16x16. 611 of them are transitions, which is 58% of the download, across 13 complete 47 mask autotile sets. There are 75 objects over 337 tiles, and 10 animations across 72 frames: 8 caustic light overlays and 2 animated objects.

A deep water palette collapses into one blue grey smear unless the values are planned, so the 8 materials were placed on a value ladder before any art existed. The smallest gap on that ladder is 18.1 luma, measured from the shipped pixels. The palette is locked to 99 colours and every pixel is checked against it on each build.

One thing to know: the caustic overlays are opaque tiles, because the pack keeps alpha strictly on or off. Put them on their own layer and turn that layer down. Every image on the store page was made at 45%.

The importers were run by the engines they target

  • RPG Maker MZ: an A2 autotile sheet plus B, C, D, E object pages. It composes against MZ's own FLOOR_AUTOTILE_TABLE, 13 of 13 sets and 624 of 624 shapes byte identical. The object pages hold every fill, every object and every animation frame. The animation itself plays in Tiled and Godot, not in MZ.
  • Tiled: 17 tsx files, all opened and rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own tmxrasterizer and compared pixel for pixel with the shipped sheets. 1,324 cells, 0 differing. The sample map's tmx renders 958,464 pixels identical to the shipped PNG.
  • Godot 4.7.2: a tres TileSet with 17 atlas sources. Godot itself counted 1052 of 1052 tiles and 164 multi cell footprints.

What is proven and what is not

The grid is exact, the palette is locked, the alpha is binary with no anti aliasing, every tile carries a declared role, and every autotile set is complete. Those are exact checks run on every build.

Seamlessness is not on that list and we do not claim it. What we do is measure every tile's wrap boundary against its own interior, and render every 4x4 repetition so it can be looked at. All 80 of those renders ship with the pack so you can judge them yourself.

It fits the rest of the series

The 16x16 Verdant packs are drawn at one cell size in one style, so this layer sits under the others. Verdant 04 — Tidewater, Verdant 10 — Harbour, Verdant 11 — Vessel.

AI disclosure

Tagged as AI assisted, and precisely: every tile is drawn by a procedural generator written for this series, and that generator's code was written with AI assistance. No diffusion model is anywhere in the tile pipeline. The store images, the cover included, are composed from the pack's own tiles and sample map.
