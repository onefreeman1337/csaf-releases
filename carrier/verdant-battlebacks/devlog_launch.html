Verdant Battlebacks is out: 40 battle backgrounds for RPG Maker, 10 biomes at dawn, day, dusk and night, $9.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-battlebacks

A battle that cuts to another artist's world is jarring when the map you walked in on is ours. So every biome here is cut from a Verdant tileset: the ground is that pack's own shipped fill tiles, laid out in perspective, and the props at the front are its own shipped props at the 3x scale RPG Maker MZ draws a 16x16 map. The sky, the distant range and the big framing trees, palms, columns and basalt come from the same procedural engine, in the same palette. 143 shipped tiles from 10 packs went into it. You do not need the tilesets to use the backgrounds.

Each scene is split the way MZ draws it. battlebacks1 is the sky and the ground; battlebacks2 is everything standing up, on transparency. Every scene keeps its horizon on the same row, so any backdrop sits on any ground: ruined columns on the meadow, taiga spruces over the highland.

The 1280x720 folder exists because of one line of arithmetic. At that resolution MZ stretches a standard 1000x740 battleback by 1.568x, which softens pixel art, and in front view only the top 459 of its 740 rows are on screen. The 1568x853 files draw at exactly 1:1.

How it was checked: we copied the folders into a blank MZ project, started real battles in the MZ runtime (headless Chrome) and compared what the engine drew with the shipped files. 40 of 40 scenes in front view, 10 of 10 in side view and 10 of 10 at 1280x720 came back with 0 pixels different. The MZ editor itself was not opened, and the VX Ace files are sized for VX Ace, not run in it.

Every image is drawn by our own procedural pixel-art engine, which is AI-written software. No image model, no scraped art and no training data were used.
