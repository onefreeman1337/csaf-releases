Verdant 68 Storeys is out: 255 tiles at 16x16, an add on for Verdant 07 Interiors. $7.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-68-storeys

Verdant 07 builds rooms on one floor. A house in an RPG has an upstairs and a cellar, and until now the series had no way to reach them. This pack is the way up and the way down.

18 stairs in plank, cut stone and a carpet runner on oak: flights that rise into a dark opening in the wall and flights that go down through the floor, wide and narrow, plus side flights along a wall. A stairwell opening you can cut into the floor in any shape, as 2 complete autotile sets. 3 banisters to rail it: painted, oak and a stone balustrade. Ladders, a rope ladder, trapdoors and iron bound hatches, a wine rack and a cask for the cellar.

The sample map is one inn on four floors, a taproom, a landing with a gallery over the room below, a cellar and a loft, built from Verdant 07 walls and furniture. In RPG Maker MZ the stairwell sits on an A2 autotile page, and in Unity it paints as a RuleTile brush. Tiled and Godot get every piece as a named tile.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
