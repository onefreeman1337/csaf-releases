The six later Verdant tile packs are now available as one purchase. Verdant Complete II covers Gothic, Interiors, Props, Harvestables, Seasons and Harbour at 70 percent off, which itch prices at $19.44 for all six against $64.94 separately. It runs until 30 September 2026.

These six share one 16 pixel grid and one palette, so tiles from any of them sit together in the same map without a seam or a colour shift. Between them they carry complete 47 mask autotile sets, props with contact shadows on their own ground, animated tiles, and the same importer set every Verdant pack ships: an RPG Maker MZ A2 autotile sheet plus object sheets, a Tiled tileset, a Godot 4 resource, loose PNGs, and a role tagged tiles.json.

Two notes on what this bundle is not. It does not include the earlier Verdant packs, which have their own sale, so nothing here is discounted twice. And it does not include the newest pack, Verdant 11 Vessel, which shipped today at its normal price.
