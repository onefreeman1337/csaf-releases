Verdant 27 is live. It is a dungeon of four elemental halls: coursed firebrick with live coals, faceted ice, a moss floor with roots, and fractured storm slate. All four open off one dark flagstone nave, and a rune channel carries each hall's light out across the floor.

The pack ships 864 tiles at 16x16. 611 of them are transitions, which is 71% of the download, across 13 complete 47 mask autotile sets. The halls meet each other as well as the nave: four element meets element sets form a ring, so halls laid in that order touch at every corner with a real transition. There are 56 objects over 105 prop tiles and 108 animation frames, with one ornament metal per hall.

Four halls turn into one muddy dark unless the values are planned, so the floors were placed on a value ladder before any art existed. The smallest gap on that ladder is 16.0 luma, measured from the shipped pixels. The palette is locked to 74 colours and every pixel is checked against it on each build.

One thing we fixed before it shipped: the ice crystals were drawn in a blue close to the ice floor and nearly vanished on it. A census of every object against its own floor found them, and a rubble heap on the flagstone, far below everything else. They now carry a dark frame and a darker body, and a check in the build keeps them there.

One thing to know: the nave floor is Verdant 05 Depths' floor, byte for byte. If you own Depths, these halls lay straight onto the dungeon you already built.

The importers were run by the engines they target

  • RPG Maker MZ: an A2 autotile sheet plus B, C, D, E object pages. It composes against MZ's own FLOOR_AUTOTILE_TABLE, 13 of 13 sets and 624 of 624 shapes byte identical. The object pages hold every fill, every object and every animation frame. The animation itself plays in Tiled and Godot, not in MZ.
  • Tiled: 17 tsx files, all opened and rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own tmxrasterizer and compared pixel for pixel with the shipped sheets. 1,120 cells, 0 differing. The sample map's tmx renders 1,146,880 pixels identical to the shipped PNG.
  • Godot 4.7.2: a tres TileSet with 17 atlas sources. Godot itself counted 864 of 864 tiles and 64 multi cell footprints.

What is proven and what is not

The grid is exact, the palette is locked, the alpha is binary with no anti aliasing, every tile carries a declared role, and every autotile set is complete. Those are exact checks run on every build.

Seamlessness is not on that list and we do not claim it. What we do is measure every tile's wrap boundary against its own interior, and render every 4x4 repetition so it can be looked at. All 40 of those renders ship with the pack so you can judge them yourself.

It fits the rest of the series

The 16x16 Verdant packs are drawn at one cell size in one style, so this dungeon sits beside the others. Verdant 05 — Depths, Verdant 25 — Infernal, Verdant 13 — Castle.

AI disclosure

Tagged as AI assisted, and precisely: every tile is drawn by a procedural generator written for this series, and that generator's code was written with AI assistance. No diffusion model is anywhere in the tile pipeline. The store images, the cover included, are composed from the pack's own tiles and sample map.
