The tiles a monster tamer route is made of, at 16x16: 490 tiles in one 55-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Tall grass, ledges, a pond, a cave

Encounter grass that sways (3 frames, played back and forth), a worn road, one way ledges you hop down and cannot climb (13 drop tiles carry "oneWay": "south" in tiles.json, with stone steps to put where the route climbs back up), a still pond, and a cave with its mouth and its rock walls. Every one is a complete 47-mask autotile set, so every corner, edge and junction is drawn.

What is in it

  • 423 transition tiles in 5 complete 47-mask sets: tall grass and the pond animated, the road, the ledge and cave rock still.
  • 10 fill tiles (meadow grass, cave floor, road variants) and 9 animated open-water tiles so a big pond does not repeat one cell.
  • 19 objects, each standing on the grass with a contact shadow: route signs, a shrub and its cut stump, a boulder, a cracked rock, berry trees, a mailbox, a lamp, fences, a bench, the town sign, an item satchel, stone steps, a cave mouth, and a small route town (a slate-roofed hall and a cottage). All original designs.
  • A finished 40x26 sample map, Route One, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.

Made with the Verdant series

The meadow is Verdant 01 — Overworld's own grass, byte for byte, so a route drops straight into a Verdant 01 map; the pond is Verdant 38 — Cascades's own water. This pack also works on its own.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on an A2 page as MZ autotile brushes (240 of 240 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), the tall grass and the pond also on an animated A1 page (384 of 384), and the objects and fills on the B and C pages. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Passability and the ledge direction are yours to set in the Database.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with the animations written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,064,960 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 4 points in time its animation playback matched our frames.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (490 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantTamerRoutes/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 62 Tamer Routes > Build Unity Assets). It builds 490 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 5 RuleTile brushes, 3 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,280 of 1,280), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 55-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 5 sets carry all 47 masks in every frame.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model. The buildings, roofs and emblem are original designs, not any franchise's.
