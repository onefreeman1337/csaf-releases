Verdant 50 Bloom and Blight is out, an enchanted wood for top down RPG maps at 16x16, in two states. 431 tiles in one 65 colour palette and 36 objects, for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot and Unity. $7.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-50-bloom-and-blight

The idea: every healthy floor, transition set and object has a blighted twin drawn from the same generator and the same seed, with the same silhouette. 16 objects come in pairs, and the blighted silhouette is checked identical to the healthy one on every build. Swap a grove for its twin, or paint the spreading edge, withered grass laid over the living grass it is killing, and one map shows the curse taking hold.

Bloom has rose and cream blossom trees, saplings, flowering bushes, giant blossoms, lilies and a petal meadow. Blight has withered grass, an ash meadow, blight ground split by glowing cracks, thorn brambles, a twisted dead tree and a pulsing spore pod on 4 frames. We checked that Tiled plays it by having Tiled's own renderer draw the sample map at 8 points in time and comparing every pixel with ours.

Unity: one menu command builds 431 Tiles, 7 RuleTile brushes and 2 AnimatedTiles in your project, tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project. The 48x30 sample map ships as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model, no scraped art and no training data.
