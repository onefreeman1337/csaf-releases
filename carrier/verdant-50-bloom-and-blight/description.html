One enchanted wood, healthy and cursed, built tile for tile: 431 tiles at 16x16 in one 65-colour palette. Every healthy floor, transition set and object has a blighted twin drawn from the same generator and the same seed, with the same silhouette, so one map can show the blight spreading through one place. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • Bloom: living grass with tiny gold and cream flowers, a petal meadow, flower-forest loam, rose and cream blossom trees, saplings, bushes, giant blossoms, lilies, a flowering stump, a blossoming boulder and a fallen blossoming log.
  • Blight: withered grass, an ash meadow and bruised-violet blight ground split by glowing cracks; the blighted twin of every bloom object; and what only a cursed wood has: thorn tangle, thorn hedge, blight-twisted tree, and a pulsing spore pod (4 frames at 200 ms).
  • 36 objects, 16 of them twin pairs. Each ships transparent for your own ground and grounded on ours, with a contact shadow drawn from that ground's own colours.
  • 329 transition tiles in 7 complete 47-mask sets: three healthy sets, their three blighted twins, and the spreading edge, withered grass laid over the living grass it is killing, so you can paint the curse creeping across a map.
  • 24 fill tiles: three floors and their three twins, four variants each.
  • A finished 48x30 sample map, The Spreading Blight, with 77 objects placed and every set used, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Engines

  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantBloomAndBlight/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 50 Bloom & Blight > Build Unity Assets). It builds 431 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 7 RuleTile brushes, 2 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,792 of 1,792) and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with the spore pod's animation and timing written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,474,560 px, 0 differ), and drew it again at 8 points in time with the spore pods on the right frame each time (2,949,120 px).
  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on the A2 sheet as MZ's own autotile brush; every shape was composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (336 of 336 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B and C sheets; MZ does not animate object tiles, so the spore pod's frames are there as tiles.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (431 tiles built). The TileSet carries no animation.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 65-colour palette, and alpha is strictly on or off. Every blighted object is checked to have exactly its healthy twin's silhouette, and the sheets are compared with the loose tiles before every release.
