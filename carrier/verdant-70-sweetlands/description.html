A candy world you can build a level from: 550 tiles at 16x16 in one 59-colour palette: a mint sugar-grass meadow under gumdrop trees, pink candyfloss thickets standing up out of it, a chocolate river that flows through its cocoa-crumb bank, a biscuit path, and frosted peaks of lavender icing with amethyst rock-candy crags. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • A chocolate river that flows: 141 animated transition tiles (the river set, 3 key frames played 1, 2, 3, 2 at 450 ms a beat) plus every chocolate variant as an animated fill. The middle frame of the flowing set is the static river set, tile for tile, so you can paint the still river and swap the moving one in with no seam.
  • 423 transition tiles in 7 complete 47-mask sets: the candyfloss thicket and the rock-candy crags standing up off their ground (a lit face and a shadow at the foot), the cocoa bank meeting the meadow, the chocolate river in its bank, the icing climbing out of the meadow, and the biscuit path.
  • 31 objects: gumdrop tree (cherry, lime, lilac), giant lollipop (cherry swirl, sky swirl), candy cane, frosted pine, ice cream cone, biscuit signpost with a lantern, gingerbread house, wafer fence, cupcake, iced donut, chocolate-chip cookie, half-wrapped chocolate bar, truffles in paper cups, wrapped sweet (lemon, lilac), candy corn, marshmallow stack, stack of macarons, scattered jelly beans, cherry pair, slice of layer cake, candyfloss on a cone, licorice bush, rock candy crystals, sugar cubes, iced chocolate boulder, cocoa pod, toffee chunks. Each ships transparent for your own ground and grounded on ours, with a contact shadow drawn from that ground's own colours.
  • 2 animated objects: a chocolate fountain and a fizzing soda spring, 4 frames each at 180 ms.
  • 28 fill tiles: mint sugar-grass, pink candyfloss, cocoa crumb, molten chocolate, lavender icing, amethyst rock candy and biscuit crumb, 4 variants each.
  • A finished 40x26 sample map, The Sweetlands, with 77 objects placed, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: the flowing chocolate on the A1 page in MZ's own animated-water layout; every static set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, so you paint and the edges draw themselves, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (336 of 336 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B, C and D sheets; MZ does not animate object tiles, so the fountain and spring frames are there as tiles.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantSweetlands/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 70 Sweetlands > Build Unity Assets). It builds 550 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 7 RuleTile brushes (the river brush animated), 8 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,792 of 1,792), the animated brush handed back every frame in order (256 of 256), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation and its timing written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,064,960 px, 0 differ), and, advanced to the middle of every beat, drew exactly our frames for the river, the fountain and the spring (21 checks, 2,131,968 px). Transition tiles are placed by name; every tile's mask is in tiles.json.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (550 tiles built). The TileSet carries no animation; the frames are there as tiles.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 59-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 7 sets carry all 47 masks, the flowing one in every frame.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration was made with an image model (OpenAI); it is not part of the download.
