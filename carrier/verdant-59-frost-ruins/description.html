Your ruins, in deep winter: 698 tiles at 16x16 in one 45-colour palette. Every tile of Verdant 23 — Ruins has a snowbound twin here under the same name, in the same sheet, at the same position, so a ruins map you built with it goes into winter by swapping the tileset, without moving a cell. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • 698 winter twins of Verdant 23's tiles. The winter is in the drawing, not a blue filter: a fine snow over the meadow with a few dry stalks standing through it, a snow cushion on every block of the wall, snow mounded on the myrtle crowns and settled on the lit faces of the fallen roof tiles, the plaza trodden with its joints still showing, the earth frozen bare and grey, the cistern frozen to black ice.
  • 23 objects under snow, every one Verdant 23 ships (fallen column drum, fallen column shaft, standing column stub, doric capital, plinth block, fallen arch fragment, stylobate step, broken statue, inscribed stele, altar, votive plaque, tethering ring, amphora, smashed amphora, wellhead, drum in the basin, cypress, laurel, acanthus clump, vine over stone, cut stone pile, broken roof tile, loose mosaic panel), snow-capped inside its own outline so each is exactly the size of its summer twin, plus the spring still running and the brazier still burning, 4 frames each, and three frozen cistern variants with a glint sliding across the ice.
  • 564 transition tiles in 12 complete 47-mask sets, the same 12 as Verdant 23, and 32 fill tiles.
  • A finished 36x26 sample map, The Excavated Courtyard, Under Snow: Verdant 23's own map, every cell and object in place, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

This pack also stands alone: every tile is in the download, and you do not need Verdant 23 to use it as a snowy ruin.

The swap, measured

  • tiles.json: 698 of 698 twins at the same id, sheet, x, y and size as Verdant 23's, every one different in pixels.
  • RPG Maker MZ: the same A2 slots and 698 of 698 B to D positions, so a second Tileset in the Database and the event command Change Tileset turn the season. The slots are proven file by file; the swap was not run inside the MZ editor.
  • Tiled: all 698 of Verdant 23's tile entries keep their ids. Godot 4: all 743 of its atlas lines keep their coordinates.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (576 of 576 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B, C, D sheets.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (958,464 px, 0 differ).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (698 tiles built).
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant59FrostRuins/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 59 Frost Ruins > Build Unity Assets). It builds 698 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 12 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (3,072 of 3,072), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 45-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 12 sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model.
