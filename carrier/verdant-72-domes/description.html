A domed desert city you can build a level from: 535 tiles at 16x16 in one 81-colour palette: whitewashed houses that stand up from the street, 9 domes in three sizes and three finishes, three minarets, a covered bazaar, market stalls and a palace court with a pool that moves. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • Domes and minarets: a great ribbed dome with its lantern, an onion dome and a small dome, each in turquoise glaze, gilded copper and whitewash; a white domed kiosk with its door; a tall sandstone minaret with two balconies, a white minaret and a square minaret with a tile panel. The coloured domes ship grounded on the roof terrace and transparent for your own roofs.
  • A covered bazaar you paint: its roof of small domes with light wells is a 47-mask autotile set over a mud brick wall, so it runs along any street; arched souk gates cut into that wall, lanterns glinting inside. Four market stalls (carpets, spices, textiles, pottery) under striped awnings, a carpet frame, spice cones, jars, bolts of cloth and a coffee pot.
  • A palace court: cream tile with a turquoise eight pointed star in each and terracotta crosses between, laid into the street behind a sandstone kerb; a pool behind a marble kerb; planted beds; an arcade of horseshoe arches that lays side by side to any length; a fountain, orange trees in glazed pots, cypresses, a tiled bench, water channels and a palace gate of cedar doors under a striped arch.
  • A pool that moves: an animated transition set (141 tiles, 3 key frames played 1, 2, 3, 2 at 400 ms a beat) plus every pool variant as an animated fill. The middle frame is the static pool set, tile for tile, so you can paint the static one and swap the moving one in with no seam.
  • Houses with doors and roofs to live on: the house set stands up from the street with a sunlit wall along its south edge and a lit parapet on its north and west; a door porch already cut into that wall; stair heads and carpets airing for the roofs.
  • 376 transition tiles in 6 complete 47-mask sets: houses, bazaar, court, pool, planted beds, and the moving pool.
  • 36 objects, 2 of them animated (a pierced brass lantern and the fountain, 4 frames each at 160 ms). Each ships transparent for your own ground and grounded on ours, with a contact shadow drawn from that ground's own colours.
  • 24 fill tiles: roof terrace, bazaar roof, court tile, pool water, planted bed and the street, 4 variants each.
  • A finished 48x30 sample map, The Domed Quarter, with 69 objects placed, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Made for Verdant 24 Desert Kingdom

It joins Verdant 24 — Desert Kingdom: the street is that pack's own dust (its four tiles are included, unchanged, so this pack paints a city on its own) and the roof terraces are drawn only in its palette, so the domes and minarets drop onto the town you already built.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: the moving pool on the A1 page in MZ's own animated-water layout and order; every static set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, so you paint and the edges draw themselves, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (288 of 288 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B to E sheets; MZ does not animate object tiles, so the lantern and fountain frames are there as tiles.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant72Domes/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 72 Domes > Build Unity Assets). It builds 535 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 6 RuleTile brushes (the pool brush animated), 10 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,536 of 1,536), the animated brush handed back every frame in order (256 of 256), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation and its timing written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,474,560 px, 0 differ), and, advanced to the middle of every beat, drew exactly our frames for the lanterns, the fountain and the pool (21 checks, 2,951,168 px). Transition tiles are placed by name; every tile's mask is in tiles.json.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (535 tiles built). The TileSet carries no animation; the frames are there as tiles.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 81-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 6 sets carry all 47 masks, the moving one in every frame.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover is a generated illustration.
