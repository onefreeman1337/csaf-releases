Verdant Ancient Realms is a 16x16 pixel art tileset volume for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: three ancient realms in one download, a wuxia Sect Mountain above a sea of cloud, a Jade Temple step pyramid in the jungle, and the Elven Terraces. It holds 921 tiles in one 113 colour palette: 15 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 48 kinds of still object plus five animated ones, and three finished sample maps. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler and the browser Verdant Playground.

The Sect Mountain

A granite peak rising out of a sea of cloud, with a cliff face all round, and a cultivation sect on its stone terrace. The main hall with its tiled roof and red pillars, a hexagonal pavilion, a gate archway, white sect walls with a lattice window and a moon gate, stone stairs, guardian lions, a dark formation court with a gold octagon array, a jade lotus pond, a training yard with wooden dummies, a weapon rack and meditation cushions, a stele, red lanterns, an incense burner whose smoke rises, and a sect banner that sways. On the peak: a bamboo grove, mountain pines and red-crowned cranes. 5 sets, 338 tiles, sample map 30x22.

The Jade Temple

A temple court of cut limestone in the jungle, with a red stucco step pyramid on it and a lighter summit platform on that, so each level reads raised. On top, a temple with a perforated roof comb; down the south face, a two wide stairway to feathered serpent heads and burning braziers. Stelae carved in the step fret, a round altar, the ball court wall with its stone ring, a white causeway, and a cenote sunk into the jungle with a ladder down to the water. Ceiba trees, ferns, hanging vines, a scarlet macaw, offering vessels and fallen carved blocks. 5 sets, 293 tiles, sample map 30x22.

The Elven Terraces

A terrace paved in pale moonstone octagons with gold inlay, raised over a silver lawn by a stone wall under a gold coping. A domed moon pavilion, a gold kerbed reflecting pool with fountains, white trees in lawn plots, lilac urns, a lectern and a moon crystal; stairs down to an arch gate; and on the lawn below a moonlit stream, starbloom flower beds, curved benches, a trellis, a stag statue, moonflowers and elven lamps with circling motes of light. 5 sets, 290 tiles, sample map 30x22.

What is in it

  • 64 ground tiles (four variants of each ground) and 705 transition tiles in 15 complete 47 shape sets.
  • 48 kinds of still object and 5 animated objects (an incense burner, a sect banner, a brazier, an elven lamp and a fountain).
  • Three sample maps (the Sect Mountain, the Jade Temple, the Elven Terraces) as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.
  • Same grid as the rest of the Verdant series, so it sits beside Verdant East Asian Village, Verdant Pyramid Tombs, Verdant Wilds: Tropics and Verdant Royal Gardens. You do not need them; everything here is in this download.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: all 15 sets on an A2 page (720 of 720 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the grounds, objects and animation frames on the B, C, D, E pages at 48px. MZ does not animate those pages, so the animated objects are frames you swap by event. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation written in at its own timing. Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew all three sample maps identical to ours (2,027,520 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 15 instants with the animations advanced, its playback matched our own render exactly.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (921 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantAncientRealms/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant Ancient Realms Wuxia Sect Mountain, Jade Jungle Temple and Elven Terraces Tilesets [16x16] > Build Unity Assets). Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: it built 921 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, and 15 RuleTile brushes drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (3,840 of 3,840); Unity's render of the sample scene matched the map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 and every sheet an exact multiple of it, every pixel is inside the 113 colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 15 sets carry all 47 shapes.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced.
