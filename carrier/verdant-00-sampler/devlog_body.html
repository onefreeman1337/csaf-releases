The free Verdant 00 download has been rebuilt. It used to be a 64 tile sample cut from one pack. It is now a sampler of the whole series: 878 tiles at 16x16, one complete autotile set and its grounds from each of 16 paid Verdant packs.

Every tile in it is byte identical to the same tile in the paid pack it comes from, and each one names that pack in tiles.json. The build refuses to write a tile whose bytes differ from what the paid pack ships, so what you try here is exactly what those packs contain. The sampler gives you the range of the series; each paid pack gives you the depth, with more sets, props and animation.

What is in it: 16 complete 47 mask sets (752 tiles), the grounds they stand on, a 64x40 catalogue map with every set placed, and importers for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot. Grid, palette lock and autotile completeness are checked on every build. Seamlessness is not claimed; the 4x4 repetition renders ship with the pack so you can judge that yourself.

Why we did it: this free listing is the most visited Verdant page we have, at 64 tiles. Making it the widest pack in the series, and linking every biome in it to the pack it comes from, is the most useful thing that page can do.

The 16 packs it samples: Verdant 05 — Depths, Verdant 06 — Gothic, Verdant 08 — Mire, Verdant 12 — Scarp, Verdant 13 — Castle, Verdant 16 — East Asian Village, Verdant 17 — Urban Decay, Verdant 23 — Ruins, Verdant 24 — Desert Kingdom, Verdant 25 — Infernal, Verdant 26 — Deep Ocean, Verdant 27 — Elemental Dungeon, Verdant 28 — Deep Forest, Verdant 29 — Crystal Hollows, Verdant 32 — Orbital Station, Verdant 33 — Fungal Hollows.

AI disclosure: every tile is drawn by a procedural generator written for this series, and that generator's code was written with AI assistance. No diffusion model is anywhere in the tile pipeline.
