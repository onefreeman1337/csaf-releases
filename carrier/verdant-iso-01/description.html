Verdant Iso 01 is 240 isometric pixel art tiles on a 64x32 grid for Godot 4 and Tiled: a meadow, a river valley and a rocky highland. Every ground blends into its neighbours through generated corner tiles, so a map reads as one landscape. Commercial licence, no credit needed.

What is inside

240 tiles · 10 terrains · 9 blended overlay sets · 48 cliff blocks · 26 props · one 91 colour palette · PNG, Godot 4 and Tiled files

  • 40 terrain fills: 10 terrains x 4 variants.
  • 126 corner transitions: all 14 corner shapes for each of the 9 overlays.
  • 48 cliff blocks: 1, 2 and 3 levels high, for terraces with real height.
  • 26 props: oaks, a willow, pines, a haystack, fences, reeds, stepping stones, boulders, a standing stone, ruin walls.
  • A 24x24 sample map as PNG, Tiled .tmx and JSON, plus tiles.json with role, mask and passability per tile.

The sample map in the download, at 1x. Every tile in it is a shipped tile, placed by id.

Godot 4

The .tres TileSet loads from any folder, one terrain set per overlay. Paint "Blank (paint first)", then the terrain, and Godot picks every corner tile. Loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2: its painter chose the predicted tile in 168 of 168 cells for every terrain, and the Readme's steps rebuilt the sample map cell for cell.

Tiled

16 .tsx tilesets with every tile named by id, and the sample .tmx, opened and rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own tmxrasterizer: 591,602 pixels, 0 different from the shipped PNG. Transitions are placed by id; the Tiled terrain brush is not set up.

One overlay, every corner shape. All nine ship the full set.

Proofs in the download

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: 91 colours, all 135 corner slots present.

For Godot 4, Tiled and any engine that reads PNG and JSON. There is no RPG Maker, Unity or GameMaker importer, and nothing is animated.
