Verdant 67 Scarp Slopes is out: 72 pieces at 16x16, an add on for Verdant 12 Scarp. $5.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-67-scarp-slopes

Scarp draws elevation that reads at sixteen pixels, but only with square corners: turn a plateau and you get a staircase of steps. This pack turns every corner on a 45 degree diagonal instead, with the rock face and its shadow carried round the southern diagonals and the rim round the northern ones.

It covers all seven of Scarp's sets, so the drove road, the bracken, the scree and the meadow edge turn too: 30 diagonal edges, 28 shoulders for the elbow of each step, 6 caps and 2 elbows where a run meets a straight scarp, plus 3 stairs and 3 ramps cut into the scarp face.

How we know it joins: every terrace was rendered whole with Scarp's own drawing rules and then cut into tiles. Every cell that is not one of the new pieces came out byte for byte identical to the tile Scarp already ships, and every render was rebuilt from tile names alone, 6,254 cells with 0 different. You need Verdant 12 Scarp: its straight edges, corners and fills are used as they are.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
