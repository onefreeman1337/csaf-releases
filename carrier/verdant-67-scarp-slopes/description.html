An add-on for Verdant 12 — Scarp: 72 pieces at 16x16 in Scarp's own 21-colour palette that turn its cliffs, roads, bracken and scree on a 45 degree diagonal, plus stairs and ramps cut into the scarp. You need Verdant 12 Scarp: its straight edges, corners and fills are used as they are. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • 30 diagonal edges across all 7 of Scarp's sets: the scarp itself (the rock face and its shadow carried round the southern diagonals, the rim on the northern ones), the scree fan, the drove road on both levels, bracken on both levels, and Scarp's meadow meeting Verdant 01's grass.
  • 28 shoulders, 6 caps and 2 elbows: the pieces for the elbow of each step of a diagonal run, and for where a run meets a straight scarp.
  • 3 stairs and 3 ramps cut into the scarp face, one cell wide or the two halves of a two-cell flight. At Scarp's low face the ramp is a short taper.
  • A 30x18 sample map, Angled Terraces, as PNG and as JSON with every cell named (Scarp's tiles by their Scarp names).
  • Hand-placed pieces: no engine autotiler draws diagonals, so you paint Scarp as usual and set these where an edge turns. Nothing animates.

How we know it joins

Every terrace was rendered whole with Scarp's own drawing rules and only then cut into tiles. Every cell that is not one of these pieces came out byte for byte identical to the tile Scarp already ships, and every render was rebuilt from tile names alone: 6,254 cells over 53 renders, 0 different. Grid, palette lock and binary alpha are machine-proven on every file.

Engines

  • Tiled: one .tsx per Scarp set beside its sheet, rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer and compared with the shipped sheets.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet of plain atlas tiles, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2.
  • Unity: one menu command builds 72 pixel exact sprites, Tiles and Tile Palettes; tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project.
  • RPG Maker MZ: the pieces on MZ's object grid at 48px, to place above Scarp's A2 ground. Not tested inside the MZ editor.

More from the series

Verdant 12 — Scarp is the elevation pack this extends, and Verdant 38 — Cascades adds tall cliffs and waterfalls to the same scarp.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover is designed from the pack's own tiles.
