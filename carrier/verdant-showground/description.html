Verdant Showground is a 16x16 pixel art tileset volume for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: three town venues in one download, the Sports Grounds, the Playhouse and Big Top, and the Aquarium. It holds 1,076 tiles in one 149 colour palette: 15 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 116 kinds of still object plus ten animated ones, and four finished sample maps. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler and the browser Verdant Playground.

The Sports Grounds

A stadium pitch with a running track round it and a grandstand, a tennis court, a basketball court, a volleyball sand pit, an ice rink inside its dasher boards and a baseball infield. Every surface is its own complete set (6 sets), and every marking is a separate piece you lay on top: lines in every direction and junction, the centre circle, penalty arcs, rink lines and face off circles, so a court or pitch can be any size. Goals, hockey goals, nets both ways, hoops, grandstand pieces, floodlights, team benches, hurdles, the bases and the pitcher's mound, and a scoreboard that flashes. 401 tiles, sample map 50x30.

The Playhouse and the Big Top

A stage raised over the house, its wood front facing the stalls, with a proscenium, curtains that close, part or tie back, a valance, scenery flats, a grand piano and footlights that glow along the lip. Behind it a backstage with a fly rail, a costume rack, a dressing table, a prop trunk and a follow spot; in front, rows of seats, raised dress circle galleries, the house doors with flickering candle sconces, and a marble foyer with a velvet rope. Then the circus: the big top's canvas wall and entrance, bleachers, king poles, podiums, a balancing ball, a cannon, a hoop of fire, and the sawdust ring in its painted kerb. 376 tiles, two sample maps (24x22 and 22x16).

The Aquarium

A display tank standing in the hall, its glass front lit along the top, with a walk through glass tunnel in both directions and four corners, so the tunnel can wind through the water. In the tank: kelp, sea fan and tube coral, live rock, a diver helmet, a stingray, a bubbler, fish schools, a circling shoal and a shark. Around it: viewing windows (one with fish swimming past), an information panel, jellyfish columns that pulse, a touch pool with starfish and an anemone, a penguin enclosure with its own pool and penguins, the ticket booth, turnstiles, a gift shelf, a floor map, benches and planters. 299 tiles, sample map 24x20.

What is in it

  • 80 ground tiles (four variants of each ground) and 705 transition tiles in 15 complete 47 shape sets.
  • 116 kinds of still object and 10 animated objects (a scoreboard, sconces, footlights, a hoop of fire, a fish window, jellyfish, a bubbler, fish, a shoal and a shark).
  • Four sample maps (the Sports Grounds, the Playhouse, the Big Top, the Aquarium) as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.
  • Same palette and grid as the rest of the Verdant series: it sits beside Verdant Festival Fair, Verdant School, Verdant Museum and Verdant Deep Ocean in one world. You do not need them; everything here is in this download.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: all 15 sets on an A2 page (720 of 720 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the grounds, objects and animation frames on the B, C, D, E pages at 48px. MZ does not animate those pages, so the animated objects are frames you swap by event. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation written in at its own timing. Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew all four sample maps identical to ours (2,928,640 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 20 instants with the animations advanced, its playback matched our own render exactly.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (1,076 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantShowground/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant Showground Sports Grounds, Playhouse and Aquarium Tilesets [16x16] > Build Unity Assets). Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: it built 1,076 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, and 15 RuleTile brushes drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (3,840 of 3,840); Unity's render of the sample scene matched the map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 and every sheet an exact multiple of it, every pixel is inside the 149 colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 15 sets carry all 47 shapes.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced.
