Verdant 75 Springs is out: 1,137 tiles at 16x16 in one 86 colour palette. $7.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-75-springs

A hot spring hillside on three levels: an orange runoff fan at the foot, cream travertine, and a white sinter crown, each lighter than the one below so the hill brightens as it climbs. The two raised levels are complete 47 mask autotiles whose whole south edge is the step down, a bright lip over a shaded rock face. A pool on a level is held right up to that lip by dam pieces, and where the water should go over, a pour of the same shape spills it in an animated sheet. Lay a drop on the tile below the edge and the step is a full tile tall, with its cast shadow, and the falling water lands in the pool below as foam. 7 dams, 7 pours and 16 drops in all.

Around it: a geyser with an eight frame eruption, sulphur crust with bubbling mud pots and fumaroles, rising steam, and a paved bath yard with a bathhouse, a sunken stone bath fed by a bamboo spout and stone lanterns that flicker.

In RPG Maker MZ the four hot waters are A1 water, so MZ animates them itself, and every set is also an A2 autotile brush. In Tiled every animation plays at its own timing, and Unity gets RuleTile brushes with the water animated. Godot gets every piece as a named tile.

The meadow, the upland and its cliff edge are Verdant 12 Scarp's own tiles, 55 of them byte for byte, the same ground Verdant 38 Cascades runs its waterfalls on, so the springs drop into either map without a seam.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag. The cover and every gallery image are made from the pack's own tiles.
