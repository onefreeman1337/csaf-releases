Verdant 33 is live. It is a fungal cavern: a damp rot floor with violet shale breaking through it, golden spore drifts, a mycelium ribbon winding between them, and giant toadstool caps you raise as masses and stand on their own great stems. In it grows the cast a fungal hollow is recognised by, from toadstools, puffballs and a shaggy ink cap to glowcaps, a lantern mushroom, bracket fungi and a fairy ring.

The pack ships 527 tiles at 16x16. 376 of them are transitions, which is 71% of the download, across 8 complete 47 mask autotile sets. The mycelium and the spore drifts each spread over the rot and over the shale, and the shale breaks through the rot with a hard lit edge. There are 24 objects over 67 prop tiles, and 64 animation frames across 4 animated objects: glowcaps with a pulse of light travelling through them, a puffball venting spores, an ink cap dripping from its hem, and spores drifting up off a golden mound. The silhouette holds still and only the light, the spores and the drops move.

The detail the pack exists for is the cap. It is an autotile set, so a canopy takes any shape you paint: a lit rose shoulder on the north and west, a south flank rolling down into the dark of the gills, and a shadow cast onto the ground. There is one cap set per ground it can grow from, the rot, the shale and a spore drift, and a stem stood under the south edge turns the mass into a mushroom. A cave also collapses into one murky brown unless the values are planned, so the 5 surfaces were placed on a value ladder and on five different hues before any art existed. The smallest gap on that ladder is 19.6 luma, measured from the shipped pixels rather than declared. The palette is locked to 71 colours and every pixel is checked against it on each build.

Most of the work was redrawing things that passed every check and still looked like something else. The first cap was drawn with a hard rim and rendered as a pink slab, a raised table, so the rim is now grown rather than ruled. One cap outline grew a spike on its crown and two legs and read as a pink crab, so cap outlines stay close to an oval. The giant toadstool's stem read as a fluted Greek column on a plinth until it was given a taper, a hanging ring and a bulb at its foot. The stems for the cap masses read first as a pillar stub, then as a table under the canopy, then as a stump beside it; they are now two cells tall with their tops drawn in the cap's shadow. The coral fungus read as an orange hand and became a bush of short forked branches. The bracket fungi read as stacked gold ingots and became lens shaped shelves. The mycelium read as camouflage, then as dry straw, before it became a pale felt of fine threads. And the warts on the cap sat at the same spot in every tile of a canopy, so a whole cap read as a grid of dots; the edge tiles now carry a bare variant and the warts scatter through the middle. All of these were found by opening a render and judging it, under gates that were green throughout, which is why every 4x4 repetition ships with the pack.

The importers were run by the engines they target

  • RPG Maker MZ: an A2 autotile sheet plus B, C, D object pages. It composes against MZ's own FLOOR_AUTOTILE_TABLE, 8 of 8 sets and 384 of 384 shapes byte identical. The animation itself plays in Tiled and Godot, not in MZ.
  • Tiled: 12 tsx files, all opened and rendered by Tiled 1.12.2's own tmxrasterizer and compared pixel for pixel with the shipped sheets. 604 cells, 0 differing. The sample map's tmx renders 1,146,880 pixels identical to the shipped PNG.
  • Godot 4.7.2: a tres TileSet with 12 atlas sources. Godot itself counted 527 of 527 tiles and 23 multi cell footprints.

What is proven and what is not

The grid is exact, the palette is locked, the alpha is binary with no anti aliasing, every tile carries a declared role, and every autotile set is complete. Those are exact checks run on every build.

Seamlessness is not on that list and we do not claim it. What we do is measure every tile's wrap boundary against its own interior, and render every 4x4 repetition so it can be looked at. All 20 of those renders ship with the pack so you can judge them yourself.

It fits the rest of the series

The 16x16 Verdant packs are drawn at one cell size in one style, so this cavern sits beside the others and a passage can run straight out of it into another pack. Verdant 29 — Crystal Hollows, Verdant 28 — Deep Forest, Verdant 08 — Mire.

AI disclosure

Tagged as AI assisted, and precisely: every tile is drawn by a procedural generator written for this series, and that generator's code was written with AI assistance. No diffusion model is anywhere in the tile pipeline. The store images, the cover included, are composed from the pack's own tiles and sample map.
