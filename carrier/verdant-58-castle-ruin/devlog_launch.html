Verdant 58 Castle Ruin is out: 616 tiles at 16x16 in one 65 colour palette, for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot and Unity. $4.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-58-castle-ruin

Every one of the 603 tiles in Verdant 13 Castle has a ruined twin here, under the same name, in the same sheet, at the same position. So a castle map built with Verdant 13 turns to ruin by swapping the tileset, without moving a cell. We checked that file by file against Verdant 13's own download: the tiles.json slots, every Tiled tile entry, every Godot atlas line and every RPG Maker MZ page position.

The damage is in the tiles, not in a darker palette: the wall walk broken out to its earth core, blocks knocked out of the curtain, ivy over the towers, the roof burned through to its rafters, rubble and ash across the ward, the turf trampled by the camp and grey ash on the moat. Every object keeps its exact outline, charred or withered inside it.

On top of the twins come 4 new pieces (13 tiles): a rubble heap, fallen merlons, a broken tower stump and a burned gate leaf. To breach a wall, leave a gap in the wall run and set a rubble heap in it; the wall set closes each side on its own. The 34x24 sample map does it twice, and ships as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile; the cover illustration is made with one, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
