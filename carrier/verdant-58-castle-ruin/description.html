Your castle, after the siege: 616 tiles at 16x16 in one 65-colour palette. Every tile of Verdant 13 — Castle has a ruined twin here under the same name, in the same sheet, at the same position, so a castle map you built with it falls to ruin by swapping the tileset, without moving a cell. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

What is in it

  • 603 ruined twins of Verdant 13's tiles. The damage is structural, not a darker palette: the wall walk broken out to its earth core, blocks knocked out of the curtain, ivy over the towers, the roof burned through to its rafters, rubble and ash across the ward, the sward trampled and churned, grey ash on the moat.
  • 4 new pieces for the ruin: rubble heap, fallen merlons, broken tower stump, burned gate leaf. To breach a wall, leave a gap in the wall run (the set's own end tiles close each side) and set a rubble heap in it.
  • 18 ruined objects, every one Verdant 13 ships (portcullis, oak gate doors, rampart stair, arrow slit, trebuchet, scaling ladder, archery butt, weapon rack, pavise shield, draw well, water trough, hay cart, barrel stack, firewood stack, anvil and block, stocks, moat weed, lily raft), charred or withered inside its own outline so each is exactly the size of its intact twin, plus the brazier still burning and the banner scorched, 4 frames each.
  • 517 transition tiles in 11 complete 47-mask sets, the same 11 as Verdant 13, and 24 fill tiles.
  • A finished 34x24 sample map, The Castle, After the Siege: Verdant 13's own plan with two breaches cut into it, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

This pack also stands alone: every tile is in the download, and you do not need Verdant 13 to use it as a ruined castle.

The swap, measured

  • tiles.json: 603 of 603 twins at the same id, sheet, x, y and size as Verdant 13's, every one different in pixels; the 13 new tiles come after them.
  • RPG Maker MZ: the same A2 slots and 603 of 603 B to D positions, so a second Tileset in the Database and the event command Change Tileset turn the castle to ruin. The slots are proven file by file; the swap was not run inside the MZ editor.
  • Tiled: all 603 of Verdant 13's tile entries keep their ids. Godot 4: all 618 of its atlas lines keep their coordinates.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (528 of 528 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B, C, D sheets.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (835,584 px, 0 differ).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (616 tiles built).
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant58CastleRuin/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 58 Castle Ruin > Build Unity Assets). It builds 616 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 11 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (2,816 of 2,816), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 65-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 11 sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model.
