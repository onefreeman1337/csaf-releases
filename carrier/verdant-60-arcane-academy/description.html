A wizard school's library, alchemy hall and observatory: 382 tiles at 16x16 in one 97-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Bookcases any height

The bookcase is the autotile set itself. Paint a run one cell deep for a low shelf, deeper for a taller case, round a corner for a wall of books: every end, corner and turn closes on its own, with a plinth at the foot and a cornice at the top, and the shelves inside come in four different arrangements. The alchemy hall's potion shelving is built the same way.

What is in it

  • 282 transition tiles in 6 complete 47-mask sets: the bookcases, the potion shelving, a night-sky floor inlaid in brass, the alchemy tiles set into the flagstone, and stone walls on both floors.
  • 27 fill tiles: violet flagstone, teal alchemy tiles, the star floor, walnut boards, and the bookcase and shelf fronts.
  • 19 objects (rolling library ladder, reading carrel with lamp, reading table, pile of books, books and a folio, scroll pigeonholes, card catalogue, wingback reading chair, slant writing desk, condenser worm tub, mortar and pestle, apothecary drawers, herb drying rack, glass flasks, specimen jar, transmutation circle, armillary sphere, astrolabe on a stand, star-chart table), each transparent and grounded on its floor with a contact shadow, plus 4 animated ones: alembic, bubbling, retort over an oil lamp, athanor furnace, glowing, floating candles, 4 frames each.
  • A finished 48x26 sample map, The Athenaeum of Vael, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on the A2 page as MZ's own autotile brush, composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (288 of 288 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Objects on the B, C sheets.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,277,952 px, 0 differ).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (382 tiles built).
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant60ArcaneAcademy/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 60 Arcane Academy > Build Unity Assets). It builds 382 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 6 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,536 of 1,536), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 97-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 6 sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model.
