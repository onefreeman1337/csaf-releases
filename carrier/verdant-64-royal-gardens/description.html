A hedge maze, parterres and a lily canal

A square-clipped box hedge that stands on gravel with a lit cap and a five-pixel south face: paint the corridors and the set draws every corner, dead end and junction of a maze. Box-edged parterre beds for knot gardens, raked gravel walks, a formal canal in two courses of dressed stone coping, and a herbaceous border. Every one is a complete 47-mask autotile set.

What is in it

  • 235 transition tiles in 5 complete 47-mask sets: gravel walks, the clipped hedge, parterre beds, the lily canal and the herbaceous border.
  • 8 fill tiles: the lawn and four raked gravels.
  • 29 objects, each with a contact shadow: topiary balls, cones and spirals, a sundial, urns, an obelisk, a birdbath, a round fountain, a classical statue, a vase on a pedestal, wooden and stone benches, a balustrade that repeats along a row with its pier, a gazebo, a pergola with roses, a glasshouse, a wrought-iron gate, a rose arch, a clipped hedge archway, a trellis obelisk, a planter of tulips, lily pads, and the gardener's barrow, pots and watering can.
  • A finished 40x26 sample map, The Royal Garden, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.

Made with the Verdant series

The lawn is Verdant 01 — Overworld's own grass, byte for byte, so the garden meets a Verdant 01 meadow without a visible edge. It sits beside Verdant 13 — Castle and Verdant 15 — Palace as the grounds of a castle or a palace, and it also works on its own.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on an A2 page as MZ autotile brushes (240 of 240 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the objects and fills on the B and C pages. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Passability is yours to set in the Database (tiles.json carries an advisory flag for every tile).
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,064,960 px, 0 differ).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (318 tiles built), with relative texture paths so the folder works anywhere in your project.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantRoyalGardens/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 64 Royal Gardens > Build Unity Assets). It builds 318 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 5 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,280 of 1,280), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. The brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 50-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 5 sets carry all 47 masks. Stated plainly: the parterre beds plant their blooms in a regular grid, as formal bedding is, and the herbaceous border repeats once per tile along a narrow strip.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model.
