What every pack contains

  • Single tiles and packed sheets, with a tile map (tiles.json) naming every tile's role.
  • A Tiled .tsx and a sample map, and a Godot 4 TileSet.
  • RPG Maker MZ tileset sheets in 6 of 7 packs: every pack except the isometric Verdant Iso Rooms.
  • Its own Readme, its own LICENSE.txt and its proof renders.

Compatibility

  • Godot 4: a TileSet in 7 of 7 packs, each loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 from its folder inside this download.
  • Tiled: a .tsx in 7 of 7 packs, opened and rendered by Tiled's own tmxrasterizer.
  • RPG Maker MZ: tileset images in 6 of 7 packs; the A2 pages compose against MZ's own autotile table.
  • Unity: an import layer in 4 of 7 packs (Verdant 07 Interiors, Verdant 57 Keep, Verdant 71 Tamer Interiors and Verdant GBC 4), each passed a fresh install in a blank Unity project on these same bytes.
  • GameMaker: an import layer in 1 of 7 packs (Verdant 07 Interiors), passed a fresh install in a blank GameMaker project on these same bytes.
  • GB Studio: Verdant GBC 4 ships GB Studio colour mode backgrounds, built to its background rules (every 8x8 tile 4 colours or fewer from one of 7 palettes), counted on the shipped PNGs by the rule GB Studio's documentation states. GB Studio itself was not run on them.

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven; this download's 3,394 tiles were proved again from their folders here.
