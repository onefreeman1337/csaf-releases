Two packs joined the Verdant Interiors Library today. Verdant GBC 4 is a Game Boy Color volume of interiors with an inn, a throne hall, a chapel and a wizard's study, each in a lit and a dark table: 730 tiles and 12 autotile sets at 16x16. Verdant Iso Rooms is a tavern, a cottage and a bookshop on a 64x32 isometric grid: 179 tiles and 3 autotile sets, the family's first isometric pack.

If you own this Library they are already yours, at no extra cost. Download again from your itch library, or let the itch app update, and their folders are there beside the others.

That makes 7 packs and 3,394 tiles on this page, still $14.99. Neither new pack has a page of its own: both are sold only in Libraries, here and in the Verdant Library ($19.99, 107 packs).

Like every pack here each keeps its own Readme, LICENSE and tile map. Verdant GBC 4 ships RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity files and is built to GB Studio's colour mode (every 8x8 quarter of every tile 4 colours or fewer, from one of 7 palettes). Verdant Iso Rooms ships Tiled and Godot 4 files; RPG Maker MZ cannot draw an isometric map, so it has no RPG Maker sheets.
