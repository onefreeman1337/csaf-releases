Verdant Ironworks is a 16x16 pixel art tileset volume for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot 4 and Unity: three machine-age places in one download, a working mine with a rail network and gold in its walls, the inside of a clock tower with turning gears and a dial that shows any hour, and a steam train as four cars (coach, sleeper, dining car and freight). It holds 867 tiles in one 122 colour palette: 13 complete 47 shape autotile sets, 59 kinds of still object plus ten animated ones, and three finished sample maps. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack. Free to try first: the Verdant 00 Series Sampler and the browser Verdant Playground.

The Mine Works

Tunnels cut through dark rock, with a wall face along every north wall and a second wall set whose face carries gold veins, so a seam can run along a drift and around a stope and stop where the plain rock starts. A rail network of ten pieces: straights both ways, four curves, a crossing, a buffer stop, and a switch drawn in both states, the points set straight or turned with a pale or red target on its stand to say which. An ore skip that stands on the rail, timber sets across a tunnel and timber props, a shaft station of plank deck with the shaft collar, the hoist cage and a winding drum that turns, a flooded sump sunk into the floor with a hand pump, a winze ladder, a crate of blasting sticks and a plunger, a cave-in with a snapped beam, ore heaps, a tool rack and a miner's lamp that flickers. 5 sets, 305 tiles, sample map 30x20.

The Clocktower

The clock chamber: old oak floorboards, the tower wall in brick under a stone coping with arched windows, an open drop cut in the floor with an iron catwalk across it, and the bell loft's flagstones. A great brass gear and a small one that turn, mounted on the wall. The dial ships with twelve hand overlays, one for every hour, laid on its centre cell, plus a set of hands that turn, so a cutscene clock can show any hour. A pendulum that swings over the drop, the weights on their chains, the clock movement in its iron frame, the great bell with its striped rope, a ladder, a clockmaker's bench and a crate of spare wheels. 4 sets, 316 tiles, sample map 28x20.

The Express

Train car interiors in mahogany and cream: car walls with a brass rail, windows (night glass, or lay the scenery piece on one and green hills and telegraph poles slide past), an end door, an aisle carpet with a gold border and a tread plate gangway. Plush seats facing east or west across a window table, sleeping berths with a pillow and blanket, compartment partitions, dining tables laid in linen with a lamp that flickers, chairs, the bar with its bottles, a coal stove that glows, and for the freight car rough planks, crates, mail sacks, steamer trunks and the express strongbox. 4 sets, 246 tiles, sample map 26x25.

What is in it

  • 64 ground tiles (four variants of each ground) and 611 transition tiles in 13 complete 47 shape sets.
  • 59 kinds of still object and 10 animated objects (a winding drum, a miner's lamp, a pump, two gears, the turning clock hands, a pendulum, the window scenery, a dining lamp and a stove).
  • Three sample maps (the Mine Works, the Clocktower, the Express) as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.
  • Same grid as the rest of the Verdant series, so it sits beside Verdant Rail Terminus, Verdant Crystal Hollows, Verdant Victorian Industrial and Verdant Mechanisms. You do not need them; everything here is in this download.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: all 13 sets on an A2 page (624 of 624 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the grounds, objects and animation frames on the B, C, D, E pages at 48px. MZ does not animate those pages, so the animated objects are frames you swap by event. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation written in at its own timing. Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew all three sample maps identical to ours (1,853,440 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 15 instants with the animations advanced, its playback matched our own render exactly.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (867 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantIronworks/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant Ironworks Mine, Clocktower and Steam Train Interior Tilesets with Rails and Turning Gears [16x16] > Build Unity Assets). Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: it built 867 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, and 13 RuleTile brushes drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (3,328 of 3,328); Unity's render of the sample scene matched the map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 and every sheet an exact multiple of it, every pixel is inside the 122 colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 13 sets carry all 47 shapes.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced.
