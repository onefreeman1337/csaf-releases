Which packs belong: the series' overworld, town and castle packs drawn in its standard top down style, with their winter, sacked and ruined twins. Verdant packs on these subjects drawn for another format (the Game Boy Color, 8 bit and side view packs) belong to families of their own. Every paid Verdant tile pack, these included, is in the Verdant Library.

What every pack contains

  • Single 16x16 tiles and packed sheets, with a tile map (tiles.json) naming every tile's role.
  • A Tiled .tsx and a sample map, and a Godot 4 TileSet.
  • RPG Maker MZ tileset sheets.
  • Its own Readme, its own LICENSE.txt and its proof renders.

Compatibility

  • Godot 4: a TileSet in all 7 packs, each loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 from its folder inside this download.
  • Tiled: a .tsx in all 7 packs, opened and rendered by Tiled's own tmxrasterizer.
  • RPG Maker MZ: tileset images in all 7 packs; the A2 pages compose against MZ's own autotile table.
  • Unity: an import layer in 3 of 7 packs (Verdant 01 Overworld, Verdant 09 Town and Verdant 58 Castle Ruin), each passed a fresh install in a blank Unity project on these same bytes.
  • GameMaker: an import layer in 4 of 7 packs (Verdant 01 Overworld, Verdant 09 Town, Verdant 10 Harbour and Verdant 13 Castle Tileset), each passed a fresh install in a blank GameMaker project on these same bytes.

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven; this download's 3,569 tiles were proved again from their folders here.
