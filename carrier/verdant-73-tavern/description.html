The tavern every RPG town needs, at 16x16: 428 tiles in one 98 colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

A bar counter that turns any corner

The counter is a complete 47 shape autotile: paint it and it draws its own ends, edges, outer corners and inside corners, so it runs straight, turns into an L, closes into a U or stands as an island. It has a honey oak top with a lit edge, a dark panelled front with a brass rail and a contact shadow, and the same counter ships on flagstones for a kitchen. Taps, a till, tankards, bottles, a candle and a lift flap stand on its top; stools go along its front.

What is in it

  • A taproom: booths, round tables, a trestle table with benches, a hearth with a spit roast, the back bar with shelves of bottles, a keg rack and a cask stillage.
  • A raised bard stage of dark walnut boards (walkable), with a lute, a hand drum, a stool, a music stand and footlights.
  • A kitchen on flagstones: an iron range, a stewpot over coals, a stone sink, pantry shelves, sacks and a work counter.
  • An inn: four guest room doors marked with one to four pips (no text, so it works in any language) to hang on a corridor's back wall as doors to your room maps, double, single and bunk beds, a wardrobe, a writing desk, a wash stand and trunks.
  • 8 floor tiles (floorboards and flagstones, four variants each), 18 back wall tiles (a two tile high wall for the taproom, the kitchen and the inn, with ends for every doorway), and 282 transition tiles in 6 complete 47 mask sets (the counter, the stage and the room wall, each on both floors).
  • 64 objects, 5 of them animated (hearth fire with a spit roast, wall candle sconce, candle in a dish, stewpot over coals, stage footlight candle) and 17 made to hang on the walls. Each standing piece carries a contact shadow drawn for its floor.
  • A finished 36x30 sample map, The Wayfarer's Rest, with the taproom, kitchen, corridor and three guest rooms, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.

Part of the Verdant series

The floorboards and flagstones are Verdant 07 Interiors's own tiles, byte for byte, so this tavern joins the houses, shops and halls of that pack without a seam. For the streets outside, see Verdant 09 Town; for a castle's halls and kitchens, Verdant 57 Keep Interiors. This pack also works on its own: nothing in it needs another pack's files.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on an A2 page as MZ autotile brushes (288 of 288 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the floors, back walls and objects on the B, C pages. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Passability is yours to set in the Database.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with the animations written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,105,920 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 4 points in time its animation playback matched our frames.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (428 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant73Tavern/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 73 Tavern > Build Unity Assets). It builds 428 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 6 RuleTile brushes, 6 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (1,536 of 1,536), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 98 colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 6 sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover and every gallery image are made from the pack's own tiles.
