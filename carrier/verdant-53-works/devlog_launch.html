Verdant 53 Works is out, a factory automation layer for top down maps at 16x16. 2,490 tiles in one 74 colour palette: conveyor belts in 3 tiers, 12 inserter arms, 11 animated machines, a pipe set, power poles and 10 complete autotile sets for ore fields and a mill floor, for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot and Unity. $7.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-53-works

The headline is the belts. Wood, iron and brass, 32 pieces each: straights, corners both ways, side load merges, underground entries and exits and two cell splitters. Every piece runs on one shared phase, so a chevron leaving one tile enters the next on the same frame. We checked every joinable pair at all 8 frames, 576 pairs and 36,864 edge pixels, with no breaks.

Around them: a burner miner, a stone smelter, a brass assembler, a steam press, a boiler, a pump, a sawmill, a power loom, a bottle kiln, an ore crusher and a research table, each grounded on the mill floor or on grass. Coal, iron ore, copper and stone fields paint onto Verdant 01's own grass and dirt. Tiled plays every animation: its own renderer drew the 40x24 sample map at 8 points in time and matched our frames.

Unity: one menu command builds 2,490 Tiles, 10 RuleTile brushes and 242 AnimatedTiles in your project, tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project. RPG Maker MZ gets an A2 autotile sheet composed back through MZ's own autotile table.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine and hand authored pixel rows run through the same code; that software is AI written. No image model, no scraped art and no training data.
