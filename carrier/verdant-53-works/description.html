What is in it

  • 96 belt pieces that link: wood, iron and brass, 32 pieces each. Straights, corners both ways, side-load merges, underground entries and exits and two-cell splitters, all on one shared phase, so a chevron leaving one tile enters the next on the same frame. Checked: 576 joinable pairs at all 8 frames, 36,864 edge pixels, 0 breaks.
  • 12 inserter arms: burner, standard and long-reach, each in four facings, the arm swinging round to its drop and back over 8 frames.
  • 12 machines: a burner miner, stone smelter, brass assembler, steam press, boiler, pump, sawmill, power loom, bottle kiln, ore crusher and research table (all animated), and an iron-bound chest. Each ships grounded on the mill floor and on grass with a contact shadow, and transparent for your own ground.
  • 16 iron pipe pieces covering every way a pipe can join, and a wooden power pole and an iron pylon.
  • 470 transition tiles in 10 complete 47-mask sets: coal, iron ore, copper, a stone quarry and a raised mill floor, each over grass and over dirt.
  • 28 fill tiles: the grass and dirt are Verdant 01 Overworld's own tiles, byte for byte, so this pack drops straight into a Verdant map; plus the mill floor and the four ore fields, four variants each.
  • A finished 40x24 sample map, "The Works": miners on four ore fields, belts dipping through an underground crossing and into a hall of machines, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx.

Engines

  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant53Works/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 53 Works > Build Unity Assets). It builds 2,490 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 10 RuleTile brushes, 242 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (2,560 of 2,560) and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package. No collision is set; add colliders where your game needs them.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with every animation and its timing written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (983,040 px, 0 differ), and drew it again at 8 points in time with every animation on the right frame (1,966,080 px).
  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on the A2 sheet as MZ's own autotile brush; every shape was composed back out of the sheet with MZ's own autotile table (480 of 480 identical). Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Machines, belts, arms, pipes and fills on the B, C, D, E sheets; MZ does not animate object tiles, so the frames are there as tiles.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (2,490 tiles built). The TileSet carries no animation; every frame is there as a tile.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16, every pixel is inside the 74-colour palette, and alpha is strictly on or off. The sheets are compared with the loose tiles before every release.

AI disclosure
