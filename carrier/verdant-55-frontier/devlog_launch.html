Verdant 55 Frontier is out, a Wild West setting for top down RPG maps at 16x16: 344 tiles in one 67 colour palette, 25 objects, for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled, Godot and Unity. $7.99. csaf.itch.io/verdant-55-frontier

What is in it: a boomtown with a two storey saloon, a general store with a striped awning and a sandstone jail, all fronting a plank boardwalk with hitching rails, a horse trough, lantern posts and a wanted poster board; open golden prairie with a fenced corral, a covered wagon, a windpump and boot hill; and a red rock canyon with raised slickrock benches, saguaros, hoodoos and a mine adit.

Three things move: a tumbleweed rolls, a lantern flickers and a windpump turns, at 180 ms a frame. We checked that Tiled plays them by having Tiled's own renderer draw the sample map at the middle of every frame and comparing every pixel with ours.

Unity: one menu command builds 344 Tiles, 5 RuleTile brushes and 6 AnimatedTiles in your project, tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project. The 40x26 sample map ships as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, and every one of the 5 autotile sets appears on it.

Every tile is drawn by our own procedural pixel art engine, which is AI written software. No image model draws any tile; the cover illustration is made with one, and the listing carries the Generative AI tag.
