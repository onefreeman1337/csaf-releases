The dungeon half of a map, at 16x16: 760 tiles in two locked palettes. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Paint the plan, and the walls draw themselves

Rooms and corridors are one complete 47 shape autotile: paint the floor plan and the ink walls, the square corners and the corridor mouths draw themselves, so a corridor runs into a room with no seam, turns, forks, crosses and dead ends. Around the plan is hatched rock, the way a cartographer draws it. Caverns get a rough wall of their own, and water and the chasm paint on top of the plan.

Two palettes, one drawing

Every tile ships twice: in the parchment and ink of Verdant 40 Atlas, the world map this pack pairs with, and in the old school blue dungeon map style with solid blue rock, white rooms and a blue grid. Each blue tile is its paper tile's twin, drawn from the same picture (380 pairs, each in the same cell of its twin sheet), so swapping one sheet for its twin restyles the whole dungeon without moving a cell.

What is in it

  • 564 transition tiles in 12 complete 47 mask sets, six per palette: hewn rooms and corridors, the same plan on a gridded floor (one grid square per cell), rough caverns, a flooded room, an underground lake and a chasm.
  • 50 fills: hatched rock, the plain and gridded floors, cave floor, water, the chasm and a fog of war cover for rooms the party has not seen.
  • 73 marks in each palette: 12 doors for both corridor directions (plain, double, secret, locked, a portcullis, an archway), 8 stairs, ladders and a trapdoor, 23 furnishings and hazards (pillars, statues, an altar, a fountain, a well, sarcophagi, a throne, a pit, a spike trap, a rope bridge and more), 15 map symbols (the boss, treasure chests, a key, a quest pin, a rest point, a merchant, a monster lair, the party arrow, a goal star, a save crystal) and 15 ornaments (a compass, a scale bar, a blank level tablet, a map border). No mark carries a letter, so the pack works in any language.
  • A finished 58x28 sample map of three levels, in both palettes, as PNG and JSON, and the paper map as a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.

Part of the Verdant series

Pairs with Verdant 40 Atlas for the world map outside; for the dungeon itself at character scale, see Verdant 05 Depths and Verdant 52 Delves. This pack works on its own: nothing in it needs another pack's files.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on an A2 page as MZ autotile brushes (576 of 576 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the fills, marks and every autotile shape on the B, C, D pages. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Passability is yours to set in the Database.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,662,976 px, 0 differ).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (760 tiles built).
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/Verdant74DungeonCharts/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 74 Dungeon Charts > Build Unity Assets). It builds 760 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 12 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (3,072 of 3,072), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette, twins and autotile completeness are machine proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the compass an exact 2x2), every pixel is inside its palette (21 colours in paper, 17 in blue), alpha is strictly on or off, every paper tile has a blue twin of the same outline, and all 12 sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover and every gallery image are made from the pack's own tiles.
