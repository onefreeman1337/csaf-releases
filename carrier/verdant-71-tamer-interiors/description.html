The rooms of a monster tamer town, at 16x16: 694 tiles in one 92-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Five rooms, each with a real back wall

A healing hall with restoring machines behind a service counter, a mart, a gym with a clay court and bleachers, a research lab with microscopes and specimen tanks, and the player's house. Every room stands behind its own two tile high back wall: papered, with a rail, a wainscot and a skirting, an end piece for every doorway and corner, and windows, a clock, posters, a notice board, a painting and a map to hang on it. The walls draw a lit top edge, so a partition reads as standing up.

What is in it

  • 20 floor tiles (checker, terrazzo, lab tile, sport boards, floorboards, four variants each) and 30 back wall tiles, six per room.
  • 517 transition tiles in 11 complete 47-mask sets: a rug, a waiting carpet, a clay court, two counters, a lab bench and a wall for every room.
  • 58 objects, 7 of them animated (restoring machines, terminals, a specimen tank, a television, a brazier) and 6 made to hang on the walls. Each standing piece carries a contact shadow drawn for its floor. All original designs: the only emblem in the pack is a leaf.
  • A finished 44x28 sample map, Route Town, Indoors, with all five rooms, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.

Part of the Verdant series

Drawn in the same palette and on the same 16px cell as Verdant 62 Tamer Routes, the outdoor routes and town these rooms belong to, so a door on a route map opens into a room that matches it. For homes, inns and shops of a classic fantasy town, see Verdant 07 Interiors. This pack also works on its own.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on an A2 page as MZ autotile brushes (528 of 528 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the floors, back walls and objects on the B, C, D pages. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Passability is yours to set in the Database.
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet with the animations written in; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (1,261,568 px, 0 differ), and rendered at 4 points in time its animation playback matched our frames.
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (694 tiles built). Godot gets the frames as separate tiles, not as animations.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantTamerInteriors/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 71 Tamer Interiors > Build Unity Assets). It builds 694 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 11 RuleTile brushes, 7 AnimatedTiles and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (2,816 of 2,816), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. Brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 92-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 11 sets carry all 47 masks.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model and is not part of the download. The machines, emblems and furniture are original designs, not any franchise's.
