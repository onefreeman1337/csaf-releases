A castle built of ice, complete enough to build a level from, at 16x16: 587 tiles in one locked 40-colour palette. Commercial use permitted in unlimited projects, no attribution required; you may not resell the tiles themselves as an asset pack.

Walls of ice, towers of glacier, a moat of black ice

Curtain walls of glassy ice slabs with a battlement of ice merlons and a deep blue face that ascends to a lit lip; towers of deep glacial ice blocks with frost seams and long reflections; ice-plate roofs with icicle drips; a frozen moat of black ice; a ward of trodden snow; and a snowfield. Every wall, tower, roof, moat and ward edge is a complete 47-mask autotile set.

What is in it

  • 517 transition tiles in 11 complete 47-mask sets: ice wall walks and glacial towers standing in the ward, the snow and the moat, an ice roof, the ward, the frozen moat, and the snowfield meeting grass.
  • 24 fill tiles: snow, trodden snow, black ice, ice slabs, glacial ice and ice plates.
  • 21 objects, every one made of ice, each with a contact shadow: crystal spires and a three-spire cluster for the tower tops, an ice gate with a frosted portcullis, frost braziers and an ice lantern of cold cyan fire, royal banners with a gilt snowflake, an ice throne with a violet cushion, ice knights, an ice swan and an ice crown, a frozen fountain hung with icicles, an ice pillar and obelisk, a chest of ice, an ice bench and steps, a sledge of cut ice blocks and a stack of them, violet ice crystals, a snow drift and a saw-cut fishing hole for the moat.
  • A finished 36x26 sample map, The Ice Palace, as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, every tile from this pack.

Made with the Verdant series

The snowfield is Verdant 03 — Worlds's own snow, byte for byte, the snow Verdant 41 — Taiga uses too, so the palace meets either map without a visible edge. The eleven sets follow the shapes of Verdant 13 — Castle's eleven, battlement rhythm and all, so a castle plan drawn for one draws the other. It also works on its own.

Engines

  • RPG Maker MZ: every set on an A2 page as MZ autotile brushes (528 of 528 shapes composed back with MZ's own autotile table), and the objects and fills on the B, C and D pages. Tested against the table, not inside the MZ editor. Passability is yours to set in the Database (tiles.json carries an advisory flag for every tile).
  • Tiled: one .tsx per sheet; Tiled 1.12.2's own renderer drew the sample map identical to ours (958,464 px, 0 differ).
  • Godot 4: one TileSet, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 (587 tiles built), with relative texture paths so the folder works anywhere in your project.
  • Unity: copy importers/Unity/VerdantIcePalace/ into your project and run one menu command (Tools > CSAF Verdant > Verdant 63 Ice Palace > Build Unity Assets). It builds 587 pixel exact sprites and Tiles, 11 RuleTile brushes and a scene holding the sample map. Tested in a blank Unity 6000.5.7f1 project: every RuleTile drew the right tile in all 256 neighbour arrangements (2,816 of 2,816), and Unity's render of the scene matched the sample map pixel for pixel. The brushes need the free 2D Tilemap Extras package.

The proofs

Every tile's wrap boundary is measured against its own interior, and every 4x4 repetition is rendered and reviewed. Grid, palette and autotile completeness are machine-proven: every tile is exactly 16x16 (the multi-cell objects exact multiples), every pixel is inside the 40-colour palette, alpha is strictly on or off, and all 11 sets carry all 47 masks. Stated plainly: 8 wall and tower tiles are the same picture in two sibling sets (where the wall's face and shadow cover the ground), and the ice blocks are laid in a regular bond, as masonry is.

AI disclosure

Every tile is generated by our own procedural drawing engine or drawn as pixel rows in code, all of it AI written software. No image model draws any tile, no scraped art and no training dataset were used, and nothing was traced. The cover illustration is made with an image model.
