29 medieval townsfolk sprites for side-scrolling games. A pixel art character pack for platformers, brawlers and side-view RPGs: the town trades, the keep household and its guards, and the folk of the road and the tavern. Every character has an idle, a walk and a work animation; 5 fighters also attack, flinch and fall. 1,108 frames facing left and right at 160x160 and 80x80, with sprite sheets, frames.json and Godot 4 SpriteFrames. Commercial use allowed, no credit required. A free Hearthhands sampler is linked below.

What is inside

  • 29 characters in one style and one 128-colour palette: smith, baker, butcher, fishmonger, carpenter, washerwoman, apothecary, pieSeller, townCrier, townChild, king, steward, maid, cook, jester, scribe, priest, keepGuard, shieldGuard, merchant, pilgrim, bard, hunter, farmer, beggar, tavernKeeper, barmaid, brawler, bandit.
  • Every character: idle (4 frames, breathing), walk (6) and a job loop (6): the smith swings his hammer, the crier rings his bell, the king waves, the jester capers, the barmaid raises a toast.
  • 5 fighters (keepGuard, shieldGuard, hunter, brawler, bandit): attack, hurt and death (6 frames each, one-shots), so they work as enemies.
  • Both facings: right-facing frames are the drawn art; every left-facing frame is its exact horizontal mirror (checked pixel for pixel in all 554 pairs at 160x160), so a prop held in one hand swaps hands facing left.
  • Two sizes: 160x160 native and an exact 2:1 80x80 reduction, palette-locked. All characters share one ground row, so they stand together at 1:1.
  • Engine-ready: per-animation sprite sheets, single frames, frames.json with every rectangle, and Godot 4 SpriteFrames (.tres) with one-shots set not to loop. Works in Unity, GameMaker, Construct and any engine that reads PNG sheets.

The consistency proof

Re-derived from the shipped files by our pack gate before upload: 2,624 PNG files, all real PNGs; 11,449,044 opaque pixels, every one inside the declared palette; 106,368,000 pixels of strictly binary alpha (no anti-aliasing, no soft upscale); 4,432 frame-map checks.
