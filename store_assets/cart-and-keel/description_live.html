Cart & Keel is 25 pixel art medieval vehicle sprites for Godot 4, RPG Maker MZ and any engine that reads PNG sheets: carts, wagons, riverboats and small ships. Use them in commercial and free games, no credit needed. A free two vehicle demo is on this page.

What is inside

25 vehicles · 4 directions · a still and a 6 frame moving loop · PNG sheets at 200 and 100 px

  • The road (13): hay wagon, covered wagon, ox cart, merchant's wagon, noble's coach, reliquary coach, timber wagon, brewer's dray, prison wagon, handcart, wheelbarrow, pedlar's cart and water cart.
  • The river (7): rowboat, fishing skiff, dugout canoe, log raft, plank ferry, river barge and swan boat.
  • The harbour (5): small sailboat, fishing smack, longship, trading cog and caravel.
  • In side view the wheels roll forward on 11 of the 13 road vehicles and the oars row; sails shift and some hulls bob a little.
  • 700 frames at each size, every frame as its own PNG and on a sheet, with frames.json mapping all of them.
  • One palette. Every still pose sits on one ground row and one centre column, so one vehicle swaps for another in the same facing without moving.

Works with

Godot 4: 50 ready SpriteFrames files, loaded and checked in Godot 4.7.2. RPG Maker MZ: 50 character sheets for img/characters, usable on events or as a Boat, Ship or Airship image. Any other engine: the PNG sheets, frames and frames.json.

Good to know

Each vehicle is the vehicle alone, with no driver and no draught animals, so you can hitch your own. Head-on and rear loops show the vehicle lifting or riding over its wheels; twenty-eight loops are a mechanical bounce, on three vehicles only the wheels move in side view, and on the two coaches the wheels stay still in side view while the body rides over them. Some vehicles change width or detail between facings, the plank ferry and river barge most. Head-on, the tow pole is the lowest point, so wagons sit higher in that facing. Boats have no waterline or wake. The Readme lists the details, including the small marks that remain.
