Every song your player learns becomes an engraved plate they collect - and the plate plays what it shows.

A learnable-songs system for bards, ocarinas, shanties and spells. Give it a song id and a tradition; it composes the melody, engraves it as real notation on aged paper, and synthesises it into GameMaker audio buffers. No image files and no audio files ship: a song that does not exist until your player finds it still has a score and a sound.

my_book = sng_book_create();
sng_book_learn(my_book, "millers-daughter", "folk", "The Miller's Daughter");
sng_book_learn(my_book, "hours-of-ash", "chant");
my_songbook = sng_songbook_create(my_book);

// Step:     sng_songbook_step(my_songbook, up, down, play, stop);
// Draw GUI: sng_songbook_draw(my_songbook, 0, 0, display_get_gui_width(), display_get_gui_height());

What it gives you

  • Ten traditions, ten kinds of page - Plainchant, Courtly Air, Reel, Muster Call, Lament, Fae Round, Hymnal, Shanty, Incantation, Work Song - each with its own melodic construction, mode, metre, paper, ink, page frame, instrument and tempo.
  • Five notation systems - square neumes with sung syllables, white mensural notation, modern staves, a percussion grid over a charted horn call, and a spiral stave that comes back round.
  • Real engraving. One letter per scale degree and a real key signature, stems that flip at the middle line, beams that slope with the melody and never cross a barline, ledger lines, dotted notes, flags. A musician can read it.
  • It plays. sng_play starts a song in under 20 ms and streams the rest; the playhead lights each notehead as it sounds. Nine synthesised instruments.
  • A songbook screen - desk, index of sheets, the selected plate and its playhead - plus a one-call plate sprite for anywhere else in your game, generated titles, WAV export and a tiny save format.

Measured, not estimated

  • 4,886 of 4,886 notes identical to our Song Sheets for RPG Maker MZ across 300 songs, all ten traditions and three tonics: the same id is the same melody in both engines.
  • 159 of 159 notes in tune: every note of ten synthesised songs, found in the audio by a pitch tracker and compared with the note the plate shows, within 25 cents. No clipping, no dead air.
  • Forty songs engrave as forty different pictures, and every two plates of one tradition differ by more than a one-note edit would.

Tested

  • Igor package build clean on the shipped source, and proven able to fail on it.
  • 157 in-engine assertions on a real built exe.
  • 18 more in a blank project that installed the package the way you will and ran the Readme's quickstart, asserting that each screen actually draws.
  • 164 adversarial calls - undefined, negative, huge and wrong-typed arguments against all 41 documented functions - with zero crashes.

Requirements

GameMaker 2024.14 or newer (built and tested against runtime 2024.14.4.268). Pure GML: no extensions, no DLLs, no shaders. Import the .yymps, open rm_sng_demo, press Play.
