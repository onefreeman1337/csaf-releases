Brinemark is 13 top-down pixel art sprites for Godot 4, RPG Maker MZ and any engine that reads PNG sheets: pirates, navy and smugglers. Use them in commercial and free games, no credit needed.

What is inside

13 characters · 5 animations each · 4 directions · sheets and single frames at 160 and 80 px

  • The pirate crew (4): captain, gunner with a blunderbuss, quartermaster with an iron buckler and deckhand with a belaying pin.
  • The crown navy (4): admiral with a silver pistol, marine with a musketoon, lieutenant in white gloves and powder hand who boxes.
  • Smugglers and islanders (5): masked smuggler with a pistol, fence with a gold knuckle duster, pearl diver who fights with her fists, harbour witch with a glowing lantern and castaway with a driftwood club.
  • Every character has an idle, a walk (6 frames), an attack (6), a hurt (6) and a death (6) facing south, west, east and north: 1,300 frames at each size.
  • Five fire a pistol, blunderbuss or musketoon with the muzzle flash drawn in; the witch swings a lantern that flares; the rest fight hand to hand.
  • One 128 colour palette and one human build, shared with Hearthhands 05: Harbour Hands, so the harbour villagers and this cast stand in one scene at 1:1.

One frame from every state, every character, at 1x of the 160 px master.

Every character in all four facings, back views included.

Works with

Godot 4: 26 ready SpriteFrames files, checked in Godot 4.7.2. RPG Maker MZ: 26 walk sheets for img/characters. Any engine: sheets, single PNG frames and frames.json.

Good to know

Attacks, hurts and deaths are one-shots; every death ends on the ground. Gunfire is drawn as muzzle flashes, with no projectile sprites. One side view of each character is the other mirrored, so props swap hands between east and west. A few strikes turn toward the camera mid-swing, and some props drop out of single frames, mostly in deaths and back views. The full Readme lists what each attack does and every small variation we found.

Licence and how it was made

Use the sprites in commercial and free games, no credit needed. Do not resell them as a sprite pack.

The sprites were generated with an AI image model (PixelLab), then palette locked and checked frame by frame by our tools. The packaging code was written with AI.
