A race meeting your players can bet on. They read a race card, back a racing snail to Win or Place, and watch the race run on a garden course this package draws: moss, slate, sand, puddles, leaf litter and gravel, each suiting some runners more than others. Close finishes go to the judge's photograph. Pure GML, no image files.

Drop a meeting into a fairground, a tavern garden or a noble's lawn. It takes the player's gold in, runs up to five races, and hands the purse back when they leave. Every runner's form carries on from one visit to the next.

my_meeting = dby_meeting_create({ seed: 2026, races: 3, purse: my_gold, record: my_form });
if (dby_meeting_step(my_meeting) == "done") my_gold = my_meeting.purse;          // Step
dby_meeting_draw(my_meeting, 0, 0, display_get_gui_width(), display_get_gui_height());   // Draw GUI

What your players see

  • The race card: every runner's number cloth, its name, the going it likes, its silks, its last five placings with wins in red, and its Win and Place prices chalked on a slate. A map of the course beside it.
  • The bet slip: Win or Place, a stake stepper that never passes the purse, what the bet returns, and the purse. Keyboard, mouse or gamepad.
  • The race: the course scrolling under the field, snails wiggling and bobbing, trails glistening behind them, a lane board, a progress strip in every runner's silks, and live commentary: a new leader, a burst of speed, a stop for a nibble, the leaders reaching the sand. Hold Enter to watch at triple speed.
  • The photograph: when the first two finish within a head, the judge's print of the line, toned to sepia, with a red stamp.
  • The winners' enclosure: rosettes for the first three, starting prices and margins ("won by three-quarters of a shell"), and the payout.

Everything is generated

  • The stable: twelve runners a seed, each with a name, a spiral shell in one of ten colourways with bands, spots, flames or rays, a body colour, two silks from twelve and a saddle cloth in one of six patterns. Hidden speed, stamina, surge, temper and a going they prefer.
  • Five garden courses: a kitchen-garden wall with potted herbs, a rockery of flowering stones, a greenhouse bench of tomatoes, an orchard, a mossy bank of ferns and toadstools, each laid with four to six sections of surface.
  • The prices are measured, not guessed. Every runner is raced alone 400 times over that card's course and going by the same model the player watches, the results are combined exactly, and every price is set on the upper bound of the chance and rounded down. The race on screen IS the race the book priced.

Requirements

GameMaker 2024.14 or newer (built and tested against runtime 2024.14.4.268). Pure GML: no extensions, no DLLs, no shaders, no sprites. Import the .yymps, open rm_dby_demo, press Play: a three-race meeting with 500 gold. Derby ships no sounds; an on_event hook tells you when to play yours.
