Hearthhands 05: Harbour Hands is live at $7.99. Ten people who work the village waterfront: a harbourmaster, a lighthouse keeper and a bosun who keep the harbour, a dockhand, a shipwright, a ferryman and a deckhand who work the boats and the quay, and a net mender, a salt raker and a crabber who work the shore. Each has an idle, a six frame walk and a six frame job loop in all four directions, at 200x200 and 100x100.

The job loop is the trade itself. The spyglass goes up to the eye, the mallet goes overhead, the brush scrubs, the rake pulls, the crab pot comes up to be looked into. Every one of them stands on the ground line shared by all six Hearthhands packs, so the ferryman stands beside the smith from pack 01 at true relative height, and the palette holds all 43 series colours unchanged.

One thing we changed before launch

A tool carried in one hand sat on the far side of the body in one profile, and the generated art there hid it: the oar, the deck brush, the lantern, the crab pot and the mallet went missing from that side view. Two more side views drew something wrong: a satchel the harbourmaster does not wear, and the dockhand's rope coil floating off his shoulder with no hand on it. For those eight of the ten we ship that side as an exact mirror of the other side, so the tool stays in view in every frame and idle, walk and job agree in that facing; the tool moves to the other hand there. Every mirrored cycle is listed by name in the Readme and on the page, and our checker confirms each one pixel for pixel.
