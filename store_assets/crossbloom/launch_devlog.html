CROSSBLOOM is out: a free orchard incremental where an apple tree sets fruit only beside a different variety that blooms when it does.

You take on your grandmother's orchard: seven old trees on a slope, all one variety, so they barely crop. Every variety blooms in its own window of spring, early, mid or late, and a tree sets fruit only on the days a partner beside it is in bloom too. A wilding crab blooms all spring and pollinates any other variety near it, though crab pollen sets only half a crop, and one dwarfing tree of any variety is free every year.

On frost nights cold air runs down the slope and pools in the low ground, killing open blossom. A hedge shelters the bees from the wind but dams the cold air above it unless you leave a gap. Dwarfing trees crop young and small, vigorous ones crop big and shade their neighbours. The press pays for a blend of sharp, sweet and bittersweet cider apples, so you need every bloom window working. Grow all eight named varieties to thirty apples in one year with a 90% blend and the village comes up the hill with lanterns for the wassail.

It saves as you go, and the orchard keeps growing while you are away, for up to a year. It plays in the browser on desktop and phones, with its own tall layout on an upright phone. The wassail takes a little under half an hour at normal speed.
