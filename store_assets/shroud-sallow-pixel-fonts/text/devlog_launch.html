Shroud & Sallow is a pair of original pixel fonts drawn for horror games.

  • Sallow is the dialogue face (14 px line): a narrow hand for diary pages, notes, dialogue, item text and menus, drawn to stay readable over a whole page.
  • Shroud is the display face (22 px line): tall condensed letters with cut tops and drips, for title screens, chapter cards and the word written on the wall.

Each face has 197 characters (printable ASCII, the Latin-1 Supplement and six typographic marks) and ships as a glyph sheet, an AngelCode BMFont file, a TrueType file that draws the same pixels, and a metrics JSON. The ink is white, so your engine tints it to any colour.

The drips follow a rule rather than chance. 34 drips hang from 32 letters, digits and marks, and only from flat strokes. While drawing them we found that a drip under a round letter reads as an accent (an S turns into a Ş), so the round letters carry none, and no drip turns one letter into another.

Checked on the download itself: 394 glyphs, one ink colour, 84 kerning pairs, and in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project every glyph drawn with the .fnt and with the .ttf matched the sheet pixel for pixel.

The glyphs were drawn by an AI agent as code, and the page is tagged that way. The rooms in the screenshots come from our Haunted House tileset and the wraith from our Mourncrest sprites; neither is in this download.
