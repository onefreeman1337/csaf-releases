Six dragonborn adventurers for the same company. A knight, a brawler, an archer, a warden, a sorcerer and a priest of the dragon folk, each with a scaled dragon's head in a different lineage colour. Every one of them has a standing pose and a 6-frame walk in all four directions, front, back and both profiles, at 128x128 and 64x64.

Drawn at the height of the tall folk. In the 128 cell they stand 104 to 121 pixels tall (the priest's horns are the top of that range), beside the 95 to 117 of the Wildmark 01 and 02 adventurers. All six stand on one ground line (row 123 of the 128 cell), the same row as Wildmark 03.

It joins the Wildmark party. Wildmark 01: The Party, Wildmark 02: The Second Rank and Wildmark 03: The Far Kin hold twelve adventurers on the same 128 and 64 grids. This pack's 256-colour palette contains every one of pack 03's 224 colours unchanged (and pack 03's holds all of 02's, and 02's all of 01's), plus 32 more fitted to the scale colours, so all 18 can share a screen without a colour clash.

The cast

  • Knight: A crimson-scaled dragonborn in ridged steel pauldrons over a deep teal tabard.
  • Brawler: A bronze-scaled dragonborn, bare to the waist, with linen-wrapped forearms and a green sash.
  • Archer: An emerald-scaled dragonborn in a long tan coat, with a quiver at her hip.
  • Warden: A silver-grey-scaled dragonborn in a heavy green mantle over an orange vest.
  • Sorcerer: A sapphire-scaled dragonborn in a plum coat with a high gold collar.
  • Priest: A gold-scaled dragonborn in a white vestment with a wide collar and a gold sun disc.

The consistency proof

The honest worry about generated art is that frame 40 will not match frame 1. This pack proves it against the decoded pixels of every file in the download:

  • 396 files are real PNGs and all 396 decode
  • 2,421,860 opaque pixels, every one inside the declared 256-colour palette
  • 11,796,480 pixels carry strictly binary alpha, no anti-aliasing, no soft upscale
  • 672 frame-map checks: every one of the 168 frames per grid is declared, lands inside its sheet and has a real file at both grids
  • every asset present at one grid size is present at the other (396 slots)
  • every pair of characters is mechanically distinct: 11 of 15 pairs clear the 25% outline floor, and the other 4 (closest pair 18.6% apart in outline, the same grown build) differ in 80.2% to 84% of their pixels against a 70% floor

What you get

  • 168 frames at 128x128 (the master) and 168 at 64x64 (an exact 2:1 reduction)
  • 12 sheets per grid, a walk sheet and an idle sheet per character, plus every frame as its own PNG
  • frames.json with every frame's sheet, x, y, w, h, and pack.json with the palette, grids and cast
  • RPG Maker MZ: 12 $-prefixed 3x4 walk sheets (6 per grid), drop into img/characters
  • Godot 4: a SpriteFrames .tres per character per grid, every animation named and sliced, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 before shipping

Honest notes

  • One facing is a mirror: the warden's east-facing pose and walk are flipped from her west-facing ones, because the generator drew her east view as a three-quarter turn twice. Every other direction of every animation was generated in that direction.
  • The archer's quiver hangs at her right hip; in her east walk her near arm swings across it on two of six frames.
  • Walking away from the camera, the warden's mantle covers her to the calves, so the stride shows in her boots.
  • Seen from the side or behind, the sorcerer's high collar reads as a raised hood.
  • The Wildmark 01 and 02 casts stand between rows 117 and 123 of the cell, so anchor mixed parties on the feet.
