Wildmark 04: Dragonkin is live at $5.99. Six dragonborn adventurers for the Wildmark party: a knight, a brawler, an archer, a warden, a sorcerer and a priest, each with a scaled dragon's head in its own lineage colour (crimson, bronze, emerald, silver grey, sapphire and gold). Each has a standing pose and a six frame walk in all four directions, at 128x128 and 64x64.

They are drawn at the height of the tall folk. In the 128 cell they stand 104 to 121 pixels tall, where the eight adventurers of packs 01 and 02 stand 95 to 117, and all six stand on the same ground row as the Wildmark 03 cast.

One company

The palette holds every one of Wildmark 03's 224 colours unchanged, and those already hold every colour of packs 01 and 02. Thirty two more colours were added for the scale colours. The four packs nest, so all eighteen characters can share one screen without a colour clash.

What ships

  • 168 frames per grid, a walk sheet and an idle sheet per character, and frames.json with every frame's position
  • RPG Maker MZ walk sheets and Godot 4 SpriteFrames, both loaded and checked before shipping
  • One mirrored facing, stated in the Readme: the warden's east view is her west view flipped, because the generator drew her east view turned three quarters on two attempts. Every other direction was generated in that direction.
