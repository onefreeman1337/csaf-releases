You are the cave. Cut chambers into a hill, put things in them, and decide what happens to the people who come down looking for whatever they think is in there.

It is an incremental — numbers go up, the board gets bigger, you can leave it running — but the thing you are optimising is a route, and the thing walking that route is somebody else's run.

The two things that fight

A delver who dies leaves remains, and your sinks render remains into spoil and marrow. A delver who gets out alive raises your renown — the only thing that makes the next cohort bigger, and the only thing that brings the richer kinds of delver down at all — and strips your workings on the way through.

Kill everyone and nobody comes. Kill nobody and you render nothing. The answer is a long route that bleeds them slowly and kills most of them deep, beside a sink, while a few crawl back out to tell everyone about it.

The second thing that fights

Every row you cut raises the water table. Under it, wards lose most of their bite and moss drowns — but sinks render half again as fast, and springs only work at all. Sumps and the CHANNEL vein push it back down, and they cost the same spoil as everything else. Going deep is both the goal and the tax.

What is in it

  • Twelve fixtures in four families, three levels each — roots, wards, lures, sinks, and drainage. A new one every two rows.
  • Four bands of rock, each with a different problem in it: soft wet loam, dry flint-shot chalk, laminated leaking shale, and granite that renders what dies in it twice over.
  • Five kinds of delver, from scroungers to adepts, each with their own grit, greed and wit — and each worth a very different amount dead.
  • Eight veins of permanent upgrade, bought with marrow. The last one ends the story; the hollow keeps working afterwards.

It never resets

There is no prestige wipe and no death screen. The runs belong to the delvers. Your hollow only ever grows, it saves itself every ten seconds, and the roots keep working while you are away. The worst thing that can happen to you is a delver reaching the heartstone at the bottom — you lose a fifth of your marrow and the deep floods for a minute, and drainage is the answer to that.

Made without any stock art

Every plate, emblem and figure in the game is generated at build time by our own palette-locked pixel engine: five seamless 256×256 material tiles and fifty-six sprites. No asset pack, no diffusion model, nothing bought or downloaded. The music is rendered from an authored score by our own tool and every sound effect is synthesised in your browser as you hear it.

Controls: mouse. M mutes, Escape backs out of a screen.

AI disclosure

Code: yes, AI-written. Graphics: yes — the in-game art is generated by our own pixel engine rather than by a model, but the cover illustration is an image-model render, so this reads yes. Sounds: no — all audio is self-contained procedural synthesis from oscillators, which itch is explicit is not generative AI. Text: yes.
