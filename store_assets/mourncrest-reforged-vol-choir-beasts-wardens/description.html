11 top-down pixel art characters for RPG Maker MZ, Godot 4 or any engine that reads PNG sprite sheets. Three casts in one dark-fantasy style: a cult (the Pale Choir), four grave beasts (the Barrow Beasts) and two armoured hunters (the Iron Wardens). Each ships idle, walk, strike, hurt and death in four directions, 1,100 frames at 160x160 plus the same 1,100 at 80x80. Commercial use allowed, no credit required. The original Mourncrest roster is free on our page.

Built to fight beside Mourncrest Reforged 01: The Restless Dead. Same 160x160 cell, same ground line, same palette world, so the cultists, beasts and wardens stand on the same floor as its skeletons, ghouls and wights.

Every character fights: 100 frames each, a 6-frame walk, a 6-frame strike, a 6-frame hurt and a 6-frame death in all four directions. The 160x160 frames are the native master (nothing upscaled); the 80x80 set is an exact 2:1 reduction, palette-locked so it stays crisp.

The strikes

  • Acolyte (the novice. Bare-headed, carries the book): lifts the open book high and slams it down.
  • Carrion crow (a great carrion crow, dagger-beaked): ducks its head in a fast hard peck (seen from behind, the peck shows as a flare of the wings).
  • Censer-bearer (carries the Choir's smoking censer): reaches the free hand out in a clawing grab while the censer hangs at the hip.
  • Crypt hound (a gaunt crypt hound with a skull head): gapes its skull jaws wide and snaps forward.
  • Cultist (hooded rank and file of the Pale Choir): winds both clawed hands out and slashes them across the chest into a crouch (front and back) or rakes one claw out to full reach (side views).
  • Dire bat (a huge dire bat of the barrow vaults): lunges with its jaws open and its wings flung wide.
  • Hierophant (the Choir's high priest, mitred and tall): raises both long hooked hands and slashes them down and across into a crouch (front and back) or rakes one hand out to full reach (side views).
  • Inquisitor (the order's eye, with a witch-lantern): raises a gloved fist and hammers it down and across (front and back), punches straight out (east) or thrusts the witch-lantern at the target (west).
  • Knight (the order's breaker, with a war-hammer): swings the war-hammer up and brings it down in a heavy blow.
  • Thrall (a hulking bound thrall in burial rags): throws both wrapped arms up and clubs them down.
  • Tomb spider (a big tomb spider of the burial niches): rears up and stabs its two front legs down (front and back); in profile it makes a smaller forward lunge.

Every hurt flinches and returns to the standing pose; every death ends on the ground (strike, hurt, death are one-shots).

The consistency proof

The honest worry about generated art is that frame 40 will not match frame 1. This pack proves it against the decoded pixels of every file in the download:

  • 2,442 files are real PNGs and all 2,442 decode
  • 12,832,584 opaque pixels, every one inside the declared 144-colour palette
  • 109,824,000 pixels carry strictly binary alpha, no anti-aliasing, no soft upscale
  • 4,400 frame-map checks: all 1,100 entries land inside their sheets and every frame has a real file at both sizes
  • every asset present at one grid size is present at the other

What you get

  • 1,100 frames at 160x160 (native) and 1,100 at 80x80 (an exact 2:1 reduction)
  • 55 sheets per grid, one per character per state, plus every frame as its own PNG
  • frames.json with every frame's sheet, x, y, w, h, and pack.json listing the states and which are one-shots
  • RPG Maker MZ: 22 $-prefixed 3x4 walk sheets (both grids), drop into img/characters
  • Godot 4: a SpriteFrames .tres per character per grid, every animation named and sliced, strike, hurt, death set to play once, loaded and verified in Godot 4.7.2 before shipping
  • the palette holds all 112 colours of the Reforged 01 palette, plus 32 fitted to this cast

Honest notes

  • The EAST death is the generated WEST death mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: direBat.
  • The EAST hurt is the generated WEST hurt mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: direBat.
  • The WEST hurt is the generated EAST hurt mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: knight.
  • The EAST idle is the generated WEST idle mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: direBat.
  • The EAST strike is the generated WEST strike mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: direBat, tombSpider.
  • The WEST strike is the generated EAST strike mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: acolyte, hierophant, knight.
  • The EAST walk is the generated WEST walk mirrored, pixel for pixel, for: direBat.
  • Strike, hurt and death are one-shots: every strike ends on the blow, every hurt closes back on the standing pose, and every death ends lying on the ground.
  • The dire bat hovers above the ground line the walkers stand on. Its walk is a wing-beat in place, and when it dies it drops out of the air and lands on that ground line.
  • The tomb spider dies by rolling onto its back with its legs in the air.
  • The dire bat's and the tomb spider's deaths end on a frame we authored and the generator animated toward: the creature's own standing art turned over (the bat onto its side or back, the spider onto its back) with its eyes put out. Every other death is generated end to end.
  • Where the generator painted impact sparks, flashes or debris into a strike or flinch frame, we removed them, so every frame carries only the character. Add your own hit effects on top.
  • The inquisitor carries his witch-lantern on his far side in the east views. In his east hurt and east strike it swings into view, and its flame there reads warm yellow rather than the cool teal of his other facings.
  • The mirrored cycles listed above swap sides, so the knight holds his hammer in the other hand in his west strike and west hurt.
  • Seen from behind (north), several strikes and flinches draw the arms crossing over the back rather than hidden in front of the body: the cultist, hierophant, thrall and knight, and in the acolyte's north strike the raised book shows as a pale slab across his shoulders.
  • Near the end of a few deaths a carried prop drops out of sight: the knight's hammer in the last frame of his south, east and north deaths, and the inquisitor's lantern in the last frame of his south death and the last three of his north death.
  • The thrall twists its body during the wind-up of its side-view strikes (toward the camera facing east, away from it facing west) and turns back to profile for the blow.
  • The crow's small teal eye drops out for a few frames of its west walk and its east strike, which reads as a blink.
  • The tomb spider's west hurt opens the seam on its abdomen into a wound for four frames; its east hurt does not.
  • Small costume variations a sharp eye will find: the censer-bearer's robe front lightens in his south strike, his belt lightens as he falls east, and a strap swings at his hip in his north hurt.
