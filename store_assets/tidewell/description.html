Tidewell is 48 animated pixel-art fish and water creatures at 64x64 for Godot, GameMaker, Unity or any 2D engine, each with a swim loop and a catch animation. Commercial licence, no credit required.

Made for fishing games, aquariums and underwater levels. Every creature has a still for your inventory or catch log, a 6-frame swim loop, and a 6-frame catch animation of the creature landed and flopping. They come sorted into four habitats with a ready catch table.

What is inside

  • 48 creatures: 13 freshwater, 15 sea, 11 deep and 9 rare, from trout and pike to an anglerfish, a giant squid and a golden koi
  • 624 frame PNGs and 144 animation strips at 64x64, all but the head-on Blue Crab facing right (flip them for left)
  • 48 Godot 4 SpriteFrames, ready for an AnimatedSprite2D
  • Strips named for GameMaker's Create Sprite From Strip (_strip6)
  • A catalogue in CSV and JSON: name, habitat, rarity, real-world size class and a suggested coin value

One style, checked on every file

Every pixel of every image sits in one 128-colour palette, every frame is exactly 64x64, and the edges are hard with no anti-aliasing. 39 of the creatures, all the fish and eels plus the shrimp, animate by moving their own pixels, so their scales and spots stay the same in every frame. The crayfish, crab, lobster, squid, octopus, jellyfish, seahorse, sea dragon and isopod use generated cycles that we checked frame by frame.

Good to know

  • One size, 64x64. Each creature fills its cell like a catch-log icon; the catalogue gives its real size class so you can scale it in a scene.
  • The swim loops are gentle and hold their place. Move the creature in your game.
