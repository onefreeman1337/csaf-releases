Kithfell Companions is 16 animal side-view battlers for RPG Maker MZ, each with all 18 battle motions, ready for img/sv_actors with no plugin. Use them in commercial and free games, no credit needed.

What is inside

16 animals · 18 motions each · 32 sheets · 800 frames at each of two sizes

  • Cats and dogs: tabby, black and calico cats, a herd dog, a beagle and a corgi.
  • Small companions: a rabbit, a ferret and a hedgehog.
  • Wild beasts: a red fox, a grey wolf, a wild boar, a brown bear and a mane lion.
  • Hooved mounts: a red stag and a draught horse, which rear, stamp and charge.
  • Every animal bites, swipes, pounces, howls, guards, flinches, hops, cheers and falls.
  • Sheets at 720x480 (80 px cells) and 1440x960 (160 px cells).
  • One 224 colour palette and one ground line, so the hedgehog and the stag share a party at true size.

The background is an illustration and is not in the pack.

Works with

RPG Maker MZ: copy a sheet to img/sv_actors, set it as the actor's [SV] Battler, turn on side view. We did exactly that in a blank project and ran a real battle at both sizes. Godot 4: 32 SpriteFrames files. Any engine: sheets, single PNG frames and frames.json.

Good to know

Battle sprites only: the map walking sprites are in Kithfell 01, 02 and 03, and this pack works without them. Leave the animals unarmed, because MZ draws a weapon's image over an actor that attacks with one. A few rows are looser than the rest: ears lift on the beagle and herd dog, the tall animals stay on their feet in the low-health row, the lion lies long when it is down, and some closed eyes were painted shut by hand. The full Readme lists every motion and the looser rows by name.

Licence and how it was made

Do not resell the sprites as a sprite pack.

The art is AI generated: each animal's motions were made with an AI image model (PixelLab) from its own Kithfell sprite, then palette locked and checked frame by frame by our tools. The packaging code was written with AI.
