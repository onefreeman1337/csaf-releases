Ductus — Letter Tracing Generator

A Unity editor tool from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Unity. Genre: Educational. Paid.

Ductus — Letter Tracing Generator is a Unity editor tool for Unity, paid.

Ductus generates letter tracing lessons from your own font, in Latin, Cyrillic and Greek. Every other tracing asset on the store ships hand drawn paths for one alphabet. Ductus carries the one thing a font cannot tell you, the order and direction a letter is written in, and takes the letterform from whatever font your game already uses. Pick a font, pick a script, and you have 177 tracing plates that match your game.

What you get 177 glyph lessons. Latin A to Z, a to z and the figures (62), the full Russian alphabet including Ё and ё (66), and alpha to omega including the final sigma ς (49). 341 authored strokes, each in the order and direction a child is taught to write it. No font file records that, so it cannot be read off an outline. It is written out, once, per glyph. Font fitting.

The strokes are warped onto your font's own letterforms, and every glyph is graded: fitted, approximate, or fell back. You see the survey before you export anything. Designed plates. Ruled paper, a letter shaped track to keep the crayon inside, a dashed centre guide, numbered start dots and a direction arrow at every turn. Six colourways, any plate size to 2048.
