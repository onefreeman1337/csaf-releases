Signet: Generated Run Result Cards for Unity is a Unity editor tool for Unity, paid.

At the end of a run, Signet composes a card. Not a screenshot with a frame on it. A struck metal plate that draws the outcome as a device, sets every stat beside its own glyph, and stamps a seed sigil no other run in your game will ever get. It comes out as a Texture2D or a PNG, sized for a social post, and it is built to be posted. Nothing in this package is a texture.

No atlas, no sprite sheet, no material and no font. Twenty-four run-stat glyphs, nine outcome plates, fifty struck letterforms and figures, a seed sigil with eight hundred distinct shape combinations and seven standing palettes are all drawn in C# by a software rasteriser that ships with the package. A thousand runs cost a thousand different cards and zero import time. The labels are the interface.

There is no enum to map and no second field to fill in.
