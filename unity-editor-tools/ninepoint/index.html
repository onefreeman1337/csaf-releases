Ninepoint - Steam Deck Text Size Check and Fixer for Unity UI is a Unity editor tool for Unity, paid.

Its title or description names Unity 6.

Valve's Steam Deck review asks that the smallest text in your game is at least 9 pixels tall at 1280x800, and recommends 12. A HUD authored at 1920x1080 or 4K shrinks on the Deck, and nothing in Unity tells you which labels went under. Ninepoint measures every label and fixes the ones that are too small.

It reads every uGUI Text and TextMeshPro label in your open scenes, your build scenes and your canvas prefabs, works out its size on a Deck at 1280x800 and 1280x720 through that canvas's own Canvas Scaler maths, and gives each one a verdict against the 9 px floor and the 12 px recommendation. Then it raises the font sizes that are too small, in one step you preview first and can undo.

Measured on the demo that ships with it (Unity 6000.5.7f1) A fantasy HUD authored for 3840x2160 plus a switched-off settings menu prefab authored for 1920x1080: 60 labels, 26 under 9 px, 3 under 12 px, 30 fine, and one world-space nameplate skipped with its reason. The default fix: 26 changes, 0 labels left under 9 px.

Six of them were written once to the settings menu prefab, because the scene instance does not override the sizes.
