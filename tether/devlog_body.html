Tether is out: a relationship chart for RPG Maker MZ that draws every bond between your characters as a cord.

Each character is a gilt medallion holding their own face graphic. Each bond between two of them is a cord, and the cord carries the whole relationship at a glance:

  • The material is the kind of bond. Love is silk, friendship is rope, an oath is chain, a rivalry is barbed wire, family is ribbon.
  • The weight is the value. Six tiers, from a thread of silk to a heavy glossy cord, from a light chain to a heavy one.
  • Knots are events. Switch names from your database, or your own words through a plugin command. Oaths are padlocked, family ribbons are tied in bows.
  • Strain frays it. A love or an oath that falls below zero frays, a chain hangs a broken link, barbed wire snaps.

It reads the values your game already keeps in variables, or keeps its own small record through five plugin commands. It never invents a relationship: a pair with no value gets no cord.

The chart is its own scene with no windows and no default font, drawn once when it opens. In the engine it measured zero texture uploads per frame at rest, for twelve characters and for sixteen.

Built and tested by us with AI assistance for the code and the graphics. The portraits in the store images are demonstration art and are not included; the chart uses your actors' own face graphics.
