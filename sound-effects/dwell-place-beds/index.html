Dwell — Long-Form Place Beds

A sound effect pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Paid.

Dwell — Long-Form Place Beds is a sound effect pack, paid.

Seventeen places, each shipped in the layers it is made of. Most ambience packs hand you an atmosphere. This hands you a place you can change: a full bed for when you just want it working, plus the two layers that bed is built from, so the room can react to the game. Bring the water up as the player nears the edge. Push the high wind as the storm rises.

Drop the bed entirely and run only the air when a scene needs to breathe without being noticed. Long-form on purpose. Beds run 30 seconds and layers 20, against a category norm nearer six. That is the difference between a loop a player notices inside a minute and one they do not.

The seventeen places Crypt · Cavern · Forge · Forest at night · Rain on canopy · Harbour · Scriptorium · Moor wind · Sanctum · Machine hall · Sewer · Snowfield Each ships one 30-second bed, its two 20-second layers, and three accents that belong to it — six files per place, 102 in total, 51 of them seamless loops.
