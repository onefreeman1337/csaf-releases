Squall — Weather Systems, Layered and Rampable is a sound effect pack, paid.

104 sounds of weather, built to be mixed rather than chosen. Most weather packs give you rain_light and rain_heavy and leave you to crossfade between them. That works until the player can hear it, because a crossfade between two recordings of rain is two rains, briefly, and then one. This pack is built the other way round.

Every weather system is a set of separate loopable layers, each tagged with the intensity at which it enters. You do not swap files to change the weather. You add and remove layers over a bed that never stops. Tier 1, Light. the system floor, audible at any intensity Tier 2, Steady. enters at moderate intensity Tier 3, Severe. enters at full intensity Play tier 1 alone for a drizzle. Fade tier 2 in as it settles.

Bring tier 3 in for the worst of it, and take it back out as the front passes. Nothing restarts, nothing crossfades, and the storm can change in response to what the player is doing.
