Built for the way a western game fires sounds One source, three places: the carbine, the cannon and the bullwhip each on the open range, in a log stockade's yard and in a narrow rock gulch. The herd as a grid: a small and a big herd at a walk, trot, lope and stampede, each pace its own loop length so a game can crossfade as the herd speeds up.

Ladders: the cannon from near to distant, a drill squad at three paces, and gold in the pan from a flake to a big nugget.
