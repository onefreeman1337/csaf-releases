One saga, one palette Every sound is built from the materials of a Norse world: oak plank, linden shield boards under rawhide, forged iron, bone and antler, a drinking horn, hacksilver, amber, a granite runestone, pine, beach shingle and fire. Indoors is one timber longhouse. Outdoors is the fjord.

What is inside War horn: five signals from a short call to a long held blast, one pitch each, so a game can wire the rung to how urgent the order is. Plus a rally, an advance, a retreat, a distant call and an answer.
