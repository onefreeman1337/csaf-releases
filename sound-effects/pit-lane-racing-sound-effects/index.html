Pit Lane: Racing Sound Effects is a sound effect pack, paid.

Pit Lane is 110 racing sound effects for Unity, Godot, GameMaker or any engine: five cars, plus tyres, crashes, boost, the race and the pit stop. Each car has its own engine, which ships as a rev ladder of five seamless loops, so it can follow a rev counter. Royalty free for commercial games, with no credit required.

What is inside 110 sounds · 5 engines · 47 seamless loops · WAV + OGG · 16 bit 44.1 kHz · free demo on this page 5 cars, a two stroke kart, a hot hatch four, a muscle V8, a turbo rally four and an electric racer, each with five rev loops, start, stop and a rev limiter loop. Gear up and gear down for the four combustion cars, a launch and a regen for the electric one.

20 pass bys, every car at 79, 137, 209 and 288 km/h. 10 skid loops: five surfaces by two kinds of slip. Collisions from a tap to a wreck, boost, race cues, a pit stop and five ambience beds. Each pass by's pitch drop is measured off the file and matches the speed within 0.9 percent. Pick the row from the surface and the column from how hard the player brakes.
