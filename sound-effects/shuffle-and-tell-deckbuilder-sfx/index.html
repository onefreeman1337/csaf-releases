Six ladders A deckbuilder fires the same action thousands of times in a run, so the question is whether the eighth one still tells the player something.

Six families are ordered rungs, each step measurably past the one below it: Cards dealt: 5 rungs, length up Shuffle thoroughness: 5 rungs, length up Card tier revealed: 5 rungs, brightness up Energy pip: 6 rungs, brightness up Chips stacked: 5 rungs, length up Deck pollution: 5 rungs, brightness down.

As the deck fills with cards the player never chose, the shuffle dulls, so by the fifth stage the deck itself sounds heavy.
