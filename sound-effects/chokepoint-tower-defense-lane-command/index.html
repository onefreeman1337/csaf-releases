Chokepoint — Tower Defense Lane Command is a sound effect pack, paid.

132 sounds for tower defense, lane strategy and autobattlers — built as a state language, not a bag of impacts. A tower defense player learns the lane by ear. They need to know a placement was refused, a tower reached tier 4, and an enemy leaked, without ever looking away from the lane. Chokepoint is built for exactly that: every cue is designed to be told apart from the cues it sits next to.

The escalation ladders are the point, and they are proven One action in this genre fires thousands of times, so eight of the slots here are not single cues but ordered ramps: tower upgrade tier, kill bounty, wave incoming, tower charge, projectile impact, enemy leak, construction cost, and threat level.

A gate re-derives all 35 adjacent steps from the shipped audio and refuses the pack unless every rung clears the one below it by at least 8% in spectral centroid, or 12% in duration, and unless every ladder spans at least 1.5× end to end. On the first render it refused six steps. The synthesis recipes were rewritten until the audio actually escalated. The thresholds were not touched.

The enemy leak ladder runs downward on purpose.
