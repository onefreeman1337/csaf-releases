Footfall: Footstep Matrix, 12 Surfaces x 4 Gaits x 3 Weights is a sound effect pack, paid.

Footfall is 576 footstep sound effects for Unity, Godot, Unreal, GameMaker and the web, on 12 surfaces at 4 gaits and 3 weights. Royalty free, with a free demo. Every step is made by the same foot: one heel, roll and toe for each gait, one sole, one body mass. Only the ground changes, so a character walking from grass onto stone sounds like one character changing ground.

What is inside 588 files · 576 footsteps · 12 walking loops · 4 takes per cell · WAV + OGG · 44.1 kHz, 16 bit mono, dry 12 surfaces, all dry: carpet, wood floor, dirt path, snow, metal grate, stone, sand, tile, grass, gravel, ice and dry leaves 4 gaits: walk, run, sneak and land, which is both feet after a jump and then the weight settling 3 weights: light, medium and heavy.

Heavier bodies land darker on every surface 12 walking loops: 4 s, eight steps, 120 steps a minute, with sample accurate loop points in the file A naming manifest listing every file, and import notes for Unity, Godot, Unreal, GameMaker, FMOD, Wwise and the web The file name is built straight from your game state: step_<surface>_<gait>_<weight>_<take>.ogg, for example step_gravel_run_heavy_03.ogg.
