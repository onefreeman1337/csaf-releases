Akatsuki — Anime / JRPG Sound Suite is a sound effect pack, paid.

One palette. Every slot a JRPG needs. Bronze, glass, bamboo, cypress and steel — struck, and left to ring in one room. Most anime-flavoured packs are a folder: some bright chimes, some sword sounds, some sparkle, assembled from wherever. This is built the other way round. Ten hand-authored modal tables define what the objects in this world are made of, and all 112 sounds are those materials excited differently.

The temple bell's table is inside the holy spell impact, inside the revive, and inside the shrine ambience. The steel in the katana draw is the steel in the blade clash and in the distant fighting on the battlefield bed. That reuse is not a shortcut. It is the reason a menu blip and a room sound like they belong to the same game.
