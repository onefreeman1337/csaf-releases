Built for the way a fishing horror game fires sounds Four events, five waters: the lure landing, the wrong bite, a breach beside the boat and the float pulled under, on every water. Ladders: the line pulled back at four strengths, each its own loop length so a game can crossfade as the tension climbs, the catch reveal by wrongness, a knock on the hull by distance, and the fish finder return by size.
