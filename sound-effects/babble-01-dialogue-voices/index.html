Babble 01 is 650 royalty free dialogue sound effects for RPG Maker, Godot and any engine: 10 speakers who talk in made up syllables, in 5 moods. Each syllable is a consonant and a vowel, so a line sounds like speech in an invented language rather than a beep. Every speaker has its own consonants, vowels and twelve syllables.

What is inside 650 sounds · 10 speakers · 5 moods · 5 cues each · WAV + OGG, 16 bit, 44.1 kHz, mono · letter map · Godot 4 helper 10 speakers, from Bram the giant at 64 Hz to Snik the trickster at 358 Hz, with a robot, a child, a noble and a spirit between them. 12 syllables per speaker in each of 5 moods: neutral, happy, angry, sad and question. 5 cues per speaker: open, advance, close, hmm and laugh.
