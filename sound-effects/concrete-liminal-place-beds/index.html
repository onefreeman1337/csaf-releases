Concrete — Urban and Liminal Place Beds

Concrete — Urban and Liminal Place Beds is a sound effect pack, paid.

103 sounds. 17 modern places that feel empty. Car parks, subway platforms, laundromats, stairwells, a mall after hours, a server room, an office at night, a storm drain, a closed arcade, an indoor pool. Every one of them shipped in separable layers, not as one baked atmosphere. Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required.

Synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples, no recordings and no upstream licence behind this one. Use it in a commercial game, keep using it, ship it. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack. A place comes apart Every place gives you a seamless bed (30s), 2 layers you fade independently, and accents you fire on top.

Start the bed, leave it running, and ride the layers underneath — one room covers empty, occupied and busy without a crossfade and without the loop restarting. The beds and layers are deliberately different lengths, so a room left running never repeats on a fixed cycle. Every interior hums. The exteriors deliberately do not.

A 60 Hz mains hum — fluorescent ballast, transformer, the supply in a sleeping monitor — runs under all 12 interiors.
