Harness: Cloth, Armour and Gear Movement Sound Effects is a sound effect pack, paid.

Harness is 152 cloth, armour and gear movement sound effects in WAV and OGG for Godot, Unity, GameMaker or any engine, royalty free, with a free demo. It is the layer that plays on top of your footsteps: what a character's clothes, armour and kit do when the character walks, runs, sneaks, lands, rolls or takes its gear off.

What is inside 152 sounds · 6 garments · 4 gaits · 12 loops · WAV + OGG · loop points on every loop A step layer for six garments (cloth, heavy cloak, leather, mail, plate and an adventurer's kit) at walk, run, sneak and land, three takes each: 72 files. Every walk, run and land file has its jolt at the start, where the heel is, so one call with your footstep plays both.

36 body moves: turn, jump, crouch, rise, roll and fall in every garment. 12 sounds for putting each garment on and taking it off, using the fastening it really has: laces, a brooch, buckles, a belt, plate straps, a chest strap. 20 handling sounds: buckles, straps, pouches, a pack, gauntlets, a visor, a sword belt and a cloak swirl. 6 walk loops and 6 idle loops, one of each per garment.

Takes are declared as sets, so you can pick one at random and avoid repeats.
