Wade — Liquid, Wet and Submerged Movement

A sound effect pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Paid.

Wade — Liquid, Wet and Submerged Movement is a sound effect pack, paid.

135 sounds of water, indexed by how deep it is. Most packs answer "I need water footsteps" with a folder called water holding a handful of splashes. The problem you actually have is different: a character walks onto wet stone, crosses a stream, swims, climbs out and drips. One splash cannot do that. So the organising idea here is depth.

Every movement sound is authored at five depths, and depth is audible rather than a folder name.

Mean spectral centroid of the 55 movement sounds, measured off the shipped files: Damp ground (wet stone, rain soaked earth, no standing water) — 4221 Hz Puddle (a film of standing water, ankle at most) — 3381 Hz Shallow (shin deep, the classic river crossing) — 2697 Hz Knee deep (movement is visibly resisted) — 2304 Hz Thigh deep (wading, not walking) — 1906 Hz Deeper water is darker water, with no inversions.

That gradient is an assertion inside the generator, not a sentence in this description: if it ever inverted, the build would refuse to finish. Drop the depth suffix into your footstep code the same way you already switch grass for stone, and a river crossing sounds like a river crossing. Licence, up front Royalty free. Commercial use.
