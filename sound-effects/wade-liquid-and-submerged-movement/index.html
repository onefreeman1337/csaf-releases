Wade — Liquid, Wet and Submerged Movement is a sound effect pack, paid.

Wade is 135 water sound effects for Unity, Godot, RPG Maker and GameMaker, with every movement sound authored at 5 depths. Royalty free for commercial games, with a free demo. A character walks onto wet stone, crosses a stream, swims, climbs out and drips. Drop the depth suffix into your footstep code the same way you already switch grass for stone, and the sound follows the water.

What is inside 135 sounds · 5 depths · 43 variation sets · 10 seamless loops · WAV + OGG · 44.1 kHz 16 bit mono Movement through water (55): walk, run, sneak and land at every depth Swimming and submersion (19): diving, slipping in, strokes, kicks, going under, surfacing Wet aftermath (17): squelching boots, dripping cloth, wringing out, shaking off Water handled (25): pouring, filling, dunking, splashes at three sizes, washing Water beds (8): stream, shore, rain on water, a far waterfall, a dripping cave, bubbling, a drain Wet interface (11): menu and feedback cues built from the same water Five depths Damp ground, puddle, shallow, knee deep and thigh deep.
