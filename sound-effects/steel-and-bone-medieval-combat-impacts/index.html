Steel and Bone — Medieval Combat Impacts is a sound effect pack, paid.

144 medieval combat impacts, filed by what hits what. A combat system does not ask for "axe". It asks for "axe on mail", because the armour decides the sound as much as the weapon does. Steel and Bone is built the way that code queries it: every impact is filed under its material pair, striker_on_receiver_NN, and every cell of the grid is its own synthesis recipe, never a re-pitch of its neighbour.

8 strikers × 6 receivers = 48 material pairs Strikers: warhammer, arming sword, flanged mace, bearded axe, spear, arrow, gauntleted fist, rondel dagger. Receivers: plate armour, wood shield, mail, flesh, stone, boiled leather. Fast strikers (sword, arrow, fist, dagger) carry three takes per cell and heavy ones two, because a fast weapon lands more often and repeats sooner.

The grid is proven, not asserted A gate re-derives every cell from the shipped audio and refuses the pack unless the grid is complete and, along each axis, every pair of cells is at least 9% apart in brightness or 18% in length. That is 120 comparisons along the rows (the armour must change the sound) and 168 down the columns (the weapon must change it). Indistinct pairs on the shipped files: 0.
