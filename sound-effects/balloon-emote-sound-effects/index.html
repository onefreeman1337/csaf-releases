Balloon: 120 Emote Sound Effects is a sound effect pack, paid.

Balloon is a pack of 120 emote sound effects for RPG Maker MZ and any game engine: a cue for every speech balloon, in 4 matched voices. Royalty free for commercial games, no credit required. A free demo of 18 sounds and the plugin is on this page.

What is inside 120 sounds · 14 emotes · 4 voices · 2 takes of each · WAV + OGG · 18 sound free demo The ten stock balloons: exclamation, question, music note, heart, anger, sweat, frustration, silence, light bulb and Zzz, each numbered to its RPG Maker MZ balloon id. Four more reactions: surprise, sparkle, gloom and blush, on ids 11 to 14, the rows the stock sheet leaves empty.

Four voices: soft cozy, retro chip, anime and wood and paper. Each plays the same gesture on its own instrument, so a question always rises and gloom always sags. Two takes of every cue, different in length and in brightness, so characters reacting in turn do not repeat each other. A balloon pop in and pop out in each voice.

A small RPG Maker MZ plugin: turn it on and Show Balloon Icon plays the matching cue by itself. Every waveform is a shipped file: the emotes down the side, the voices across, two takes of each.
