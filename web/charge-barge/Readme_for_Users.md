# CHARGE BARGE

**Drive a storm-powered ironclad up an insane hostile coastline. Everything in
this game is charge: the run is a charge, the money is charge, the miracles are
charge, and the ram is a decision.**

Built for **AI Browser Game Jam 4** — theme: *CHARGE!*

---

## The two charges

- **VOLTS** (amber) — stored charge. Stripped from wrecks, trickled in by the
  trade dynamo while you sail. Spent in **coves** on permanent upgrades:
  weapon, defence, speed, income-over-time, income-per-kill, and more. Volts
  survive everything, including sinking.
- **SURGE** (blue) — battle charge. Built by kills, spent mid-run on instant
  abilities: **RAMMING SPEED**, boarding archers, a hired mercenary sloop,
  the TEMPEST, extra gun crews, a patch crew. New abilities unlock as your
  **renown** grows — press the number key or click the chip.

The two feed each other: the guns you buy with volts make the kills that build
surge; the surge you spend keeps the hull alive to earn more volts. Slow water
pays better through the dynamo — the throttle is an economic decision.

## The coast

The coastline comes in **legs**, each ending in a safe cove — shop, breath,
save. Skiffs ram, branders explode, towers lead their shots, gunboats pace you,
mortar barges drop warning rings you do not stand in. Rocks break the hull;
reefs drag the keel; derelict wrecks glow amber for a reason. It gets narrower,
meaner and richer the further north you charge.

## Sinking

**Sinking is never a restart.** The wreckers tow you to the last cove. Every
volt, every upgrade and every point of renown is kept. Buy something and charge
again — that is the game.

Saving is automatic: at every cove, every purchase, every unlock and every
sinking. Close the tab; CONTINUE picks up where the chart left off.

## Controls

| Input | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or ← → | steer |
| W / S or ↑ ↓ | throttle up / down |
| Mouse | aim the pivot gun |
| Click (hold) | fire |
| 1–6 or click a chip | spend surge on an ability |
| M | mute (persists) |

The coil broadside fires itself. Your job is the wheel, the pivot gun, and the
timing of the surge.
