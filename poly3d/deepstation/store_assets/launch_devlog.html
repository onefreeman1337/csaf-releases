Deep Station is out: 14 low poly sci-fi horror crew in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The crew of a remote deep space mining station, the night something got loose: a station engineer, a captain, a medic, a security officer, an eva technician, a company liaison, an android, a shuttle pilot, a research scientist, a stowaway, a cargo loader, an infected crewman, a hydroponics tech and an infected deck officer. Each one is a different body and a different painted costume, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Three palette skins each, 42 characters in total. The jumpsuit or uniform changes colour: the engineer's orange work jumpsuit goes green or blue, the captain's slate blue uniform goes burgundy or dark green, the pressure suit's orange panels go blue or red. Faces, hair, skin, boots and black gear keep their painted colours. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including the ones a station needs: holding a flashlight, pistol aim, shoot and reload, crouch and crouch walk, sprint, roll, kneeling repair work, interact, hit reactions, swimming for zero gravity and a death.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second, independent AI review looked again before release; what it found was fixed.

Try one free: the sampler is the station engineer, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Deep Station sits beside Last Shift (PSX survival horror survivors, same skeleton and shader). Our other PSX packs: Fort Vale, Hollow Congregation, Ironvale and Greyhollow. Count so far: 7 PSX listings live, 2 of them sold.
