Greyhollow is out: 14 low poly medieval townsfolk in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $6.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The working people of a walled market town: a blacksmith, a washerwoman, a town guard, a innkeeper, a village priest, a miller, a merchant, a town crier, a herbalist, a beggar, a noblewoman, a farmer, a fisherwoman and a stable hand. Each one is a different body and a different painted costume, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin. They are the NPCs a fantasy RPG needs between the dungeons.

Three palette skins each, 42 characters in total. The main garment changes colour: the washerwoman's rose bodice goes blue or green, the guard's red tabard goes blue or green, the noblewoman's purple velvet goes sapphire or wine. Faces, hair, linen, steel, trim and boots keep their painted colours. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including the ones a town needs: talking idle, sitting idle and sitting talking, kneeling work, pick up from a table, push, interact and a formal walk, plus a sword idle and swing for the guard, hit reactions and a death.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second, independent AI review looked again before release; what it found was repainted.

Try one free: the sampler is the innkeeper, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Greyhollow is the town for Ironvale (a fantasy adventuring party, same skeleton and shader). Our other PSX packs: Last Shift, Hollow Congregation and Fort Vale. Count so far: 6 PSX listings live (5 paid packs and a free sampler), 2 sold, 3 purchases.
