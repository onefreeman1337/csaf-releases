Greyhollow is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style medieval townsfolk characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips, comes in 3 palette skins (42 characters to place), and has 1494-1498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required. A free sampler (the innkeeper, every skin and clip) is on this page.

The working people of a walled market town, for PlayStation era fantasy role playing games: the smith, the inn, the market, the mill, the church and the stables. The NPCs your hero walks past, talks to and buys from.

What you get

  • 14 townsfolk, each a different body AND a different painted costume: blacksmith, washerwoman, town guard, innkeeper, village priest, miller, merchant, town crier, herbalist, beggar, noblewoman, farmer, fisherwoman, stable hand.
  • 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place. The main garment changes colour (a rose bodice to blue or green, a mustard waistcoat to green or wine, a red guard's tabard to blue or green); faces, hair, linen, steel and boots keep their painted colours, so a crowd of the same body still reads as different people.
  • 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose, with the ones a town needs: talking idle, sitting idle and sitting talking, kneeling work, pick up from a table, push, interact, a formal walk, holding a torch, plus walk, jog, sprint, jump, a sword idle and swing for the guard, punches, hit reactions and a death.
  • 1494-1498 triangles per character, one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering. The PlayStation look, not a modern model with a filter on top.
  • Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures, and the Blender source file.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog. Every setting is a slider.
  • Readme with import notes, LICENSE, NOTICE.

Honest limits: the hands are simple mitten hands and nothing is held (tools hang from belts, painted on); the bodies are fitted, so there are no long skirts, cloaks or wide brimmed hats.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. Tested here in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles on every character, the clips play (Godot names Walk_Loop as Walk and loops it). The in-engine images on this page are that test.

Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX (skeleton and clips included) and set the textures to point / nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid, built to retarget (Unity Humanoid, Unreal IK Rig). These two engines were not run here; the FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.
