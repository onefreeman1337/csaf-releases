Grimsnout is out: 14 low poly fantasy enemies in the PlayStation 1 look, orcs, goblins and an ogre, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The cast: orc warrior, goblin scout, orc shieldmaiden, goblin tinker, ogre brute, goblin cutpurse, orc shaman, orc warchief, goblin hexer, orc huntress, goblin warboss, orc berserker, orc camp cook and orc jailer. Each one is a different body and a different painted costume, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Real heights, 1.28 m to 2.10 m. This is our first pack where the characters are not all one height: the goblins stand well below the orcs and the ogre towers over everyone. The scale sits on the armature, so every clip scales with it.

Three palette skins each, 42 characters in total. One cloth garment changes colour: the warrior's red tunic goes navy or ochre, the shieldmaiden's mustard surcoat goes rose or blue, the berserker's black leather goes brown or navy. The green skin, faces, iron and boots keep their paint. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including sword idle and attack, jab and cross punches, a spell cast for the shaman and the hexer, crouch, sprint, roll, hit reactions and a death. No weapon models ship and the hands are mittens. The tusks, ears and armour are painted on fitted bodies, not modelled.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second, independent AI review looked again before release; what it found was fixed.

Try one free: the sampler is the orc warrior, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Grimsnout is the enemy side of Ironvale (fantasy adventurers, same skeleton and shader) and sits beside Greyhollow (medieval townsfolk). Count so far: 9 PSX listings live before this one, 6 of them sold, 9 purchases.
