Grimsnout is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style orcs, goblins and an ogre for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1493-1499 triangles and a 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

The enemy side of a dark fantasy RPG: an orc warband, goblin raiders and an ogre brute, at three real heights.

What you get

  • 14 characters, each a different body and a different painted costume: orc warrior, goblin scout, orc shieldmaiden, goblin tinker, ogre brute, goblin cutpurse, orc shaman, orc warchief, goblin hexer, orc huntress, goblin warboss, orc berserker, orc camp cook, orc jailer.
  • Real heights, 1.28 m to 2.10 m. The goblins stand well below the orcs and the ogre towers over them. The scale sits on the armature, so the clips scale with it.
  • 3 palette skins each, 42 characters to place. One cloth garment changes colour (the warrior's red tunic to navy or ochre, the shieldmaiden's mustard surcoat to rose or blue, the berserker's black leather to brown or navy); the green skin, faces, iron and boots keep their paint.
  • 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose, with the ones a fantasy fight needs: sword idle and attack, jab and cross punches, a spell cast and its idle for the shaman and the hexer, crouch, sprint, roll, chest and head hits and a death, plus idle, talking and sitting.
  • 1493-1499 triangles per character and one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures and the Blender source.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog.

Honest limits: no weapon models ship and the hands are simple mittens, so nothing is held. The bodies are fitted human bodies with the orc and goblin faces, tusks and armour painted on, so there are no modelled tusks, long ears, capes or loincloths.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. Tested here in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles on every character, the clips play.

Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX and set the textures to nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid, built to retarget. These two engines were not run here; the FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.

Documentation: the full Readme has the import notes, every height and the clip list.
