Night Beat is out: 14 low poly police officers, detectives and SWAT in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The cast: patrol officer, homicide detective, SWAT pointman, sergeant, SWAT breacher, motorcycle officer, state trooper, plainclothes detective, riot officer, county deputy, forensic technician, federal agent, dispatcher and prison guard. Each one is a different body and a different painted uniform, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Real heights, 1.70 m to 1.94 m. The scale sits on the armature, so every clip scales with it.

Three uniform palettes each, 42 characters in total. One garment or uniform changes colour: the patrol officer's navy goes county tan or night black, the sergeant's light blue shirt goes tan or white with her trousers following, the motorcycle officer's high visibility vest goes orange or blue. Skin, faces, belts, helmets and boots keep their paint. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including all six pistol clips (idle, aim up, aim down, aim, shoot, reload), a flashlight idle, talking, driving, crouch, sprint, roll, punches, hit reactions and a death. No weapon models ship and the hands are mittens, so the pistol clips aim an empty hand: attach your own pistol to the hand bone. Badges are plain, with no text and no real agency marks.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second, independent AI review looked again before release; what it found was fixed.

Try one free: the sampler is the patrol officer, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Night Beat is the police side of Fort Vale (modern soldiers, same skeleton and shader) and sits beside Last Shift (survival horror survivors). Count so far: 10 listings live before this one, 6 sold.
