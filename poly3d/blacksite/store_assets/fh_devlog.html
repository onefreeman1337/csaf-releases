Black Site is now on this page: 14 low poly characters of a secret underground containment facility, in the PlayStation 1 look, on the same skeleton, clip names and shader as the Deep Station crew. That makes 28 horror characters on this page, 84 with the palette skins: the station that lost contact, and the facility on the ground that was studying what got loose.

The cast: researcher, security guard, site director, containment specialist, senior researcher, response operative, security chief, decontamination technician, facility engineer, response leader, field medic, night janitor and two test subjects. Each is a different body and a different painted outfit, about 1,500 triangles, one 256 px texture per skin, 45 clips including the pistol set, a flashlight idle, crouch, sprint and kneeling repair work. Every character is an adult.

Caps, helmets and the hard hat are modelled. The guard's peaked cap, the response team's helmets, the engineer's hard hat and the janitor's knit cap sit on the head as geometry, so they hold from the back and through every clip. The hazmat suit has its hood and a half face respirator with the eyes visible. Honest limits: lab coats end at the hip, nothing is held in the simple mitten hands, and no costume carries a number, symbol or text.

Three palettes each: the one main garment changes colour, like department colour codes; faces, hair, lab coats, black vests, helmets, gloves and boots keep their paint.
