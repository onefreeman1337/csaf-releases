Deep Station and Black Site are 28 low poly PS1-style horror characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 crew of a deep space mining station and 14 staff, guards and test subjects of an underground containment facility. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips and comes in 3 palette skins (84 to place), about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Black Site: 14 containment facility staff and response team (added November 2026)

  • The people of the facility your player breaks into or out of: researcher, security guard, site director, containment specialist, senior researcher, response operative, security chief, decontamination technician, facility engineer, response leader, field medic, night janitor and two test subjects. Every one an adult, each a different body and painted outfit.
  • Built for a facility level: lab coats, a hazmat suit with a half face respirator, a brick red decontamination coverall, a response team in helmets and plate carriers, grey coverall test subjects. The cap, helmets, hard hat and knit cap are modelled, not painted. Heights 1.76 m to 1.88 m.
  • 3 skins each: the main garment changes colour like department codes; faces, hair, lab coats, black gear and boots keep their paint.
  • Honest limits: mitten hands hold nothing (the pistol, flashlight and repair clips are on every character); no numbers, symbols or text on any costume.

Deep Station: 14 space horror crew

  • The crew of a remote deep space mining station, the night something got loose: station engineer, captain, medic, security officer, eva technician, company liaison, android, shuttle pilot, research scientist, stowaway, cargo loader, infected crewman, hydroponics tech and infected deck officer.
  • 3 palette skins each; the infected are humanoid, on the same skeleton and clips.

Every character, both packs

  • 45 animation clips plus a T pose, with the ones a horror level needs: holding a flashlight (torch idle), pistol aim, shoot and reload, crouch idle and crouch walk, sprint, roll, kneeling repair work, interact, hit reactions and a death, plus idle, talking, walk, jog, jump, sitting, swimming and punches.
  • The PlayStation look: 1,494 to 1,499 triangles per character and one 256 x 256 texture per skin, quantised to 15 bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4: vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering and distance fog, every setting a slider.

Works with

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb and put the PSX shader on the mesh. Both packs were imported into a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles and the clips play.

Unity 6 and Unreal 5: import the FBX (standard humanoid skeleton, clips included) with nearest filtering. Not run here; every FBX was re-imported in Blender to check it.

Licence and how it was made

Use the models in any number of commercial or free games and projects, with no attribution required, and modify them freely. Do not resell or redistribute the models themselves as assets. The skeleton and clips are the CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Library, and the bodies are generated with MakeHuman (CC0 output).

Every character here was made with AI tools: the bodies were built by scripts an AI system wrote, and every texture is an AI image model's painting projected onto the mesh (both packs: OpenAI image models). Code: yes. Graphics: yes. Sounds: no. Text: no.
