# PSX Characters Sampler, Godot cut (v1.16.0)

Six free low poly PS1 style 3D characters for Godot 4, cut from the free PSX Characters Sampler (24 characters) by Core Systems Asset Factory.
Each character is rigged to one 53 bone humanoid skeleton and carries 45 animation clips plus a T-pose (idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, punches, sword, pistol, spell, hits, death, sit, swim, interact and more), about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin.

## What is in this cut

- `models/<id>/<id>_skin0.glb`: the mesh, the skeleton and every animation clip.
- `models/<id>/<id>_skin1.glb` and `_skin2.glb`: the same mesh and skeleton with the other two palette skins (no clips; play the clips from skin0 or retarget them).
- `models/<id>/textures/`: the three 256 px skin textures.
- `shaders/godot_psx.gdshader`: the PSX look for Godot 4 (vertex snap, affine texture mapping, 15 bit colour, dither, fog).

This cut carries GLB files only. The full free sampler adds an FBX with clips, an OBJ and the Blender source for every character, and all
24 characters: https://csaf.itch.io/psx-characters-sampler

## Godot 4 quick start

1. Copy the `models` and `shaders` folders into your project.
2. Drag a `.glb` into a scene. Each file flags nearest texture filtering for the PS1 look; keep it if you change the material.
3. For the full look, give the mesh a ShaderMaterial using `shaders/godot_psx.gdshader` with the skin texture as its albedo.
4. The clips are on the AnimationPlayer inside each skin0 scene.

## Made with AI

The bodies were generated by scripts an AI system wrote (MakeHuman output, CC0), and every texture is an AI image model painting projected onto the mesh. The skeleton and clips are the CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Library.
