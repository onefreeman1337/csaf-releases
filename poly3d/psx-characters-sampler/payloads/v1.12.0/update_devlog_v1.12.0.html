New in 1.12.0: the black knight from Oathfall joins the free PSX Characters Sampler, which now has 20 characters, one from each of our twenty paid PSX packs.

Blackwater and Camp Redfern are sold on the Last Shift page, all three casts in one purchase; Oathfall is sold on the Ironvale page, both casts in one purchase; Saint Oswin and Eight Angles are sold on the Hollow Congregation page, all three casts in one purchase; Crosstown, Ashfall, Syndicate and Foxhole are sold on the Fort Vale page, all five casts in one purchase; Hollyfrost is sold on the Greyhollow page, both casts in one purchase. Each of those pages also has its own free sampler file for each cast.

Every character comes with the same model files its full pack ships: a GLB with all 45 animation clips and the reference T pose, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, 256 px textures and the Blender source. Across the twenty that is 61 skins. Each file is byte for byte the one in the paid pack. Eight Angles also ships every enemy as eight direction sprite sheets; the fire fiend's sheets are in the Eight Angles free demo file on the Hollow Congregation page.

We ran the tests again on the new download: a blank Godot 4.7.2 project imported every GLB, the PSX shader compiled on all 20 characters under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile, and Blender imported every FBX (all 53 bones and every clip) and every OBJ back in. Unity and Unreal were not run here.

The full packs: Last Shift + Blackwater + Camp Redfern (41 characters, $9.99), Ironvale + Oathfall (28 characters, $7.99), Hollow Congregation + Saint Oswin + Eight Angles (42 characters, $8.99), Fort Vale + Crosstown + Ashfall + Syndicate + Foxhole (70 characters, $9.99), Greyhollow + Hollyfrost (28 characters, $8.99), Deep Station (14 characters, $7.99), Pale Hours (14 characters, $7.99), Outpost (14 characters, $7.99), Grimsnout (14 characters, $7.99), Night Beat (14 characters, $7.99). Those are list prices: as we post this, seven of the ten pages are 30% off (Last Shift + Blackwater + Camp Redfern $6.99, Ironvale + Oathfall $5.59, Hollow Congregation + Saint Oswin + Eight Angles $6.29, Greyhollow + Hollyfrost $6.29, Deep Station $5.59, Pale Hours $5.59, Grimsnout $5.59).

Every character was made with AI tools: bodies built by scripts an AI system wrote, textures painted by AI image models and projected onto the mesh.
