PSX Characters Sampler is 7 free low poly PS1 style 3D characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, each rigged with 45 animation clips. A mechanic, a knight, a high priest, a rifleman, an innkeeper, a station engineer and a plague doctor: one from each of our seven paid PSX packs, 22 palette skins in all, 1495 to 1499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Free to use in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What is inside

7 characters · 22 skins · 45 clips and a T-pose on each · GLB, FBX, OBJ and the Blender source · 256 px PNG textures in 15 bit colour · a PSX shader for Godot 4.

  • Mechanic from Last Shift (survival horror): 1497 triangles, 4 skins.
  • Knight from Ironvale (fantasy): 1499 triangles, 3 skins.
  • High priest from Hollow Congregation (occult horror): 1495 triangles, 3 skins.

  • Innkeeper from Greyhollow (medieval townsfolk): 1497 triangles, 3 skins.

  • The clips: idle, walk, sprint, crouch, jump, punches, sword, pistol, spells, hits, death, sit, swim, interact and more.
  • The shader (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering and fog, every setting a slider.

Every skin in Godot 4 with the included shader: garments recoloured, skin tones kept.

Works with

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb and put the shader on the mesh. Tested in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile: every GLB imports and every clip plays. Unity 6 and Unreal 5: import the FBX (humanoid skeleton and clips included) and set point filtering. Those two engines were not run here; Blender re-imported every FBX with all 53 bones and every clip.
