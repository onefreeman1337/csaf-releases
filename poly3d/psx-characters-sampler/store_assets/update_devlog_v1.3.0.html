The free PSX Characters Sampler now has 7 characters, one from each of our seven paid PSX packs. The plague doctor from Pale Hours joined the mechanic, knight, high priest, rifleman, innkeeper and station engineer.

Every character comes with the same files its full pack ships: a GLB with all 45 animation clips and the reference T-pose, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, 256 px textures and the Blender source. Across the seven that is 22 skins. Each file is byte for byte the one in the paid pack.

We re-ran the tests on the new download: a blank Godot 4.7.2 project imported every GLB, the PSX shader compiled on all 7 characters under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile, and Blender re-imported every FBX (all 53 bones and every clip) and every OBJ. Unity and Unreal were not run here.

The full packs: Last Shift (13 characters), Ironvale (14 characters), Hollow Congregation (14 characters), Fort Vale (14 characters), Greyhollow (14 characters), Deep Station (14 characters), Pale Hours (14 characters). All seven are 30% off in the Halloween sale until November 4, 2026.

Every character is AI-generated: bodies built by scripts an AI system wrote, textures painted by an AI image model and projected onto the mesh.
