The free PSX Characters Sampler now has 5 characters, one from each of our five paid PSX packs. The rifleman from Fort Vale and the innkeeper from Greyhollow joined the mechanic, the knight and the high priest.

Every character comes with the same files its full pack ships: a GLB with all 45 clips, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, 256 px textures and the Blender source, 16 skins in total. Each file is byte for byte the one in the paid pack.

We re-ran the tests on the new download: a blank Godot 4.7.2 project imported every GLB, the PSX shader compiled on all 5 characters under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile, and Blender re-imported every FBX and OBJ with all 53 bones and every clip. Unity and Unreal were not run here.

The full packs: Last Shift (13 characters), Ironvale (14 characters), Hollow Congregation (14 characters), Fort Vale (14 characters), Greyhollow (14 characters). All five are 30% off in the Halloween sale until November 4, 2026.

Every character is AI-generated: bodies built by scripts an AI system wrote, textures painted by an AI image model and projected onto the mesh.
