We put up a free sampler for our PSX character packs: 3 low poly PS1-style characters, one from each paid pack, rigged, with 45 animation clips each and 10 palette skins in total.

The mechanic is from Last Shift. The knight is from Ironvale. The high priest is from Hollow Congregation. Each one ships with every file its full pack ships per character: a GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the 256 px textures and the Blender source. The Godot 4 PSX shader is included.

We tested it the way a developer would use it: a blank Godot 4.7.2 project imported every GLB, the shader compiled on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile, and every clip played. Blender re-imported every FBX and OBJ with all 53 bones and every clip. Unity and Unreal were not run here.

The full packs: Last Shift (13 characters), Ironvale (14 characters), Hollow Congregation (14 characters). The sampler page is free with an optional tip.

Every character is AI-generated: bodies built by scripts an AI system wrote, textures painted by an AI image model and projected onto the mesh.
