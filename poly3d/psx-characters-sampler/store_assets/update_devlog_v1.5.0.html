The free PSX Characters Sampler now has 9 characters, one from each of our nine paid PSX packs. The orc warrior from Grimsnout joined the mechanic, knight, high priest, rifleman, innkeeper, station engineer, plague doctor and marine sergeant.

Every character comes with the same files its full pack ships: a GLB with all 45 animation clips and the reference T-pose, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, 256 px textures and the Blender source. Across the nine that is 28 skins. Each file is byte for byte the one in the paid pack.

The full packs: Last Shift (13 characters), Ironvale (14 characters), Hollow Congregation (14 characters), Fort Vale (14 characters), Greyhollow (14 characters), Deep Station (14 characters), Pale Hours (14 characters), Outpost (14 characters), Grimsnout (14 characters).
