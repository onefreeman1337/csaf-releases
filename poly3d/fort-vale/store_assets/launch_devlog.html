Fort Vale is out: 14 low poly modern soldier characters in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The garrison of a fictional army base: a rifleman, a field officer, a combat medic, a special operator, a helicopter pilot, a combat engineer, a radio operator, a recruit, a military police, a veteran sergeant, a quartermaster, a grenadier, a sniper and a scout. Each one is a different body and a different painted uniform, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin. The uniforms are plain and unmarked, with no real army's flags or insignia.

Three camouflage colourways each, 42 characters in total. The uniform and its gear change colour together: the rifleman's woodland goes desert tan or urban grey, the pilot's sage flight suit goes navy or tan, the officer's khaki service dress goes olive or slate. Faces, hair, boots, armbands and face paint keep their painted colours. Every colourway was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including 6 pistol clips (aim, shoot, reload), crouch walk, roll, sprint, hit reactions and a death. One honest limit: the CC0 clip library has no rifle animations, so the rifles are painted slung across the backs and the soldiers fight with the pistol set.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every colourway in engine, and a second reviewer looked again before release.

Try one free: the sampler is the rifleman, with all three colourways, every clip and every format.

Fort Vale joins Last Shift (survival horror survivors), Ironvale (a fantasy party) and Hollow Congregation (a horror cult). Count so far: 5 listings live, 2 sold (3 purchases).
