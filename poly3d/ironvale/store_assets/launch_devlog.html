Ironvale is out: 14 low poly fantasy RPG characters in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The party: a knight, a ranger, a rogue, a cleric, a wizard, a barbarian, a paladin, a bard, a monk, a necromancer, a druid, a mercenary, a squire and a hedge witch. Each one is a different body and a different painted outfit, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Three skins each, 42 characters in total. For this pack we changed how the extra skins are made. On the armoured and leather characters only the main garment changes colour: the knight's red surcoat turns blue or green while his steel stays steel, the cleric's cream tabard turns navy or burgundy while her gold sun emblem and chain mail stay put. On the near black costumes a second garment changes too, so every skin reads as a different outfit at a glance.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton: idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, sword idle and attack, four spell clips, punches, hit reactions, a death, sitting, swimming and more. There is no bow clip, so the ranger uses the sword and spell clips.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs with no errors, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender (53 bones, every clip, every texture found). Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, and a second reviewer looked again; two back paintings were redone before release.

Try one free: the sampler is the knight, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Ironvale is our second PSX character pack, after Last Shift (survival horror survivors). Count so far: 2 packs live, 1 sold.
