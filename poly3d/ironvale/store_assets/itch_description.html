A party of 14 fantasy adventurers for your PS1-style RPG: low poly, hand painted, rigged and animated, ready to walk out of the tavern and into the dungeon.

What you get

  • 14 characters, each a different body AND a different painted outfit: knight, ranger, rogue, cleric, wizard, barbarian, paladin, bard, monk, necromancer, druid, mercenary, squire, hedge witch.
  • 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place. On the armoured and leather characters only the main garment changes colour (a red surcoat becomes blue or green); steel, leather, trim, hair and skin keep their painted colours.
  • 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose: idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, 3 sword clips (idle, attack), 4 spell clips (enter, idle, cast, exit), punches, two hit reactions, a death, sit, swim, push, pick up, interact, kneel, torch idle and more. There is no bow clip: the ranger uses the sword and spell clips.
  • 1495-1499 triangles per character, one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering. The PlayStation look, not a modern model with a filter on top.
  • Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures, and the Blender source file.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog. Every setting is a slider.
  • Readme with import notes, LICENSE, NOTICE.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. Tested here in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles on every character, the clips play (Godot names Walk_Loop as Walk and loops it). The in-engine images on this page are that test.

Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX (skeleton and clips included) and set the textures to point / nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid, built to retarget (Unity Humanoid, Unreal IK Rig). These two engines were not run here; the FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.
