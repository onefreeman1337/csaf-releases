Greyhollow, Plaguewick and Hollyfrost are 42 low poly PS1-style characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 medieval townsfolk, 14 people of a plague village and 14 Christmas characters. Free samplers of all three are on this page. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips and comes in 3 palette skins (126 to place), about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. One purchase downloads all three packs; if you bought this page before, Plaguewick is already in your itch library at no extra cost.

Greyhollow: 14 medieval townsfolk

  • The working people of a walled market town: blacksmith, washerwoman, town guard, innkeeper, village priest, miller, merchant, town crier, herbalist, beggar, noblewoman, farmer, fisherwoman, stable hand.
  • 3 skins each: the main garment changes colour (a rose bodice to blue or green, a red tabard to blue or green); faces, hair, linen, steel and boots keep their paint.

Plaguewick: 14 plague village characters (added November 2026)

  • The same kind of village in its plague year: town watchman, abbess, gravedigger, rat catcher, witch finder, searcher of the dead, penitent, cordon guard, cunning woman, dead cart driver, apothecary, friar, afflicted woman and afflicted man.
  • Sickness, not gore: the afflicted read by pallor, dark rings and bandages; the searcher wears a cloth over her mouth; there is no blood and no wound anywhere, so they sit beside your villagers rather than your zombies.
  • Modelled hats: the watchman's kettle hat and the witch finder's tall capotain are low poly geometry on the head bone. Heights run 1.74 m to 1.92 m; every character is a grown adult.
  • 3 skins each: the main garment changes colour (the watchman's mustard gambeson to slate blue or faded red, the abbess's black habit to brown or slate); faces, hair, steel, leather and boots keep their paint.

Hollyfrost: 14 Christmas characters (added November 2026)

  • The people of a PlayStation era Christmas town, and the ones who should not be there after dark: Santa Claus, Krampus, nutcracker soldier, Mrs Claus, head elf, toymaker elf, nisse, Father Frost, caroler, chimney sweep, chestnut seller, ice skater, toy shop keeper, present thief.
  • Modelled silhouettes: Krampus's horns, the nutcracker's shako, the sweep's top hat, the caps, beards and pointed ears are low poly geometry on the head bone. Heights run 1.39 m to 2.01 m; every character is a grown adult, the elves and the nisse too.
  • 3 skins each: the painted original, a Victorian palette and a dark Krampusnacht palette for Christmas horror.

Every character, all three packs

  • 45 animation clips plus a T-pose: idle, talking, sitting, walk, jog, sprint, jump, crouch, push, kneel, pick up, interact, a torch idle, sword and punches, hits and a death.
  • 1,493 to 1,500 triangles, one 256 px texture per skin, 15-bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • Files: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures, the Blender source and a PSX shader for Godot 4.

Honest limits: the hands are simple mittens that hold nothing (attach props to the hand bone); the bodies are close fitting, so there are no long skirts, robes, cloaks, capes, sacks, carts, sleighs or reindeer, and the plague doctor with the beaked mask is not in this set.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ in, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. All three packs were tested in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles, the clips play. Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX, set nearest filtering; the skeleton is a standard humanoid. Those engines were not run here; every FBX was re-imported in Blender to check mesh, 53 bones and clips. Each download carries its own Readme.
