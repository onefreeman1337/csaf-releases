Plaguewick is now on this page: 14 low poly characters of a medieval village shut in with its plague, in the PlayStation 1 look, on the same skeleton, clip names and shader as the Greyhollow townsfolk. That makes 42 characters on this page, 126 with the palette skins: the town on an ordinary day, the same kind of town in its plague year, and Christmas.

The cast: town watchman, abbess, gravedigger, rat catcher, witch finder, searcher of the dead, penitent, cordon guard, cunning woman, dead cart driver, apothecary, friar, afflicted woman and afflicted man. Each is a different body and a different painted outfit, about 1,500 triangles, one 256 px texture per skin, 45 clips including push, kneel and pick up. Every character is a grown adult.

Sickness, not gore. The afflicted read by pallor, dark rings and bandages, and the searcher wears a cloth over her mouth; there is no blood and no wound anywhere. The watchman's kettle hat and the witch finder's tall hat are modelled on the head, so they hold from the back and through every clip. Honest limit: the hands are simple mittens and nothing is held.

Three palettes each: the main garment changes colour; faces, hair, steel, leather and boots keep their paint.
