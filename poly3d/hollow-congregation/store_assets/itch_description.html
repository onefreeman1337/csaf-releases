Hollow Congregation is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style horror cultist characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips, comes in 3 palette skins (42 characters to place), and has 1494-1498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required. A free sampler (the high priest, every skin and clip) is on this page.

The faithful of a small rural cult, its priest, and the townsfolk it has already taken: the people your survivors are running from.

What you get

  • 14 characters, each a different body AND a different painted outfit: high priest, the vessel, masked executioner, sister of the congregation, acolyte, the chosen, chanter, converted orderly, groundskeeper, converted deputy, penitent, matriarch, scribe, bell ringer.
  • 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place. The main garment changes colour (a black vestment becomes oxblood or dark green, the vessel's linen goes charcoal or slate); trim, bone charms, ribbons, leather, hair and skin keep their painted colours.
  • 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose: idle, walk, a slow formal walk for processions, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, 4 spell clips that read as rites (enter, idle, cast, exit), a torch idle, a sword attack for the executioner, punches, two hit reactions, a death, sit, swim, push, pick up, interact, kneel and more.
  • 1494-1498 triangles per character, one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering. The PlayStation look, not a modern model with a filter on top.
  • Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures, and the Blender source file.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog. Every setting is a slider.
  • Readme with import notes, LICENSE, NOTICE.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. Tested here in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles on every character, the clips play (Godot names Walk_Loop as Walk and loops it). The in-engine images on this page are that test.

Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX (skeleton and clips included) and set the textures to point / nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid, built to retarget (Unity Humanoid, Unreal IK Rig). These two engines were not run here; the FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.
