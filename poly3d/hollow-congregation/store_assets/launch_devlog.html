Hollow Congregation is out: 14 low poly horror cultist characters in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The congregation: a high priest, the vessel, a masked executioner, a sister of the congregation, an acolyte, the chosen, a chanter, a converted orderly, a groundskeeper, a converted deputy, a penitent, a matriarch, a scribe and a bell ringer. Each one is a different body and a different painted outfit, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Three skins each, 42 characters in total. Only the main garment changes colour: the priest's black vestment turns oxblood or dark green while his gold embroidery and bone pendant stay, the orderly's whites go scrub green or pale blue with the blood handprints kept, the bell ringer's moss sweater goes maroon or navy while his brass bell stays brass. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on, because a dark dye that looks fine in a flat texture can turn back into black under 15 bit colour; four skins were redone for exactly that reason before release.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton: idle, walk, a slow formal walk for processions, a torch idle, four spell clips that read as rites, a sword attack, punches, hit reactions, a death, sitting, kneeling, swimming and more.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second reviewer looked again before release.

Try one free: the sampler is the high priest, with all three skins, every clip and every format.
