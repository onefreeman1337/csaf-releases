Pale Hours is out: 14 low poly ghost characters in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The ghosts of a haunted manor, its grounds and the drowned shore below it, plus the possessed living: drowned bride, veiled widow, hanged man, plague doctor, the sleepwalker, buried miner, music box ballerina, drowned sailor, the shrouded, burned nun, lost soldier, the butler, the host and the wailer. Each one is a different body and a different painted costume, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Three palette skins each, 42 characters in total. The garment changes colour: the bride's ivory gown goes charcoal or wine red, the butler's black livery goes midnight blue or burgundy, the sailor's yellow oilskins go orange or dark green. Faces, hair and boots keep their paint. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including the ones a ghost story needs: a slow formal walk, an outstretched spell idle, a reaching lunge, crouch, hit reactions and a death.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second, independent AI review looked again before release; what it found was fixed.

Try one free: the sampler is the plague doctor, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Pale Hours sits beside Last Shift (survival horror survivors, same skeleton and shader) and Hollow Congregation (a cult and its converts). Count so far: 8 listings live, 2 sold, 3 purchases.
