Pale Hours is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style ghost characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1495-1499 triangles and a 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

The ghosts of a haunted manor, its grounds and the drowned shore below it, plus the possessed living.

What you get

  • 14 ghosts, each a different body and a different painted costume: drowned bride, veiled widow, hanged man, plague doctor, the sleepwalker, buried miner, music box ballerina, drowned sailor, the shrouded, burned nun, lost soldier, the butler, the host, the wailer.
  • 3 palette skins each, 42 characters to place. The garment changes colour (the bride's ivory gown to charcoal or wine red, the butler's black livery to midnight blue or burgundy, the sailor's yellow oilskins to orange or dark green); faces, hair and boots keep their paint.
  • 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose, with the ones a ghost story needs: a slow formal walk, outstretched spell idle, a reaching lunge, crouch, head and chest hits and a death, plus idle, talking, sitting, sprint and swim.
  • 1495-1499 triangles per character and one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures and the Blender source.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog.

Honest limits: the hands are simple mittens and nothing is held. The bodies are fitted, so gowns, habits and shrouds are painted on and stop at the thigh, and there are no capes, brimmed hats or floating effects: the ghostliness is in the paint and the clips. Same skeleton and clips as a living character, no creature rig.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. Tested here in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles on every character, the clips play.

Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX and set the textures to nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid, built to retarget. These two engines were not run here; the FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.

Documentation: the full Readme has the import notes, the clip list and the table of characters.
