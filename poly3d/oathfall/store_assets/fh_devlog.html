Oathfall is now on this page: 14 low poly dark fantasy knights and undead warriors in the PlayStation 1 look, on the same skeleton, clip names and shader as the Ironvale adventurers. That makes 28 fantasy characters on this page, 84 with the palette skins: a party and the enemies to fight them.

The cast: black knight, templar, executioner, plague knight, inquisitor, oathbreaker, fallen paladin, sellsword, grave warden, withered soldier, deserter, hedge knight, war priest and the withered king. Each is a different body and a different painted outfit, about 1,500 triangles, one 256 px texture per skin, 45 clips. Every character is a grown adult.

Helms and a crown you can see from every side. The black knight's great helm, the templar's conical helm, the plague knight's pointed bascinet, two kettle hats and the withered king's iron crown are modelled on the head, not painted, so they hold from the back and through every clip. Honest limit: the hands are simple mittens and nothing is held, so weapons are yours to attach.

Three palettes each: surcoats, tabards and trousers change colour; steel, leather, faces and hair keep their paint.
