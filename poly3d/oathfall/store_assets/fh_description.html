Ironvale + Oathfall is 28 low poly PS1-style fantasy characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 heroic adventurers and 14 dark fantasy knights and undead warriors to fight them. Every character is rigged to the same humanoid skeleton with the same 45 animation clips, about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. One purchase downloads both packs; if you bought Ironvale before, Oathfall is already in your itch library at no extra cost. Free samplers of both are on this page.

Oathfall: 14 dark fantasy knights and undead warriors (added November 2026)

  • An enemy roster for a dark fantasy or souls-like game: black knight, templar, executioner, plague knight, inquisitor, oathbreaker, fallen paladin, sellsword, grave warden, withered soldier, deserter, hedge knight, war priest and the withered king. Every one an adult, each a different body and painted outfit.
  • Helms and a crown you can see from every angle: the black knight's flat topped great helm, the templar's conical helm, the plague knight's pointed bascinet, two kettle hats and the withered king's iron crown are modelled on the head, not painted.
  • 3 skins each (42 to place): surcoats, tabards and trousers change colour; steel, leather, faces and hair keep their paint.
  • Honest limits: the hands are simple mittens and nothing is held, so weapons are yours to attach; surcoats are painted on a skin tight body, so they read as short tunics from behind.

Ironvale: 14 fantasy adventurers

  • A party for your PS1 style RPG: knight, ranger, rogue, cleric, wizard, barbarian, paladin, bard, monk, necromancer, druid, mercenary, squire and hedge witch, each a different body and a different painted outfit.
  • 3 palette skins each (42 to place): on armoured and leather characters only the main garment changes colour, and steel, leather, trim, hair and skin keep their painted colours.
  • The ranger uses the sword and spell clips, as there is no bow clip.

Every character, both packs

  • 45 animation clips plus a T pose: idle, walk, formal walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, punches, pistol clips, sword idle and attack, spells, hit reactions, a death, sit, swim, push, pick up, interact, kneel, torch idle and more.
  • The PlayStation look: 1,495 to 1,499 triangles per character and one 256 x 256 texture per skin, quantised to 15 bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4: vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering and distance fog, every setting a slider.
  • Files: a GLB per skin, FBX with the skeleton and clips, OBJ, and the Blender source.

Works with

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb and put the PSX shader on the mesh. Both packs were imported into a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles and the clips play.

Unity 6 and Unreal 5: import the FBX, with skeleton and clips included, and set the textures to point or nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid built to retarget. These two engines were not run here. The FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that the mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.

Licence and how it was made

Use the models in any number of commercial or free games and projects, with no attribution required, and modify them freely. Do not resell or redistribute the models themselves as assets. The skeleton and clips are the CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Library, and the bodies are generated with MakeHuman (CC0 output).

Every character here was made with AI tools: the bodies were built by scripts an AI system wrote, and every texture is an AI image model's painting projected onto the mesh (Oathfall: FLUX.2 klein). Code: yes. Graphics: yes. Sounds: no. Text: no.
