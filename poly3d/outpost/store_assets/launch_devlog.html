Outpost is out: 14 low poly sci-fi characters in the PlayStation 1 look, rigged and animated, $7.99 with a free sampler on the page.

The garrison and colony crew of a remote frontier outpost: marine rifleman, android, marine sergeant, colony farmer, heavy trooper, dropship pilot, base commander, combat engineer, recon scout, field medic, smuggler, ore miner, xenobiologist and colony rebel. Each one is a different body and a different painted costume, about 1,500 triangles, with one 256 px texture per skin.

Three palette skins each, 42 characters in total. The armour or garment changes colour: the rifleman's olive plates go desert sand or dark slate, the heavy trooper's dark red goes gunmetal or sand, the pilot's sky blue flight suit goes rust or sage. Faces, undersuits and boots keep their paint. Every skin was checked in Godot with the PSX shader on.

45 animation clips on every character, on one standard humanoid skeleton, including pistol idle, aim, shoot and reload for a sidearm or blaster, crouch, sprint, roll, a kneeling repair, hit reactions and a death. No weapon models ship and the hands are mittens, and the library has no rifle clips.

Files per character: GLB with every clip, a GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ, the PNG textures and the Blender source. The pack also carries our PSX shader for Godot 4 (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering, fog).

How we tested it: a blank Godot 4.7 project imported all 42 GLBs, and the shader compiled and drew on every character under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile; the screenshots on the store page are that test. Every FBX and OBJ was reopened in Blender. Every character was looked at from four angles and in four clips, every skin in engine, and a second, independent AI review looked again before release; what it found was fixed.

Try one free: the sampler is the marine sergeant, with all three skins, every clip and every format.

Outpost sits beside Deep Station (space horror crew, same skeleton and shader) and Fort Vale (modern soldiers). Count so far: 9 listings live, 3 sold, 4 purchases.
