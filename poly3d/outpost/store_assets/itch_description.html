Outpost is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style sci-fi characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1492-1498 triangles and a 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

The garrison and colony crew of a remote frontier outpost: hard armour marines beside the people they guard.

What you get

  • 14 characters, each a different body and a different painted costume: marine rifleman, android, marine sergeant, colony farmer, heavy trooper, dropship pilot, base commander, combat engineer, recon scout, field medic, smuggler, ore miner, xenobiologist, colony rebel.
  • 3 palette skins each, 42 characters to place. The armour or garment changes colour (the rifleman's olive plates to desert sand or dark slate, the heavy trooper's dark red to gunmetal or sand, the pilot's sky blue flight suit to rust or sage); faces, undersuits and boots keep their paint.
  • 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose, with the ones a sci-fi shooter needs: pistol idle, aim, shoot and reload for a sidearm or blaster, crouch, sprint, roll, a kneeling repair, chest and head hits and a death, plus idle, talking and sitting.
  • 1492-1498 triangles per character and one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures and the Blender source.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog.

Honest limits: no weapon models ship and the hands are simple mittens, so nothing is held; sidearms are painted in holsters. The animation library has pistol clips and no rifle clips. The bodies are fitted, so the armour plates are painted on rather than modelled, and there are no capes or long coats.

In your project

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb, put the PSX shader on the mesh. Tested here in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles on every character, the clips play.

Unity 6 / Unreal 5: import the FBX and set the textures to nearest filtering. The skeleton is a standard humanoid, built to retarget. These two engines were not run here; the FBX files were re-imported in Blender to check that mesh, 53 bones and every clip arrive.

Documentation: the full Readme has the import notes and the clip list.
