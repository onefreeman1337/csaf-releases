Highcourt is now on this page: 14 low poly characters of a medieval royal court, in the PlayStation 1 look, on the same skeleton, clip names and shader as the Ironvale adventurers and the Oathfall knights. That makes 42 characters on this page, 126 with the palette skins: the heroes, the dark knights they fight, and the court that sends them out.

The cast: king, queen, prince, princess, chancellor, royal guard, jester, court mage, steward, lady in waiting, ambassador, bishop, spymaster and the herald. Each is a different body and a different painted outfit, about 1,500 triangles, one 256 px texture per skin, 45 clips including a formal walk, talking idles, sitting and kneeling. Every character is a grown adult; the prince and princess are in their twenties.

Crowns and hats are modelled. The king's and queen's crowns, the guard's helmet, the jester's two pointed cap, the mage's brimmed hat, the hennin, the mitre, the bonnet, the ambassador's tall hat and the herald's beret sit on the head as geometry, so they hold from the back and through every clip. Honest limits: no gowns, robes or capes (court coats end at mid thigh over breeches and the women wear riding dress), and the hands are simple mittens that hold nothing.

Three palettes each: the coat, doublet or tabard changes colour; faces, hair, gold trim, steel and boots keep their paint.
