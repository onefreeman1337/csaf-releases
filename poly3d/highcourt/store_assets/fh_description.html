Ironvale, Oathfall and Highcourt are 42 low poly PS1-style fantasy characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 heroic adventurers, 14 dark knights and undead warriors, and 14 people of a royal court. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips and comes in 3 palette skins (126 to place), about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. One purchase downloads all three packs; if you bought this page before, Highcourt is already in your itch library at no extra cost. Highcourt: 14 royal court and nobles (added November 2026)

  • The castle your heroes are summoned to: king, queen, prince, princess, chancellor, royal guard, jester, court mage, steward, lady in waiting, ambassador, bishop, spymaster and the herald. Every one an adult (the prince and princess in their twenties), each a different body and painted outfit.
  • Crowns and hats you can see from every angle: the king's and queen's crowns, the chancellor's bonnet, the guard's helmet, the jester's two pointed cap, the court mage's brimmed hat, the lady in waiting's hennin, the ambassador's tall hat, the bishop's mitre and the herald's beret are modelled on the head, not painted. Heights run 1.76 m to 1.88 m.
  • 3 skins each: the coat, doublet or tabard changes colour (the king's crimson to blue or green, the jester's gold diamonds to red or blue); faces, hair, gold trim, steel and boots keep their paint, and so do most hats.
  • Honest limits: no gowns, robes or capes (the body is close fitting, so court coats end at mid thigh over breeches and the women wear riding dress); the hands are simple mittens and nothing is held, so sceptres and swords are yours to attach.

Oathfall: 14 dark fantasy knights and undead warriors (added November 2026)

  • An enemy roster for a dark fantasy or souls-like game: black knight, templar, executioner, plague knight, inquisitor, oathbreaker, fallen paladin, sellsword, grave warden, withered soldier, deserter, hedge knight, war priest and the withered king.
  • Modelled helms and an iron crown; surcoats, tabards and trousers change colour across 3 skins while steel, leather, faces and hair keep their paint.

Ironvale: 14 fantasy adventurers

  • A party for your PS1 style RPG: knight, ranger, rogue, cleric, wizard, barbarian, paladin, bard, monk, necromancer, druid, mercenary, squire and hedge witch.
  • 3 palette skins each: on armoured and leather characters only the main garment changes colour. The ranger uses the sword and spell clips, as there is no bow clip.

Every character, all three packs

  • 45 animation clips plus a T pose: idle, talking, walk, formal walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, punches, sword, spells, hits, a death, sit, swim, kneel, interact and more.
  • The PlayStation look: 1,495 to 1,499 triangles per character and one 256 x 256 texture per skin, quantised to 15 bit colour for nearest filtering.
  • A PSX shader for Godot 4: vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15 bit colour with dithering and distance fog, every setting a slider.
  • Files: a GLB per skin, FBX with the skeleton and clips, OBJ, and the Blender source.

Works with

Godot 4: copy models/ and shaders/ into the project, instance a _skin0.glb and put the PSX shader on the mesh. All three packs were imported into a blank Godot 4.7.2 project: every GLB imports, the shader compiles and the clips play.

Unity 6 and Unreal 5: import the FBX (skeleton and clips included) and set nearest filtering; the skeleton is a standard humanoid. Those engines were not run here; every FBX was re-imported in Blender to check the mesh, 53 bones and clips.

Licence and how it was made

Use the models in any number of commercial or free games and projects, with no attribution required, and modify them freely. Do not resell or redistribute the models themselves as assets. The skeleton and clips are the CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Library, and the bodies are generated with MakeHuman (CC0 output).

Every character here was made with AI tools: the bodies were built by scripts an AI system wrote, and every texture is an AI image model's painting projected onto the mesh (Oathfall: FLUX.2 klein; Highcourt: OpenAI gpt-image-2). Code: yes. Graphics: yes. Sounds: no. Text: no.
