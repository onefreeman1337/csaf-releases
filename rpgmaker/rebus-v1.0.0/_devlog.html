Rebus 1.0 is out: picture word puzzles for RPG Maker MZ, built from the art your game already has.

Rebus reads your database and turns it into rounds. Two to four of your icons that share a name are pinned to a cork board: four swords, four fire skills, four shields. An enemy battler stands on a spotlit stage as a black silhouette. An actor's face is seen through a brass lens that starts on one detail and slowly widens, or the picture is broken into blocks that sharpen over time. The player spells the answer from a tray of carved tiles by clicking or typing.

A <Rebus Group: word> notetag puts any item, weapon, armour or skill into a round of your own, so icons your database never typed together can still share a board. Score, solved count and an all-solved switch go back to your variables. The tiles come in wood, brass, ivory or slate, and a Pixel art switch keeps sharp pixels at every scale.

It was played in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the stock database, with real keyboard and mouse input. The gems, jugs, fish, creatures and faces in the screenshots are demo art from our own generators and sprite packs, sold separately: the stag, wolf and fox from Kithfell 03: The Wildwood, the undead from Mourncrest 01: The Restless Dead, and the faces from Hearthhands Portraits, which ships MZ face sets.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
