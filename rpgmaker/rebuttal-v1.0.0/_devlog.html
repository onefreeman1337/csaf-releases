Rebuttal 1.0 is out: class-trial debates for RPG Maker MZ.

The cast sits in a ring and makes their statements, and each statement flies across the stage as big moving type. One of them hides a lie, glowing gold. The player turns the cylinder to the right piece of evidence, aims and fires. The right bullet breaks the statement apart and wins the debate; a wrong one costs influence.

You write a debate as a few Comment lines in a common event. Rebuttal composes how each statement moves from its own words: eight entrances taken in turn, seven paths with each speaker moving in a family of three, and punctuation setting the emphasis. Your items become the bullets through one notetag.

Everything on screen is drawn in code: the ring, the type, the gold weak point, the revolver HUD and the stamp. No stock window and no default font. It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, including real keyboard, wheel, click and touch input.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
