Jigsaw is out: any picture in your RPG Maker MZ or MV game, cut into a jigsaw the player solves on a drawn table.

Name a picture in the plugin command and Jigsaw cuts it, from 4 pieces to 256. Every edge is a knob-and-socket curve shared by the two pieces it separates, so no two seeds cut the same puzzle. Pieces are bevelled, lit and shadowed, and a piece in the hand lifts higher. A plaque keeps the count, the time and the moves; Q lifts the box lid; and when the last piece goes in, the finished picture rises to the middle of the table.

The time, the moves and a solved switch go back to your variables before the event continues, so a Conditional Branch straight after the command can reward the player.

Tested from the downloadable zip in MZ 1.10.0 and MV 1.6.1: three screen sizes (including a 1080 x 1920 phone), four piece counts and two seeds per engine, real mouse drags, every claim in the guide, and an adversarial pass. A demo picture is included so you can try it straight away.
