Timber is out for RPG Maker MZ. Every tree already painted on your maps can now be cut down: face a tree, press OK, and it shudders, a notch opens in its own bark, and on the last chop it falls with weight, bursts into its own leaves and splits into logs. A stump with rings stays, and over play time a sapling grows back into the tree.

It works on the trees you already have. MZ's stock trees fall the moment the plugin is switched on; for your own tilesets you give the tree tiles one terrain tag and name it in the tileset note. Six kinds fall six ways: a broadleaf bursts into leaves, a conifer's boughs break and slide apart, a palm whips over, a dead tree snaps and splinters, a mushroom's cap bursts into spores, and a crystal shatters where it stands.

How it works: Timber finds each tree as the tiles you painted together from the palette, lifts it off the map while you chop, and cuts every leaf, chip, log, splinter, stump and sapling from that tree's own pixels. Each picture is read once while the map fades in; chopping and falling read no pixels at all. The save keeps which tree fell and when, nothing else.

It shares its two core files with Smithereens, which breaks pots, crates, rocks and bushes the same way.

Timber is $9.99: csaf.itch.io/timber
