Pendant is out: spot the difference from any picture in your game, for RPG Maker MZ and MV.

Name a picture in the plugin command and Pendant hangs it twice in a drawn gallery, gilt frames on a damask wall, then changes the second copy in 1 to 12 places. Nothing to paint by hand: an object is added where it belongs (a kite in the sky, a fish in the water, a key on a table), a detail is mirrored, something is painted out, or an object is recoloured to a colour the picture already uses.

Every change is measured before it is kept, so a puzzle never hides a difference nobody can see. Finds are ringed in red ink on both paintings, misses and hints are counted, and the time, the finds, the misses and an all found switch go back to your events.

Tested from the downloadable zip in MZ 1.10.0 and MV 1.6.1, side by side on desktop and stacked on a phone screen, with every claim in the guide checked and an adversarial pass.
