Fray is out for RPG Maker MZ. Your party now wears the fight: every hit a hero takes is drawn into that hero's own face and side-view battler, and the wear stays until they rest.

A blade grazes a cheek or tears a coat, a blow bruises, fire scorches clothing, ice frosts it, lightning leaves a flash burn, poison a sick bruise. As HP falls the skin pales and grime sinks in, and at the last band a battler's hem frays. A potion lays a plaster or a bandage over the worst mark; an inn (Recover All) mends everything.

How it works: Fray reads each face and battler once, finds the skin, the clothing and the armour from the image's own colours, and draws every mark as pixel work on a copy. Nothing to draw, no damaged face sets. On a side-view sheet each mark is held on the same part of the body in all 54 motion frames, from the walk to the fall. The save keeps a short list of decisions per hero, never pixels.

Tested on all 15 stock MZ face sheets and all 40 stock side-view battler sheets, painted portraits and HD pixel-art battlers, in the real engine, with a frame gate on the battle. It shares its mark painter with Scathe, which does the same for your enemies.
