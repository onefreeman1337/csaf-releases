Wrack 1.0 is out: your own town burned, ruined, overgrown or abandoned, made from your own tileset, for RPG Maker MZ.

When a map loads, Wrack reads the tileset images the map already uses and derives four aftermaths from those pixels: roofs charred in their own material with holes burnt through them, roofs fallen in with heaps of rubble, ivy and moss in your own greens, or a town faded under dust with its windows boarded up.

Switch a whole map, a region or one building by notetag, switch or plugin command, and play the change on screen: a burning line that wanders across the map with flames standing on it (water never burns), the years creeping in through patches that cross tile borders, or a quick fade. The map file is never touched, so events and passability keep working.

It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, on four tilesets of our own and on the stock Outside, Inside and Dungeon tilesets in a blank project. The towns in the screenshots are drawn with our Verdant tilesets, sold separately: Verdant 09 Town, Verdant 45 Walls and Roofs and Verdant 41 Taiga among them.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
