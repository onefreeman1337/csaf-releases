Threshold 1.0 is out: animated doors for RPG Maker MZ, drawn from your own tileset.

In stock MZ a door only opens if someone drew a character sheet for it. Threshold reads the door tile you already placed when the map loads and builds its opening from that tile's pixels. Mark a tile event with <threshold: door> and it swings open. Eight kinds: a hinged door, double doors, a portcullis, a floor hatch, a sliding shoji, a curtain, a round vault door, and a cave mouth that breathes cold air.

The stock Door quick event keeps working. Its turns become the stages of the opening, so existing door events animate your tiles with no edits. At night, open doorways fill with warm light.

We ran it in a blank MZ project on MZ's own door art as well as on three test tilesets. That run found two things we fixed before release: the stock wall doors span two tiles, and the top half lagged a frame behind the bottom; and the cell bars left a shadow of themselves when raised. Both are covered by tests now.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds and no story text.
