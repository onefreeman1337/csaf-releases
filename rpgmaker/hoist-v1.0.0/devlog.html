Hoist is out: pick up and throw for RPG Maker MZ, with the lift, carry and throw poses made from your own walking sprites in all 4 facings.

Tag an event <hoist> and the hero can squat, lift it overhead, walk with it and throw it. It flies on an arc over its own shadow, stops short of walls, sinks in water, and breaks into pieces of its own picture where it lands. A <hoist target> it hits gets a self switch, and with Smithereens installed the object breaks the way its material would.

The poses are made once, when a walking sheet loads, from that sheet's own arms and colours, so there is no lift sprite to draw. Measured in the real engine on a loop of lift, carry, throw and break: 10.1 draw calls a frame, against 10 with nothing carried. Eight sample objects come with it: a pot, an urn, a crate, a barrel, a rock, a bush, a sack and a training dummy that falls over.

Hoist on itch.io, $9.99. The documentation is free to read first.
