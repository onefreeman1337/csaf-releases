Aloft is out: your own RPG Maker MZ maps as islands in the sky. Put <aloft> in a map's note, leave the sky empty or paint it with a region, and the edge of your ground falls away into a rock underside over a sea of clouds drifting far below. $9.99 on itch.

Why it exists. Our Fathom takes a map under the sea. Aloft takes one up. Floating islands and sky cities come back on the RPG Maker forums every year, and the asks are nearly always for art: the underside of the island, which nobody's tileset has and most people cannot draw. Aloft draws it from the tiles you already have. The top band of every underside is your edge tile's own bottom rows, mirrored and shaded, and the rock strata lean toward its colours, so grass leans the rock olive, snow grey and basalt near black.

What it draws. Every connected patch of ground becomes its own island with its own keel, hangers and soft shadow on the cloud tops. Five dressings: Spirit Garden (roots, ivy, flowering vines and a waterfall off the edge), Sky Ruins (cut stone, chains, broken arches, banners), Crystal Spire (geode rock and crystals that glow), Steam Isle (a riveted brass hull, pipes, gears and a turning propeller) and Storm Reach (jagged slate, lightning rods and dark anvil clouds lit by lightning). The sky follows MZ's own screen tone, so a Night tint brings out the stars. A Fall Off the Edge command drops the player or an event into the clouds for scripted moments. 49 kinds of art in all, every one generated, and no image files ship.

How it was tested. Everything ran in the real MZ engine through our own harness, with no RPG Maker install. 426 checks across ten suites: the ground and sky classifier, island finding, the keel profiles, the palette derivation, every Readme claim against the code, a zero allocation scan of every function that runs each frame, and eight deliberate sabotages that each turned their own check red. An adversarial pass tried an all sky map, an all ground map, a one tile map, a looping map, a 70 tile wide island, fifty dressing switches in a row, a menu round trip, a 32 pixel tile project and saving mid map. A fresh install from the download into a blank project floated a stock map painted with region 1 and sampled MZ's own tiles. On the showroom map the layer draws in 18 draw calls a frame, and a 60 by 40 archipelago with every setting at its maximum in 36, with no texture uploads. Then a fresh reviewer who had not built it went through every store image and wrote down 35 defects before anything was fixed, and the fixes reached the plugin itself: a keel that hung into the island below it, an island shadow whose clipped blur drew a seam across the clouds, a fall that faded on the rock face instead of dropping into the clouds, and undersides dark enough to read as a void.

Pairs with Fathom (your maps under the sea), Cloudshadow (cloud shadows drifting over your ground) and Hedgerow (the small animals that walk your maps).

The code, including the drawing code, was written with AI assistance. The download is three raw, readable JavaScript files, a Readme and the licence.
