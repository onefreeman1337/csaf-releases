Emaki is out: an RPG Maker MZ plugin, $9.99, that paints what the player did as a Japanese handscroll. Each act your game marks becomes a scene in ink line and flat mineral colour between bands of gold cloud, and the player unrolls it from right to left on a lacquer table.

The plugin draws everything itself: sixteen figures, ten compositions, five grounds, six pigment sets and a brushed hand for your titles. No art ships and none is needed.

The part worth looking at is the reading. You type what happened, and Emaki reads the words for a composition, a cast and a season. "The siege of Akasaka keep" gets a palisade and a watchtower. "The monk and the fox" has both of them in it. "The first snow at the border gate" is painted in the snow set, with the snow falling. It reads words and not sentences, so "The bridge at Uji" has no bridge, and you can always choose the composition yourself. Acts can also come from switches and variables your project already has, so a game with chapter switches gets a scroll with no new events.

Each act is frozen into the save when it is painted, so a later update can never repaint a scroll a player already has.

Before release a second reviewer looked at every store image and wrote down 53 defects. Because of that review the figures gained hands and sandals and a finer ink line, the swordsman got a real guard and a real blade, the shrine gate and the pagoda were rebuilt, a demon stopped turning up in acts that never asked for him, and the cover was redrawn so it shows the look the plugin actually paints. The plugin was tested in the real MZ engine, including an install from the shipped zip into a blank project. It extends two engine methods, with the originals saved and called, and replaces none.

Emaki is the sibling of Arras, which weaves the playthrough as a Norman hanging. The code and graphics were made with AI assistance.
