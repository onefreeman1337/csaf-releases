Any Picture Minigames Sampler is out, and it is free: three RPG Maker MZ plugins that turn any picture in your img/pictures folder into a minigame.

  • Jigsaw Lite cuts the picture into a 9 piece jigsaw on a drawn felt table. Drag pieces with mouse or finger, or carry them with the keyboard hand; when the last one goes home the whole picture rises over the table.
  • Pendant Lite hangs the picture twice in a gilt gallery and changes the second copy in 3 places, each measured to be visible before it is kept. Finds are ringed in ink on both paintings.
  • Unveil Lite hides the picture under a silk veil. Hold OK to cut into it and close the cut; everything with no creature in it falls away. Reveal 75% with 3 lives.

One plugin command each, one result switch each, and a demo picture in the zip so you can try them straight away. They patch no RPG Maker method, so they sit beside anything else in your Plugin Manager.

Each Lite is a small cut of a paid plugin from the same studio: Jigsaw, Pendant and Unveil. Install the full version and the Lite's command hands the game to it, so no event has to change. The family has eight more minigames built from your pictures or your database, all linked from the page, and all 30% off in the Halloween Sale until November 4.

Before release each Lite was checked against the paid plugin's own code, played to a win and a loss in the real MZ engine, installed fresh from the zip, saved and loaded.
