Tilestack is out: mahjong solitaire as a minigame for RPG Maker MZ and MV.

A stack of carved tiles on a felt table; the player clears it two free matching tiles at a time. Every tile is drawn by the plugin, 42 faces in five sets (ivory, bone, jade, black lacquer, porcelain), and the layout is the classic turtle or a generated one of the size you ask for, different for every seed.

Every deal can be cleared: it is dealt backwards, so a winning order always exists, and a stuck player can shuffle what is left. Hints and undo, mouse, touch and keyboard. The time, the moves and a cleared switch go back to your variables before the event continues.

Tested from the downloadable zip in MZ 1.10.0 and MV 1.6.1, at three screen sizes including a phone, with every claim in the guide checked and an adversarial pass.

$9.99. The code and the graphics were made with AI assistance.
