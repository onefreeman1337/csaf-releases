Spellwright is out: an in-game spell crafting system for RPG Maker MZ, and the sibling of Sigil Core.

Sigil Core draws a magic circle for every spell the developer writes. Spellwright lets the player write them. At a spellwright's circle the player takes the effects of spells their hero already knows, combines up to four of them, turns a magnitude dial, names the result, and watches its seal inscribe itself from what went in.

The new spell is an ordinary skill. It appears in the skill list, Game_Action casts it in battle, and it saves and loads with the game. Its id is never written into an actor's learned-skill list, so a save still loads if the plugin is ever removed.

The cost cannot break a game's balance. Each source spell is paid for once, and a crafted spell is never cheaper than the dearest spell it was made from at the same strength. We checked that over 100,000 crafted spells from a new project's own database: none came in under its source.

Every seal is drawn in code from the spell itself. Reach shapes the rim, the damage form draws the core, each state or buff writes its own glyph around the band, and magnitude adds layers. A new project's stock Sorcerer, Priest and Magic Swordsman alone produce 525 different seals.

The scene draws no MZ window and no stock button. With a player changing an aspect every second it measured 4 draw calls a frame in the real engine.

Two raw JavaScript files, a full Readme and the licence. Written with AI assistance.
