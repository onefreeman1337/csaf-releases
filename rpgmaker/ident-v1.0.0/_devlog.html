Ident 1.0 is out: an animated studio ident for RPG Maker MZ, played once before your title screen and drawn from your studio name.

Type the name, pick one of forty treatments, and the plugin sets it: struck in gold foil, bent in neon on a brick wall, cut into slate, pressed in sealing wax, powered on across a CRT, and thirty-five more. The name seeds the colours, the ground and the order the letters catch, so two studios on the same treatment still get different marks. Every letter is cut from letterforms the plugin carries in five hands, never the engine's font.

Two things changed on the way out. The arrival now redraws every second frame: measured on our engine gate, redrawing every frame meant one full-screen upload to the graphics card per frame, twice our own ceiling. And a blackletter M that read as three strokes in five treatments was redrawn as four joined strokes, found by looking at the ident in a fresh project with the engine's own splash switched on.

Made with AI: the code, the letterforms and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds and no story text.
