Round-up 1.0 is out: a herding minigame for RPG Maker MZ played on your own map.

Paint a pen with a region ID and call one plugin command. A generated flock scatters over your farmyard and the player has until the bell to drive it through the gate. The animals walk your map with your map's own passability and sort with your characters. There is no new scene.

Each species has its own rule. Sheep flock, hens zig-zag, pigs bolt and then stop dead, and geese stand their ground and may charge. A cornered animal dodges along the fence, so a corner is never a dead end. The plugin draws all of it: 89 looks across four species, a feed trough, a clock and tally, a whistle, and a tally board with prize rosettes at the bell.

We tuned it with a scripted herder that moves the way MZ's player moves. With a two tile gate and the trough it penned every animal in 51% to 57% of 200 rounds on each difficulty. A player who only chases managed 0% to 9%. It was then played in MZ's runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds of its own (it plays MZ's own sound files) and no story text.
