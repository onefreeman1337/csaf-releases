Broadside 1.0 is out: ship-to-ship battles for RPG Maker MZ, drawn broadside-on like an age-of-sail painting.

Two sailing ships duel on a painted sea. Each turn you give two orders, a manoeuvre (close, bear away, fight for the weather gauge, cross her bow, hold, board) and what to load (round, chain or grape), then fire on the roll: a brass dial swings with your ship, and firing at the bottom of the roll puts the broadside low and steady into her hull, at the top into her rig. Every hit is drawn into the ships as it lands, from shot holes along her side to a topmast hanging over the side, torn canvas, fire, a list and a ship going down.

The ships come from a generator (16 hulls, 10 rigs, 8 paint schemes, 5 states of wear, each fighting as one of 7 classes). One plugin command starts a fight; nine endings go to a variable, victory to a switch, and your hull can carry from one fight to the next. Turn escape off for a boss fight.

It was played in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the default parameters, whole fights on the keyboard from a map event to the ship's log and back. It also runs in projects at 1280 x 720 and other sizes, scaled and centred with the sea filling the edges. Nothing is loaded: no image files at all.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. No sounds of its own (it plays MZ's own sound files) and no story text.
