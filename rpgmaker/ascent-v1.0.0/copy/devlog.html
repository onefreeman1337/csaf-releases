Ascent is out: climbing minigames for RPG Maker MZ, $9.99. Your party leader climbs a sea cliff, an ice wall, a castle wall under siege, a great tree, a ship's rigging or a round tower, hold by hold, against a grip that drains while they hang.

The plugin lays out and paints every face in code. Each face has its own structure rather than a recolour: free rock on the cliff, holds pulled to vertical seams on the ice, every hold on a masonry course on the castle and the tower, a trunk that narrows as you climb, and the ratline lattice of a ship's mast. There are 36 kinds of hold, six per face in the same six roles (two firm, hard, slick, breaking, rest), so a player learns them once. Every breaking hold, on every face, trickles grit beneath it.

Reaching an anchor clips your rope in, and the rope is drawn from your second party member at the foot up through every anchor you clipped. Fall, and it catches you at the last one. Three fall rules let you choose how forgiving a climb is: rope, strict or solo.

Hazards come with a warning: rocks, icicles, stones, pine cones, rigging blocks or bricks fall with a shadow and a mark over their column; gulls, ravens, owls, bats or arrows cross the face with a mark at their height; gusts drain loose holds faster. Name an enemy image and it leans out over a stone parapet at the top of the screen to throw the stones.

Three goals: climb to the summit against the clock, collect eggs, crystals, coins, acorns, doubloons or gems on the way up (each can give one of your items), or race a rival up the next line. Before the player sees a face, the plugin's own autopilot climbs it and must reach the summit with no fall; a face that fails is laid out again. Over 7,200 faces measured, every one passed.

It was tested in the real MZ engine, including an install from the shipped zip into a blank project. Before release a second reviewer looked at every store image and wrote down 44 defects; the climber's arms, the thrower, the tower's summit and the breaking rope were all redrawn because of it. Ascent patches no engine method.

Ascent is the upward sibling of Schuss, downhill runs on a course the plugin lays out. The code and graphics were made with AI assistance.
