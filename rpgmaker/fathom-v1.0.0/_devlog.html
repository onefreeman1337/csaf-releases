Fathom 1.0 is out: your own maps under the sea, for RPG Maker MZ, from one map notetag.

Put <underwater> in a map's note and Fathom draws the map as seen through the water: the colours graded by depth with the reds going first, a network of caustic light crawling over the floor, light shafts slanting down from the surface, your tileset's grass and shrubs swaying as sea grass, bubbles rising from every character, and a refraction wobble over the scene.

The sea life is drawn by the plugin too: 48 species built from a body plan, a body, a tail, a pattern and a palette. Fish swim in schools that part around the party, rays glide overhead and cast soft shadows on the floor, jellyfish drift with the current, and eels and crabs keep to the floor.

Dive and Surface plugin commands take the party in and out with a splash, and two optional rules add slower movement and an air meter drawn as a column of bubbles.

It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, on our own reef and town maps and in a blank MZ project with the stock tileset.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
