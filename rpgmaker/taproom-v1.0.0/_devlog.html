Taproom is out: four tavern board games for RPG Maker MZ, played against your NPCs for gold, on boards the plugin draws. Nine Men's Morris, Fox and Geese, Oware and Fanorona. The player sits down across a table from anyone in your world, sets a stake, and plays. When the game ends, the result goes back to your events: a variable, a switch, and the gold.

Why these four

Chess and checkers already have free MZ plugins, and one paid collection covers nine modern and classic games in an app-style look. None of them has these four: the mill game, the fox against the flock, the sowing game, and the capture-by-approach game. They are the games people actually played in taverns, and they are the ones that look right on a tavern table.

Every board is drawn

Eight boards, each a material rendered and lit when the game opens: chalked slate, veined marble, a board knifed into the tabletop, black lacquer with gilt lines, a linen mat stitched in red, tooled leather, walnut with brass, carved soapstone. Six sets of men: river stones, bone and horn, glass, silver and copper, fired clay, boxwood and ebony, each with the seeds its Oware is played with. The board and men an opponent sets out are chosen from their name, so every regular at the inn has their own.

An opponent that thinks, and was measured

Five strengths, Novice to Master, searching ahead with alpha-beta. Each strength played the one below it 24 times in each game and came out ahead in all sixteen pairings. Two things the measuring changed: in Fanorona a Master that looked one move further than the Hustler lost to it, 7 wins to 15, so the Master now looks as far and simply never misjudges; and in Fox and Geese a shallow flock lost to any fox until the geese learned to count the room the fox has left, not how far they have marched.

One thing only looking caught

The side panel's counts froze at their first drawing: "9 in hand" beside four men already in the tray, while every count in the rules was right. The panel was being redrawn and never uploaded. It is fixed, and the in-engine suite now fails if it ever happens again (checked by putting the bug back on purpose).

Open a game with the Play a Game plugin command. Mouse, touch, keyboard and gamepad.
