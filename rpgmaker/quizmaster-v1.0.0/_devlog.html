Quizmaster 1.0 is out: quiz shows and exams for RPG Maker MZ, with the questions written from your own database.

One plugin command runs a game show: the party leader on a podium against up to three rivals, a timer ring draining, four answers to choose from. Or a solo exam with a pass mark. Quizmaster reads your items, skills, enemies, actors and classes and writes the questions itself, eleven kinds of them, checking every answer against your data. The stock MZ database alone writes 392 questions.

Six stages are drawn in code, from a curtained game show to a starlit arcane exam, and each card pictures its subject with your own art. Results go back to your variables, a switch and the party's gold.

It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, whole quizzes played through the real plugin command.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
