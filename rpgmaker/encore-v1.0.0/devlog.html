Encore is out for RPG Maker MZ: boss second phases generated from the battler image your boss already has. One note tag, and when the boss's HP falls to the threshold the fight pauses, the boss holds and shakes, and it changes in a drawn transformation into a new form of itself.

There are 12 forms (Ascended, Corrupted, Enraged, Frozen, Armoured, Crystal, Overgrown, Rot, Overdrive, Shadow, Celestial, Husk) and 4 transformations (swell, shatter, molt, burn). Pixel art at 2x or larger is forged at its own pixel size, so what the form adds sits on the art's own grid. The pictures use our own Deepwarren battlers in a hall we painted.

The change plays between actions, never inside one. The blow that crosses the threshold cannot kill, so the change always plays. A phase can raise the boss's stats or turn it into another enemy. Tested in front view and side view, in turn-based and time-progress battles, and beside our Scathe, whose damage marks start again clean on the new form.
