Scathe 1.0 is out: battle damage you can see, drawn into your own enemy art, for RPG Maker MZ.

Every enemy now shows the damage it takes. A sword leaves a gash that bleeds, an earth spell cracks it, fire chars it, ice frosts it, thunder burns a fern of lightning into it, and poison or darkness spreads a bruise with veins. As its HP falls past 75%, 50% and 25% the whole enemy looks more worn, and at the last band its outline takes bites. Nothing to draw: Scathe reads each battler once when the battle starts and generates the damage from that image's own pixels, inside its outline.

Six materials change how a blow reads (flesh bleeds, stone cracks and chips, shell fractures, metal scores to bare steel, slime splits, spirit tears), set with one optional notetag. Two plugin commands mark or mend an enemy from an event. The kind of mark is chosen by the element's name, so a database with its own elements still works.

It was played in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the stock database, battles fought on the keyboard until the troop fell. The zip includes seven painted demo battlers (an ogre, a scarab, a golem, haunted armour, an ooze, a wraith and a wyvern) and a battleback, free to use in your games. For more battle and wear tools from the same studio, see Stature (taller side-view battlers from your own sheets) and Smithereens (map props that break the way their material would).

The code was written with generative AI, and the demo art and store images were made with generative AI image tools. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
