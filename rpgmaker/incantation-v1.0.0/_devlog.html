Incantation 1.0 is out: typing battles for RPG Maker MZ, with the words taken from your own database.

One plugin command starts a Typing of the Dead style battle: foes walk down a drawn corridor carrying words over their heads, and typing a word strikes it down. The words are your skill, enemy and item names, or any words you give a record in a notetag. The stock MZ database alone gives 159 of them.

Typed letters ignite one by one on plates over each foe, on three stages drawn in code: a torchlit crypt, a moonlit grove and a floating library. Three difficulties were measured with a simulated typist over hundreds of battles. Results go back to your variables, a switch and the party's gold.

It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, whole battles typed with real key presses.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
