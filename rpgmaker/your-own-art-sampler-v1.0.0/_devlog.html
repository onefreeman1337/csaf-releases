Your Own Art Sampler is out: three free RPG Maker MZ plugins, each one mode of a paid plugin from the same family.

Threshold Lite opens a door from your own tileset. Mark a door event that uses your tile with <threshold: door> and your existing stock door event (Turn Left, Turn Right, Turn Up) swings it inward: the leaf foreshortens, its edge shows in the door's own darkest colour, and the doorway is darkness made from the door's own colours. It opens MZ's own two tile wall doors as one door.

Smithereens Lite breaks a pot, jar or urn of your own art into curved sherds cut from its own pixels, through one plugin command. Posture Lite sits a character down, the seated pose built from the walking sheet the character already has.

They are cuts of the paid code, not rewrites. Every frame each Lite draws was compared byte for byte against the paid plugin's own code for the same input: 871 door frames, 1,296 pot breaks, 560 seated frames. Each Lite reads the same note tags as its full plugin and steps aside when the full plugin is installed, so upgrading changes nothing in your events or your saves.

Three raw JavaScript files, a Readme and the licence. Free for commercial and non commercial games. Written with AI assistance.
