Ajar is out for RPG Maker MZ. The furniture already in your tileset can now be searched, and it opens when it is: a drawer slides out toward the camera with its dark inside showing, a barrel's lid lifts and comes to rest askew, a sack's top sinks into a collar round a dark mouth, and on a bookshelf one book tips out of line while two lean. What the event gives rises out of the opening as its own icon. An empty search shakes out a little dust.

Searched pieces rest ajar. A drawer stays half out and a lid stays askew, so the player can see what they have already been through, and that record is saved with the game.

How it works: you give an event a furniture tile as its graphic and write <ajar: drawer> (or lid, sack, shelf) in its note. Ajar reads that tile's pixels once while the map fades in, finds the drawer fronts, the lid's rim, the sack's neck or the row of books, and cuts every frame of the opening from those pixels. Searching afterwards reads no pixels at all. Two-tile desks and tall bookcases open as one piece. The event's own commands stay yours, so Change Items, Show Text or any loot plugin work as they did.

It is the container sibling of Threshold, which opens doors, gates and hatches the same way. Hinged cupboard doors stay Threshold's.
