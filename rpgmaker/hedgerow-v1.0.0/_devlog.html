Hedgerow is out: the animals that walk your RPG Maker MZ maps. Rabbits on the green, squirrels at the foot of the trees, mice running the base of a wall to their holes, lizards basking on the rocks, crabs on the sand, a cat asleep by the fire, and after dark hedgehogs, foxes, badgers and snails. $12.99 on itch.

Why it exists. Our Vivarium fills a map with what flies and swims: butterflies, dragonflies, fish, frogs, fireflies and bats. Its own Readme says what it leaves empty: walls, trees and rocks get no land life, and inside maps stay empty. Hedgerow lives exactly there, so the two plugins together cover the air, the water and the ground, and they read day and night the same way.

Placed by your own tiles. When a map loads, Hedgerow reads it once. A walkable tile with a wall, a hedge or a fence above it is where mice and rats run, each with a hole drawn into the wall. A tile under a tree is a squirrel's home. A tile beside a rock is where a lizard basks; sand within two tiles of water is where crabs live; a tile beside a fireplace is where the cat sleeps. MZ's own trees, boulders, sand and fireplaces need no setup at all, and a custom tileset needs one tileset note. An outside map with enough houses on it becomes a village as well, so the same Outside tileset gives you rabbits in a field and cats and rats in a town.

Each one flees its own way. A rabbit sits up, thumps and bolts in a zig zag with its white tail showing. A squirrel runs home and climbs up behind the leaves, and comes back down when you have gone. A hedgehog rolls into a ball. A mouse runs along the wall into its hole and peeks out later. A crab raises its claws, backs off sideways and digs in. A cat sits up, watches you, and walks away. 44 coats in 13 families, every one drawn in code in the four facings a character has. No sprite sheets ship.

How it was tested. Everything ran in the real MZ engine through our own harness, with no RPG Maker install. The suite covers the habitat reader, every family's calm loop and flight, day and night, rain, the save, and a zero allocation scan of every function that runs each frame; fourteen deliberate sabotages each turned their own check red. Two fresh reviewers who had not built it then went through every store image and wrote down 39 and 29 defects; the fixes reached the plugin itself: lizard tails that were clipped in every frame, a squirrel that climbed beside its tree instead of up it, and squirrels that a map with only a few trees often never got. An adversarial pass tried an all water map, a one tile map, a 200 by 200 map, a looping map, garbage parameters and the plugin enabled twice. A fresh install from the download into a blank project found MZ's own trees, boulders, sand and fireplace with no setup. On a full screen of animals the layer draws in 11 draw calls a frame with no texture uploads.

Pairs with Vivarium (the small life of the air and water), Timber (every tileset tree can be cut down) and Round-up (a herding minigame on your own map).

The code, including the drawing code, was written with AI assistance. The download is three raw, readable JavaScript files, a Readme and the licence.
