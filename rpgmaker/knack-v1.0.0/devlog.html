Knack is out: 12 work animations for RPG Maker MZ, made from your own walking sprites in all 4 facings.

Chop, mine, dig, hoe, water, fish, hammer, saw, sweep, reap, stir and gather, each with its own tool drawn in the sheet's own outline colour, and chips, sparks, clods, drops, dust or steam where the tool lands. Put <knack: axe> on a tree and the action button plays the swings, then the event's page runs, so it pairs with any gathering plugin. Put <knack worker: hammer> on an NPC and it works all day.

The frames are made from that sheet's own arms and colours, once, while the map opens, so there is no pose sheet to draw. Measured in the real engine with twelve workers on twelve jobs on one map: 10.3 draw calls a frame, against 10 with nobody working. Eight sample objects come with it: a tree, an ore rock, an anvil, a log, crops, a cauldron, a dirt mound and a stump.

Knack on itch.io, $9.99. The documentation is free to read first.
