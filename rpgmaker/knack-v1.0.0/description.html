Knack is an RPG Maker MZ plugin with 12 work animations, from chopping wood to fishing, made from your own walking sprites in all 4 facings. Three plugin files and 8 sample objects, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy.

These pictures use our own characters, our own objects and a yard painted in code. In your game the frames are made from your own sheets.

A gathering plugin handles the wood and the ore; what the worker does on screen is left to you. Until now that meant drawing mining and chopping frames for every character, or rebuilding your cast in one sprite creator. Knack makes those frames from the sheets you already have.

What you get

  • 12 jobs: chop, mine, dig, hoe, water, fish, hammer, saw, sweep, reap, stir and gather. Each is a different motion with its own tool, and each tool is drawn in the sheet's own outline colour.
  • Effects where the tool lands: chips, sparks, clods, drops, dust, flecks and steam. The rod casts a float on a line, waits, gets a bite and reels in.
  • One tag on a resource: <knack: axe> on a tree. The action button plays the swings, then the event's page runs, so it pairs with any gathering plugin or plain events.
  • One tag on an NPC: <knack worker: hammer>. It works all day, and stops when it walks or when you talk to it.
  • A plugin command for cutscenes and common events, on the player, an event or a follower.
  • Work regions: paint a region, name a job and a common event, and every tile of it can be worked.
  • 6 tool materials: iron, copper, gold, steel, crystal and obsidian, by a word in the tag, on every tool with metal in it.
  • 8 sample objects to work at: tree, ore rock, anvil, log, crops, cauldron, dirt mound and stump.

How it runs

Everything a map asks for is made once while it opens, so the first swing does not stall, and a swing reads no pixels. Measured in the real engine with twelve workers on twelve jobs on one map: 10.3 draw calls a frame, against 10 with nobody working, and no texture uploads. Keyboard, gamepad, mouse and touch all work, a worker's sound fades with distance, and a save taken mid swing loads back mid swing.

Compatibility: the question you actually have

Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: PostureCore.js, KnackCore.js, Knack.js. Any order works, and one PostureCore.js serves Knack, Hoist, Posture and Embrace together.

Core methods extended: 21 by saved-original prototype extension, none replaced. The full list is in the guide.

Tested in RPG Maker MZ 1.10.0 with Posture, Hoist, Stricken and Timber installed beside it. Untested: pixel movement, 8-frame walking plugins, VisuStella Events & Movement Core, and plugins that redraw a character's sprite themselves.

From the same studio: Timber, trees that shudder, notch and fall; with it installed, its chop swings Knack's axe. Posture, sit and sleep poses from your sheets. Hoist, pick up and throw. Creel, the fish your game catches, drawn from their own data.

Code written with AI assistance. Every character, object and ground in these pictures is our own and was made with AI assistance; every work frame and tool on them is drawn by the plugin from the character's walking sheet.
