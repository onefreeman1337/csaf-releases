Sapper 1.0 is out: a Minesweeper minigame for RPG Maker MZ played over one of your own pictures.

Name a picture and the plugin lays a minefield over it in turf, sand, snow or slate, each cell tinted from the picture underneath. Every cell the player clears uncovers that patch of the picture. Clear the whole field and the cover lifts away and the picture is shown whole.

No field ever needs a guess. The mines are laid after the first click, away from it, and laid again until a logic solver can finish the field from there. The first click never opens more than 60% of it. Three field sizes, one to nine lives, and mines drawn as iron or as the enemies of any troop in your database. Time, mines set off, flags and percent cleared go back into your variables, with a switch for a cleared field.

It was played in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the default parameters, at 1280 x 720, 1104 x 624 and 640 x 480, with odd pictures (1 x 1, 4000 x 3000, fully transparent) and a missing one.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. No sounds of its own (it plays MZ's own sound files) and no story text.
