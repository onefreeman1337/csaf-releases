Inscribe 1.0 is out: RPG Maker MZ's name screen, drawn as the object the name is written on.

Six rites: a guest register signed in ink, a name carved in stone, travel papers stamped by the office, a typed form, a sigil traced into a circle, and a headstone at night. Each has its own drawn keys (brass plates, stone tiles, rubber stamps, typewriter keys, wax seals, bone tiles) for pad, mouse and touch, and a keyboard types straight onto the object. On the sigil, every name traces its own mark.

It fills itself from your project: the register's earlier entries are your other actors, the form's occupation is the actor's class, the papers' origin is the map you are on. A Suggest key writes new names made from your own database. The same scene asks riddles and passwords, forgiving case, spacing, accents and a typo or two, and shows right or wrong on the object.

It replaces Name Input Processing by default and keeps the stock screen when you turn that off, in battle, and in Japanese projects. It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the default parameters, at 1280 x 720, 1104 x 624 and 640 x 480.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. No sounds of its own (it plays MZ's own sound files) and no story text.
