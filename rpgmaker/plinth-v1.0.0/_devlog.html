Plinth 1.0 is out: statues of your own characters and monsters for RPG Maker MZ.

Give an event a note such as <statue: actor 1, bronze, age 40> and, when the map loads, Plinth reads that actor's walking sprite and strikes it as a statue: the eyes blanked, the top edges lit, a surface for the material and weathering for its age, standing on a base with the name cut into it. Enemies work the same way from their battler images, so a dragon in your database is a dragon statue in your town square. There are seven materials: granite, marble, sandstone, bronze, gilt, obsidian and ice.

The same strike drives a Petrify command, which turns the player, a follower or any event to stone from the feet up on the map, and a state note that shows a battler in stone while the state holds.

The hard part was making stone read as stone rather than as a grey tint. We measured it: on seven stock subjects a flat tone scores between 1.6 and 3.7 on our surface measure and every material scores above 11, and the test suite fails if a material ever drops into the tint range. It was run in MZ's runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project, including a pass that loads a save made with Plinth after Plinth is switched off.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds and no story text.
