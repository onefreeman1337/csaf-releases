Stricken is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that shows your party's states on the map, struck into each member's own walking sprite with 43 generated looks. Two plugin files, no art to draw, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy.

These pictures use our own sprites and a ground painted in code; none of them ship. On your map the looks are struck from your own sheets.

MZ keeps a state on a party member after a fight, a poison swamp or a cursed chest, and shows nothing for it on the map. Players have asked for state icons over the map sprite since 2018. Stricken changes the sprite itself.

What you get

  • 43 looks: 28 ailments (poison, burn, frost, frozen solid, sleep, stun, curse, doom, petrify and more), 14 boons (regen, haste, shell, blessing, float) and a knocked-out look.
  • Struck from your own sprites, read from each frame's silhouette: flames rise off the head, an ice shell follows the outline. Any cell size, single and 8-character sheets.
  • The leader, every follower, and any event you tie to an actor with one note tag.
  • Database driven: <stricken: frost> in a state's note, or nothing at all: Poison, Sleep or Burning find their look by name.
  • Twelve animation steps per look, and a burst when a state lands or wears off.

How it runs

Each look is drawn once per sheet in memory, then the sprite only changes frame. Measured in the real engine: 9 draw calls a frame with five struck sprites on screen, the same 9 as the plugin with nothing to show, and no texture uploads while they animate. Battles are left to MZ and your battle plugins.

Compatibility: the question you actually have

Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: StrickenCore.js, Stricken.js. Any order works.

Core methods extended: 15 by saved-original prototype extension, none replaced. The full list is in the guide.

Nothing is clobbered, but a member with a look walks on a struck copy of their sheet, so plugins that also swap a character's bitmap or frame grid are untested with it. Tested with the stock scripts; not with VisuStella Events & Movement Core.
