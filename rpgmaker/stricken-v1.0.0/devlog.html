Stricken is out for RPG Maker MZ at $9.99: your party's states shown on the map, struck into each member's own walking sprite.

MZ keeps a state on a party member after a fight and shows nothing for it on the map, so a poisoned or cursed hero walks exactly like a healthy one. Stricken reads each frame's silhouette and draws the state into it: flames off the head, rime on the shoulders, an ice shell round the outline, Zs over a sleeper.

What is in it

  • 43 looks: 28 ailments, 14 boons and a knocked-out look, each animating in twelve steps.
  • The leader, every follower, and any event tied to an actor with a note tag.
  • A note tag on the state picks its look; states named Poison, Sleep or Burning find theirs by name.
  • A burst when a state lands or wears off on the map.

Where it stops

It is the map side only: battles are left to MZ and your battle plugins. It has not been run with VisuStella Events & Movement Core or with plugins that swap a character's frame grid. The guide lists every look and parameter.
