Rally Field is in. One plugin command makes the player, a follower or any event strike a combat pose on the map, in the direction it faces: 13 poses in all four facings, built from the character's own walking sheet. The duel in a cutscene, the guard who raises his spear at the gate, the hero who falls in the snow, with no pose art to draw. Four plugin files, and this page is where it ships first.

The GIF is engine frames of three CSAF test characters on a map, close up: a spearman and a swordsman duel left and right while a mage casts toward the camera, each beat captioned with the poses it shows. Each frame is held so the poses can be read. The courtyard was painted for the capture; in your game it is your own map.

  • The 13 poses: swing, thrust, missile (shoot or throw), skill, guard, damage, evade, victory, chant, spell, item, dying and dead, three drawings each.
  • All four facings. The left and right facings are the same drawings Rally Actors and Rally Foes fight with in battle, so a character looks the same on the map and in the fight. The down and up facings are drawn from the sheet's own down and up rows.
  • Play once, hold the last drawing, or loop, until a Stop Pose command. The event can wait for the pose to end.
  • The weapon is the character's own: an actor's equipped weapon, an event's <rally: spear gold> tag, 12 weapons and bare hands in six metals.
  • Tested on MZ 1.10.0: the poses of all 116 test characters with nothing cut at the cell edge and the weapon visible from the front and from behind, the real plugin command through MZ's own event interpreter, and a frame gate at 9 draw calls and 0 texture uploads.

New in the download: the folder 03-battle/rally-field (four plugin files, the Readme and the licence). Rally Actors is now version 1.0.2 and Rally Foes version 1.0.1: all three ship RallyCore 1.2.0, the core they share, which builds every actor and foe sheet exactly as before, byte for byte.
