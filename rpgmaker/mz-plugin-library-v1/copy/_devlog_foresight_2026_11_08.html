Foresight is in. It shows, above every enemy in an RPG Maker MZ battle, what that enemy is about to do, drawn from the skill it has already chosen. Three plugin files, and this page is where it ships first.

  • The plate: a blade, a spell's element, a heal, a buff, an affliction or a guard, in that element's colour, with the damage it will do to the party member it picked, as the range MZ will roll.
  • Five pips say how hard that is for that target. All five lit red means it would knock them out.
  • Point at an enemy and a card spells it out for each party member, with KO where a hit would be lethal.
  • Note tags for feints (a Mimic shows a Wait and bites), sealed plates and a charge ring. A Scan state cracks the feint and opens the seal.
  • The clash: when your attack meets the attack an enemy aimed at you, the two moves collide and the bigger hit breaks the other. A broken move costs nothing.
  • Works on day one with no note tags: MZ's own database gives 77 different plates. No image files: everything is drawn by the plugin.
