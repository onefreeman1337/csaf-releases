Every RPG Maker MZ map has the same problem. The innkeeper stands behind the counter all day, the guests stand at their tables, and the hero sleeps standing up next to the bed. Pose sheets fix it, but someone has to draw them for every character in the game.

Posture draws them for you. When a character first takes a pose, the plugin reads that character's own walking sheet and builds the pose from its pixels: sitting on a chair or bench, sitting on the floor, kneeling with the head bowed, reading a book, lying down, collapsed, waving and cheering. Nothing is resampled, so every pose keeps the sheet's own outline and colours.

Sleep got the most work. MZ's beds stand upright with the pillow at the top, so a Posture sleeper lies in one: two tiles tall, head on a white pillow, eyes closed, the sheet turned down under the chin and a quilt in the colour of the character's clothes. Facing left or right turns the head on the pillow, and the quilt rises a pixel on each breath.

We tested every pose on all 96 stock MZ characters as well as our own sheets in three other styles. Every pose derives, keeps the character's colours and stays inside its cell. Eyes closed on 77 of the 96 stock sleepers; on the rest the face is left as drawn. The plugin extends eight engine methods by saved original prototype extension and replaces none.

Set a pose with an event note, a page comment, <pose: auto> on chair and bed regions, two plugin commands or one line of script. Poses are saved with the game.
