Goose 1.0 is out: party board games for RPG Maker MZ, laid out and drawn by the plugin.

One plugin command drops a dice board into your game. The party leader races up to three rivals along a trail of spaces, choosing at every fork between a shortcut full of hazards and a scenic route full of coins. Coins, losses, chests, warps, gusts, boosts, snares, roll-again spaces, and event spaces that run your own common events.

Every board is generated from a seed and drawn in code across six landscapes, from a meadow to a volcano, with the party's faces riding on the pawns. Rivals come in three tempers that genuinely play differently, measured over 9,000 games. Results go back to your variables, a switch and the party's gold.

It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, whole games played through the real plugin command.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
