Sentiment 1.0 is out: emotion battles for RPG Maker MZ, drawn over your own art.

Every battler gets a feeling, happy, sad or angry, with three rungs each. The feelings play rock paper scissors: happy beats angry, angry beats sad, sad beats happy, for x1.5 or x0.5 damage, and every rung bends the stats further.

The feeling is drawn on top of the portraits and battlers you already have: a wash that reads the picture under it, an outline scribbled around the face or the creature's own silhouette, and the feeling's marks. Battle portraits carry a small emotion wheel with the rung's name, so a player reads the whole party at a glance. Side-view actors are treated on every cell of the sheet.

Notetags push a feeling from any skill or item, cap a battler, or send an enemy in already angry; two plugin commands set and calm feelings from events. It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the stock database.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
