Fetch is out: mirror-match bosses for RPG Maker MZ. When the fight starts, your party is copied into real enemies, drawn from each hero's own battler sheet in one of ten looks.

People have asked for this on the RPG Maker forums since VX Ace. The usual answer is to copy an actor into the enemy database by hand, which freezes it the day you type it. A twin here is read from the live hero when the battle begins: level, stats, equipment, traits and learned skills.

The part that took the most care is the choosing. MZ's enemies pick when the turn opens. A twin picks again at the moment it moves, so a healer can answer the ally that just dropped. It also keeps a record of what its hero has really been used for, and leans toward it. In one measured run of 400 choices, with the history set by hand to 60 casts, the Mage's twin went from 120 Fire II casts to 310. It still uses the rest of its kit.

Two things the testing changed. A fresh review of the first gallery tore apart looks that lost the hero's face, so Mirror, Ink, Ember and Crimson were redrawn. And at full HP a four on four ran about 20 turns at level 12, so Max HP defaults to 60%. Set it to 100% for an exact copy. The guide has the measured table.

It is two plugin files, and it works in side view and front view, turn-based and Time Progress. VisuStella Battle Core is not tested, because we have not run the two together, and the guide says so. Please try one fight before relying on that pair.
