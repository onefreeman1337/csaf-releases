Leadlight 1.0 is out: a brick-breaker minigame for RPG Maker MZ where the bricks are a stained-glass pane cut from one of your own pictures.

Name a picture in your project and the plugin makes the glass from it: every piece is the picture itself, coloured as glass, set in lead. Bounce the ball off the lead bar and break the pane piece by piece, and the real picture shows through. Break the target share and the rest of the pane falls away, and the picture is shown whole in a gilt frame. The busiest parts of the picture make the toughest glass, and they crack before they break.

Four panes (leaded, cathedral, frost, brick), three power-ups (a wider bar, two more balls, a slower ball), and the results go back into your game: seconds taken, balls lost and percent broken into variables, and a switch for a cleared pane. One plugin command starts it; the event waits until it is over.

It was played in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project with the default parameters, and tested at 1280 x 720, 1104 x 624 and 640 x 480, with odd pictures (1 x 1, 4000 x 3000, fully transparent) and a missing picture. Nothing is loaded but your picture.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. No sounds of its own (it plays MZ's own sound files) and no story text.
