Over the Shoulder is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that shows your party from behind in battle, from your own walk sheets, with 3 stagings and 50 floors. Two plugin files, no art to draw, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy.

These pictures use our own sprites, enemies and backdrops; none of them ship. Your figures come from your own sheets.

MZ can show your party from the side, or not at all. Back-view battles have been asked for since 2016, and the answer was always the same: draw every actor a new sheet. Every walk sheet already holds a back view, and this plugin uses it.

What you get

  • Your party from behind, from each actor's own sheet: they walk in, step up, lunge on an attack, recoil on a hit, and collapse when they fall.
  • Three stagings: a line, a rank into the distance, and a duel on raised discs with enemy HP plates.
  • 50 floors laid to the horizon and tinted toward your battleback. Auto picks one from the file name.
  • Eight themes for the plates, commands and battle messages, with your faces in the medallions.
  • A camera that follows the action, and a party that turns round at victory.

What it leaves alone

Skills, states, damage formulas, troops and your enemy database stay MZ's, in a side-view or front-view project. There is no catching or type chart: the duel is a layout. It draws no weapons. A rough back row stays rough at three times the size, and <ots-back: Name> takes hand-drawn art.

Compatibility: the question you actually have

Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: OverTheShoulderCore.js, OverTheShoulder.js. Any order works.

Core methods extended: 30 by saved-original prototype extension, none replaced. The full list is in the guide.

Nothing is clobbered, but while a battle is staged the plugin draws the actors itself, so a plugin that changes MZ's side-view actors will not show. It is tested with the stock scripts only.

It defines 4 methods its classes only inherited: if another plugin redefines one of those, load Over the Shoulder after it.

Not tested with VisuStella Battle Core, which rewrites the same battle sprites. While that plugin is on, Over the Shoulder stands down and says so in the console; one setting runs it anyway, at your own risk.

From the same studio: Stature, taller side-view battlers from your sheets. Posture, stances on the map. Fray, your cast worn by damage. Fetch, your party as a boss. The creatures on one plate are Pocketfolk 01.
