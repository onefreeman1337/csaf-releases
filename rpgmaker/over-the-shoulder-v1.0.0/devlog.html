Over the Shoulder is out for RPG Maker MZ at $9.99: back-view battles, using the up-facing row of the walk sheets you already have.

People have been asking for rear-view battles in RPG Maker for about ten years. The usual answer is a positioning plugin plus a new back sheet drawn for every actor. Every walk sheet already has a back view, so this plugin strikes that row up into a battler and restages MZ's own battle behind the party.

What is in it

  • Three stagings: a line, a rank into the distance, and a duel on raised discs.
  • 50 floors laid to the horizon, tinted toward your battleback's colours, or your own battleback laid flat.
  • Eight themes for the status plates and battle windows, with your own faces in the medallions.
  • A camera that drifts toward whoever acts, and a party that turns round to face the player at victory.

What it does not do

It leaves skills, states, formulas, troops and your enemy database alone. It draws no weapons. It is not a monster-catching system: the duel is a layout. It has not been run with VisuStella Battle Core, so while that plugin is on it stands down and says so in the console, and one setting runs it anyway at your own risk.
