Pelt 1.0 is out: team dodgeball for RPG Maker MZ.

Call one plugin command and your party plays a troop, or a team of rival actors, on a court the plugin draws. The players are the side-view battler sheets your project already has, so your party is the team on day one: guard is the catch stance, swing is the throw, evade is the dodge. Three hits and a player is out; catch a throw and the thrower is out and one of your own players comes back.

Each player's special throws come from its own skills. The element decides the look and the way the skill reaches its target decides the flight: a straight shot, a rush, a rising spike or a high drop that comes down from above and cannot be caught, a skim along the floor you have to jump, a burst, or a drain that heals the thrower. Every path was measured in play to land at least as often as a plain throw from the same distance.

We tuned four computer strengths with scripted players over 240 matches each. A newcomer wins most matches against Beginner, and mashing the buttons wins under half against Beginner and less at every strength above it. The plugin draws five courts, the ball, the sparks, the HUD and the lettering, and it was run in MZ's runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds and no story text.
