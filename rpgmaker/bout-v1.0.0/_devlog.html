Bout 1.0 is out: a one-on-one fighting scene for RPG Maker MZ.

Call one plugin command and two of your actors fight side-on, best of three rounds against the clock. They fight with the side-view battler sheets your project already has, so every actor is a fighter on day one. Each fighter's specials come from its own skills: the element decides the look and the way the skill reaches its target decides the move, from a projectile to a rush, a rising strike, a wave along the ground, a rain, a burst or a draining tether.

It plays like the genre: a low attack only a crouching guard stops, a jump-in only a standing guard stops, a throw that beats any guard but loses to any attack, and frame data per move. We tuned four AI strengths with scripted players over 120 matches each. A newcomer wins more than half their matches against Beginner, and a competent player wins about a third against Hard.

The plugin draws the arenas, the bars, the lettering, the sparks and the specials. It was run in MZ's runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds and no story text.
