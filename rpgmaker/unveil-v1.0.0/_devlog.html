Unveil 1.0 is out: a Qix-style minigame for RPG Maker MZ where the prize is one of your own pictures.

One plugin command puts a picture from img/pictures under a veil. The player walks the edge of the revealed land and, holding OK, cuts a line into the veil; close the line on revealed land and every part of the veil with no creature in it falls away. A creature touching the line, or a spark on the edge touching the player, costs a life. Reveal the target share and the whole picture is shown.

Three veils are drawn in code: silk with damask folds, frost with ice ferns, and black lacquer with gold waves. Target, lives, creatures and sparks are arguments of the command, and each setting was played 200 times by a scripted player to measure how hard it is. Results go back to a variable and a switch.

It was tested in MZ's own runtime from the exact zip you download, with held keys, mouse and touch-drag input.

Code and graphics were made with generative AI. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.
