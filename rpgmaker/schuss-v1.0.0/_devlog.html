Schuss 1.0 is out: SkiFree style downhill minigames for RPG Maker MZ, on a course the plugin lays out and draws.

Call one plugin command and the screen scrolls down a seeded course. Ski, snowboard or sled down a slope of pines, rocks, moguls, ice and ramps; ride a mine cart that can only change rails at a switch; or pole a raft down a river with a current. Race the clock through gates, collect coins that can be your items, or stay ahead of a yeti, a boulder, a flood wave or one of your own enemy images. The rider is your party leader, using the walking picture your project already has.

Every course is laid out around a hidden safe line and ridden once by the plugin's own autopilot before the player sees it. Over 7,500 generated courses that check found none that could not be finished cleanly, while a rider who never steers failed 95.7% of them. The same seed is the same course, so best times are kept per course.

The plugin draws everything on screen: 42 obstacle forms, the gates, coins, pursuers, vehicles, tracks, spray and the lettering. It was run in MZ's runtime from the exact zip you download, in a blank project, and the scrolling slope, the tracks and the clock upload almost nothing per frame.

Made with AI: the code and the images were produced with generative AI tools. It has no sounds of its own (it plays the OK and Buzzer sounds from your database) and no story text.
