Portcullis — Dungeon Mechanisms: Doors, Chests, Levers, Traps is out. 123 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack ships 24 mechanisms as whole lifecycles rather than as single cues. 123 state files were resolved and 186 within-mechanism pairs were compared; 22 states are loopable and every one of them carries real loop points, read out of the rendered file rather than out of the manifest. No state pair sits closer than 9% in brightness or 18% in length, which is what stops a lifecycle being five names for one gesture.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 1138 checks, 0 failed. Loudness sits inside 1.07 dB across the pack, so nothing jumps when you swap one cue for another. 9 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
