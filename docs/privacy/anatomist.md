# ANATOMIST for Android — Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Core Systems Asset Factory · **App:** ANATOMIST (package `com.coresystemsassetfactory.anatomist`) · **Effective:** 2026-08-30

## Summary

ANATOMIST does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. None.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no user accounts and no sign in.
- **No permissions.** The app requests no Android permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any device identifiers.
- **No network use.** The game runs entirely offline from files packaged inside the app. The app makes no network requests of its own.
- **External link.** The page inside the app contains one link to the game's store page on itch.io. Tapping it opens your device's web browser; from that point itch.io's own privacy policy applies (https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Children.** Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.
- **Local storage.** The app may keep small settings in the app's own private storage on your device only. This never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
