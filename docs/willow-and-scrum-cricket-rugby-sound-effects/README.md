# Willow and Scrum: Cricket and Rugby Sound Effects in Three Packs (Cricket, Rugby and the Ground)

**319 sound effects in 3 kits, WAV and OGG, for any game engine: Cricket (106 sounds, the bat and the ball), Rugby (107 sounds, the kick and the tackle) and Ground (106 sounds, the ground both games share).**

The two big field sports, each in its own ground, and the ground they share: the bat and the ball of cricket, the kick and the tackle of rugby, and the crowds, the public address, the scoreboard, the sirens and the ground staff's machines both games need. Every pack is built as structure a game wires to its systems: grids that answer two numbers the game already holds, ladders by power, weight and crowd excitement, and lifecycles with a seamless loop to hold. Each pack is complete in its own folder, with its own Readme, licence, naming manifest and proof, so you can copy one folder into a project and leave the others.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Every sound is synthesised from code: no samples, no recordings, no third party audio, no generative audio model. Nothing here is musical.

**Cricket**: Cricket is a summer ground in the open: a far slapback off the pavilion and the stands and a short dry tail, willow on a hard leather ball, spikes on a dry strip, mown grass. The contact grid is the heart of it: each thing the ball can meet owns its own material (the middle of the blade rings, the edge is a thin nick, the pad is canvas and foam, the glove is leather over fingers, the helmet is a steel grille on a shell, the stumps are ash and the bails fly), and the bowler's pace moves the same contact harder and longer, so a game picks the file from what the ball hit and how fast it came. The shot and the pitching are ladders; the delivery and the wicket are lifecycles.

**Rugby**: Rugby is a winter ground with stands on all four sides: close concrete and a fuller, darker tail than the cricket field, a pimpled leather ball with a hollow bladder, studs in soft turf, bodies in heavy cotton. The contact grid puts every thing the ball meets on a dry day and a wet one: the wet take darkens the same contact, lengthens it as the ball skids and adds mud, so a game swaps one word when the weather turns. The place kick and the tackle are ladders; the scrum and the lineout are lifecycles.

**Ground**: Ground is a big open bowl: long slapbacks off the stands across the field and a wide tail, concrete steps, tin and plastic, diesel and two stroke engines, wet tarpaulin. A crowd here is band noise and hands, never a voice: a Test match afternoon is sparse, polite and dark with long tails, a T20 night is dense and bright with plastic clappers, and a rugby crowd is deep and chest heavy with feet on the stand. The two crowd ladders are seamless loops made to crossfade by one excitement number; the reaction grid puts every reaction in all three crowds under one name, so a game swaps one word to move from a Test match to a night game. The covers, the roller and the mower are lifecycles.
