# Spirit Beasts: Xianxia Beast Voices and Sound Effects in Three Packs

**530 sound effects in 3 kits, WAV and OGG, for any game engine: The Four Symbols (150 sounds: the four guardian beasts and the centre: the azure dragon, the white tiger, the vermilion bird, the black tortoise and the yellow dragon), The Auspicious Beasts (210 sounds: seven lucky beasts for companions and mounts: the qilin, the pixiu, the nine tailed fox, the bai ze, the spirit crane, the jade rabbit and the dragon carp) and Demon Beasts and the Beast Bond (170 sounds: five demon beasts (the demon wolf, the giant centipede, the serpent demon, the nian and the taotie) and the sounds of binding a spirit beast).**

17 beasts of a xianxia or wuxia world in three packs, each at three sizes (hatchling, grown and sovereign), for a cultivation game, a beast tamer, a sect adventure or a boss fight: the four guardian symbols, the auspicious beasts and the demon beasts, plus the sounds of binding a spirit beast. Every beast is its own voice and every beast ships the same six states at every size, so one creature script drives all of them. They are built to sit beside the studio's Jianghu wuxia and xianxia sound effects. Each pack is complete in its own folder, with its own Readme, licence, naming manifest and proof, so you can copy one folder into a project and leave the others.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Every sound is synthesised from code: no samples, no recordings, no third party audio, no generative audio model. Nothing here is musical.

**The Four Symbols**: The Four Symbols gives every guardian one element you hear in every strike: rolling thunder under the azure dragon, a ring of struck metal under the white tiger, flame under the vermilion bird, stone on stone and a serpent's hiss under the black tortoise, and a bronze gong bloom under the yellow dragon. Every beast is its own throat (its own pitch, throat size, roughness and breath), every beast ships the same states in the same order, and every beast comes at three sizes, so one script drives a hatchling companion, a grown guardian and a sovereign boss by changing two words in the file name.

**The Auspicious Beasts**: The Auspicious Beasts are the light and lucky side of the spirit world, and each one carries an element you hear in every strike: a small high bell under the qilin, coins and jade clinking in the pixiu's jaws, fox fire flickering around the nine tailed fox, a low bronze bell under the calm bai ze, broad wing beats under the spirit crane, soft pestle thumps under the jade rabbit, and water around the dragon carp. Every beast is its own throat (its own pitch, throat size, roughness and breath), every beast ships the same states in the same order, and every beast comes at three sizes, so one script drives a hatchling pet, a grown companion and a sovereign mount by changing two words in the file name.

**Demon Beasts and the Beast Bond**: Demon Beasts gives every demon one element you hear in every strike: a low snarling hiss under the demon wolf, clicking chitin legs under the giant centipede's dry chitter, a long bright hiss around the serpent demon's low voice, firecracker crackle around the nian's roar, and wet gulping bubbles in the taotie's throat. Every beast is its own throat, ships the same states in the same order and comes at three sizes, so one script drives a hatchling pest, a grown demon and a sovereign boss. The beast bond set is struck jade, bronze and moving air with no voice in it: a seal pressed, qi swelling and drawing back, a pill crunched, one bright strike as a bond grows, a gong bloom under a rising wind for a realm advance, and jade cracking when a bond breaks.
