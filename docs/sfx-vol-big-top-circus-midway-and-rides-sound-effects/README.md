# Big Top: Circus, Carnival Midway and Amusement Park Ride Sound Effects in Three Packs (Circus, Midway and Rides)

**333 sound effects in 3 kits, WAV and OGG, for any game engine: Circus (121 sounds, the ring under the big top), Midway (107 sounds, the carnival midway between the booths) and Rides (105 sounds, the rides themselves).**

Three parts of the fairground, each in its own room: the circus tent and its acts, the carnival midway and its booths, and the amusement park rides. Every act and every ride ships twice under one name, in a working daylight register and in an abandoned one (rusted, unpowered, wind driven, failing), so a bright park and a horror park are one download. Each pack is complete in its own folder, with its own Readme, licence, naming manifest and proof, so you can copy one folder into a project and leave the others.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Every sound is synthesised from code: no samples, no recordings, no third party audio, no generative audio model. Nothing here is musical.

## The kits

| Folder | Kit | Sounds | Loops |

| `Circus/` | Big Top Circus: Circus Act and Big Top Sound Effects | 121 | 5 |
| `Midway/` | Big Top Midway: Carnival Booth Game and Stand Sound Effects | 107 | 8 |
| `Rides/` | Big Top Rides: Amusement Park Ride Sound Effects | 105 | 9 |
| | **Total** | **333** | **22** |

**Circus**: Circus is one canvas big top: wide but soft, the canvas swallowing the highs, sawdust on packed earth, timber bleachers and steel rigging. Every act is a grid cell twice under one name: a DAYLIGHT take of the act working and an ABANDONED take of the same object left to rust in a torn tent (an empty trapeze squealing on its chains, a slack wire in the wind, a cannon that fizzles and thunks, a cage door swinging on a rusted hinge, a unicycle wheel turning in a draught, a spotlight that buzzes, flickers and pops), so a bright show and a horror circus are one download. The drum roll and the applause are ladders; the curtain and the clown car are lifecycles.

**Midway**: Midway is an open air aisle between two rows of timber booths: a short slapback off the booth fronts and almost no tail, painted plywood, canvas, tin targets, glass bottles and packed earth. Each booth game is a grid row with a HIT (the body the game is about: the ring on a bottle neck, the bottles going down, the tin target, the balloon) and a MISS (what the throw hits instead), so a game picks the file from the outcome it already knows. The striker and the tickets are ladders; the dunk tank, the shutter and the four food and game machines are lifecycles.

**Rides**: Rides is an open park under a big sky: one far slapback off the buildings and a long thin tail, steel track on steel columns, chains, hydraulic rams and electric motors. Every ride is a grid cell twice under one name: RUNNING is the machine powered and doing its job, ABANDONED is the same steel with the power off (a carousel bearing squealing as the wind turns the platform, a dead wheel's gondolas swinging, a loose cable slapping the drop tower, swing chains clinking, one bumper car nudged into the rail, a teacup rocking on its pivot, a log knocking in a dry trough), so a bright park and a horror park are one download. The coaster track is a ladder; the coaster and the safety gate are lifecycles.
