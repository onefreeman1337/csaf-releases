# BONEFOLD

A necromancer action game by Core Systems Asset Factory. Whatever you kill gets up as exactly what it was, keeps its fight, and
carries its kind's one gift into the world. Three areas, twelve rooms, three bosses, thirteen kinds to raise.

## Play

- **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run `BONEFOLD.exe`. No install, no account, no internet needed.
- Progress (which areas you have reached and THE OSSUARY of kinds you have raised) saves on its own.

## Controls

| action | keyboard + mouse | gamepad | touch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| move | WASD or arrows | left stick or d-pad | drag the left half |
| bone bolt (hold to keep firing) | J or left click | RT | BOLT |
| aim | the mouse | right stick | the nearest foe ahead |
| step (nothing can touch you) | SHIFT | RB | STEP |
| raise a body (hold over it) | E or K | A | RAISE |
| SEND the horde where you aim (hold) | SPACE or right click | LT | SEND |
| HOLD: plant the horde / call it back | Q or H | X | HOLD |
| let the nearest raised body fall | X or R | B | FALL |
| pause | ESC or P | Start | the pause button |
| the ossuary | TAB or B | Back | from the pause menu |

## The rules

- **Raise.** A foe you fell leaves its body. Hold RAISE over it and it gets up as its own kind, on your side.
- **Bones.** Every kind costs bones to keep raised (a crow 1, a cultist 2, a troglodyte 3). The pips at the top are your cap: 6 on the
  moor, 8 in the warrens, 10 in the keep. LET FALL gives a body's bones back.
- **Kinds have gifts.** Crows and bats FLY: they reach levers and warding bells across water and chasms. Censer bearers and kobold
  shamans BURN brambles, webs and hangings. Troglodytes, knights and thralls SMASH cracked walls. Rats SQUEEZE through grates.
- **SEND** at a lever, a bell, a bramble or a crack: a body that can answer it goes and does it. SEND at open ground and the horde
  fights there.
- **HOLD** plants the horde where you stand, and a held body stands its ground. A pressure plate holds its gate open while the horde
  on it weighs enough (the plate shows weight / need). Every plate gate has a WINCH on its far side: stand at it to lift the gate again.
- **Bosses** sleep until you come near. The Hierophant is warded while a warding bell stands; the Stone Warden shrugs off your bolts
  but not your dead; the Crowned Wight is warded while the reliquary plate is light.
- **Dying** sends you back to the start of the room with the horde you walked in with.
