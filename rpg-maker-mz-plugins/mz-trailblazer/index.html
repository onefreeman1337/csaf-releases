Trailblazer gives the player three things to do to your own RPG Maker MZ map, in three parts (8 plugin files): bomb walls and rocks open, cut tall grass and brush, and hop ledges, fences and gaps. Three parts, 8 raw JavaScript plugin files, no image files.

The cracks, breaches, rubble, clippings, stubble, shadows and dust are drawn from the tiles already on your map; the bomb, its fireball and the hop guide are drawn in their own colours, sized to your tiles. Each part works alone or with the others; all three were tested together on RPG Maker MZ 1.10.0.
