Vista is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws 240 kinds of illustrated battle background from your map's terrain, time and weather, with no image files. Use it in unlimited games, commercial or free, and keep 100% of your revenue. The arena is generated at whatever size the engine asks for, so it is as sharp at 1920x1080 as at 816x624.

What is inside 8 biomes · 5 hours · 6 weathers · 44 motifs · 4 plugin files · raw, readable JavaScript 8 biomes: forest, coast, desert, cave, ruin, marsh, tundra and ashland, each with its own ground, and real water for coast and marsh with a specular path from the sun or moon. 5 hours: dawn, day, dusk, night and storm. 6 weathers: clear, rain, snow, fog, embers, and drip for underground.

44 silhouette motifs across six families: trees, rock, ruins, flora, structures and bone. The 8 biomes, each drawn from nothing. The same place across the 5 hours. One seed, one biome. It follows your game Terrain tags: fill in one table and every region of your world fights differently, with no per map work. Map notetags: <vistaBiome:cave>, <vistaHour:night>, <vistaWeather:fog>.

Variables: point it at your day and night clock and the arenas follow it.
