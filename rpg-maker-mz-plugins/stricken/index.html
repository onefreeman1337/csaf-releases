Stricken is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that shows your party's states on the map, struck into each member's own walking sprite with 43 generated looks. Two plugin files, no art to draw, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy. These pictures use our own sprites and a ground painted in code; none of them ship. On your map the looks are struck from your own sheets.

MZ keeps a state on a party member after a fight, a poison swamp or a cursed chest, and shows nothing for it on the map. Players have asked for state icons over the map sprite since 2018. Stricken changes the sprite itself. What you get 43 looks: 28 ailments (poison, burn, frost, frozen solid, sleep, stun, curse, doom, petrify and more), 14 boons (regen, haste, shell, blessing, float) and a knocked-out look.

Struck from your own sprites, read from each frame's silhouette: flames rise off the head, an ice shell follows the outline. Any cell size, single and 8-character sheets. The leader, every follower, and any event you tie to an actor with one note tag. Database driven: <stricken: frost> in a state's note, or nothing at all: Poison, Sleep or Burning find their look by name.
