Tray - Packing Puzzle Minigame in Inlaid Wood for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A packing puzzle for your RPG, generated and drawn in inlaid wood. A shaped tray sits on the table and its pieces wait in a box beside it, turned every which way. The player picks them up, turns and flips them, and fits every one back in. When the last piece goes home, the tray is a finished marquetry panel and the result goes back to your events. Every puzzle is generated, and every one can be solved.

The tray (a rectangle, a cross, a diamond, a round, a notched or an arch) is carved into pieces of three to six squares, all different even turned over, so the pieces always go back. A built-in solver grades each puzzle by how many different ways the tray can be filled, and the generator keeps the tightest of several carvings for the difficulty you ask for: 4 or 5 pieces on easy, 6 or 7 on normal, 8 to 10 on hard.

Every piece is marquetry. Each is cut from its own wood (walnut, maple, cherry, oak, ebony, ash, rosewood, yew, olive, holly) with that wood's grain and figure, carries an inlaid motif in brass, mother-of-pearl, holly or ebony, and has a line of stringing run inside its edge. Grain and inlay turn with the piece as they would on a real one.
