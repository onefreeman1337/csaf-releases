Tray is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that adds a packing puzzle minigame: 6 tray shapes cut into 4 to 10 marquetry pieces, and every puzzle can be solved. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. The player picks the pieces up, turns and flips them, and fits every one back into the tray. When the last piece goes home, the tray is a finished marquetry panel and the result goes back to your events.

What is inside 3 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 6 tray shapes · 3 difficulties · 10 woods · 4 inlay materials · source included Generated puzzles: each tray is carved into pieces of three to six squares, all different even turned over, so the pieces always go back. A built in solver grades every carving: 4 or 5 pieces on easy, 6 or 7 on normal, 8 to 10 on hard.

Pieces in walnut, maple, cherry, oak, ebony, ash, rosewood, yew, olive and holly, each with its own grain, an inlaid motif in brass, mother of pearl, holly or ebony, and stringing inside the edge. Turn, Flip, Hint and Leave, with keyboard, gamepad, mouse and touch. A hint places one more piece that keeps every placed piece, or lifts the one that cannot stay.
