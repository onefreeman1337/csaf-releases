Three free RPG Maker MZ plugins that work from your own art. Your Own Art Sampler is three free plugins for RPG Maker MZ (1.x core scripts). Threshold Lite swings open a door tile from your own tileset when its stock door event turns. Smithereens Lite bursts a pot, jar or urn of your own art into curved sherds cut from its own pixels.

Posture Lite sits a character down on a chair or bench, the seated pose made from the walking sheet that character already has. No sprite sheets to draw, no images to install. Free to use in commercial and non commercial games. Each one is a single mode of a paid plugin from the same studio, cut from its code: one door kind of Threshold's eight, one material of Smithereens' seven, one pose of Posture's nine.
