Break the glass, win the picture. Leadlight is a brick-breaker minigame for RPG Maker MZ. Name any picture in your project and the plugin cuts a stained-glass pane from that picture: every piece of glass is the picture itself, coloured as glass would colour it and set in lead. Keep the ball in play with the lead bar, break the pane piece by piece, and the true picture shows through.

Break the target share and the rest of the pane falls away, and the picture is shown whole in a gilt frame. Nothing is loaded but your picture.

The glass, the lead, the bar, the ball, the shards and the frame are all drawn by the plugin, so a CG, a portrait, a map screenshot or a title image becomes a minigame with one plugin command. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: LeadlightCore.js — Leadlight 1/3 - the rules: a brick-breaker over a pane read from your picture. No engine code. LeadlightDraw.js — Leadlight 2/3 - the look: stained-glass panes generated from your picture, shards, the lead bar, the window frame. No engine code.
