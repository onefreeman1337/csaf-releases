Leadlight is a brick breaker minigame plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 4 stained glass panes: the player breaks one cut from your own picture to reveal it. Royalty free, and the only art it needs is the picture you name.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 4 pane styles · 3 powers · 1 plugin command · RPG Maker MZ · source included Name any picture in img/pictures (a CG, a portrait, a map screenshot, a title image) and the plugin cuts a pane of glass from it, every piece set in lead. Four panes: Leaded, Cathedral, Frost and Brick. Leave it on auto and the seed picks one. The picture sets how tough the glass is.

The busiest parts (an edge, a face, a line of text) take two or three hits and show a crack first. Break the target share (85 percent unless you change it) and the rest of the pane falls away, and the picture is shown whole in a gilt frame. About one piece in ten drops a power: Wide, Multi or Slow. Seconds taken, balls lost and percent broken go into your variables, and a switch says whether the pane was cleared.

Keyboard, mouse and touch. A seed gives the same pane, powers and serves every time.
