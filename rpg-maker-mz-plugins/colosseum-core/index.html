Colosseum Core is a 3 file tournament plugin for RPG Maker MZ: seeded brackets, rivals that level and remember you, weight classes, seasons and belts. Use it in unlimited games, commercial or free, with no royalties. What is inside 3 plugin files · 9 plugin commands · 18 parameters · RPG Maker MZ · raw JavaScript A tournament engine, a championship board and a season ledger.

The two scenes are optional and the engine is required. Brackets of 4, 8 or 16, seeded by rating, with byes for the top seeds when the field runs short. Rivals with a career record, a form guide and a memory of every result against you. Weight classes, growing purses and belts whose lineage is kept. A visual struct editor for tournaments, rivals and belts, and a Readme with a five step setup.

Rivals that train between seasons Each rival trains by temperament: steady grinds at its own pace, hungry chases your level, and volatile swings but never collapses. Their gained levels change the fight you have, so the troop you meet in round two is stronger than its database entry.
