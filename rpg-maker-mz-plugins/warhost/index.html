Warhost is an army battle plugin for RPG Maker MZ and MV: up to 240 drawn figures a side, 4 formations and 5 painted fields, with no image files to import. Royalty free. Two hosts of regiments meet on a painted field. The player picks a formation, then the battle plays out soldier by soldier: volleys arc overhead, spells burst, cavalry charges, regiments break and run.

A war record closes it, and the survivors go back into your variables. Call one plugin command from any event and a new project has a war. What is inside 5 troop types · 4 formations · 5 painted fields · 15 ordinaries and 13 charges · 3 plugin files · 1 plugin command · source included Soldiers, drawn: foot, pikes, archers, horse and mages, in each army's colours.

Bigger armies are carried at several soldiers per figure, and every number you get back is in soldiers Formations: line breaks a loose screen, a wedge breaks a line, a square stops a wedge, and a loose screen outlasts a square under arrows.
