Its title or description names RPG Maker MV and MZ.

Army battles for RPG Maker MZ. Two hosts of regiments meet on a painted field. Every soldier is drawn: foot, pikes, archers, horse and mages, in each army's colours. Every regiment carries a banner whose arms are generated from its name. The player picks a formation, then the battle plays out soldier by soldier: volleys arc overhead, spells burst, cavalry charges, regiments break and run.

A war record closes it, and the survivors go back into your variables. Nothing to draw, nothing to import. No sprite sheets, no battlebacks, no image files at all.

Turn it on, call one plugin command from any event, and a new project has a war. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, and against RPG Maker MV 1.6.1 with the bridge file, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
