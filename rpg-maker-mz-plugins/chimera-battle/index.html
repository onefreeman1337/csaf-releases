Chimera Battle is a creature battle plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 18 editable types, moves with PP, status effects and an AI that reads the matchup. It works inside MZ's own battle system, and a skill you have not tagged is left alone. Use it in unlimited commercial or free games with no royalties.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 18 types · 6 statuses · stat stages from minus 6 to plus 6 · 4 AI difficulties · RPG Maker MZ · raw source A type chart the player can see. Same type attack bonus, dual types and true immunities, with a live preview of the multiplier while the player picks a target. Moves with their own PP, per creature and separate from MP and TP.

The skill list shows the type and PP 12/15 at a glance, with level up learnsets, four slot movesets and the forget a move prompt. Burn, poison, paralysis, sleep, freeze and confusion. Burn cuts physical attack only, and types grant immunity: a Fire creature cannot be burned. Stat stages bounded at x0.25 and x4, applied on top of what MZ already computed, so your traits, buffs and equipment still count.

An AI that reads the matchup. Smart maximises expected damage; Expert also prefers the reliable knockout. Deterministic battles.
