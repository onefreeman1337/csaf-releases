Escapement is a clockwork gear puzzle plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 4 payoffs, 5 metals and 5 grades, generated from a seed and always solvable. Royalty free in your games. Every lock becomes a mechanism the player finishes: a plate with fixed arbors, a tray of loose wheels, a mainspring that drives and a payoff that moves when the train is right.

One plugin command on an event, and the event waits, then carries on with your switch set.
