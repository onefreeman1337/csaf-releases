A relationship chart for RPG Maker MZ: 5 kinds of bond, 6 weights, up to 16 characters Tether draws every bond between your characters as a cord you can read at a glance. Each character is a gilt medallion holding their own face graphic. The cord's material says what kind of bond it is, its weight says how strong, its knots mark the events that shaped it, and a strained bond frays. Royalty free for commercial games.

Five kinds, six weights Love is silk, friendship is rope, an oath is chain, a rivalry is barbed wire, family is ribbon. They differ in structure, not just colour, so they read at a glance even at twelve characters. A bond's value places it on six tiers (the thresholds are a parameter), from a thread to a heavy cord.

Knots and strain Every switch you list on a pair that is ON ties a knot labelled with the switch's own name, or tie one in your own words with Strike Knot. Oaths are padlocked and family ribbons tied in bows. A love, friendship, oath or family bond below zero is strained, and Set Strained marks any bond: silk and rope fray, a chain hangs one broken link, barbed wire snaps and curls back.
