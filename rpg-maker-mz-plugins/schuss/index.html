Schuss is a downhill minigame plugin for RPG Maker MZ: 5 ways down and 42 drawn obstacle forms, on courses the plugin lays out itself. Royalty free. Call one plugin command and the screen scrolls down a groomed slope, a mine or a river. Your own hero rides it: the party leader or any actor you choose, using the walking picture your project already has. No image files ship and none are needed.

What is inside 5 modes · 3 goals · 42 obstacle forms · difficulty 1 to 5 · courses from 15 to 240 seconds · 3 plugin files · source included Ski and Snowboard: carve between the trees, with tracks left in the snow behind you and spray on the hard turns Sled: slower to turn than skis, so the line has to be read early Mine cart: change rails only at a switch, and hop the gaps in the track Raft: the current pulls you toward the middle of the river, eddies shove you sideways, and rocks, logs and snags stop you dead The riders in these pictures are Wildmark 01: The Party, a sprite pack from the same studio.

In your project the rider is your own actor. 20 forms on the snow, 11 in the mine and 11 on the river, every one drawn by the plugin's code. Three goals Slalom: race the clock through the gates.
