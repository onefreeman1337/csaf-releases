Table Service - Restaurant Serving Minigame on Your Own Map for RPG Maker MZ

An RPG Maker MZ plugin from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with RPG Maker. Paid.

Table Service - Restaurant Serving Minigame on Your Own Map for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A serving rush played on the map you already built. Customers walk in and take the seats at your own tables. They ring for service, order, wait for the kitchen, eat, pay and tip, or run out of patience and walk out. The player carries the dishes from the pass to the right tables, two at a time. When the last party leaves, the night's ledger opens and the result goes back to your events. No separate minigame scene.

Mark the chairs with <Table>, mark the kitchen counter with <Pass>, call Start Shift. The shift runs on your map with your player walking your floor, and it saves with the game. Every dish is drawn from its own name. Pumpkin Soup comes in a bowl the colour of pumpkin, Pale Ale in a wooden tankard with a head of foam, Honey Loaf as a scored loaf, Grilled Trout as a whole fish with lemon.

The reader knows about 170 words for 19 kinds of dish and reads colour words too; a <Dish: soup> tag covers anything it does not. Every customer is generated. Face, hair, hat, coat, scarf or glasses from a seed, seated and facing your table, outlined like MZ's own characters.
