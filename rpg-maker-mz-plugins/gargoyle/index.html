They move when you look away. What this is, in plain words: Gargoyle is an RPG Maker MZ plugin (four JavaScript files, MZ 1.x) that turns any event into a stone statue of the character or monster it shows, struck at map load from that character's own walking sprite in one of seven materials.

A statue moves toward the player only while nobody is looking, freezes the instant it is seen, and is found nearer each time, in a different held pose, with a fresh crack. Touch one, or let one reach you, and the stone splits into plates and falls away: the monster steps out and your event, or a troop battle, takes over.

No art to draw and no image files to import: it works on MZ's own characters and on any sheet you already use. Commercial use in your games is included. A gaze rule your players can see. By default the map goes dark except the player's lantern: a beam where they face and a little glow all round.

Whatever the lantern lights is watched, walls cast shadow, and a statue in the dark is not drawn at all, so it is never seen moving. Prefer the classic?
