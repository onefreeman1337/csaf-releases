Climbing minigames for RPG Maker MZ, climbed by your own hero. What this is, in plain words: Ascent is an RPG Maker MZ plugin (three JavaScript files, MZ 1.x) that adds a climbing minigame to your game. Your party leader climbs a sea cliff, an ice wall, a castle wall under siege, a great tree, a ship's rigging or a round tower, hold by hold, against a grip that drains while they hang.

There are 36 kinds of hold (six per face), anchors that clip a real rope in, falls caught at the last anchor, hazards that come with a warning, and three goals: climb to the summit against the clock, collect on the way up, or race a rival up the next line. The plugin lays out and paints every face in code and proves each one can be climbed before the player sees it.

The climber, the belayer and the rival are your own characters, so it works on day one with no new art. Commercial use in your games is included. Start it in one step: an event's Plugin Command, Ascent > Start a climb, with the face, the goal, the difficulty (1 to 5), the length and a seed.
