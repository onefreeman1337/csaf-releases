Broadside - Ship-to-Ship Naval Battles for RPG Maker MZ: Drawn Ships, Fire on the Roll is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Ship-to-ship battles for RPG Maker MZ, drawn broadside-on like an age-of-sail painting. Two sailing ships fight on a painted sea under a painted sky. Your ship rolls on the swell in the foreground; the enemy lies beyond her. Each turn you give two orders, a manoeuvre and what to load, then fire on the roll: a brass dial swings with your ship, and the moment you fire decides where the broadside goes.

Every hit is drawn into the ships as it happens: shot holes along her side, a topmast shot away and hanging over the side, canvas torn to strips, fire on deck, every mast gone by the board, a list, and at the end a ship going down.

A ship's log closes the fight and the result goes into your variables and switches. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
