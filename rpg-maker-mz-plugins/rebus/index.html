Rebus - Picture Word Puzzles for RPG Maker MZ, Built From Your Own Database is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Picture word puzzles for your RPG, built from the art your game already has. Rebus turns your database into a word game. It pins two to four of your icons that share a name to a cork board and asks what they have in common. It stands an enemy battler on a spotlit stage as a black silhouette. It shows an actor's face through a brass lens that slowly widens, or breaks a picture into blocks that sharpen over time.

The player spells the answer from a tray of carved tiles. You add one plugin command; every round comes from your weapons, armours, skills, items, enemies and actors. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: RebusCore.js — Rebus 1/3 - the rules: rounds harvested from your database, the letter tray, hints and scoring. No engine code. RebusDraw.js — Rebus 2/3 - the drawing: letter tiles in four materials, slots, the board and the stage. Canvas only. Rebus.js — Rebus 3/3 - picture word puzzles from your own database: four pictures, silhouette, close-up and mosaic rounds.
