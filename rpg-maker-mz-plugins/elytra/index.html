Elytra is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that draws an insect specimen for each creature in your database: 224 from names alone, with no image files. Use it in unlimited games, commercial or free, with no royalties.

What is inside 12 body plans · 8 elytra sculptures · 7 antennae · 7 materials · 4 wing states · 8 plugin files Bodies: ground, stag, rhino, weevil, leaf, longhorn, click, tiger, scarab, firefly, diving and rove. Sculptures cut into the wing cases as a height field the shell is lit through. Materials: matte, satin, lacquer, metallic, iridescent, pruinose and velvet.

Hindwings folded, ajar, fanned or full, the one axis that changes the outline. A specimen cabinet scene with its own type and layout: creatures down one side, the selected one pinned on a card. What a project gets Install it and every row resolves from its name and note text, in the same way every time, and reordering your database changes nothing.

On plain creature names with no tags, 60 species came out as 60 visibly different specimens, 53 of them distinct in body, sculpture and antenna alone.
