A sanity meter tells your player a number. This decides what is in the room. Lucidity is one value your game erodes, five tiers it falls through, and a director that makes each tier true in the world. You author thresholds — not five copies of your game.

Forty-four apparitions, and not one of them is a file Every manifestation is drawn at runtime by the plugin’s own routines: figures, watchers, architectural lies, surface phenomena, script, light lies, doubles. There is no sprite sheet to license, no atlas to author, and nothing for you to draw.
