Cipher Core - An Invented Language Your Players Learn, for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

RPG Maker will happily show the player ancient writing. It will not make them earn it. A picture of runes, a switch, a translated message — the engine can fake a mystery, but it cannot make a language a puzzle.

Cipher Core can: glyphs render untranslated in ordinary message windows and on carved surfaces, the player presses rubbings of what they cannot read, guesses meanings from context, and the moment a guess is proven the glyph resolves — permanently, everywhere, in every text box in the game. Heaven's Vault and Chants of Sennaar, as an engine for your project.

A complete language in the box Cipher Core does not arrive empty. Eldrin ships whole: 122 glyphs built from 14 radical families, 10 validation anchors, and a 20-inscription story corpus — the tale of a drowned moon-temple, told entirely in a language your players cannot read yet.

Because every glyph is one radical frame plus differentiator strokes rendered from vector paths, the whole script is one hand, one weight, one visual grammar. Related meanings look related — which is exactly what makes deduction playable instead of guessy. The loop See. Write \LEX[sea] in any message and it renders as an untranslated glyph.
