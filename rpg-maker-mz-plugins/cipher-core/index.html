Cipher Core is 3 RPG Maker MZ plugins for an invented written language: 122 glyphs stay unread until the player proves what they mean. Royalty free in commercial games. Think Heaven's Vault or Chants of Sennaar, as an engine for your own project. Write \LEX[sea] in any message and it renders as a glyph the player cannot read yet.

Once a guess is proven, that glyph reads as its meaning in every text box in the game, for good.
