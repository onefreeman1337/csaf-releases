Posture - Sit, Kneel, Sleep and Wave Poses From Your Own Sprites (RPG Maker MZ)

Posture - Sit, Kneel, Sleep and Wave Poses From Your Own Sprites (RPG Maker MZ) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your characters sit, kneel, sleep and wave. Every pose is made from the walking sheet they already have. Every MZ map is full of characters who can only stand and walk. Pose plugins on the shelf play pose sheets you have to draw yourself, for every character. Posture makes the poses for you, from each character's own walking sheet, the first time it takes one.

Nine poses for every character in your project: sit on a chair or bench · sit on the floor · kneel, head bowed · read a book, seated · lie down · sleep in bed (pillow, eyes closed, a quilt in the character's own colours, breathing) · collapsed, face down · wave · cheer. Made from the character's own pixels.

Posture reads the standing and walking frames and builds each pose by moving, dropping, mirroring or turning whole pixels. Nothing is resampled or blended, so the pose keeps the sheet's outline and colours. The arms of a wave or cheer are the ones that swing when the character walks, turned up about the shoulder. Your image files are never changed. Sleep, done properly. MZ's beds stand upright, pillow at the top.
