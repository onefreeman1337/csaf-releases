Posture is an RPG Maker MZ plugin with 9 poses, made from each character's own walking sheet: sit, kneel, read, lie down, sleep, wave and more. Royalty free. A hamlet where nobody has to stand and walk. No pose sheets to draw: Posture makes each pose the first time a character takes it. Try it free The sit pose is in the free Your Own Art Sampler as Posture Lite.

It reads the same <pose: sit> tags and steps aside when Posture is installed, so upgrading changes no event and no save.

What is inside 9 poses · normal and $ sheets at any cell size · 2 plugin files · 2 demo sheets · MZ 1.x · source included Sit on a chair or bench, sit on the floor, kneel with head bowed, read a book seated, lie down, sleep in bed, collapsed face down, wave and cheer Sleep in bed: two tiles tall, head on the pillow, eyes closed, a quilt in the character's own colours, breathing Whole pixels are moved, dropped, mirrored or turned, never resampled, so the pose keeps the sheet's outline and colours.

Your image files are never changed Works on big head chibis and tall 48 x 64 sprites too.
