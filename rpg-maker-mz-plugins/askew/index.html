Walk the corridor again. Something is wrong. Or is it? What this is, in plain words: Askew is an RPG Maker MZ plugin (two JavaScript files, MZ 1.x) that turns any map you already made into an Exit 8-style anomaly loop. The player walks the same corridor, room or street again and again.

Each pass the map is either exactly as you built it, or one thing in it has changed: walk on if nothing changed, turn back if something did. Right answers count up on a drawn pass sign; a wrong one cuts the lights and the count falls to 0. Reach the goal and an exit opens. There are 50 kinds of anomaly, and every one is made from your own map: your tiles, paintings, props, walls, floors, characters and enemy images.

No art to draw and nothing to import. Commercial use in your games is included. Set up in three steps. Put <askew> in the map's Note, paint the far end with region 61 and the way back with region 62, and (optionally) note one event <askew: sign> where the pass sign should hang.
