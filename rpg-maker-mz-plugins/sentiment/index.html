Sentiment - Omori-Style Emotion Battles for RPG Maker MZ

Sentiment - Omori-Style Emotion Battles for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Emotion battles you can see, drawn over your own art. Sentiment gives every battler in RPG Maker MZ a feeling: happy, sad or angry, each with three rungs. The feelings play rock paper scissors. Happy beats angry, angry beats sad, sad beats happy. An attacker whose feeling wins deals x1.5 damage, and one whose feeling loses deals x0.5. Both numbers are parameters, and heals are never scaled.

The feeling is drawn over the portraits and battlers you already have. Your faces are never swapped for stock ones. Sentiment draws on top of them the way an artist marks up a sheet: a wash that reads the picture under it, an outline scribbled by hand around the face or the creature's own silhouette, and the feeling's marks. Gold stars for happy. Falling streaks for sad. Popped veins and jagged ticks for angry.

Manic looks nothing like Happy, and Furious looks nothing like Angry. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
