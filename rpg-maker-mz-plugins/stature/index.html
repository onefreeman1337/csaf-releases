Stature is a battler plugin for RPG Maker MZ that makes your side view battlers taller from the sheets you already have, in all 18 battle motions. Royalty free. MZ's side view battlers are chibi, with a big head on a short body. Stature reads each actor's sheet once, when it loads, and gives the battle a taller copy.

The body between the neck and the feet is lengthened, and the head and the feet stay exactly as you drew them. Your image files are never changed. What is inside 18 motions · 2 plugin files · 4 demo battlers and a battleback · 1 core method extended, none replaced · source included Whole pixel rows only.

The taller body repeats whole rows, spread evenly between the neck and the feet, so nothing is resampled and no colour is invented Default stature: 1.25 is a quarter taller, up to 2, on every actor or on tagged actors only Actor notetag: <Stature: 1.3> for a taller knight beside a short halfling The weapon rises with the hands, so a swing still comes from the grip Any cell size, read from the sheet, so large cell battler packs work too Upright poses grow in full, kneeling ones by half, and lying ones stay as they were.
