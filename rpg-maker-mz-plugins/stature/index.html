Stature - Taller Side-View Battlers From Your Own Sheets for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Taller side-view battlers from the sheets you already have. MZ's side-view battlers are chibi: a big head on a short body. Stature reads each actor's own sheet once, when it loads, and gives the battle a taller copy. The body between the neck and the feet is lengthened; the head and the feet stay exactly as you drew them. All 18 battle motions are handled, and your image files are never changed. No new art, no blur.

The taller body is made by repeating whole pixel rows, spread evenly between the neck and the feet. Nothing is resampled or blended, so the pixel art stays crisp and no colour is invented: every row of the taller frame is a row of your original.

No editing 54 frames per character by hand, and no second set of sheets to keep in step with the first. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: StatureCore.js — Stature core - re-proportions a side-view battler sheet to a taller body, pose by pose, at native pixels. Place ABOVE Stature.
