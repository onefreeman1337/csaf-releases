Cloudshadow is a 2 file RPG Maker MZ plugin that reads your map and casts 5 kinds of cloud sky, with a shadow that lands differently on water, roof and grass. Royalty free in commercial games, with a painted demo map in the download. Cloud masses have their own shape and altitude and drift at three different speeds. Drop it in and every outdoor map in your project already has weather.

What is inside 2 plugin files · 5 skies · 3 cloud altitudes · 6 parameters · 1 demo map · raw JavaScript The sky is derived: Cloudshadow reads the tileset kind, the region ids, how much open water there is, how built up the map is and the screen tone a day and night plugin leaves behind, then picks clear, fair, scattered, overcast or storm Direct a scene when you want to: <cloudshadow: storm> in the map note, region ids 61 to 65, or a variable for weather that changes while the player watches Interior maps opt out on their own The shade has a ceiling: a cloud removes the sun, so a shadow never reaches black, however heavy the storm A save resumes the weather exactly where you left it The demo is a painted parallax with its map and tileset records, and you may use it in your own games.
