Quires is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws every book in your database as a bound volume, from 10 coverings and 19 tooled devices. It shelves them all in a case with a collation card, and you tag nothing: the name and price you already wrote decide each binding. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

What is inside 6 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 10 coverings · 19 tooled devices · 6 spine treatments · source included Books found by name in Items, Armors and Weapons, so a tome modelled as armour still gets bound. The family comes from the name, the tier from the price, the device from the subject and the raised bands from the description's length. Tier thresholds read off RPG Maker's own shipped item prices.

Furniture, edge decoration and wear chosen by the book's value and station. Note tags to force a covering or a device, or to keep an entry out of the case. One volume, derived from the item with nothing tagged. Six spine treatments. Works with RPG Maker MZ, tested in the real engine with the stock scripts.

Copy the six files into js/plugins in any order, turn them on and add the plugin command Open the case to an event.
