Fathom - Underwater Maps for RPG Maker MZ: Caustics, Light Shafts, Kelp, Sea Life and a Dive is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your own maps, under the sea. One notetag, and the plugin draws the rest. RPG Maker MZ has no underwater mode. The usual answer is a recipe of four or five plugins: a fog with a water picture, a blue tint, a particle plugin for bubbles, a displacement filter, a layer for caustics. Fathom is that whole scene as one plugin, and it draws every part of it itself.

Put <underwater> in a map's note and that map, even an ordinary town, is seen through the water. No image files ship with Fathom. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
