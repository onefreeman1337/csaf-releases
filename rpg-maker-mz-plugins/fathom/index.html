Fathom is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that puts your own maps under the sea from one notetag, with caustics, light shafts and 48 drawn sea creatures. Royalty free in your games. Put <underwater> in a map's note and that map, even an ordinary town, is seen through the water. No image files ship with Fathom. It draws over whatever tileset your maps use.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 48 species · 2 transition commands · 7 notetag words · MZ · source included Light graded by depth: the reds go first, then the greens, and the light falls away.

Murk sets how much of the water's colour hangs in the scene Caustics: a network of light crawls over the floor, read from which kind of tile each cell is Light shafts that slant down from the surface and waver as the camera moves Sea grass: any tile with the Bush flag sways in the current from its foot Bubbles from every character, drifting plankton and a refraction wobble over the whole scene Optional slower movement, an air meter made of bubbles, and a command to set a map underwater or dry at run time Our test hamlet at <underwater: depth 2, murk 22, life 200, shafts 5>, at actual size.
