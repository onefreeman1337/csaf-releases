Ajar - Searchable Furniture for RPG Maker MZ: Drawers, Lids, Sacks and Shelves in Your Own Tileset Open is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Search the furniture, and watch it open. Ajar turns the dressers, desks, barrels, jars, pots, baskets, sacks and bookshelves already in your tileset into furniture the player can search. A drawer slides out toward the camera and its dark inside shows. A barrel's lid lifts, tips and comes to rest askew on its rim. A sack's tie lets go and its top sinks into a collar round a dark mouth.

On a shelf, one book is pulled out and two more lean into the gap. What the event gives rises out of the opening as its own icon, and the searched piece rests ajar, so the player can see which drawers they have already been through. Nothing to draw. Every moving pixel is cut from the furniture tile you painted, whatever tileset it comes from. No opening sprite sheets, no frames, no hand-drawn stages.

One note on the event: <ajar: drawer>. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
