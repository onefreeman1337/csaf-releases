Ajar is a 2 file RPG Maker MZ plugin that makes the drawers, barrels, jars, sacks and shelves in your own tileset open when searched. Royalty free in commercial games, with no art files to draw. Every moving pixel is cut from the furniture tile you painted. What the event gives rises out of the opening as its own icon.

What is inside 2 plugin files · 4 ways to open · 5 plugin commands · raw readable JavaScript · no art files · one note per event: <ajar: drawer> Drawer (dressers, desks, nightstands, cabinets): Ajar finds the drawer fronts and pulls one out toward you. <ajar: drawer 2> picks another and <ajar: drawer all> opens them in turn Lid (barrels, crates, jars, pots, urns, baskets, boxes): the lid lifts, tips and settles askew on the rim Sack (sacks, bags, pouches): the gathered top sinks into a collar round a dark mouth Shelf (bookshelves, bookcases): one book slides out and two lean into the gap The event's own commands run as always, so Show Text, Change Items or a loot plugin all work.
