Creel is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws a distinct fish for every species your game declares: 12 body plans and 8 fin sets make 96 silhouettes. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. Creel draws the fish and leaves the catching to your fishing system. One plugin command, recordCatch, is all that system needs to call.

What is inside 7 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 12 body plans · 8 fin sets · 10 markings · 12 liveries · 11,520 specimens 96 silhouettes from 12 body plans and 8 fin sets, before any colour is chosen. 10 markings and 12 liveries on top of those outlines, for 11,520 distinct specimens. A catch ledger scene: a tally, every species with its size, a specimen card with a caliper, and a stringer of silhouettes.

Five plugin commands: open, close, recordCatch (with an optional size for a personal best), clearRecord and countCaught. A versioned caught record that keeps personal bests in the save file. 12 body plans by 8 fin sets: 96 outlines before any colour. How you declare a species Put <creel> in an item's note box and Creel derives all four axes from the item's name, so Bog Lamprey becomes an eel and Marsh Gar a pike.
