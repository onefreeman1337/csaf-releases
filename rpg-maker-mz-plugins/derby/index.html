A race meeting your players can bet on. They read the race card (every runner's form, the going it likes, its silks and its price), put gold on a runner to Win or to Place, and watch the race run on a drawn garden course. Close finishes go to the judge's photograph. Winnings are paid into the party's gold.

The runners are racing snails, and every one is generated: its name, shell colour, shell pattern, spiral, body and silks. Every race draws its own field, course, going and prices. Nothing is loaded from an image file. One plugin command on an event opens the meeting; the event waits, and the net winnings can go straight into a variable. And the odds are measured, not guessed.

The bookmaker runs the same race model the player watches 2,000 times to price the card, then checks every price on a second, independent 2,000 races. The house keeps its edge whichever runner the player backs. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
