I Spy scenes made from your own items and characters. Rummage is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that composes seek-and-find scenes out of what your project already has.

I Spy scatters the items, weapons and armour in your database across a surface the plugin draws (a dragon's hoard, a merchant's cloth, a tavern table, the forest floor, a beach at low tide, an alchemist's bench, a sunken wreck, a library desk), buries them in drawn clutter, and writes their names on a parchment for the player to find.

Crowd fills a market square, a fair meadow or a quay with your characters walking about, and asks the player to find one person by face and costume. No scene to paint and no object to place. Every scene comes from a seed, so a chest, a stall or a search can be different every time or the same every time. The list is your own names: "Find: Potion, Ruby Ring, Brook Trout".

Every listed thing can be found, and Rummage proves it. It samples each one's outline, counts how much nothing drawn above it covers, and moves the clutter until enough is in plain view (80%, 66% or 52% by difficulty).
