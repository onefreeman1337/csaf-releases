I Spy scenes for RPG Maker MZ from your own database: 8 surfaces, 3 crowd grounds Rummage is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that builds seek and find scenes out of what your project already has. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free, with no royalties. I Spy hides the items, weapons and armour in your database inside drawn clutter and lists their names for the player to find.

Crowd fills a market square, a fair meadow or a quay with your own characters and asks the player to pick out one person. What is inside 8 drawn surfaces · 3 crowd grounds · 1 to 12 things per scene · up to 80 walkers · 3 difficulty levels · 3 raw JavaScript files No scene to paint and no object to place.

The surfaces and their clutter are drawn in code: coins and dragon scales in the hoard, dice and cards on the tavern table, shells and sea glass at low tide. The list uses your own names, such as "Find: Potion, Ruby Ring, Brook Trout". Two records that share an icon or a name never appear together, so each name has exactly one answer.

Every scene comes from a seed, so a chest or a stall can be new every time or the same every time.
