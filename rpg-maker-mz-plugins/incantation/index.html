A typing battle for your RPG, with the words taken from your own database. Incantation is Typing of the Dead for RPG Maker MZ. Foes walk toward the party down a drawn corridor, each carrying a word over its head, and typing the word strikes it down. The words are yours: the names of your skills (say the incantation to cast it), your enemies and your items, or any words you give a record in a notetag.

You add one plugin command; the battle, the stage and the lettering are Incantation's. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
