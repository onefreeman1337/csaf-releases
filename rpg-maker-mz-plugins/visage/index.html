Visage: Carved Bestiary Masks for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Visage carves a mask for every creature in your RPG Maker MZ database from its own record: 10 face structures, 12 superstructures, 6 inlays and 5 ages. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. Change the record and the mask changes. Every mask is drawn at run time in code from the enemy's type, its standing in your database, the element it strikes with and the goods it carries.

What is inside 10 face structures · 12 superstructures · 9 pigment states · 6 inlays · 5 ages · 32,400 combinations · 4 plugin files and 2 fonts, source included The face structure comes from what the creature is: beast, humanoid, undead, spirit, construct, dragon, insect, aquatic, plant or fiend.

The superstructure comes from its rank against every other record you wrote, so your biggest monster wears the crown even in a small project. The pigment comes from its attack element, then its skills, then its resistances: eight painted elements, and a physical striker left as bare wood or bone. The inlay comes from its house: a faction table you declare, a notetag, or the drops it carries.

Wear grows with rank and hardiness: exposed grain, chipped edges and a crack that lengthens across five ages.
