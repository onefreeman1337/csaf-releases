Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Your players write their own spells. At a spellwright's circle the player takes the effects of spells their hero already knows, combines them, turns a magnitude dial that drives the cost, names the result, and watches its magic circle inscribe itself from what they put in. Fire III and Sleep become Drowsing Ember Tempest. Add Quake I and it becomes a two-element spell, drawn in two colours.

The new spell is a real skill. It shows in your skill list, casts in battle through MZ's own Game_Action, and saves and loads like any other.

Every seal is drawn in code from the spell's own composition: no icons to draw, no seal art to buy. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: either order works (Spellwright.js finds the core the first time it needs it); this one is tidy.
