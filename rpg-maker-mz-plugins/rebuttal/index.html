Class-trial debates, inside your RPG Maker MZ game. The cast sits in a ring. One after another they make their statements, and every statement flies across the stage as big moving type. Somewhere in one of them is a lie, glowing gold: the weak point. The player turns the cylinder to a truth bullet from the evidence they hold, aims, and fires.

The right bullet on the right weak point breaks the statement apart and wins the debate. A wrong one costs influence. Run out of influence or time and the debate is lost. You write only the words. A debate is a few Comment lines in a common event.

Rebuttal composes how every statement moves from its own text and its speaker, so a debate looks directed without a single keyframe. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: RebuttalCore.js — Rebuttal rules: reads a debate written in comments and composes how every statement moves. Load FIRST. RebuttalDraw.js — Rebuttal's look: the trial ring, kinetic type and the weak-point glow, drawn in code. Load after RebuttalCore.
