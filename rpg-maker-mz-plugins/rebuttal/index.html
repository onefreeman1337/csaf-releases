Rebuttal is a class trial debate plugin for RPG Maker MZ: statements fly past as big moving type, with 8 entrances and 7 paths, and the player shoots the lie. One licence covers any number of your games, commercial or free, with no royalty. You write only the words, as a few Comment lines in a common event, and the plugin composes how every statement moves.

What is inside 8 entrances · 7 motion paths · 3 emphasis marks · 3 plugin files · 1 plugin command · keyboard, mouse and touch The debate: the cast sits in a ring. A lie glows gold in one statement, the player turns the cylinder to the right evidence, aims and fires. Wrong or right: a wrong bullet costs influence. The right one breaks the statement and wins. Run out of influence or time and the debate is lost.

Motion from your text: neighbouring statements enter differently, each speaker moves within their own family of 3 paths, and!,? and... set the emphasis. Evidence from your items: tag an item <Rebuttal Bullet: lantern> and it joins the cylinder under its own name. Drawn in code: the trial ring, spotlight, type, crosshair, cylinder and REFUTED stamp, with no stock window in the scene. Comment lines in, staging out.
