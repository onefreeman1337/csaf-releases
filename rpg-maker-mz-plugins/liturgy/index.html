Liturgy is a 9 file RPG Maker MZ plugin that redraws the main menu, item list and status page as one printed ledger, in 6 house styles. Royalty free in commercial games. Your command list, the commands your other plugins add, your handlers, your selection and your input all stay where they are. Liturgy prints them, and every mark is drawn at run time from what is already in your project.

What is inside 9 plugin files · 6 houses · 50 drawn letterforms · 24 command devices · 5 markers · raw JavaScript source 50 letterforms, cut 6 ways by a broad pen: stem weight, pen angle, serifs, set width and tracking all change, with no font file in the package. 24 devices: chosen from each command's symbol first, so the stock eight are right in any language.

An unrecognised command gets a monogram of its own initial. 6 papers and 6 rule systems: laid and chain wires, foxing and a deckle edge, with rules inked, doubled, tripled, scored or braced at the corners. 5 markers: a pointing hand, a lozenge with a tail, a dagger, a bracket and a chevron mark the chosen entry.
