Armorial is a 3 file RPG Maker MZ plugin that ends your game with a fate plate for each character, and a coat of arms built from what the player did. Royalty free in commercial games. Your switches and variables already hold what happened. Armorial reads them when you call it, picks a fate for each person or faction you named, and draws their shield.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 3 plugin commands · 19 parameters · 320,000 possible shields · raw JavaScript source 50 charges: the figure on the shield, from a spear to a chalice 10 field divisions and 10 tinctures: the shape of the field and the tone it is read in 8 ordinaries and 8 bordures: a band across the field and a border around it Drawn at any size: every shield is vector geometry, and the package holds no image files Kept in the save: a compiled armorial is saved with a schema version, and an older save compiles fresh when asked All 50 charges, each on a shield the plugin drew.

The same person after two playthroughs: two shields and two fates. A character with no recorded deeds gets a plain field, and the plate says so. What you fill in Subjects: who gets an epilogue. A party member, a rival, a town, a guild, a god.
