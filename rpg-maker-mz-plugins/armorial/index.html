Armorial - Generated Heraldic Epilogues & Character Fates for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your game already knows what happened. Switches got flipped, variables went up, someone was spared or wasn’t. Armorial reads that at the end of the game, works out a fate for each person or faction you named, and shows it as an epilogue plate — with a coat of arms it generates from that character’s own deeds. The arms are not pictures we shipped. There are no image files in this plugin at all.

Every shield is composed per character, per playthrough, and drawn as vector geometry at whatever size it is needed — so it is crisp at any resolution and adds nothing to your deployment size.

Charges — the figure on the shield 50 Field divisions — the shape of the arc 10 Tinctures — the tone it is read in 10 Ordinaries — a broad band across the field 8 Bordures — how the world regarded them 8 Distinct achievable devices 320,000 Two players who finish your game do not get the same arms.
