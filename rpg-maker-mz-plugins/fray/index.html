Fray - Battle Wear on Your Own Heroes' Faces and Side-View Battlers, for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your party wears the fight. Fray is battle wear for RPG Maker MZ, drawn into your own heroes. Every hit a hero takes leaves a mark on that hero's face and side-view battler: a blade grazes a cheek or tears a coat, a blow leaves a bruise, fire scorches the clothing, ice frosts it, lightning leaves a flash burn, poison a sick bruise.

As HP falls the hero looks more spent: the colour drains from the skin and grime sinks in. The wear does not vanish when the battle ends. It is there in the menu, on the status screen, in the next battle and in the save file, until the hero rests at an inn. A potion lays a plaster or a bandage over the worst mark. Nothing to draw.

Fray finds the skin, the clothing and the armour in each face and battler from the image's own colours and draws every mark as pixel work on a copy of it. No damaged face sets to paint, no image swaps to set up. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
