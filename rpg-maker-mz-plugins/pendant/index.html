Pendant - Spot the Difference From Any Picture, for RPG Maker MZ and MV (generated, measured changes) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MV and MZ.

Any picture in your game becomes a spot-the-difference puzzle, hung as a pair of paintings in a drawn gallery. A CG, a parallax, a title screen, a portrait: name it in the plugin command and Pendant hangs it twice and changes the second copy in as many places as you ask, 1 to 12. When the last difference is found, the time, the finds, the misses and an all-found switch go back to your events.

Try it free first: the Any Picture Minigames Sampler includes Pendant Lite, a free one-round cut of this plugin (3 differences, 2 of the 4 kinds), plus free cuts of Jigsaw and Unveil. Install Pendant later and the Lite's command hands its rounds to it, so your events carry over. Every change is generated, and every change is measured.

No second picture to paint by hand, and no two puzzles alike: leave the seed blank for new differences every time, or fix it to get the same ones.

Each change must visibly move enough of the picture before it is kept, or it is undone and another place is tried. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
