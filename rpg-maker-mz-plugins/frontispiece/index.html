Frontispiece is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that generates your title screen: 6 houses, 8 grounds and your title cut from 79 letterforms, with no image files. One licence covers unlimited games, commercial or free. You add the plugin, and it draws a ground worked as a real material, a border, a cartouche and your title letter by letter.

What is inside 6 houses · 8 grounds · 6 borders · 6 cartouches · 12 ornaments · 6 menu treatments · 5 hands with 245 glyphs · 5 plugin files 6 houses: Chancery (ink on skin), Sanctum (cut stone), Folio (gold on hide), Hall (carved oak), Foundry (etched steel) and Vigil (silver in black lacquer), each with its own ground, border, hand and menu.

5 hands: Lapidary, Uncial, Textura, Stave and Grotesk, from 79 letterform skeletons through one broad nib pen simulation. 4 cuts: incised, raised, inlaid and printed, each set for contrast on its own ground. Read from your project: on auto, it reads the element, class, weapon and armour names you changed, and falls back to a hash of your title. Stable: a game gets the same screen every boot.

Swap any single part, or change the house by plugin command as the story moves.
