Hallmark is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that gives every weapon and armour its own struck mark, chosen from 2,688 drawn in code. One licence covers unlimited games, commercial or free. Keep your loot system: Hallmark reads what it rolled and draws the result, with no image files to make or load.

What is inside 56 guild forms · 8 metals · 6 rank ornaments · 24 affix glyphs · 3 plugin files · 8 parameters · 3 commands 14 guilds, 56 forms: an anvil, a stag, a crucible, a wax seal, a chalice and more. Each weapon or armour type belongs to one guild, so players learn to read the shelf. 8 metals: Iron, Steel, Bronze, Silver, Electrum, Cold Iron, Meteoric and Blackened.

6 ranks: Struck, Ringed, Banded, Cartouche, Laurel and Sunburst, set by the item's price against steps you choose. 24 affix glyphs: struck along the lower arc from rolled bonuses, elements and state traits. Stable marks: the same item strikes the same mark in every save. Change the Mark Seed and the whole world re-casts.

The Assay Ledger: a book of proof marks the player fills while handling items, set in its own serif and monospace type with no windowskin.
