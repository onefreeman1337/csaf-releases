Scales is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that adds two weighing puzzles on a drawn brass balance: find the false coin among 3 to 13, or weigh out an exact order with a box of weights. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. It is also one of the plugins in the MZ Plugin Library. The player loads the pans, weighs, reads which side sank and names the false coin within a set number of weighings.

The coin they name is cut open with a banker's chisel to show the metal underneath. What is inside 3 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 2 puzzles · 5 metals · 6 devices · 3 rims · 5 weight boxes · source included The counterfeit: 3 to 13 coins, the player either told or not told whether the false one is heavier or lighter.

The weighings allowed are the fewest that always suffice, found by an exact search before the puzzle is shown: 12 coins, not told, gets three. The false coin is not fixed in advance. After every weighing it is wherever the weighings so far leave it best hidden, so a correct method always wins and a lucky guess never does.

The measure: weigh out saffron, pepper, salt or tea with one of five weight boxes, and a weight may go in either pan.
