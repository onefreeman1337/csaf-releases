Scales - Counterfeit Coin and Weighing Puzzles for RPG Maker MZ

An RPG Maker MZ plugin from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with RPG Maker. Genre: Puzzle. Paid.

Scales - Counterfeit Coin and Weighing Puzzles for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Weighing puzzles for your RPG, on a brass balance at a money-changer's counter. One coin in the hoard is false. The player loads the pans, releases the beam, reads which side sank, and names the counterfeit within a set number of weighings. When they name it, the coin is cut open with a banker's chisel to show the lead or copper underneath. Two puzzles in one plugin.

The counterfeit (3 to 13 coins, told or not told whether the false one is heavier or lighter) and the measure: weigh out an exact order of saffron, pepper, salt or tea with a box of weights that may go in either pan. Every mint, hoard and order is generated from a seed. Nothing is loaded from an image file. One plugin command on an event opens it; the event waits, and a switch and a variable tell you how it ended.

And it cannot be guessed. The weighings allowed are the fewest that always suffice, found by an exact search of the whole game before the puzzle is shown (12 coins, not told: three weighings, the classic). The false coin is not fixed in advance: after every weighing it is wherever the weighings so far leave it best hidden.
