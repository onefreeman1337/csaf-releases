Verdict Core is an investigation plugin for RPG Maker MZ and MV with 4 scenes: a case file, a cross examination, a corkboard and a deduction sheet. Royalty free. The player presents a piece of evidence against a claim, and the game answers with one of three results.

You author what each exhibit establishes and what each statement claims, and the engine derives which statements an exhibit can break, which blanks it can fill, and which conclusions the corkboard will accept. Contradiction: the right exhibit, aimed at the right line. The statement breaks, and what the break reveals goes on the record as a fact Close: the right exhibit, aimed at the wrong line.
