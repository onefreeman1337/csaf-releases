Windowskin Foundry - 44 Worked-Material Windowskins for RPG Maker MZ, Re-struck From Your Tilesets is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Give every window in your game a worked-material frame. 44 windowskins in RPG Maker MZ's own layout: a chamfered rim lit from the upper left, a plate seated in each corner, a shadow cast onto the window back, and a back textured with the material's own finish. Eleven materials (Iron, Steel, Blued Steel, Pewter, Silver, Brass, Bronze, Copper, Gold, Oak, Cut Card) by four corner ornaments (Stud, Bolt, Rosette, Volute).

Then make them yours. A playtest command reads the tileset of the map you are standing on, finds its three main hues, and re-strikes any skin into the one you pick, writing a new PNG into img/system.

Your game's frames end up in your game's colours, with the text colours re-checked to read at 4.5:1 or better. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: WindowskinFoundryCore.js — Windowskin Foundry core: reads a palette from tileset pixels and re-strikes a windowskin into it. Load with WindowskinFoundry.js.
