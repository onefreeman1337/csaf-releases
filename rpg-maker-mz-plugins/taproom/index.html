Taproom - Tavern Board Games vs NPCs (Morris, Fox and Geese, Oware, Fanorona) for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Four old tavern board games your player can sit down and play against the people of your world, for gold. Nine Men's Morris, Fox and Geese, Oware and Fanorona, each on a board the plugin draws, against an opponent who thinks. When the game ends, the result goes back to your events: a variable, a switch, and the stake won or lost. Every board is drawn, and every opponent has their own.

Eight boards: slate with chalk lines, veined marble with inlaid lines, a board knifed into the tabletop, black lacquer with gilt, a linen mat stitched in red thread, tooled leather, walnut with brass, and carved soapstone, each with its border worked round the edge and the scratches and chips of use.

Six sets of men: river stones, bone and horn, clear and cobalt glass, silver and copper coins, fired clay, boxwood and ebony, each with the seeds its Oware is played with. The board and men an opponent sets out are chosen from their name, so the old man in the corner always plays on the same scratched slate. Nothing is loaded from an image file. The four games are the ones nobody ships.
