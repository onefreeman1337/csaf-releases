Goose - Party Board Games for RPG Maker MZ (Generated Dice Boards, Forks, Rivals) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

A party board game for your RPG, laid out and drawn by the plugin. Goose drops a Mario Party-style dice board into your game: a fairground, a tavern night, a festival side quest. The party leader races up to three rivals along a trail of spaces, rolling the die and choosing at every fork between a shortcut full of hazards and a scenic route full of coins.

You add one plugin command; Goose lays out the board from a seed and draws every bit of it: the landscape, the trail, each space, the pawns and the dice. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: GooseCore.js — Goose 1/3 - the rules: generated boards, the race, the rivals. No engine code. GooseDraw.js — Goose 2/3 - the drawing kit: landscapes, trails, tiles, tokens, dice. No engine code. Goose.js — Goose 3/3 - party board games for MZ, laid out and drawn by the plugin.
