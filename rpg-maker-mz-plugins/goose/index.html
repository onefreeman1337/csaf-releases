Goose is a party board game plugin for RPG Maker MZ: a dice race across 6 generated landscapes, laid out and drawn by the plugin, against up to 3 rivals. Use it in any number of your own games, commercial or free. It suits a fairground, a tavern night or a festival side quest, and you add one plugin command.

What is inside 6 landscapes · 11 space types · boards of 24 to 60 spaces · up to 3 forks · 3 plugin files · keyboard, mouse and touch Six landscapes: meadow, desert, snowfield, coast, forest and volcano, each with its own scenery and tile material.

Eleven spaces: Coins (+3), Loss (-3), Chest (5 to 10 coins), Event (runs one of your common events), Warp, Roll again, Snare (lose a turn), Gust (blown back 2 to 4), Boost (forward 2 to 4), Start and Goal. Forks: a shortcut full of hazards or a scenic route full of coins. Rivals who play differently: bold always cut through, steady always take the scenic route, and canny take it until they fall four moves behind.

Checked as it is laid out: the goal is reachable on every route, with no dead ends, no overlapping spaces and no two hazards side by side on the open trail. The six landscapes, each generated from a seed.
