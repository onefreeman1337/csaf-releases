Pinfall - Generated Pachinko and Plinko Boards for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A tavern pin board for your RPG. The player slides the launcher along the rail, stakes gold and drops a steel ball through brass pins and jewelled bumpers into a row of prize pockets, each paying a multiple of the stake. Every board is generated from a seed: the pin pattern, the bumpers, the number of pockets and the payout table, painted in one of five themes. Nothing is loaded from an image file.

One plugin command on an event opens it; the event waits, and the net winnings can go straight into a variable. And it cannot be farmed. A pin board where the player chooses where to drop is only fair if no spot on the rail is a gold mine.

Pinfall calibrates every table against a player who aims, and checks it a second time on fresh balls, so the house keeps its edge however well the player aims. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
