Hearsay is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that charts the places your party has only heard about, using 41 drawn marks and 4 grades of confidence. One licence covers unlimited games, commercial or free. There is no rumour table to fill in: every named map is a place, every line that names one is an informant, and every Transfer Player command is a route somebody knows.

What is inside 41 marks · 10 coast forms · 12 devices · 10 hazards · 8 informant traditions · 4 plugin files · 6 parameters · 3 commands Rumour: one informant. A dotted open outline, with the name set in parentheses. Report: two or more informants. The line closes into dashes and the interior takes a stipple. Charted: a route exists. A committed line, full hachure and the hazard somebody warned you about.

Surveyed: the party arrived, and the place is redrawn true. The ghost: when a place was described wrongly, the old claim stays struck underneath at the size the informant gave it. A fitting page: give it four places or twenty and it solves for the largest cell that fits. A grade is earned from separate informants, so one villager repeating himself is still one rumour.

Three grades of knowing, each drawn a different way.
