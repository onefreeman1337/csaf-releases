Ident - Animated Studio Splash Generated From Your Studio Name, Before Your Title Screen (RPG Maker MZ) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your studio's animated ident, before your title screen. Type your studio name and pick a treatment. When the game starts, before the title screen, your name is struck in gold foil, bled into rice paper, bent in neon on a brick wall, cut into slate, stitched in satin thread, pressed in sealing wax, assembled from pixel blocks or powered on across a CRT. Then it holds, and fades to your title.

No images to make, no fonts, no audio to install: the plugin draws the whole thing from your name. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
