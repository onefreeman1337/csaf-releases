Scathe is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws battle damage into your own enemy art, with 6 materials and 3 HP bands: gashes, cracks, char, frost and burns. Royalty free. Scathe reads each battler image once when the battle starts and generates the damage from that image's own pixels, inside its own outline. Your enemies, your art, no damaged variants to draw.

What is inside 6 materials · 3 HP bands · marks by element · 7 demo battlers + 1 battleback · 2 plugin files · MZ 1.x · source included The element chooses the mark: physical leaves a gash, earth a crack network, fire char with a smouldering edge, ice a coat of frost, thunder a lightning burn, poison and darkness a creeping bruise with veins Scathe reads the element's name, so a database with its own elements still works The size of a mark follows the damage, and each new hit lands away from the marks already there Flesh bleeds, stone cracks and chips, shell fractures, metal scores to bare steel, slime splits and spirit tears.

Tag an enemy with <Scathe Material: stone> Below 75%, 50% and 25% of max HP the whole enemy looks one step more worn.
