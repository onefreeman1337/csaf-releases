Scathe - Battle Damage Drawn Into Your Own Enemy Art, for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Battle damage you can see, drawn into your own enemy art. Every enemy shows the damage it takes. A sword leaves a gash that bleeds, an earth spell cracks it, fire chars it, ice frosts it, thunder burns a fern of lightning into it, poison and darkness spread a bruise with veins. As its HP falls the enemy looks more and more worn.

Scathe reads each battler image once when the battle starts and generates the damage from that image's own pixels, inside its own outline. Your enemies, your art, no damaged variants to draw. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: ScatheCore.js — Scathe 1/2 - the damage: generated from each enemy's own battler pixels, by element, HP band and material. No engine code. Scathe.js — Scathe 2/2 - enemies show the damage you deal, generated from their own battler art by element and HP.
