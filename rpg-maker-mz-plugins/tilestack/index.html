Its title or description names RPG Maker MV and MZ.

Mahjong solitaire, inside your game. The player sits down at a table where a stack of carved tiles is laid out and clears it two at a time: remove any two free tiles that match. Clear the board to win; the time, the moves and a cleared switch go back to your events. Every deal can be cleared.

Tilestack deals backwards, taking two free tiles off the full layout and giving them a matching pair until the layout is empty, so a winning order always exists. A stuck player can shuffle what is left, dealt the same way. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: TilestackCore.js — Tilestack rules: layouts, the free-tile rule and deals that are always solvable. Load FIRST. TilestackDraw.js — Tilestack renderer: draws every tile (body and face) in several sets, and the table. Load SECOND. Tilestack.js — Tilestack: mahjong solitaire as a minigame. Generated layouts, drawn tile sets, every deal solvable.

It patches no core RPG Maker method at all, so there is nothing of anyone else's for it to collide with.
