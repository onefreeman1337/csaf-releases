Tilestack is a mahjong solitaire minigame for RPG Maker MZ and MV, with 42 tile faces drawn by the plugin in 5 sets and no image files. Every deal can be cleared, because it is dealt backwards, two free tiles at a time, so a winning order always exists. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

What is inside 3 plugin files and an MV bridge · RPG Maker MZ and MV · 42 tile faces · 5 tile sets · layouts of 8 to 144 tiles · source included 42 faces: three suits of nine, four winds, three dragons, four flowers and four seasons. 5 sets: ivory, bone, jade, black lacquer and porcelain. The classic 144 tile turtle, or a generated layout of the size you ask for, different for every seed.

Hints and shuffles you set per game, and a shuffle that is dealt to stay clearable too. The time, the moves and a cleared switch written back into your variables. Five sets, all drawn. A hint lights a matching pair. Works with RPG Maker MZ, tested in 1.10.0, and RPG Maker MV 1.6.1 with the included bridge. Copy the three files into js/plugins in any order and start a game with the plugin command or one script call.
