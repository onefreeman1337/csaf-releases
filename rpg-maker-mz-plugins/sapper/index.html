Sapper - Minesweeper Minigame for RPG Maker MZ: Clear a Field Over Your Picture, No Guessing is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Clear the field, uncover the picture. Sapper is a Minesweeper minigame for RPG Maker MZ. Name any picture in your project and the plugin lays a minefield over that picture: turf, sand, snow or slate, each cell tinted from the picture beneath it, so the field hints at what it hides. Every cell the player clears uncovers that patch of the picture, and a number says how many mines touch it.

Clear every safe cell and the rest of the field lifts away: the picture is shown whole. No guessing. Every field is laid after the first click, away from it, and laid again until a logic solver can finish it from there.

A careful player never has to guess, and no game ends on a coin flip. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: SapperCore.js — Sapper 1/3 - the rules: a minefield laid over your picture, every board solvable without a guess. No engine code.

SapperDraw.js — Sapper 2/3 - the look: the minefield drawn as turf, sand, snow or slate in your picture's colours, the marks, the frame. No engine code.
