Generated potion and item icons for RPG Maker MZ: 14 pot forms, 12 glazes, 8 kiln marks Glaze is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that reads your item database and draws a piece of pottery for every consumable, reagent and container in it. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free, with no royalties.

Your Potion, Ether and Antidote stop sharing one bottle icon: each gets its own pot, in the inventory, the shop and battle. The package is four JavaScript files with no image file in it.

What is inside 14 wheel forms · 12 glazes · 8 kiln marks · 168 form and glaze pairings · 2 scenes · 4 raw JavaScript files The form comes from what an item is: an ether gets a tall corked phial, a cure a lugged ampulla, a damaging item an alembic, a party restorative a krater.

The size comes from how much is in it: each kind is a family of three vessels, scaled to MZ's own item database, so a stock Potion is standard and a Super Potion is great. The glaze comes from what it does: fire in copper red, ice in celadon, thunder in metallic lustre, water in alkaline turquoise, a cure in crackled raku white. Every pot is signed on the foot with one of 8 kiln marks, cut into the clay of the base.
