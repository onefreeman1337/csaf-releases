Plinth - Statues of Your Own Characters and Monsters, Live Petrify and Stone Battlers for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Statues of your own characters, struck from their own sprites. Plinth turns your cast and your monsters into statues. Give an event one note, <statue: actor 1, bronze, age 40>, and when the map loads Plinth reads that actor's walking sprite and strikes it in stone: the eyes blanked, the top edges catching the light, a surface for the material, weathering for its age, standing on a base with the name cut into it.

No image files. Every statue comes from the sprites and battlers already in your project, so every character you have is a statue on day one.

The same strike drives two more things: Petrify, a plugin command that turns the player, a follower or any event to stone from the feet up, right there on the map, and a state that turns a battler to stone in battle. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: PlinthCore.js — Plinth 1/2 - the statue: struck from each character's or enemy's own pixels in seven materials. No engine code.
