Round-up is a herding minigame plugin for RPG Maker MZ, played on your own map: drive a flock of up to 32 generated animals into the pen before the bell. Royalty free. Paint a pen with a region ID and call one plugin command. No new scene: the animals walk your map, behind your trees and in front of your fences.
