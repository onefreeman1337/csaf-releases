Threads is a player facing story flowchart plugin for RPG Maker MZ and MV: every branch, branch jump, and an ending gallery with 48 emblem motifs. Royalty free. Your game has multiple endings and your players finish on one of them. Threads gives them the chart: every scene they reached, every branch they took, every branch they can see and have not opened, and every ending they have collected.

From that chart they can jump back to any decision they have reached and take the other road.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 48 ending motifs in 6 families · 70 generated marks · 6 plugin commands · 11 parameters · no image files · source included The chart: you declare the shape of your story and Threads computes the layout, the columns, the convergences and the states The road left open: a branch out of a place they have been is drawn dashed, so they can see a road exists without being told where it goes Branch jump: store a snapshot at a decision and the chart can return them to it.
