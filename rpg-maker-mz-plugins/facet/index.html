Facet is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws every gem, ore and crystal in your database as a cut, lit stone: 13 cuts and 16 minerals, all drawn in code. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. What is inside 5 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 13 cuts · 16 minerals · 7 inclusions · 5 settings · source included 13 cuts in four facet layouts (brilliant, step, rose and rough) plus the cabochon.

16 minerals, each with its own published refractive index, dispersion and absorption. 7 inclusions, from rutile silk and feathers to asterism and chatoyancy on cabochons. 5 settings: loose, in the palm, bezel, claw and socket. A tray scene that shows every stone your database defines, the selected one at size. A script call, bitmapFor(item, 96), that returns a cached Bitmap for your own menus.

How your database decides An entry becomes a stone when its last word names one: Guard Stone, Ruby, Fire Opal. Firestone is claimed and Whetstone is left alone, and Stone Flail stays a flail. The name picks the mineral (Amethyst Shard is an amethyst), otherwise the element it implies.
