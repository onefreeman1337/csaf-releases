Wrack - Burned, Ruined, Overgrown and Abandoned Towns From Your Own Tileset (RPG Maker MZ) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your own town after the fire, the war or the years, made from your own tileset. Every story with a war, a dragon or a time skip needs the same town twice: before and after. Wrack makes the after from the before.

When a map loads it reads the tileset images your project already uses and derives four states from those pixels, then switches the whole map, a region or a single building into one of them, and can play the change on screen. Your map file is never touched, so events, cutscenes and passability stay exactly as you made them.

No image files ship with Wrack, and nothing is drawn by hand: every changed tile is made from your own art. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: WrackCore.js — [Wrack] Core: derives burned, ruined, overgrown and abandoned tiles from your own tileset pixels. Wrack.js — [Wrack] Your own town burned, ruined, overgrown or abandoned, re-struck from your own tileset at load time.
