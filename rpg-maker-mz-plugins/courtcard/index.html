Courtcard — Every Card In Your Deck, Drawn By The Plugin, for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Courtcard draws the face of every card in your game, from the data you already have. A card is a made object: a stock, a frame with worked corners and ornament bands, a central charge composed from what the card actually does, a suit and cost rail, a nameplate set with your own title text, and a foil, edge and wear pass on top. There is no PNG in this package, no atlas and no icon sheet.

Every mark is geometry drawn at run time, so a card is sharp at any size you ask for, and the whole product is nine JavaScript files. Four axes, and every one of them is read from your database The type decides the charge.
