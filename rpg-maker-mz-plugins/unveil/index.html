Unveil - Qix-Style Picture Reveal Minigame for RPG Maker MZ (Your Own Pictures) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A Qix-style minigame for your RPG, and the prize is one of your own pictures. Unveil puts one of your pictures under a veil. The player walks the edge of the land already revealed and, holding OK, cuts a line into the veil; when the line reaches revealed land again, every part of the veil with no creature in it falls away.

A creature drifting under the veil that touches the line, or a spark running along the edge that touches the player, costs a life. Reveal the target share and the whole picture is shown.

You add one plugin command and name a picture; the veil, the creature, the sparks and the scoring are Unveil's. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
