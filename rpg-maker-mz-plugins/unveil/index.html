Unveil is a Qix style minigame plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 3 veils drawn in code: the player claims the board to reveal one of your own pictures. Royalty free, with a free lite version. The player walks the edge of the land already revealed and, holding OK, cuts a line into the veil. When the line reaches revealed land again, every part of the veil with no creature in it falls away.

A creature that touches the line, or a spark that touches the player, costs a life. You add one plugin command and name a picture.

What is inside 3 veils · 3 plugin files · 1 to 4 creatures · 0 to 6 sparks · no images, fonts or sounds · source included Silk with draped folds and a woven damask diamond, frosted glass with branching ice ferns, and black lacquer struck with gold waves Your picture is the reward: any picture in img/pictures goes under the veil, scaled to cover the board at your screen size Results go back to your events: a variable for the percent revealed and a switch for the win, so the next event can branch The command sets the difficulty: target share, lives, creatures and sparks Controls: arrow keys walk the edge and OK or Shift cuts.
