Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

A Qix-style minigame for your RPG, and the prize is one of your own pictures. Unveil puts one of your pictures under a veil. The player walks the edge of the land already revealed and, holding OK, cuts a line into the veil; when the line reaches revealed land again, every part of the veil with no creature in it falls away.

A creature drifting under the veil that touches the line, or a spark running along the edge that touches the player, costs a life. Reveal the target share and the whole picture is shown. You add one plugin command and name a picture; the veil, the creature, the sparks and the scoring are Unveil's.

Try it free first: the Any Picture Minigames Sampler includes Unveil Lite, a free one-board cut of this plugin (the silk veil at the default difficulty), plus free cuts of Jigsaw and Pendant.

Install Unveil later and the Lite's command hands its games to it, so your events carry over. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
