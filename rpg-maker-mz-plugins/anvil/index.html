Anvil - Forging Minigame for Crafting in RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A hot-metal forging minigame for your RPG that plugs into your crafting. The player works a glowing bar on the anvil toward a pattern chalked on its face, reading the heat by the colour of the metal the way a smith does: blows move hot metal and crack cold metal, the hearth reheats it a limited number of times, and the quench fixes it, best at cherry red.

Then the piece is shown finished, polished and hilted, exactly as the player shaped it. The pattern is your weapon. Name a weapon from your database and Anvil generates what to forge from it: the weapon type picks the family (sword, dagger, axe, spear, mace, claw), its name picks the metal (a Mithril Sword forges in mithril), and its id, name and attack pick the proportions.

MZ's own stock database gives 40 distinct patterns for its 40 weapons. Nothing is loaded from an image file. And the result goes back to your game. A quality from 0 to 100 and a grade (Crude, Serviceable, Fine, Masterwork) land in your variables, a switch turns on at the bar you set, and Anvil can give the weapon itself.
