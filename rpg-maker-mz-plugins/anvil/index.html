Anvil is a forging minigame plugin for RPG Maker MZ: the player hammers a glowing bar to a pattern generated from your own weapon, reads the heat by colour, then quenches it. Use it in unlimited games, commercial or free, with no royalties. Nothing is loaded from an image file, and the plugin ships no audio.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 6 weapon families · 40 patterns from MZ's stock weapons · 3 difficulties · keyboard, mouse and touch The pattern is your weapon. The weapon type picks the family (sword, dagger, axe, spear, mace, claw), its name picks the metal (a Mithril Sword forges in mithril), and its id, name and attack pick the proportions. The heat is the skill.

A blow moves the metal a fixed step, larger when hotter. Hot metal moves, cold metal cracks, the hearth reheats a limited number of times, and the quench is best at cherry red. The smithy: a brick hearth with live coals, a quench trough and an anvil seen from above, with a colour chart on the wall as the heat gauge.

The finished piece: the player's own shape, ground and polished in its metal, with any cracks, fitted with a hilt, haft or socket, and a brass plaque with its grade.
