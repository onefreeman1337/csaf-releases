Screenshot plugins are free. This is not one. A screenshot plugin writes a PNG to the player's hard drive, and nothing in your game ever knows it happened. Aperture Core gives the player a lens. They frame a shot through a real viewfinder, rack focus, choose an aperture, and press the shutter — and the engine judges the photograph. What was in it. How it was composed. Whether the subject was sharp.

Whether it was looking at them. The result is a developed print that lands in an album, satisfies photo commissions, and can be read back by your events. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: ApertureCore.js — The camera your player actually aims. Viewfinder, focus, framing score, and an album of developed prints. ApertureLens.js — The look of Aperture Core: viewfinder chrome, real depth-of-field blur, focus peaking, the shutter, and developed prints. ApertureAlbum.js — The camera and the album, as playable scenes. Binds Aperture Core to RPG Maker.
