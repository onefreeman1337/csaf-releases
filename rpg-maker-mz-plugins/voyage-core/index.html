Voyage Core - Sailing, Wind, Cargo & Storms for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

RPG Maker gives you a boat, a ship and an airship. They are teleports with a sprite. You board, you steer, you arrive, and nothing about the passage mattered. Every travel plugin on the market makes that faster. None of them makes it a journey.

Voyage Core is the journey. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: VoyageCore.js — Sea travel as gameplay. Wind, points of sail, cargo weight, spoilage, storms and ports whose prices move because you sailed there. VoyageChart.js — The sea chart.

Hand-drawn parchment, live wind, a course you ink in as you sail it. Requires VoyageCore. VoyageManifest.js — The cargo manifest: a ruled ship's ledger with wax seals, keeping-time bars and a weight beam. Requires VoyageCore. VoyageCore is required; VoyageChart and VoyageManifest are optional scenes.
