Asterism is a skill tree plugin for RPG Maker MZ where every node draws its own star from that skill's data: 56 motifs and 336,000 possible stars. Use it in unlimited commercial or free games with no royalties, and with no image files to make or import. What is inside 4 plugin files · 4 plugin commands · 56 motifs and 10 palettes · RPG Maker MZ · raw, commented source A star per skill.

The core comes from the skill's type and damage type, the corona from its element, the orbit from its scope and the mantle from the states it applies. 20 cores × 14 coronas × 10 orbits × 12 mantles make 33,600 forms, and 10 palettes make 336,000 stars. A chart players learn to read. A single target skill wears one companion star and an all enemies skill a full ring, and every fire skill flares the same way.

Change a skill's element in the database and its star changes to match. Unlit stars. A skill the player has not taken hangs in outline at low light, so the shape of a branch shows before they spend on it. Converging prerequisites. A node's Requires field takes any number of parents; prefix the list with any: and one will do.
