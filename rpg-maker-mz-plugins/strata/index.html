Strata - Generated Excavation Digs for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Give the player a rock face and two tools. Every blow cuts the rock back a layer and puts stress on the face. Somewhere under it lie fossils, relics, crystals and ore. Free a find completely before the face gives way and it goes into the party's inventory. Push too hard and the face collapses, taking whatever is still buried with it.

Every site is generated from a seed: the finds, the bedrock, the thickness of the rock and the stability of the face. Stability is measured against how a careful digger does on that exact site, so no dig is impossible and the grade decides how forgiving it is. Nothing is loaded from an image file: the bedded rock, the cracks and every find are drawn in code. One plugin command on an event.

Choose a grade, a rock and the items that can be found. The event waits while the player digs, then carries on. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
