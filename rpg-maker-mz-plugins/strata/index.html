Strata is a dig minigame plugin for RPG Maker MZ: chip back 5 kinds of rock with a pick and a hammer to free fossils and relics before the face collapses. Royalty free, and nothing is loaded from an image file. What is inside 3 plugin files · 10 families of finds · 5 rocks · 5 grades · 2 tools · RPG Maker MZ · source included The pick cuts two layers at the target and one on the four cells beside it, for stress 2.

The hammer cuts two at the target and beside it and one on the diagonals, for stress 4. Every blow puts stress on the face. Free a find completely before the face gives way and it is the player's; push too hard and the face collapses with whatever is still buried. Every site comes from a seed: the finds, the bedrock, the thickness of the rock and the stability of the face.

Stability is set from how a careful digger does on that exact site, so no site is impossible. Grades 1 to 5. Measured over 600 generated sites, about 9 in 10 careful diggers clear a grade 1 face and about 2 in 5 clear a grade 5. Each find becomes an item you choose. A note such as <Strata: ammonite> sets its look, or the item's name is read for a hint.
