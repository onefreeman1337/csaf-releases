Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Ephemeris is an almanac for RPG Maker MZ: each month your project declares prints its own leaf from 12 engraved devices, 4 seasons, 5 papers and 5 rulings. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. Each leaf is laid paper with a torn deckle edge, a ruled calendar grid, an engraved device composed from that month's character, a seasonal colourway, and your festivals struck into the days they fall on.

A project with thirteen invented months gets thirteen devices. What is inside 12 month devices · 4 seasons · 5 paper stocks · 5 rulings · 12 feast marks · 8 moon phases · 8 plugin files, source included 48 distinct leaves before you change a parameter (12 month characters across 4 seasons), and 1,200 combinations once papers and rulings are crossed in.

Each season has its own paper, ink, rubricated second colour and garland. Twelve feast marks from feast and market to crowning and omen; when two share a day, the higher ranked mark wins. Moon phases struck on the day each phase begins, the way a printed almanac marks them. Every letter and numeral is drawn by the plugin: 26 capitals, 10 numerals and 3 marks, with no font file.
