Jigsaw is an RPG Maker MZ and MV plugin that turns any picture in your game into a jigsaw of 4 to 256 pieces on a drawn table. Royalty free, with a free lite cut. A CG, a parallax, a portrait or a screenshot of a map: name it in the plugin command and Jigsaw cuts it. The time, the moves and a solved switch go back to your events.

What is inside 3 plugin files + 1 MV bridge file · 4 to 256 pieces · mouse, touch and keyboard · demo picture · user guide · raw JavaScript Every cut is generated: each edge is a knob and socket curve shared by its two pieces, and no two seeds cut the same puzzle.

Fix the seed to cut the same one A felt mat in a wooden rim, with a faint ghost of the picture in the frame and every loose piece on a spot of its own Pieces bevelled, lit from the upper left and shadowed like card stock. A piece in the hand lifts higher and casts a deeper shadow A plaque with the count, the time and the moves.

Hold Q or press it for the box lid When it is done, the finished picture rises to the middle of the table with a SOLVED plaque.
