Jigsaw - Jigsaw Puzzles From Any Picture, for RPG Maker MZ and MV (generated cuts, 4 to 256 pieces) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Any picture in your game becomes a jigsaw puzzle the player puts together on a drawn table. A CG, a parallax, a portrait, a screenshot of a map: name it in the plugin command and Jigsaw cuts it. When the last piece goes in, the time, the moves and a solved switch go back to your events. Every cut is generated.

Each edge is a knob-and-socket curve shared by the two pieces it separates, the corners are jittered so no grid lines show, and no two seeds cut the same puzzle. Leave the seed blank for a new puzzle every time, or fix it to cut the same one.

From 4 pieces to 256. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: JigsawCut.js — Jigsaw cut model: cuts any picture into interlocking knob-and-socket pieces from a seed. Load FIRST.

JigsawDraw.js — Jigsaw renderer: draws each piece from your picture with a bevel, rim light and shadow, and draws the table. Load SECOND. Jigsaw.js — Jigsaw: turns any picture in your project into a generated jigsaw the player assembles on a drawn table.
