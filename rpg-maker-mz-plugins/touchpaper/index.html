Touchpaper is an elemental surface plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 43 ground states: fire, water, frost, oil and lightning act on each tile, and the map remembers. Use it in unlimited games, commercial or free, with no royalties. What is inside 9 surfaces · 43 reachable ground states · 8 plugin commands · 5 plugin files · no image files Set fire to dry grass and it spreads, leaves ash and stops at a river.

Soak a line of ground first and you have built a firebreak. Freeze a lake and the party walks across it. Thaw it and they cannot. Pour oil, light one end, and the whole trail goes up. Earth a shock through standing water and it hits everything standing in it. Throw fire at water and you get steam. Five numbers per tile Each tile carries a surface, a temperature, a wetness, a charge and a fuel reserve.

The reactions come from those numbers, with no fire event and no ice event, so combinations you did not plan for still work. Standing on burning ground costs HP. A forest the player burned stays burnt, and the state of every map you changed is saved with the game. Made for events Every plugin command takes an X and a Y, and leaving them blank means the player's feet.
