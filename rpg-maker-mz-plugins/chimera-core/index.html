Chimera Core is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that gives every captured creature 8 hidden stat genes, so two of one species at one level are measurably different. It adds breeding, branching evolution and box storage on top of whatever capture plugin you already use, royalty free in commercial games. What it does Genetics: eight hidden stat genes, 25 natures, gender, ability slots and rare variants.

The player has a reason to catch the second one, and the twentieth. Breeding: genes pass down from both parents, natures can be inherited, mutations happen, egg groups decide which species can pair, and eggs hatch as the party walks. Generations are tracked so a player can chase a perfect specimen. Evolution: a condition graph, not a level check.

Evolve on level, item, friendship, time of day, gender, variant, trade or a stat comparison, so one species can become two different creatures. Genes, training, friendship and nickname survive it. Storage: 24 boxes of 30 slots by default, 720 creatures, with sort, search and filter, such as every variant you own or every creature above 80% potential.
