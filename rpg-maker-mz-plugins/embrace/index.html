Two of your characters, touching. What this is, in plain words: Embrace is an RPG Maker MZ plugin (three JavaScript files, MZ 1.x) that lets two of your characters hug, kiss, shake hands, high five, bow to each other, dance, walk hand in hand, ride piggyback and carry one another in their arms.

Nine pair poses, each in all four facings, are made at run time from the two characters' own walking sheets: no pose art to draw, no image files to import. It works on MZ's own characters and on any sheet you already use. Commercial use in your games is included; two demo character sheets come with it. Nothing to draw.

The first time a pair takes a pose, Embrace reads both characters' standing and walking frames and composes one new picture in which they touch. The arm that wraps round a partner is the character's own arm, found from the pixels that swing in its walk and turned about its shoulder; heads lean in for a kiss; the taller one leans down.
