Embrace is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 9 pair poses, made from two characters' own walking sheets: hugs, kisses, hand holding and carries. Royalty free in your games. No pose art to draw and no image files to import. Works on MZ's own characters and on any sheet you already use.

What is inside 9 pair poses · 4 facings each · 3 plugin files · 2 demo character sheets · MZ 1.x · source included Hug: cheek to cheek, an arm round the shoulders and one round the waist Kiss: faces together, eyes closed, a heart rising Handshake and high five: hands meet halfway, at the waist or over their heads with a burst Bow: both bow to each other, played once Dance: a close hold that turns through every facing, with notes Hold hands, piggyback and carry: the first character leads and the partner goes wherever it walks, drawn in the pair Two walking sheets on the left and the nine poses composed from them, at 2x.

How the poses are made The first time a pair takes a pose, Embrace reads both characters' standing and walking frames and composes one new picture. The arm that wraps round a partner is the character's own arm, turned about its shoulder.
