Strew is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that dresses the maps you made by hand, from your own tileset, in one keypress. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free. It is also part of the MZ Plugin Library, every CSAF plugin in one download.

What is inside 2 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 6 parameters · 4 plugin commands · 6 map notes · 3 tileset notes · source included Flowers gather in drifts, one kind of flower to a drift, and flowered grass appears only around them. Bushes, rocks and stumps settle against trees, walls and water, and a field of one repeated floor tile is broken up by its own variants.

Nothing lands on a path, beside an event, on a kept clear region or on an upper tile, and a blocking tile goes down only where the squares around it stay connected. F7 bakes the map, F7 again rerolls it with a new seed, and Shift+F7 takes back exactly what Strew placed. Live mode: one map note dresses the map in memory each time it loads, the same way every time, in deployed desktop and web games.

On the stock Outside sheet it places only natural objects.
