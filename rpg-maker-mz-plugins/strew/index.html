Strew dresses the maps you made by hand, from your own tileset, in one keypress. Walk onto a map in playtest and press F7: flowers gather in drifts, one kind of flower to a drift; bushes, rocks and stumps settle against trees, walls and water; a field of one repeated grass tile is broken up by its own variants. Press F7 again for a different arrangement, Shift+F7 to take it back. It knows where things must not go.

Nothing lands on a road, on or beside an event, on a region you keep clear, or on a square that already has an upper tile. Before it places anything that blocks, it proves the squares around it stay connected, so it never cuts a route.

Undo removes exactly what Strew placed and keeps anything you painted since. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: StrewCore.js — [Strew 1.0 core] The map-dressing engine Strew.js drives. Install DIRECTLY ABOVE Strew.js.
