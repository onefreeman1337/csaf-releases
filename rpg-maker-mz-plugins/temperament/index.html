Temperament - Personality Quizzes for RPG Maker MZ (Sort the Player Into Your Own Classes, Heroes or Houses) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Ask the player who they are, and let the answer choose. Temperament is a personality quiz for RPG Maker MZ, the kind of opening the Mystery Dungeon games made famous. The player answers situations where no answer is right ("A fallen star grants you one wish."), and is sorted into one of your outcomes: a class from your database, one of your actors, or a house you name.

Then a twelve-spoke wheel fills from their answers, the strongest spoke flares, and a card turns over with the outcome's portrait, its name and the player's epithet: "the Calm", "the Daring", "the Lucky". Your database is the answer key.

A class is read from its own parameter curves and the skills it learns, compared with your other classes, so a fresh project already works: MZ's eight stock classes read as eight different temperaments with no notetags. A notetag replaces the reading whenever you want the final say. A tie is never settled quietly.

When two outcomes match the answers equally, a banner says so and one more question is asked between the tied outcomes only, one answer each.
