Journeywork — Every Working NPC Gets A Workplace, for RPG Maker MZ

Journeywork — Every Working NPC Gets A Workplace, for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Your NPCs have jobs. They do not have workplaces. A routine plugin walks a smith to the corner of the map at 8am, and he stands on bare grass turning in random directions. The tileset has no anvil, so the smithy is a decorative sprite you placed by hand — if you placed one at all. Journeywork draws the workplace, from the trade.

Put <journeywork: smith> in an event's note box and that NPC gets an anvil on a tree stump, a lit hearth, a rack of stock bar, a quench tub and a stack of crates, standing on a floor of cinders and ash — arranged by a solver that measures every prop, sorted into the tilemap at the right depth, and facing the work instead of turning at random. No image files. No tileset pages to import. Nothing to configure.

Every anvil, loom, kiln, quern, mash tun and drying rack in this plugin is drawn in code at your project's own tile size.
