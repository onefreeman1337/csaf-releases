Township is an NPC daily routine plugin for RPG Maker MZ and MV: 9 plugin commands give every townsperson a real day, overnight shifts included. Royalty free. Homes, jobs, errands, overnight shifts, market days and festivals, with idle poses when they arrive and door transitions instead of pop in.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 9 plugin commands · 3 clock sources · 1 demo town with 8 townspeople and 14 places · source included Township.js: the schedule resolver, pooled pathfinding, poses, door transitions and the pause when you talk to someone TownshipBoard.js: the routine board scene TownshipDemo_Millbrook.js: a worked town with a market day, an injury switch, a rest day and two overnight shifts of different shape Built in the editor: <township_place:oven> marks a destination, <township_door:front> marks where NPCs fade in and out, and <township:baker> binds a townsperson to their event Poses on arrival: facing, step animation, a sprite swap or a balloon Schedule variants by day type, weekday, switch or variable, with priorities to break ties Your clock: already own a day and night plugin?
