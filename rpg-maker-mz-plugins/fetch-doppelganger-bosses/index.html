Fetch is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that copies your party into real enemies for a mirror-match boss, drawn from each hero's own battler sheet in 10 looks. Two plugin files, no art to draw, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy. These pictures use our own HD battlers and backdrops; neither ships. Your twins come from your art, and one plate shows stock-size sheets on a bright field.

A fetch is a folklore double, and this one is read from the live hero when the fight starts: level, stats, equipment, traits and learned skills. A database enemy freezes the day you type it. What you get Ten looks: Shadow, Void, Mirror, Ink, Ember, Frost, Phantom, Gilded, Glitch and Crimson, on every frame of the sheet.

All 18 side-view motions, and front-view battles drawn from the hero's map sprite, which walks on the spot. A brain that chooses as it moves: it heals an ally under half HP, takes a kill, and hits the hero its skills hurt most, taking the best choice about 7 times in 10 by default. Habits: each twin leans toward what its hero has really been used for.

An entrance: each hero's floor shadow crosses the field and the twin rises from it.
