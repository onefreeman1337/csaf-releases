Knack is an RPG Maker MZ plugin with 12 work animations, from chopping wood to fishing, made from your own walking sprites in all 4 facings. Three plugin files and 8 sample objects, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy. These pictures use our own characters, our own objects and a yard painted in code. In your game the frames are made from your own sheets.

A gathering plugin handles the wood and the ore; what the worker does on screen is left to you. Until now that meant drawing mining and chopping frames for every character, or rebuilding your cast in one sprite creator. Knack makes those frames from the sheets you already have. What you get 12 jobs: chop, mine, dig, hoe, water, fish, hammer, saw, sweep, reap, stir and gather.

Each is a different motion with its own tool, and each tool is drawn in the sheet's own outline colour. Effects where the tool lands: chips, sparks, clods, drops, dust, flecks and steam. The rod casts a float on a line, waits, gets a bite and reels in. One tag on a resource: <knack: axe> on a tree. The action button plays the swings, then the event's page runs, so it pairs with any gathering plugin or plain events.
