Pelt - Team Dodgeball Minigame for RPG Maker MZ: Your Party vs Any Troop, Special Throws From Skills is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Team dodgeball, inside your RPG. Pelt is a real-time dodgeball scene for RPG Maker MZ. Call one plugin command and your party plays a troop, or a team of rival actors, on a court the plugin draws: run, throw, catch, dodge, jump, and special throws made from each player's own skills. Your party is the team on day one.

Pelt plays with the side-view battler sheets your project already has, mapping MZ's own battle motions onto the game: walk to run, guard to catch, swing to throw, evade to dodge, dead when out, victory when your side wins. Any troop can be the other team, drawn from its enemy images.

No new art, no image files, no fonts: the courts, the ball, the sparks, the HUD and the lettering are all drawn by the plugin. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: PeltCore.js — Pelt 1/3 - the rules: team dodgeball on a court with a depth lane, throw, catch, dodge, outs and returns, specials from skills. No engine code. PeltDraw.js — Pelt 2/3 - the drawing: five courts, the ball, sparks, the HUD and arcade lettering.
