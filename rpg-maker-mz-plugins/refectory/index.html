Refectory: Plated Food and Dishes for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Refectory is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws each dish your cooking or crafting system makes, from 20 dish forms and 16 vessels. It reads the ingredients that went in and plates the meal: a vessel for the kind of dish, the main component, a sauce, garnish, steam if it is hot, and a menu card naming it. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

What is inside 4 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 20 dish forms · 16 vessels · 10 finishes · 2 fonts under the SIL Open Font License · source included One notetag on an item lists its ingredients; change them and the plate changes. A cooking result scene, called after your crafting plugin or whenever the party gains a dish. A tavern menu that plates the selected dish and can sell it at its database price.

Using a dish from the Item menu plates it, with its description under its name. 3 plugin commands: Serve Dish, Show Dish and Open Tavern Menu. Forty dishes from one demo database. Ten finishes, each chosen by what went into the pot. Works with RPG Maker MZ, tested in the real engine with the stock scripts. Copy the four files into js/plugins in any order and the two fonts into fonts/.
