Sampler is 3 RPG Maker MZ plugins that add generated picross puzzles, each one finished as a cross-stitch sampler in an embroidery hoop. Royalty free in commercial games.

What is inside 3 plugin files · charts of 5, 10 or 15 stitches · 4 motif forms · 4 stitched wreaths · 6 thread palettes · no image files The puzzle: fill the chart from the number clues along its rows and columns, on woven linen with a gilt needle cursor and clue tapes that grey out as each line comes right The finish: on the last stitch the threads go in from the centre outwards, and the piece is framed in a wooden hoop with a brass clamp and a plaque naming it Fair by construction: a line-logic solver works each chart before the player sees it and adds a few given stitches where the clues leave cells undecided, so every puzzle has one answer and never needs a guess Your icons: turn any icon in your own IconSet into a chart One command: Open Sampler on an event sets the size, form, threads and title.

The event waits, and a switch and a variable tell you when the chart is finished Input and saves: keyboard, mouse and touch.
