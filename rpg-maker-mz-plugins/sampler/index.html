Sampler - Picross Puzzles Stitched as Cross-Stitch for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Picross for your RPG, finished as needlework. The player fills a chart from the number clues along its rows and columns. When the chart is right, the stitches take their colours from the centre out, and the finished piece is set in an embroidery hoop as a cross-stitch sampler, inside a stitched wreath, with a brass plaque. Every chart is generated: the motif, its thread colours and the size (5, 10 or 15 stitches).

Nothing is loaded from an image file. You can also turn any icon in your own IconSet into a chart. One plugin command on an event opens it; the event waits, and a switch and a variable tell you when it is finished. And every chart is proven fair.

A line-logic solver works each puzzle before the player sees it, and where the clues alone leave cells undecided a few stitches are given until the solver completes the chart.

So every puzzle has exactly one answer and never needs a guess. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
