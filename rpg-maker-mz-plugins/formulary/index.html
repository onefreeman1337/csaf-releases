Formulary is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws each of your recipes as its own alchemical plate, from 58 motifs and 9 palettes. The ingredients sit as glyphs in a ring, their proportions as an arc, the process as a vessel and the result at the centre. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

What is inside 4 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 58 motifs · 9 palettes · 302,400 possible plates · source included 24 reagent glyphs by material family, 10 vessels for the process, 14 seals for the result and 10 borders for the tier. One notetag on the result declares a recipe; anything you leave out is derived from the item's name and data.

Recipes the party has not earned show as redacted pages that fill in as each ingredient is found. A recipe book scene with a virtualised index, and 6 plugin commands. Recipes from a crafting plugin you already use can be added as an extra source. The plate is a reading of the recipe. A recipe the party has not earned stays redacted. Works with RPG Maker MZ, tested in the real engine with the stock scripts.
