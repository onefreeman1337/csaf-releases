Smithereens is a breakable objects plugin for RPG Maker MZ: 6 materials that each break their own way, cut from the pixels of your own art. Royalty free. One note tag on an event, with no fragment images and no animations to draw. Every fragment is cut from the exact frame your event is showing, so it matches your art in any style and at any size.

What is inside 6 materials and a generic break · 181 stock MZ breakables recognised · 3 plugin commands · 6 parameters · 3 plugin files · source included Ceramic: curved sherds that spiral out from the blow and bounce Wood: staves split along the grain, tumbling in quarter turns Stone: angular chunks, more of them on bigger rocks, heavy and low Crystal: bright facets fanning from the blow, fast and far Ice: a few wide slabs split by one straight crack, sliding a long way Foliage: the stub stays rooted and the top becomes clippings that flutter Write <smash> in an event's note and the material is found for you: your own note tag first, then your terrain tags and regions, then a built in table of 181 of RPG Maker MZ's own stock breakables.

That table holds sheet names and index numbers only and ships no artwork.
