Hedgerow - Ambient Animals for RPG Maker MZ: Rabbits, Squirrels, Mice, Cats and More That Flee the Party is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

The animals that walk your maps. Hedgerow fills your maps with the small animals a real place has. Rabbits graze on the green, squirrels live at the foot of the trees, mice run along the base of a wall to their holes, lizards bask on the rocks, crabs sidle on the sand, a cat dozes by the fire, and after dark hedgehogs, foxes, badgers and snails come out. Walk up to them and each one flees in its own way.

Nothing to draw and nothing to place. The plugin reads each map once (its walls, its trees, its rocks, its sand and its fireplaces) and decides who lives where. All 44 coats in 13 families are drawn in code, in the four facings a character has. No sprite sheets and no events.

MZ's own trees, boulders, sand and fireplaces work the moment you switch it on. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: any order works (each file finds its siblings the first time it needs them); this one is tidy.
