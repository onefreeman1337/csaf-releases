Timber - Tree Cutting for RPG Maker MZ: Your Own Tileset Trees Topple, Burst and Split Into Logs is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Every tree in your own tileset can be cut down. Timber turns the trees already painted on your maps into trees the player can fell. Face a tree and press OK: it shudders, a notch opens in its own bark, chips and leaves fly. The last chop fells it with weight.

It tips slowly, then fast, lands with a bounce and a small shake, its crown bursts into its own leaves, the trunk splits into logs, and a stump with rings stays on the spot. Over play time a sapling grows from the stump and the tree comes back. Nothing to draw. Every leaf, chip, log, splinter, stump and sapling is cut from the pixels of the tree you painted, whatever tileset it comes from.

No tree sprite sheets, no event on every tree. MZ's own stock trees work the moment you switch it on. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
