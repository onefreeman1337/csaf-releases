Timber is a tree cutting plugin for RPG Maker MZ with 6 kinds of fall, cut from the trees already painted on your maps, with nothing to draw. Royalty free. Face a tree and press OK: it shudders, a notch opens in its own bark, and chips and leaves fly. The last chop fells it. It tips slowly, then fast, its crown bursts into its own leaves, the trunk splits into logs, and a stump with rings stays on the spot.

Over play time a sapling grows from the stump and the tree comes back. Every leaf, chip, log, splinter, stump and sapling is cut from the pixels of the tree you painted, with no event on every tree. MZ's own stock trees work the moment you switch it on.
