Battle ground for every enemy troop in RPG Maker MZ: 10 grounds, 14 marks, 28 pieces of furniture Bivouac is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that reads the troops you already made and draws the ground each band holds. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free, with no royalties.

Under every battle it draws the worked earth, a shadow for each battler, what the band brought and the tracks it left coming in, with no notetags and nothing placed by hand. What is inside 10 grounds · 14 marks · 28 pieces of furniture · 52 drawn things in all · 9 parameters · 4 plugin commands · 3 raw JavaScript files Member positions set the shape of the ground and where the furniture stands.

Each enemy's eight parameters set its nature: raider, warded, quick, adept or great. Experience sets the band's standing, from rabble to champion. A rabble gets crates and a spit; only a champion gets a dais. Element traits set the earth: fire scorches it, ice rimes it, water mires it. Fighting the same troop again wears its ground and belongings down. Nothing was configured between these four.

The only thing that changed is which troop the battle started.
