Vigil brings the Souls loop to RPG Maker MZ in 3 plugin files: rest at a shrine, fall, and walk back to the echo that holds what you dropped. Royalty free, with no image files and no common events to author.

What is inside 3 plugin files · 5 shrine forms · 5 fuels · 6 relics · 4 weathers · 9 altar offerings · RPG Maker MZ · source included Put <vigil: shrine> in an event's note and give it no image, and the plugin draws a shrine on that tile from a form, a fuel, a relic and a weathering. Leave them unset and the map and region choose, the same way every time.

Resting heals the party, sets where it wakes after a fall, brings back defeated enemies on every map, and can write the autosave and open the altar. Enemy notes: respawn, respawn stronger (every parameter up 25 percent per rest, 4 times at most unless you change it), or never. The altar spends what the party carries on a level or on any of the eight parameters, labelled with your own database terms.

Carry gold, a variable, or embers: battle EXP held until spent, priced on MZ's own EXP curve. A party wipe, in battle or on the map, wakes the party at the last shrine.
