Vigil - Souls-like Rest Shrines for RPG Maker MZ: Bonfires, Respawning Enemies, Echoes Where You Fell is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

The Souls loop for RPG Maker MZ. Rest at a shrine. Fall. Walk back for what you dropped. Developers have been building this loop by hand for years: a bonfire that heals and saves, enemies that come back when you rest, a place to spend what you carry, and a bloodstain where you died that gives it all back if you reach it.

Built from events, it needs a bloodstain event on every map and breaks when you die on a different map from your last rest. Vigil is the whole loop as one plugin set, and it draws every part of it itself. Tag an event <vigil: shrine> and a shrine stands on that tile.

No image files ship with Vigil and there are no common events to write. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
