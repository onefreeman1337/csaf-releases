Fete - Festival Dressings for Your Own Towns, RPG Maker MZ: Bunting, Lanterns, Stalls, Placed From Your Map is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Your own towns, dressed for their festivals.

Fete is a plugin for RPG Maker MZ that dresses the town maps you already built: bunting strung eave to eave and zigzagged across the lanes, lamp posts along the house fronts and the roads with lights strung between them, banners and wreaths on free wall, market stalls round the square and a centrepiece in the middle of it, with leaves, petals, sky lanterns, motes or wisps drifting over it all.

Nothing is placed by hand and nothing is written into your map. Fete reads where your houses are (roofs, eaves, front walls), the gaps between them, your lanes and your square, using the engine's own tile arithmetic, and puts the festival there. Switch it on with a notetag, a switch or a plugin command and it arrives on screen: strings run out from post to post, posts, stalls and the centrepiece rise into place.

Switch it off and your town is exactly as you made it. Every decoration is drawn by the plugin's code.
