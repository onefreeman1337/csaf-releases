Byplay gives every walking sprite in your RPG Maker MZ game the acting it cannot do, in three parts (10 plugin files): 15 reactions, 10 held moods, and a whole room answering a line together. Three parts, 10 raw JavaScript plugin files, no image files.

Every pose is drawn from each character's own walking sheet, in its own colours, in all four facings, the first time it is needed: no pose art to draw for the hero, the followers or any event. Reactions and Moods each work alone; Company needs Reactions' two files. All three were tested together on RPG Maker MZ 1.10.0.
