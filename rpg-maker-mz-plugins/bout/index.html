Bout - One-on-One Fighting Game Scene for RPG Maker MZ: Your Actors, Specials From Their Skills is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A one-on-one fighting game, inside your RPG. Bout is a real-time duel scene for RPG Maker MZ. Call one plugin command and two of your actors square off side-on, best of three rounds against the clock: walk, jump, crouch, guard, light and heavy attacks, a low attack, a throw, and specials made from each actor's own skills. Every actor in your database is a fighter on day one.

Bout fights with the side-view battler sheets your project already has, mapping MZ's own battle motions onto the fight.

No new art, no image files, no fonts: the arenas, bars, lettering, sparks and specials are all drawn by the plugin. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: BoutCore.js — Bout 1/3 - the rules: a real-time one-on-one fight, frame data, guard, rounds, specials from skills. No engine code. BoutDraw.js — Bout 2/3 - the drawing: arenas, health and guard bars, arcade lettering, element hit sparks and every special. No engine code.
