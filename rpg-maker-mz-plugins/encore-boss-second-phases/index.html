Encore: 12 Boss Second Phases for RPG Maker MZ is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Want the whole set? Encore gives RPG Maker MZ bosses a second phase drawn from their own battler: 12 forms and 4 drawn transformations, set with one note tag. Two plugin files, commercial use, no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy. These pictures use our own AI generated pixel art battlers in an AI illustrated hall; they are not included.

In your game the forms are made from your own enemy images, painted or pixel art. MZ's own Enemy Transform needs a second battler you draw yourself and shows only a swap. Encore makes the second phase for every boss in your database from the art you have, and draws the change.

What you get 12 phase two forms: Ascended, Corrupted, Enraged, Frozen, Armoured, Crystal, Overgrown, Rot, Overdrive, Shadow, Celestial and Husk. The creature stays recognisable and grows wings, flames, ice, crystals, vines, smoke or orbits from its own outline. 4 drawn transformations: swell, shatter, molt and burn, after a hold where the boss flashes and shakes. One note tag: <Encore: enraged 40% burn>.

More tags give more phases. A phase can raise the boss's stats or turn it into another enemy.
