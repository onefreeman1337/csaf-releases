Escutcheon is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that draws your party HUD as plates of worked metal, from 10 forms and 11 materials, with no image files. Each character gets a plate with a named rim, rivets, a device struck in the centre and gauges that end in a finial or a boss. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

What is inside 5 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ · 10 plate forms · 11 materials · 7 rims · 8 devices · 6 gauge ends · source included 10 plate forms, from slab and heater to roundel and strap, so each shape reads at HUD size. 11 materials, iron to gold, plus oak and card, each built differently rather than re-tinted. 7 worked rims, 8 struck devices and 6 gauge terminations.

Nothing to configure: the form comes from the class, the metal from the class and seniority, the device from the character's name. On a stock MZ project the starting party of four resolves to four shapes, four metals and four devices. The class decides the plate's outline. Eleven materials, each a different construction. Works with RPG Maker MZ, tested in the engine with the stock scripts and zero console errors.
