Barrage - Undertale-Style Dodge Battles for RPG Maker MZ (bullet patterns generated from your enemies) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Every enemy attack becomes a dodge. When an enemy uses a skill that damages your party, the battle pauses and a framed arena opens. The player steers a small heart through that enemy's attack: embers spiralling out of a burning point, a wall of feathers with one gap, leaves sweeping across, rings of notes. Every hit the heart takes scales the damage. Touch nothing and the attack misses. Nothing to author.

Barrage composes every attack from the database you already have. What flies comes from the skill's element name (Fire throws embers), else the enemy's own name (a Crow throws feathers), else its strongest stat. How it flies is one of seven patterns chosen by the skill's hit type and scope.

How dense and fast comes from the attacker's stats against the median of your own Enemies database, and a boss attacks in phases. Notetags override any of it. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
