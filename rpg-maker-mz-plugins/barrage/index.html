Barrage is 3 RPG Maker MZ plugins that turn every enemy attack into an Undertale style dodge, composed from your own database. Royalty free in commercial games. What is inside 3 plugin files · 7 patterns · 18 drawn forms · 5 notetags · no image files, every shot drawn in code The dodge: when an enemy uses a damaging skill, the player steers a heart through the attack.

No hits is a miss, and each hit adds half the skill's damage, up to one and a half times (both adjustable) Nothing to author: what flies comes from the skill's element name, else the enemy's name, else its strongest stat.

How it flies comes from the skill's hit type and scope Difficulty from your data: density and speed come from the attacker's stats against the median of your Enemies database Bosses: an enemy with 3 times the median Max HP attacks in two phases of different patterns, three at 6 times Input: arrow keys, a gamepad, the mouse or a finger Notetags: <Barrage Form: ember>, <Barrage Pattern: ring>, <Barrage Tier: 3>, <Barrage Boss> and <Barrage Off> override any choice Seven patterns.
