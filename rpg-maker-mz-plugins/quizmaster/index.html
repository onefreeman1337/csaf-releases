Quizmaster - Quiz Shows and Exams for RPG Maker MZ (Questions Written From Your Database) is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

A quiz show for your RPG, with the questions written from your own database. Quizmaster runs a game-show quiz in your game: the party leader on a podium against up to three rival contestants, a timer ring draining, four answers to pick from. Or a solo exam with a pass mark: a school, a guild test, a royal examiner. You add one plugin command.

Quizmaster reads your items, skills, enemies, actors and classes, writes the questions itself, and draws the stage, the cards, the tiles and the podiums. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.

Plugin Manager order: QuizmasterCore.js — Quizmaster 1/3 - the rules: questions written from your database, the quiz, the rivals. No engine code. QuizmasterDraw.js — Quizmaster 2/3 - the drawing kit: stages, question cards, answer tiles, timer, marks. No engine code. Quizmaster.js — Quizmaster 3/3 - quiz shows and exams for MZ, the questions written from your own database.
