Quizmaster is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for quiz shows and exams on 6 drawn stages, with the questions written from your own database. Royalty free in your games. The party leader on a podium against up to three rivals, a timer ring draining, four answers to pick from. You add one plugin command and Quizmaster writes the questions.

What is inside 6 stages · 11 question kinds · 392 questions from the stock database alone · show and exam modes · 3 plugin files · MZ 1.x · source included Questions on what an item costs, a skill's MP cost and element, which class learns it, an enemy's weakness or gold drop, and more.

Your own database writes more as you add to it Every answer is checked against your data, and a question is only written when exactly one choice is right Each card pictures its subject with your own art: the item's icon, the enemy's image, the actor's face Rivals answer right about 45%, 65% or 85% of the time, so a show can be lost.

A right answer earns 10 points plus up to 10 for speed Or a solo exam with a pass mark: a school, a guild test, a royal examiner Game Show, Classroom, Tavern Trivia, Arcane Exam, Royal Court and Carnival, all drawn in code.
