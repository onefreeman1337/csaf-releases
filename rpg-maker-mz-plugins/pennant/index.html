Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Capture the flag, played by your own party. What this is, in plain words: Pennant is an RPG Maker MZ plugin (three JavaScript files, MZ 1.x) that adds a real-time capture-the-flag minigame to your game. Your party plays a troop from your database, or a team of rival actors, two to four a side, on one of 5 fields the plugin draws in code.

Tag raiders in your own half and they are marched into your pen; cross into their half, lift their banner and carry it home over the midline to score. Free your teammates by reaching the gate of their pen. Each player gets field abilities made from its own skills (6 kinds), and each side flies a banner struck from its leader's name.

The players are your own side-view battlers and enemy images, so it works on day one with no new art. Commercial use in your games is included. Start it in one step: an event's Plugin Command, Pennant > Start Match, with the troop or rivals, the strength, the field, the side size and the captures to win.
