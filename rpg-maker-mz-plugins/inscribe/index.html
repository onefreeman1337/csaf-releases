Inscribe - Name Entry and Riddle Answers for RPG Maker MZ: Six Rites, Drawn Keys, Generated Sigils is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

Names and answers, written onto an object. Inscribe replaces RPG Maker MZ's name screen. The stock screen is a grid of letters in a window; Inscribe draws the moment as an object being written on: a guest register signed in ink, a name carved in stone, travel papers stamped by the office, a typed form, a sigil traced into a circle, a headstone at night.

The player types on the keyboard, or presses keys drawn in the object's own material with a pad, a mouse or a finger. The same scene asks questions. Inscribe Answer checks what the player writes against the answers you accept, forgiving case and spacing and, when you allow it, accents, punctuation and a typo or two. Right or wrong shows on the object: gilded, stamped, struck out, cracked.

The result goes to a switch, the attempts and the typed text to variables. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
