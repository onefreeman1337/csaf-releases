Aloft is an RPG Maker MZ plugin that turns any of your maps into islands in the sky: 5 dressings, 49 kinds of art, a rock underside cut from your own edge tiles and a sea of clouds below. Put <aloft> in the map's note and leave the sky empty or paint it with a region. Every connected patch of ground becomes its own island with its own underside, hangers and soft shadow on the clouds.

Your map file never changes, and sky cells cannot be walked on. What is inside 3 plugin files · RPG Maker MZ 1.x · raw readable source · 5 dressings · 49 kinds of art drawn in code Spirit Garden: earth strata, tangled roots, ivy, flowering vines and a waterfall off the edge, over white cumulus. Sky Ruins: cut stone in stepped tiers, chains, broken arches and torn banners, over long stratus banks.

Crystal Spire: geode rock with glowing veins and crystal clusters that pulse. Steam Isle: a riveted hull with brass bands, pipes, gears, a turning propeller and glowing lanterns. Storm Reach: jagged slate and lightning rods over dark anvil clouds lit by lightning, with rain. Switch a map's dressing in its note (<aloft: storm>) or at any moment with a plugin command.
