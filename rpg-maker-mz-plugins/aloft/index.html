Your own maps, as islands in the sky. Aloft is an RPG Maker MZ plugin (three JavaScript files, MZ 1.x) that turns any map into a floating island. Put <aloft> in the map's note and leave the sky empty or paint it with a region.

Under every southern edge of your ground it hangs a rock underside cut from your own edge tiles' pixels and tinted by them, and the empty map becomes open air over a sea of clouds drifting far below. Five dressings, 49 kinds of art, all generated, no image files: everything is drawn in code when the map loads. Commercial use in your games is included. Nothing to draw and nothing to place.

Every connected patch of ground becomes its own island with its own underside, its own hangers and its own soft shadow on the clouds. A wide island hangs a deep keel; a small islet hangs a small cone.

Your map file never changes, and sky cells cannot be walked on. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests. Plugin Manager order: any order works (each file finds its siblings the first time it needs them); this one is tidy.
