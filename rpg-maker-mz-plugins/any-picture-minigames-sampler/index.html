Three free RPG Maker MZ minigames made from any picture in your project. Any Picture Minigames Sampler is three free plugins for RPG Maker MZ (1.x core scripts). Jigsaw Lite cuts any picture from your img/pictures folder into a 9 piece jigsaw the player assembles on a drawn felt table. Pendant Lite hangs the picture twice in a gilt gallery with 3 generated differences to find.

Unveil Lite hides it under a silk veil the player cuts away, Qix style. One plugin command each, one result switch each, a demo picture included. Free to use in commercial and non commercial games. Each is a small fixed cut of a paid plugin from the same studio, cut from its code: one size of Jigsaw's 4 to 256 pieces, one 3 difference round of Pendant's 1 to 12 of four kinds, one board of Unveil's three veils.

Upgrade and the Lite's command hands the game to the full plugin, so no event has to change. ⚠️ Compatibility — the question you actually have Tested against RPG Maker MZ with the stock scripts unmodified, verified by running the plugin in the real engine rather than only in tests.
