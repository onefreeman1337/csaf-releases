Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Colophon is a save and load screen for RPG Maker MZ that prints every slot as a leaf, composed from 45 drawn parts by what that save contains. Use it in as many games as you like, commercial or free.

Six things are read out of each save, and each one drives part of the drawing: the printer's device from what the run has spent its time doing, the paper from where the save was taken, the binding from how far in the run is, the wear from playtime, the ink and letterform from your project's own vocabulary, and the small register marks along the foot from who is in the party.

Six saves of one playthrough print as six different leaves. What is inside 45 drawn entities · 15 printer's devices · 8 papers · 6 bindings · 5 states of wear · 6 register marks · 5 houses · 5 plugin files of readable JavaScript The device grows with the run: a plain struck roundel, then a motto scroll, then a scalloped cartouche with a crest.

Saves made before you installed it still print, from what the stock header carries, marked collation unrecorded. Five parameters: shelf columns and rows, shelf tone, a heading, and whether to print the level, fields and paces.
