Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Lineage is an evolution atlas plugin for RPG Maker MZ: it draws each species from your database with 65 body plans, 8 element palettes and 6 growth stages. That makes 3,120 possible marks and no image files, under a licence for unlimited commercial or free games with no royalties. What the player sees Generations, ruled and numbered, so descent reads at a glance.

Branches that curve between them, solid gold where this playthrough went and dashed where it did not, with your own label on the branches at the cursor. Undiscovered species as hollow silhouettes of their real shape, so the player can see what they are hunting. A clade rail across the foot, built from your data: each clade, a real mark from it and how many have been found.

A specimen plate with the body plan, element, stage and what the species can become. Two notetags <lineage plan:wolf palette:ember stage:juvenile> <lineage evolves:2,3 when:"level 16"> Everything is optional. Leave out plan: and one is chosen from the enemy's name, so the same name gives the same mark on every machine. when: is a label printed on the branch, and Lineage never evaluates it.
