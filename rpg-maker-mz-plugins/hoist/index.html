Its title or description names RPG Maker MZ.

Hoist is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for Zelda style pick up and throw, with lift, carry and throw poses in all 4 facings made from your own walking sprites. Three plugin files and eight sample objects, commercial use with no royalty. Read the documentation before you buy. These pictures use our own knight, our own objects and a yard painted in code. On your map the poses are made from your own sheets.

RPG Maker users have asked for lift sprites since at least 2014, and the free pick up and throw plugins move the object while the hero stands as before: buyers on their pages say they drew their own lift sprites. Hoist draws them for you.

What you get Three poses for each walking sheet, in all 4 facings: a squat that reaches for the object, a carry walk with both hands under it and the legs still stepping, and the throw. Made when the sheet loads, from its own arms and colours. Any event can be lifted with the note tag <hoist>: a tile from your tileset or a character sheet frame.

The throw arcs from overhead over a shadow, up to 3 tiles by default, stops short of walls and splashes into water. Where it lands it breaks into pieces of its own picture, or lands whole with <hoist: keep>.
