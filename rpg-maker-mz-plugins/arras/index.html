Arras — the playthrough, woven is an RPG Maker MZ plugin for RPG Maker, paid.

A hanging that records what your player actually did. Arras takes the switches your game set, the variables it moved, the victories and the choices, and weaves them into an embroidered frieze in the Bayeux manner. A continuous band of linen that the player can open and scroll, that grows as the game is played, and that is different in every save file because it is made out of that save file.

Nothing in it is shipped art. There is no PNG, no atlas and no sprite sheet in the package. Every figure, every border, every thread and every letter is drawn by the plugin.

What a buyer gets, in one line Seventy authored entities — 16 figure archetypes, 10 border registers, 10 scene compositions, 6 dye sets, 5 grounds, and a 23-letter alphabet stitched stroke by stroke — assembled into a frieze that is a function of the playthrough and of nothing else. Three ways to record an act, and you only need one Watched switches, no scripting at all.

List a switch and a line of text in the Plugin Manager. The moment it turns ON, the act is woven. If your project already has switches like "Chapter 3 complete", the work is already done. Watched variables. The same, on a variable reaching a value.
