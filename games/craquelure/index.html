CRAQUELURE is a Game Boy Color game about catching forged paintings, with 64 generated panels and three lamps. Three lots play free in your browser right here. A panel arrives with a docket claiming a date. One region of it may be painted in a pigment that did not exist yet. Change the lamp until the paint gives itself away, then check the date in the reference book.

What is inside 64 paintings · 6 regions each · 3 lamps · 8 lots per shift · a 256 kB Game Boy Color ROM Daylight shows only the colour family: lead, zinc and titanium white are one white. Ultraviolet splits most of them: zinc white glows yellow green and titanium white goes black. Raking light shows the surface: old hand ground pigment is gritty, and a modern one barely crazes.

The dates are real first availability dates: Prussian blue 1706, cobalt 1802, zinc white 1834, titanium white 1921. Some panels are honest. Accusing one costs a strike, and so does clearing a forgery. One panel under all three lamps. The blue cloth turns green under ultraviolet. Sixteen of the 64 panels. The cartridge composes and dithers every one itself, cracks included.
