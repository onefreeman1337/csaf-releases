Stringline is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

Every clue is a line you draw. Crossbow bolts are stuck in the walls of Castle Harrowgate and nobody saw the bow. You are the magistrate's stringer. Press on a bolt and drag its red string back along the shaft; where you think it glanced off a pillar or a piece of iron, pull the string past it and swing it round. When every string ends in the same place, that is where the archer stood.

Call the hearing and the court checks your strings against the rules. 15 cases, 40 bolts, 3 rooms. The Great Hall, the Chapel of Saint Orm and the Gatehouse, each case laid out once and solvable only one way. Four rules, learned one at a time. Stone throws a bolt back only when it comes in low. Iron sends it off one angle flatter. A bolt goes straight through a banner. How much shaft shows tells you how far it flew.

The straight answer is usually wrong. Pull every string through every surface and they meet in several places. Only the physics narrows it to one. Three candles a case. A string that breaks a rule burns one. Solve it without burning any for a clean seal. Play the free demo in your browser The first 3 cases are free to play right here, in the Great Hall.
