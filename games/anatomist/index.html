ANATOMIST is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

A real 32 KB Game Boy cartridge. The free browser demo is right here on this page — floors one to four, no download, no account. Updated to 1.2.0: descending now shows a DEPTH card instead of a blank screen, the depth number reads as a number, death and the win fade to black, and the browser demo runs at Game Boy speed on any monitor.

You descend a dungeon whose every monster is composed while you play, out of authored body parts, and the nine tells below are the rules. The rest of the body — most heads, and the wings and tail on a creature’s back — is variety, not information. Many legs — it moves twice for every step you take. Roots instead of legs — it never moves at all. A hazard, not a hunter.

A scythe arm — it reaches one square further than you expect. A stub where an arm should be — it cannot hurt you. Walk past it. A shell on its back — the first hit you land does half damage. Spines on its back — hitting it costs you a life. Every time. A heavy torso — six life. A gaunt one — two. No eyes — it is blind: once awake it wanders instead of coming for you, until you are beside it.

There is no bestiary to memorise, because there is no fixed bestiary.
