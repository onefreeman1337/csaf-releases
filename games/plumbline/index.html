PLUMBLINE is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

A metroidvania with no keys, no upgrades and no items. Every door in this game was already open to somebody who knew where it was. The only thing that changes between the start and the end is what you know. You are lowered into a sealed shaft to find out why the last three surveys disagree. You carry a plumb line: a weight on a cord that reports the true depth of a place whose layout lies about it.

What you actually do The weight goes where you cannot. It is small enough to fall through a barred floor that you are not. Set it down in the right place and something answers. The line measures true depth, and the rooms are drawn so that judging a place by eye gives the wrong answer. Trust the instrument rather than the picture.

The game is about the places where the two disagree, and you are never asked to count tiles yourself. Two numbers sit on screen and they measure different things. The reel shows how much cord is paid out, and the cord is ten marks long, so it stops there because that is all the cord there is. The reading shows the true depth of wherever the weight came to rest, which is why it is often the larger of the two.
