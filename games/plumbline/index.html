PLUMBLINE is a short metroidvania for Windows with no keys, no upgrades and no items: the only thing that opens the way forward is what you learn. The paid Windows download is the full descent: all 4 regions, both remaining gates and the ending. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory using AI tools. A metroidvania with no keys, no upgrades and no items. Every door in this game was already open to somebody who knew where it was. The only thing that changes between the start and the end is what you know.

You are lowered into a sealed shaft to find out why the last three surveys disagree. You carry a plumb line: a weight on a cord that reports the true depth of a place whose layout lies about it. What you actually do The weight goes where you cannot. It is small enough to fall through a barred floor that you are not. Set it down in the right place and something answers.

The line measures true depth, and the rooms are drawn so that judging a place by eye gives the wrong answer.
