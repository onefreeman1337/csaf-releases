The Turning Year

A game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Adventure, Puzzle. Paid; free browser demo on the page.

The Turning Year is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

Ice is a bridge. Flood is a road. Drought is a door. One overworld, drawn four times over (spring, summer, autumn, winter) and one dial that turns the whole world between them. Hold the dial and the season moves. Let go and the year slides home. The free browser demo is on this page. Play the Meadow and the Mere right here, no install. The Windows download adds the Fell, the Dunes and the ending.

Nothing in this game is ever unlocked. There is no key, no upgrade, no gate that opens because you found the thing that opens it.

The only thing that changes is what you have worked out about the year: that the brook will carry you if you freeze it and keep it frozen, that the Mere rises high enough to float a boat over its own sluice for a few seconds before the ice locks the level, that the door below the lowest water is reachable exactly once a year and only if you have spent summer somewhere else. And the year fights you the whole time.

Push a season deeper than the land's own and strain builds: the ring around the dial fills, the turn gets weaker, and eventually the year slips back under your hands. You are never holding winter. You are holding winter for now.
