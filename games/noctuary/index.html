NOCTUARY is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

NOCTUARY is a nonogram (picross) puzzle game for Windows: sixty-six picture-logic plates, and each one you solve develops into a mounted natural history specimen with a Latin name, a catalogue number and a line from the collector's journal. It is a calm game with no timer and its own music.

The free browser demo on this page holds two drawers of the cabinet; the paid Windows download is the full cabinet of sixty-six plates, and a cabinet you start in the demo carries on where it was. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory using AI tools. Sixty-six nonogram plates. Solve one and it develops.

The grid of squares you have just filled in resolves into a mounted specimen — in colour, at full resolution, with a Latin name, a catalogue number and a line from the collector’s journal. Then you file it in the drawer and open the next one. New in 1.2.0. EXAMINE now also finds a square you crossed that the specimen fills, and when The Reading runs out it points you to EXAMINE.

Row clues with a two-digit number are spaced by the width of the numbers, so 1 10 1 no longer reads as 110 1. The free demo below now holds only its own two drawers; the full cabinet's plates come with the download.
