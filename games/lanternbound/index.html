LANTERNBOUND is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

LANTERNBOUND is a short turn-based JRPG in an SNES-era style: a party of three, one oil lantern that burns down the whole time you are out, and a moor road whose lamps have gone dark. Every ability pushes a colour onto the lantern, and matching an enemy's telegraphed colour sequence breaks its attack before it lands. The whole game is free to play in your browser on this page.

The paid download is the same game as a standalone Windows build you keep and can play offline. Roughly thirty minutes to reach the barrow the first time. Made by Core Systems Asset Factory using AI tools. The lamps along the moor road have gone out, one by one. Three of you walk out from Aldermoor to find out why, carrying one oil lantern between you.

It burns down the whole time you are out there, and it is the only light in the world. LANTERNBOUND is a turn-based JRPG where the lantern is not a meter. It is the lighting, the fuel and the information, all at once. While it is lit you can read what the thing in front of you is about to do. When it goes out, the telegraph is replaced by three question marks and you are guessing.
