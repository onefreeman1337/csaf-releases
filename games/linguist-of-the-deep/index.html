Linguist of the Deep

A game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Puzzle. Paid; free browser demo on the page.

Linguist of the Deep is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

Learn a language nobody will teach you. An alien reef is talking. Shoals, rays, anglers and eels speak to each other in short sentences of glowing glyphs, and then they do what they said. Nothing in the game ever tells you what a word means. You watch, you keep a logbook of what you saw, you pencil in your guesses, and when a creature blocks your way you raise your lamp and speak to it.

The right three words move the world. 18 glyphs, 30 overheard scenes, 7 zones. Every word can be worked out from scenes you can reach before you need it, and no gate's sentence is ever shown to you whole. You compose the answer; you never copy it. A logbook that draws what you saw. Every scene you overhear is kept as the picture it happened in, with its sentence. Select a glyph and every scene that uses it lights up.

The deeper you go, the darker the water. Speaking lights you up, and in the dark the eels hunt light. A bite costs you ground, never the game. An ending that reads your notes. The whole language is revealed beside your own pencilled guesses.
