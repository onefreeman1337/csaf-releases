GLASSWRIGHT

A game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Paid; free browser demo on the page.

GLASSWRIGHT is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

Twelve commissions. One season. A shop you can lose. A patron arrives with a hole in a wall and an opinion. A form to fill, a fee, a budget, a deadline, and something they want — light, or mourning, or their guild's colour leading the window, or simply that you not spend their money. You choose a tracery, a palette, how finely to lead it and how to finish it.

The workshop cuts the glass, the kiln takes its days, and the patron judges what actually came out — not what you picked off a menu. There is no picture of a window anywhere in this game Every window you see — on this page, on the title screen, on the bench, in your gallery — is computed at the moment you look at it, from 45 AI-authored traceries, 12 glass palettes, 10 window forms, 5 lead densities and 5 finishes.

No texture, no atlas, no sprite, no AI-generated illustration. Move one slider and the glass is cut again in front of you. That is also why the patrons are hard to please. When you fire a window the workshop measures the real thing: how bright it reads, how much contrast holds it together at a distance, which glass ended up covering the most of it, how many pieces were cut, and what they cost at today's prices.
