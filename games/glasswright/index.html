GLASSWRIGHT: Stained Glass Workshop Game, Free Demo is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

GLASSWRIGHT is a stained glass workshop management game for Windows with a free browser demo: 12 commissions, one season and a shop you can lose. Each patron brings a brief, a budget and a deadline. You choose the tracery, the palette, the leading and the finish, and the patron judges the window that actually came out of the kiln.

The demo plays the first 4 commissions; the paid download is the full season, ending with the West Window. Every window is computed No window in the game is a picture file. Each one is drawn by the game's code the moment you look at it, from 45 traceries, 12 glass palettes, 10 window forms, 5 lead densities and 5 finishes. Move one control and the glass is cut again in front of you.

When you fire a window the workshop measures it: how bright it reads, how much contrast holds it together, which glass covers most of it, how many pieces were cut and what they cost at this season's prices. The patron reacts to those numbers, so the only way to meet a brief is to make a window that comes out the way they asked.

The workshop is a business The glass market drifts every season, so the same design is cheap one month and dear the next. Wages are real.
