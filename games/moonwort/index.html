MOONWORT

A game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Role Playing. Free.

MOONWORT is a game, free to play in the browser.

MOONWORT is a short single-player narrative RPG for Windows, with a free demo you can play in your browser on this page. This is Episode One: one night in a physic garden, three roads, four creatures and four turn-based duels, fought with spells you compose from the plants you gather.

The game draws every plant plate and every spell seal itself, from each plant's own data: 26 plants, 33 spells and 59 plates, each drawn five ways for the five faces of the moon. The demo is the first road; the paid download is the whole night, including the lunar dial. Written in plain JavaScript with no engine by Core Systems Asset Factory, using AI tools for code, art and text.

You are the warden of a physic garden, and last night the moon changed its mind. Every night for eleven years someone has taken the reading at Lowmoor: what the moon is doing, and what the beds are doing about it. Tonight the moon is wrong, and the beds have agreed with it. Moonwort has thrown three bells where the book promises five. Rimeleaf has opened flat.

Something on the chalk downs has gone over into inflorescence and is starving, because what it has become does not know how to eat.
