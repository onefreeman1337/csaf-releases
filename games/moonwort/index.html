MOONWORT

A game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Role Playing. Free.

MOONWORT is a game, free to play in the browser.

You are the warden of a physic garden, and last night the moon changed its mind. Every night for eleven years someone has taken the reading at Lowmoor: what the moon is doing, and what the beds are doing about it. Tonight the moon is wrong, and the beds have agreed with it. Moonwort has thrown three bells where the book promises five. Rimeleaf has opened flat.

Something on the chalk downs has gone over into inflorescence and is starving, because what it has become does not know how to eat. You have one night, three roads, and a book of empty plates. The spell art is the recipe, rendered This is not a game with pictures of spells in it.

Every working draws its own seal from the actual plants composing it — their root form, their leaf shape, their bloom form and count, their humour. Change a reagent and the lattice changes, because the lattice was never a drawing. The same is true of the plants.

Every plate in the book is generated from that plant’s live state, so a herb cut under the wrong moon is drawn differently — not recoloured, drawn: buds where bells were, ranks of leaves the book does not mention at all.
