HOLLOWAY

HOLLOWAY is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

HOLLOWAY is a top-down adventure on a hand-built highland: a sword, four tools and three barrows. Every new game reshuffles which chest holds which tool or key, and which barrow sits behind which mouth. Before a seed is offered, the game's own solver plays it through and proves it finishable. Play the free demo in your browser above: Holloway Lane, the Sward and the first barrow with its boss, on four checked seeds.

The full game is the Windows download. In the full game 24 hand-built overworld screens across seven regions: Holloway Lane, the Sward, the Scarp, the Tarn, the Crag, the Moor and the Barrow Gate. 3 barrows with 21 rooms: the Barrow, the Mere Halls and the Crag Halls, each with two small keys, a bronze seal and a boss.

3 bosses, each with one attack drawn on the floor before it lands: the wight's slam, the roper's lash and the wraith's wedge of dark. Each has a window only its barrow's tool opens. 4 tools that open the highland: bombs, the hook, the lantern and the boots. 14 shuffled chests. A chest with no tool or key pays a shard of heart; three shards make a heart. Seed codes: type any code on the title, or step to the next one.

Tagged Action-Adventure, Boss battle, Dungeon Crawler, Exploration, Fantasy, Pixel Art, randomizer, Singleplayer, Top-Down and zelda-like.
