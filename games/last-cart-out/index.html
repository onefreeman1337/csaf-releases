LAST CART OUT

A game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Racing. Free.

LAST CART OUT is a game, free to play in the browser.

LAST CART OUT is a one-thumb HTML5 mine cart escape you play in the browser: six levels, 26 bends, and a mine coming down behind you. It is free, it runs on a phone with nothing to install, and a clean run takes a little over two minutes. You have the last cart. Hold anywhere to drive. Let go to brake. Slide your thumb to lean into a bend.

That is every control, and the whole game is one question asked at speed: how late can you brake? Two ways to die, one thumb between them Too fast. Every bend has a red number. Go in over it and the cart tips onto two wheels, throwing sparks. Stay over it and you derail, and your miner carries on down the tunnel without you. Too slow. The fuse along the top is your lead on the collapse.

When the spark reaches the cart, you are buried. Blue numbers are gaps in the rail. Hit the lip slower than that and you fall in. Clear it and time slows for a moment while you hang in the air. When you crash, the card gives you the real numbers: you took it at 112, that bend takes 78. A tap puts you back at the gate of the level you were on, so you are riding again in a second.
