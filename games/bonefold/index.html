BONEFOLD is a top-down necromancer action game for Windows with a free browser demo: 12 rooms, 3 bosses and 13 kinds of foe that get up and fight for you. Whatever you kill gets up as exactly what it was. Try the whole first area, THE GALLOWS MOOR, right here on the page. You fight with a bone bolt and a quick step, and every body you drop can be raised where it lies. A raised crow is a crow: it flies.

A raised censer bearer still carries its fire. A troglodyte still breaks walls, and a rat still fits through a grate. So the horde you keep is your key ring as well as your army, and bones decide how big it can be. Two orders, held on buttons SEND the horde where you aim. Aim at a lever across the water and only the bodies that fly go. Aim at a bramble and the ones that burn go.

HOLD plants them where you stand, and they stand their ground. A pressure plate holds its gate while the horde on it weighs enough. LET FALL gives a body's bones back, so you can raise the kind the next room asks for. The body stays where it fell. Three areas, three bosses THE GALLOWS MOOR is free. THE WARRENS and THE CHOIR KEEP come with the full game.
