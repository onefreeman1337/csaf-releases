WARDLOCK is a logic puzzle game on a real Game Boy ROM with 48 warded locks, each shipped with its proven fewest moves. Play the first 16 free in your browser on this page. A lock is a set of wards, some shut and some already open. You have three or four levers, and throwing one flips a set of wards. Which set depends on the cam, a linkage with two or three phases, and some levers also turn the cam.

The channels engraved into the plate show which wards share a lever. They never tell you which lever or which phase. What is inside 48 locks · 6 tiers · 48 different plates · a 16 page manual · English and Spanish cartridges · undo up to 64 moves Six tiers, from BRASS at three moves to THE VAULT at thirteen The proven fewest moves for every lock, on the cartridge, one button away.
