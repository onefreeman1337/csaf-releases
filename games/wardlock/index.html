A locksmith's bench, a brass plate, and a lock that will not open until you understand it. WARDLOCK is a real Game Boy cartridge — a 32,768-byte ROM you can put on a flash cart or an Analogue Pocket — containing forty-eight warded locks. Play the first sixteen free in your browser, right here on this page. New in 1.4.0 The theme now rests.

The low drone under the melody used to sound on every screen without a break; now it fades and stops halfway through each phrase. SELECT on the docket (RIGHT SHIFT in the browser) turns the sound off and back on, and the docket says which. Two buttons pressed on the same frame are now both acted on instead of the second being dropped. How a lock works A lock is a set of wards, some shut and some already open.

Open all of them and it springs. You have three or four levers. Throwing one flips a set of wards — but which set depends on the cam, a linkage with two or three phases. Some levers also turn the cam and some leave it where it is (the PHASE readout shows which), so the same lever can do different work each time round.

Three levers against a three-phase cam is nine different effects to hold in your head, through two buttons.
