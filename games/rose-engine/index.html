ROSE ENGINE is a game, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

A rose engine is the lathe that cuts guilloche, the woven ornament on the face of a pocket watch. It works by moving a cutter against a rotating plate. Everything it draws is a rose curve. This cartridge points one at you. You are a burnisher loose on the plate. The lathe starts at the rim and works inward, cutting a figure around you. Fresh metal is black and it is hot, and it will end your dial.

A moment later it cools to grey and becomes finished ornament you can cross freely. Survive until the figure closes and the dial is yours. The figures are computed, not drawn There is no picture of any dial anywhere in the ROM. The cutter follows r = band + amp x cos(petals x angle + phase), which is the equation of a rose.

The band walks from the rim to the centre across the round, and the phase advances a little every revolution. That drift is the rose engine's own index, and it is what turns a single closed curve into a woven dial instead of one line retraced forever. Five numbers define a cut. The cartridge draws them itself, so you are not working through a set of authored levels.
