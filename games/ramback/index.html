RAMBACK: a PS1-style ram charge through five eras is a game for Three.js, free to play in the browser.

Made for the PS1 Aesthetic Game Jam (theme "Bash to the Past"), November 2026, and built in Bash.tv. RAMBACK is a free PS1-style browser game: a ram charges through five eras of walls, and every wall it smashes rolls the year back, from 1999 to 300 BC. Play it in the browser with keyboard, touch or gamepad. A full run takes a few minutes. How it plays Each wall is a row of blocks.

Solid blocks bounce you off and cost a horn. Cracked blocks burst, but only if you hit them with the speed bar past its SMASH mark. Charging is fast and stiff and drains stamina, so you line up while trotting and commit late.

Each era ends at a time gate with one glowing keystone: smashing the last wall before it refills your stamina, and a hit with the bar past its GATE mark sends you back to the next era with your three horns refilled. Lose all three and you retry that era, not the whole run. The first three walls of 1999 coach you instead of costing horns.

Five eras, five hazards 1999 office (rolling office chairs) · 1925 railway yard (a mine cart) · 1660 timber village (rolling barrels) · 1250 castle (a portcullis) · 300 BC temple (rolling column drums). Then a meadow at dawn.
