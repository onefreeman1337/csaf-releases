HEDGEMAGE is a short top-down wizard action game for Windows, with a free demo you can play in your browser on this page: the first area, THE RILL, and its boss. You build every spell on the spot from four elements: tap one or two of FIRE, FROST, STORM and EARTH, then cast. Four elements make fourteen spells, and each one does something to the world and something to a fight.

The paid download is the whole game: three areas, twelve rooms, eleven kinds of foe, three bosses and the ending. Hand-written HTML5, no engine, made by Core Systems Asset Factory using AI tools. The world answers your spells Frost freezes the river into a road you can walk. Fire burns through a bramble hedge and the bookstacks of a drowned library. Earth smashes boulders and fallen masonry.

Storm wakes the old rune stones, and when every stone is awake the gate opens. The rooms are built out of those four obstacles, so the way forward is always a spell. And the foes do too A soaked foe conducts: hit it with storm and the spark leaps to every wet foe near it. Frost freezes a wet foe at once, or any foe you chill twice, and a stone then shatters it for triple damage.
