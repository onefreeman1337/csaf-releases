Sundial is a time-of-day HUD kit for Godot 4 and five more engines: a clock, a day-night arc, weather badges and a calendar, 87 widgets in pixel and HD. Made for farming, life-sim and cozy RPG games. Tested in Godot 4.7, Unity 6 and RPG Maker MZ, with files for Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py. Royalty free for commercial games. A free sampler of 9 widgets is on this page.

A clock that slices cleanly The dial, the hour hand, the minute hand and the pin are four images of one size. Stack them and the clock tells the time, with no offsets to set. Each hand ships as 12 frames, one per hour or per five minutes, and every frame turns about the same pixel, so a grid slicer cuts the sheet in one pass.

Dawn to night, and the weather Day-night arc: 6 frames from dawn to night over four skies, with the sun rising and setting and the moon and stars after dusk. The sun and moon markers also ship alone. Digital time: a time plate that stretches in width, a blinking colon and AM / PM tags.

Badges: 4 seasons and 7 weathers (clear, cloudy, rain, storm, snow, wind, fog) in round brass bezels, plus an empty one for your own icon.
