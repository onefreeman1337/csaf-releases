A UI kit drawn in the Verdant tileset palette: mossy stone frames, leaf tufts, ready for six engines 58 widgets in 125 widget states, in pixel mode and HD mode, generated from one theme and wired for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal Engine, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ and Ren'Py.

Every one of its 58 colours is an entry of the palette our Verdant tilesets are drawn in, so the menus, dialogue box, bars and slots sit in the same colours as the 16x16 tiles, one kit pixel the size of one tile pixel: cool blue grey cliff stone frames with a moss green line inside, dark slate flagstone panels, Verdant leaf greens for the main buttons and headers, cloth reds for danger, water blue for MP and bloom gold for XP and coins.

Ivy grows over the top corners of the heavy frames with a small daisy tucked in, and grass stands at the bottom corners. Free: try it before you buy. The UI Kit Sampler has this theme's dialogue box, buttons, HP bar and cursors, plus the same from our eleven other UI kits, in one free download with a Godot project that shows all twelve side by side.

The two gallery plates over Verdant 01 and Verdant 09 show the kit on real Verdant maps at the same 2x scale.
