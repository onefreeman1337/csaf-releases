Crucible is a crafting, cooking and alchemy UI kit for Godot 4 and five more engines: 107 widgets of copper pipe, oak and amber glass, in pixel and HD. Made for RPG, survival and cozy games that have a workbench. Tested in Godot 4.7, Unity 6 and RPG Maker MZ, with files for Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py. Royalty free for commercial games. A free sampler of 13 widgets is on this page.

A crafting screen, ready to wire up A 3x3 grid on a copper-rimmed bench, an arrow that fills, and a round porthole that lights when the recipe is ready. Slots come in five states, and the fifth is a red missing state for an ingredient the player does not have. Recipe cards come as unknown, known, hover, selected and locked.

The frame is plumbing Every window and dialogue box is a run of verdigris copper pipe with a quarter-round elbow and polished collars in each corner. The elbows sit inside the corners of the 9-slice, so a panel stretches to any size and every corner stays as drawn. The same tube ships as modular pieces: runs, four elbows, a tee and a valve.
