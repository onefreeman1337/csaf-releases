Health and mana orbs that your engine fills, plus a full action RPG HUD to match Wellglass is an action RPG HUD kit: glass health and mana orbs shipped as layers your engine fills (socket, liquid, glass), 44 baked fill frames for engines without a vertical fill, skill sockets with an 8 frame cooldown wipe and a ready flash, a potion belt with stack counters, an XP strip and buff frames, plus a full game UI in one runed obsidian theme.

94 widgets in 217 widget states, pixel mode and HD mode, with files for Godot 4 (4.7 tested), Unity 6 (tested), RPG Maker MZ (tested), Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py. Royalty free licence for commercial games. A free sampler with one orb is on this page. Every element in the gallery is a file in the pack.

The animation at the top is built only from the kit's sprites: a burn ticks the HP orb down, a flask is drunk and its count drops, the orb refills; a fireball is cast, the MP orb drops, its socket runs the cooldown wipe and flashes gold when it is ready again. The orbs Four liquids: health (red), mana (blue), stamina (green) and soul (violet), each a separate image that sits inside one shared socket.
