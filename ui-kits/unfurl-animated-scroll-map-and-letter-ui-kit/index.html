Unfurl is a scroll, map and letter UI kit for Godot 4 and five more engines: 79 widgets in pixel and HD, and scrolls that unroll on screen at any size. Made for fantasy, pirate and adventure RPGs. Tested in Godot 4.7, Unity 6 and RPG Maker MZ, with files for Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py. Royalty free for commercial games. A free sampler of 9 widgets is on this page.

Every frame is a scroll The window is vellum between two walnut rollers with brass finials; the dialogue box is a sideways scroll. The finials sit in the 9-slice corners, so a scroll stretches to any size and keeps every finial as drawn. It opens on screen In Godot 4, the included script unfurl.gd unrolls a scroll at whatever size your layout gives it: the window downward, the dialogue box from its centre.

It draws the 9-slice at a growing size and reveals your text as the paper opens. Call unfurl() or roll_up(), or set the progress yourself. Other engines get six-frame strips of the window, the dialogue box and the banner opening. The scroll set (21 widgets) A ribbon banner with swallowtail ends, for a title. It stretches in width.
