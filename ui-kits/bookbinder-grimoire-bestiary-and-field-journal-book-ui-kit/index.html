Three book UIs in one kit: a spellbook, a monster bestiary and an explorer's field journal Bookbinder is a book and journal UI kit for RPGs and adventure games: three complete books, the Grimoire, the Bestiary and the Field Journal, each with 83 widgets in 183 widget states, in pixel mode and HD mode, with files for Godot 4 (4.7 tested), Unity 6 (tested), RPG Maker MZ (tested), Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py.

Every book has open pages that make a spread at any size, a cover-opening and a page-turn animation, ribbon bookmarks, index tabs, quest or entry rows, stamps, a wax seal and a quill cursor, plus a full set of windows, buttons, bars and controls. The three books share every widget name, so a game can switch book with one line. Royalty free licence for commercial games.

A free sampler of all three books is on this page. Grimoire: an arcane spellbook. Black leather around vellum pages, a verdigris bronze lozenge holding a slit-pupil eye on every corner, violet glyph ink for the main buttons, blood-red ink for danger. Bestiary: a hunter's monster bestiary.
