Bookbinder is a book UI kit with 3 complete books for RPGs and adventure games, for Godot, Unity, RPG Maker MZ and three more engines. The Grimoire, the Bestiary and the Field Journal each have 83 widgets in 183 widget states, in pixel mode and HD mode. A free sampler of all three books is on this page. Three books, one set of names Grimoire: an arcane spellbook.

Black leather around vellum pages, a bronze eye on every corner, violet glyph ink and blood red ink for danger. Bestiary: a hunter's monster bestiary. Weathered green hide around aged parchment, three claw gouges across every corner and iron book furniture. Field Journal: an explorer's notebook. Kraft paper covers, a strip of masking tape across every corner, pen on pale paper and red pencil for danger.

The three books share every widget name, so a game can switch book with one line. The book in every book Two pages make a spread at any size: the cover rim and page edges sit in the outer margins and the gutter in the inner one, so the fold stays a fold.
