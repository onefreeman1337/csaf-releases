Iron Bastion is a castle UI kit for strategy games, for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ and Ren'Py: 58 widgets with stone wall frames and turret corners. Royalty free for commercial games, with a free sampler on this page.

What is inside 58 widgets · 125 states · pixel and HD The heavy frames are castle walls: a turret with three merlons and an arrow slit above each top corner, a stone footing below each bottom one. Grey ashlar frames over dark oak panels, a heraldic blue accent with white ink and a crimson danger family. 35 nine slice widgets: the turrets sit in the corners and never stretch, the wall between them stretches cleanly.

Resource strips in five colours for food, wood or gold meters, beside a gold coin, a star and a notice badge. 11 button styles in normal, hover, pressed and disabled; tabs, a text field, slots, checkboxes, radios and toggles in every state. Pixel mode at 1x and HD mode at 3x, same names and layout. The kit's own Godot 4 demo project: every control themed, pixel mode beside HD mode.

Works with Godot 4: two themes (pixel and HD) and a demo project. Unity: sprites with their 9 slice borders in.meta files, plus USS.
