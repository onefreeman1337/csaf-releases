Dusk, Mint and Contrast is a UI kit volume for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ and Ren'Py: three complete modern themes, a dark mode, a soft pastel and a high contrast theme. Royalty free for commercial games, with a free sampler of all three on this page.

What is inside 3 themes · 58 widgets and 145 states each · pixel and HD Dusk: charcoal panels with light hairline edges, a cyan accent and an amber focus ring. It pairs with our light kit, Clean Slate. Mint: soft mint outlines, pill shaped buttons and cream panels, with a violet focus ring. Contrast: black fills, thick white outlines that turn yellow on hover, every text colour at 7.15:1 or better.

One set of widget, state and Godot theme type names across all three, so a theme switch is one line of code. 20 interactive widgets per theme also ship a focused state for keyboard and gamepad navigation. 35 nine slice widgets per theme, the dialogue box with its name plate among them. One running Godot scene, switched to each of the three themes.

Works with Godot 4: two themes (pixel and HD) per theme, a demo per theme and a theme switch project. Unity: sprites with their 9 slice borders in.meta files, plus USS.
