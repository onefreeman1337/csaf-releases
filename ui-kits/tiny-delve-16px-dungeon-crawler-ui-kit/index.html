A dungeon crawler UI kit: skull-cornered windows, a minimap, counters and torches, ready for six engines Tiny Delve is a pixel art dungeon crawler and roguelike UI kit with 86 widgets in 155 widget states, in pixel mode and HD mode, with files for Godot 4 (4.7 tested), Unity 6, Unreal Engine, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ and Ren'Py.

It includes 37 stretchable 9 slice frames and plates, 28 dungeon HUD pieces (minimap rooms, keys, bombs, potions, chests, a torch animation, status icons), a Godot theme and demo, and a royalty free licence for commercial games. A free sampler is on this page, and a free sampler of all our UI kits is linked below.

Blue slate stone frames with a small bone skull over every corner, torch amber for the main buttons, blood red for danger, bone white type. Every element in the gallery is a file in the pack: the dungeon HUD at the top of the gallery is built only from the kit's own sprites, 9 sliced to size.

The dungeon HUD A minimap: 8 room cells (unknown, visited, you are here, boss, treasure, shop, stairs down, secret), each 11x9 pixels, and two door connectors.
