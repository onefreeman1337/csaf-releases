Felt Table is a card game and deckbuilder UI kit for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ and Ren'Py: 88 widgets on mahogany rails and green baize. Royalty free for commercial games, with a free sampler on this page. What is inside 88 widgets · 173 states · pixel and HD · 4 card frames 4 card frames (attack, skill, power, curse) in four states. Each stretches like a card: only the art window grows.

A card back, a card slot that lights as a drop target, an End Turn plaque, a turn banner, a price tag and a pile count badge. 18 table icons: the cost gem, energy orbs, a block shield, four enemy intents, rarity gems, draw, discard and exhaust piles and booster packs. 46 nine slice widgets in all, with a brass suit in every heavy rail corner.

12 button styles and every other widget in every state, plus HP, MP, XP, stamina and boss bars. The card art and text are yours: the Readme gives the pixel position of every text area. A card battle screen built only from the kit's own sprites, 9 sliced to size. Works with Godot 4: two themes (pixel and HD) and a demo project. Unity: sprites with their 9 slice borders in.meta files, plus USS.

Unreal: Draw As Box margins.
