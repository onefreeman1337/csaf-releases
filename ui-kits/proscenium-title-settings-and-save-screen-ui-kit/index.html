Proscenium is a UI kit for Godot 4 with 9 runnable menu screens (title, settings, pause, save and more) and 89 widgets in pixel and HD. Every screen is a real scene you open in the editor, tested on Godot 4.7, with arrow keys and gamepad focus already wired. The art also comes as files for Unity 6 (tested), RPG Maker MZ (tested), Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py. Royalty free for commercial games.

A free sampler of 9 widgets is on this page. The front of your game, already built Press Play and the curtain opens on your title screen. 18 scene files ship: each screen in a 640 x 360 pixel set and a 1920 x 1080 HD set, driven by short scripts you can change. Title: a theatre stage, drapes that part, flickering footlights, your game's name on a plaque. Settings: four tabs.

Sliders drive your audio buses, keys rebind into the Input Map, a clash is flagged on both rows, and it all comes back at launch. Pause, Save, Load: four slots with thumbnail, chapter, date and playtime, as real files, with a dialog before any overwrite or delete.
