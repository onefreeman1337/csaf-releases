Duct Tape is a grunge UI kit for Godot 4 and five more engines: 80 widgets of torn cardboard, duct tape, ruled paper, marker and spray paint, in pixel and HD. Made for survival, horror, post-apocalyptic and street games. Tested in Godot 4.7, Unity 6 and RPG Maker MZ, with files for Unreal Engine, GameMaker and Ren'Py. Royalty free for commercial games. A free sampler of 9 widgets is on this page.

Torn and taped, and it still stretches Every window, dialogue box and portrait frame is kraft cardboard held by a strip of silver duct tape on two corners and torn off at the other two, with the pale fibre of the board along the tear. The tape and the tears sit inside the corners of the 9-slice, so a panel stretches to any size and every corner stays exactly as drawn.

Finished PNG atlases with the margins written down: nothing to extract or rasterise. The grunge set (22 widgets) Tape labels: a strip of duct tape and a strip of masking tape with torn ends, for a label written in marker. They stretch in width. A notebook page: ruled paper with a red margin and punched holes; its lines are spaced for the kit's typeface, so a quest list sits on them.
