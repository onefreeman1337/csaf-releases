One Bit is a 1-bit UI kit for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ, Ren'Py and Playdate: 74 widgets drawn in exactly two colours, ink and paper. Royalty free for commercial games, with a free sampler on this page.

What is inside 74 widgets · 141 states · pixel and HD · 16 dither tiles · 16 colourways 35 nine slice widgets: frames, panels, the dialogue box with its name plate, tooltips, buttons, bars, tabs, fields and slots. States told apart by shape, never by a grey: a hover thickens a rule, a press swaps ink and paper. Crossed rule frames: an outline and a keyline that run on past each other at every corner.

16 ordered dither tiles (8x8) for 1-bit dims, shadows and fades. 16 colourways of the same art, each an exact palette swap, every pair over 9:1 contrast. A playdate folder with image tables and a Lua helper that builds a nineSlice for any widget. Three 320x180 screens built only from the kit's own sprites, dither tiles and typeface. Works with Godot 4: two themes (pixel and HD) and a demo project.

Unity: sprites with their 9 slice borders in.meta files, plus USS. Unreal: Draw As Box margins. GameMaker: nine slice JSON. RPG Maker MZ: a 192x192 Window.png, no plugin.
