A strict two-colour (1-bit) UI kit, ready for six engines and Playdate One Bit is a 1-bit game UI kit: 74 widgets in 141 widget states, drawn in exactly two colours (ink and paper), in pixel mode and HD mode, with files for Godot 4 (4.7 tested), Unity 6 (tested), RPG Maker MZ (tested), Unreal Engine, GameMaker, Ren'Py and the Playdate SDK.

It includes 16 ordered dither tiles for 1-bit screen dims, shadows and fades, and 16 colourways (eight ink and paper pairs, each also inverted). Royalty free licence for commercial games. A free sampler is on this page.

Every element in the gallery is a file in the pack: the three 320x180 screens at the top of the gallery (a menu, a battle, a shop with a dimmed confirm box) are built only from the kit's own sprites, 9 sliced to size, with its own dither tiles and its own typeface.

Two colours, and it is checked Every pixel of the pixel art is ink or paper at full opacity (or fully transparent): no blends, no translucent overlay, no grey. Our gate checks every pixel of the atlas and every sprite against the theme's two colours, and the theme itself is refused if it declares a third. States are told apart by shape, never by a grey.
