Everything a casual or idle game shows around its play: the menus and HUD, the screens between rounds, and the idle screen. Three of our kits on one page, each with its own look, and a new Godot project that switches between them.

What is inside Casual Pop (58 widgets): the chunky bright base kit. Glossy buttons in four states, toggles, sliders, tabs, HP and XP bars, inventory slots, a dialogue box with a name plate, cursors. Bonus Round (106 widgets): between rounds.
