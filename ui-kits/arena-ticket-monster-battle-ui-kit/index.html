Arena Ticket is a pixel art UI kit for turn based monster battle games, for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, RPG Maker MZ and Ren'Py: 101 widgets, every big frame bitten out like a ticket stub. Royalty free for commercial games, with a free sampler on this page. What is inside 101 widgets · 210 states · 43 battle pieces · pixel and HD Foe and ally name plates with level stubs that stretch to any width.

8 type chips with glyphs, 8 move tickets and 4 command tickets (Fight, Bag, Team, Run) in four states. A party row in normal, hover, selected and fainted, tally tokens and a capture charm in 4 frames. 6 status chips, stat arrows, three hit marks, a VS badge, two arena pads and a stage backdrop that stretches to any width. Stadium night: navy panels with a gold band, arena red, and cream ticket paper with dark ink.

The kit ships no monsters: your creatures stand on its pads. Two battle screens built only from the kit's own sprites, 9 sliced to size. Works with Godot 4: two themes (pixel and HD) and a demo project. Unity: sprites with their 9 slice borders in.meta files, plus USS. Unreal: Draw As Box margins. GameMaker: nine slice JSON. RPG Maker MZ: a 192x192 Window.png, no plugin.
