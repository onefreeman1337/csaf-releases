Canopy is a 44 track jungle adventure music pack for any game engine: one expedition in six legs, in OGG and MIDI. It ships 38 full looping pieces and 6 short cues, royalty free for commercial games, with four full tracks free on this page.

What is inside 44 tracks · OGG + editable MIDI · 1:20:00 of music · every loop at least 58 seconds A whole expedition, in order: The Last Port, Up the River, The Canopy, The Ruins, The Lost Temple and The Way Out. Port town, shop, map room and camp; the river by day and by night; canopy, waterfall, rope bridge and a village in the trees; ruins, traps and a chase; the temple from its antechamber to its heart.

Fights for each part of the map: Battle on the River, Battle in the Canopy and Battle in the Ruins, plus The Jaguar and The Old One as bosses. The band changes on the way in. Every instrument belongs to the expedition (trumpet, clarinet, steel guitar and strings) or to the jungle (marimba, kalimba, pan flute and ocarina).

Counted from the notes in the MIDI, the jungle plays 0% of Leg I, 4% of Leg II, 55% of Leg III, 84% of Leg IV and 100% of Leg V. Leg I and Leg V have no instrument in common. Leg VI plays both.
