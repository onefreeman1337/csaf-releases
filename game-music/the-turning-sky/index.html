The Turning Sky - An Open World Music Pack, Day and Night - 44 Tracks is a game music pack, paid.

The Turning Sky is a 44 track exploration score for open world games, built in pairs. Twelve places, each written twice: once in daylight and once after dark. Both halves of a pair share a tonic and state the same melodic cell. What changes is everything that is not the tune: the mode darkens, the tempo drops, the melody moves down and passes to a different instrument played softer, and the texture thins. Why pairs.

An open world field is walked at noon and at midnight, and the player is meant to know it is the same field. Here the night version of a place is its day version in a different light, so switching at nightfall keeps the same tonic and the same tune. Checked on the shipped MIDI, not asserted.

For all 12 pairs, a check that reads the finished.mid files confirms that both halves state the pair's cell, the night half is slower, its melody sits lower on average and is played softer, and it has fewer notes. That second part matters: sharing a tune is trivially true of one track shipped twice under two names, so the check also requires the night half to differ in exactly the ways described here.

You can confirm any pair yourself by opening the day and night.mid side by side.
