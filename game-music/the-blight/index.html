The Blight is a 44 track folk horror music pack for any game engine: a plague village and a witch trial, from May Day to the ashes, in five acts. It ships 38 full looping pieces and 6 short cues (61:14 of music) as OGG with loop tags, plus all 44 MIDI sources. Royalty free for commercial games, and three full tracks are free on this page.

What is inside 44 tracks · 38 loops of a minute or more · 6 cues · OGG + editable MIDI · 61:14 Five acts in one village: Before the Blight (12), The Sickness (10), The Accusation (7), The Trial (7) and Ashes (2), plus 6 cues: The Bell Tolls, A Mark on the Door, Witch!, Guilty, Spared and The Blight Spreads. Warm, then wrong: twelve village tunes are heard twice.

Before the blight each is in a bright folk mode; after it, the same tune comes back note for note in a darker mode, slower. The passing bell: tubular bells sound in none of the 12 tracks before the blight and in all 32 after it. One seed theme, The May Song, in all 44 tracks, and five more themes for the mill and market, the river, the May dance and the pyre, the sickness and the accusation.
