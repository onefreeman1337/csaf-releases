Longships - A Norse Saga Music Pack - 44 Tracks is a game music pack, paid.

Longships is a 44 track Norse saga score for RPGs: the longhall and the whale road, the barrow and the ice, a battle set, the jarl, the sea serpent and Ragnarok. Every track stands on its tonic drone, the way a hurdy-gurdy or a bowed lyre holds one note under a Nordic folk tune. The drone, checked on the shipped MIDI.

In every one of the 44 tracks the lowest sounding note is the tonic for 100% of the time the track is sounding. The drone is its own track in every.mid, named drone, played by a cello, a double bass or a bassoon depending on the track, re-sounded twice a bar and swelling from the opening into the main section.

Mute it and the other four parts still sound in every track: the lead, the pad and the bass, plus a counter line (24 tracks) or a colour line (20 tracks). What is in it. 35 full pieces and 9 short cues, 52:01 of music. The title theme: The Cold Coast. At sea: The Whale Road, Oars Out, The Grey Swell, Past the Skerries, The Gale and Landfall.

At home: The Longhall and The Longhall by Firelight, The Steading, The Skald's Bench, The Amber Trader, The Smith's Fire, The Mead Feast, The Thing at Midsummer.
