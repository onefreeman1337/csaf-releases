Spooky Season is a 44 track Halloween score for games: the trick-or-treat lane and the pumpkin patch, a haunted manor room by room, the churchyard and the crypt, a battle set, the headless horseman and the witching hour. It is written playful rather than gory, and every track has a bass that tiptoes, the creeping detached bass of "In the Hall of the Mountain King". The tiptoe, checked on the shipped MIDI.

In every one of the 44 tracks, every bass note except the last is let go before the next one begins: it sounds for at most half the time until the next bass note.
