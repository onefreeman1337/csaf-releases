Compliance is a 44 track corporate dystopia music pack for any game engine: one megacorp building, from the welcome video to the fire exit. It ships 38 full looping pieces and 6 short system cues as OGG and editable MIDI, royalty free for commercial games, with three full tracks free on this page.

What is inside 44 tracks · 38 loops of a minute or more · 6 cues · OGG + editable MIDI · 58:07 Five acts from the first day to the way out: Onboarding (8), The Floors (9), The Audit (9), The Purge (7) and Outside (5), plus 6 system cues: Access Granted, Access Denied, Incident Logged, Quota Met, Terminated and Security Alert.

The clock: a quiet side stick ticks on every beat of tracks 1 to 39 and stops at track 40, Service Tunnels, when the record leaves the building. It is its own part in the MIDI, so you can drop it and render your own. The voice: choir and voice oohs sound only in the last 5 tracks. Until then the only voice on the record is a synth voice, and those last 5 tracks use no synth at all.

The decay: the harmony slows act by act, a new chord every bar, then every 2, 4 and 8 bars, then every bar again outside.
