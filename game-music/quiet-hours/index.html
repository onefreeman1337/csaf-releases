Quiet Hours is a 44 track lofi music pack for any game engine: nine game screens at four times of day, in OGG and MIDI. It ships 36 full looping pieces, 8 short cues and a drumless version of every full piece, royalty free for commercial games, with four full tracks free on this page.

What is inside 44 tracks · 36 drumless versions · OGG + editable MIDI · 1:06:54 of music, not counting the drumless files Nine screens: menu, home, garden, workshop, market, map, reading, rain and waiting. Each one four times: morning (100 BPM), afternoon (86 BPM), evening (74 BPM) and night (60 BPM). The rain pieces are written for a rainy screen and carry no rain sound. One clock per time of day.

All nine pieces of a row share one tempo and one loop, so their LOOPSTART and LOOPLENGTH tags match to the sample, and every track is levelled to -22 LUFS. Start two together, one muted, and fade between them on the same beat.

A whole row loaded into one AudioStreamSynchronized in Godot 4.7.2, a test that read the streams and played nothing; in another engine, how tightly they stay together depends on that engine's audio. One scale per time of day.
