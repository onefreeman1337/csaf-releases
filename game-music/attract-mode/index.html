Attract Mode is a 46 track retro arcade music pack for any game engine: the complete cue sheets of four 1983 style coin op games. It ships 32 full looping pieces and 14 short cues as OGG and editable MIDI, royalty free for commercial games, with four full tracks free on this page.

What is inside 46 tracks · 32 loops · 14 cues · OGG + editable MIDI · 38:25 Four cabinets from one studio, eleven tracks each: a maze game, a space shooter, a climbing game and a racer. Each has an attract mode, three rounds, a fourth round (or a boss, or a final lap), a bonus round, an intermission and a high score entry, plus start, miss and game over cues. The studio adds Insert Coin and Extra Life.

Every cue plays once and ends on the home chord; the music runs 2.9 to 10.9 seconds before the tail. The Readme lists the length of every track in seconds, so you can time a READY screen or a respawn to it. Written to a dual PSG budget (two AY-3-8910 class sound chips): lead, counter line, arpeggio and bass, each one note at a time, two channels left empty for your sound effects, and drums from four noise sounds.

No part ever holds a chord; never more than 4 pitched notes at once.
