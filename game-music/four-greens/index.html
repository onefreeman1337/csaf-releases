Four Greens - A Handheld Adventure Score - 42 Tracks is a game music pack, paid.

A 42 track Game Boy adventure score in MIDI and OGG for RPG Maker, Godot, Unity and GameMaker Four Greens is 42 tracks of adventure music written inside the original Game Boy's pitched voice budget: melody on pulse 1, one companion on pulse 2, bass on the wave channel, never more than 3 notes at once. Royalty free for commercial games. Four full tracks are free on this page.

There is no pad channel, so a chord is an arpeggio, and counterpoint costs the arpeggio. That choice is made track by track: 23 tracks give pulse 2 a countermelody and 19 give it a broken chord. The noise channel is never written. On real hardware that is where the drums live, so your own sound effects get it to themselves.

What is inside 42 MIDI files, the editable sources, one per track, with LOOPSTART and LOOPLENGTH markers. 42 OGG files, 44.1 kHz stereo renders. 36 loop, with sample accurate loop points in the Vorbis comments; the other 6 are four stingers, the ending and the credits.
