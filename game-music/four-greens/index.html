Four Greens - A Handheld Adventure Score - 42 Tracks is a game music pack, paid.

The original Game Boy could play four notes at a time. Two pulse channels, one wave channel, one noise channel, one note each. Three of them are pitched. Every piece of music anybody remembers from that machine was written inside that budget, and it is a completely different compositional problem from writing a modern arrangement and putting square-wave patches on it. This album is written inside the budget.

Melody on pulse 1, one companion on pulse 2, bass on the wave channel. No pad, because there is no pad channel. If you want a chord here you arpeggiate it, and if you want counterpoint you give up the arpeggio to get it. That choice is made track by track across the record: 23 tracks give the second channel a countermelody and 19 give it a broken chord. And you can check it in about a minute.

The pack ships channel_budget_proof.json: 210 assertions over all 42 tracks, measured on the MIDI you are buying, saying that no track ever sounds more than three notes at once, that every voice is monophonic, and that every track is scored two-pulse-one-wave. The.mid files are right there if you disagree with any of it. The fourth channel is yours.
