Horror Manor is a 42 track PSX survival horror music pack for RPG Maker MZ, Godot, Unity and GameMaker, every room built from one five note lullaby. It is royalty free in games, and two full tracks are free to download on this page.

One house, one lullaby Somewhere upstairs there is a music box, and the tune it plays is five notes long: a child's rock down a third and back, then one step up onto the wrong note and home. The halls fall down them, the thing in the walls knocks them, the oratory chants them, and the one warm room left plays them with the wrong note put right.

Every room, corridor, chase and ambient bed is written in a mode where that step up is a semitone. The safe room, the discovery, the epilogue and the final cue are the only tracks in a mode where it is a whole tone. The Readme lists the key and mode of all 42 tracks, so you can check that yourself.
