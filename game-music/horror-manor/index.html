Horror Manor - PSX Survival Horror Music Pack - 42 Tracks is a game music pack, paid.

One house. One lullaby. 42 tracks. Somewhere upstairs there is a music box, and the tune it plays is five notes long: a child's rock down a third and back, then one step up onto the wrong note and home. Every room in this score is built from those five notes. The halls fall down them, the thing in the walls knocks them, the oratory chants them, and the one warm room left plays them with the wrong note put right.

The wrong note is a rule, not a mood. Every room, corridor, chase and ambient bed is written in a mode where that step up is a semitone. The safe room, the discovery, the epilogue and the final cue are the only tracks in a mode where it is a whole tone, so nothing is wrong there. The Readme lists the key and mode of all 42 tracks, so you can check that for yourself.

Written for a whole survival horror game, not one screen. The foyer, the long gallery, the library, the nursery, the cellar, the attic and the night garden get three pieces each; the oratory and the thing in the walls get two.
