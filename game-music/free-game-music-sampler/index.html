Overture is a free game music sampler: 68 tracks, two from each of our 34 game music albums, as OGG Vorbis (44.1 kHz stereo) with loop points in the file, plus the editable MIDI source of every track. 87:49 of music across fantasy and JRPG, retro console, dark and horror, folk and world, exploration, science fiction and cozy scores. Free to use in commercial and non commercial games, no attribution required.

67 of the 68 carry LOOPSTART and LOOPLENGTH, which RPG Maker MZ and MV read with no plugin; the Readme gives the same points for Godot 4, Unity and GameMaker. Why it exists. Every album we make is one musical voice: one set of themes carried through a whole game's worth of tracks. This download lets you hear all 34 voices before you pay for any of them.

From each album you get its opening piece and the last full piece it offers as a free preview, usually its boss or its climax. Tested in two engines. All 68 files were copied into a blank RPG Maker MZ project's audio/bgm and played through MZ's own AudioManager: 68 of 68 decoded at their own length, and MZ read the loop points of all 67 looped tracks with no plugin.
