Forty-two tracks for a game set at sea, and not one of them is a shanty. The harbour at the change of the tide, the chandler's shop, the taproom, weighing anchor, four kinds of open water, three night watches, fog, the becalming, a three-track storm that escalates, the reef, the boarding, counting the living afterwards, the lighthouse, the wreck, and landfall. It is built on one idea.

The seed is a swell: it lifts a fourth, then rocks - down, up, down - which is what a hull does for the rest of the watch. Every other cell in the album is that shape under a different condition. Becalmed is the seed with the rise taken out, so it rocks without ever having lifted. Capstan is it squeezed into working rhythm, and it can run under a scene for minutes without tiring.

Squall is every interval of it widened until the ship cannot settle. Undertow is it turned over, sinking where the seed rose. Landfall refuses to come back down the last time and lifts instead - the only cell on the album that resolves upward, and it arrives late. That is not a description, it is a measurement.
