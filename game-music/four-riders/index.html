Four Riders is a 44 track western music pack for any game engine: a frontier score cast like a film, with one instrument for each of four riders. It ships 38 full looping pieces and 6 short cues as OGG and editable MIDI, royalty free for commercial games, with three full tracks free on this page.

What is inside 44 tracks · 38 loops · 6 cues · OGG + editable MIDI · 63:10 Five acts across one territory: The Territory (8), The Town (12), The Trail (6), The Trouble (9) and Sundown (3), plus 6 cues: Wanted, The Draw, Gold Strike, New Day, Justice and Six Feet Under.

The cast: the Stranger on harmonica (13 tracks), the Outlaw on clean electric guitar (12 tracks), the Marshal on French horn (12 tracks) and the Widow on cello (13 tracks). Each instrument sounds only in that rider's scenes, stating that rider's own theme, so you can score a scene by who is in it. album.json lists the riders of every track. The land: a whistle leads the 7 empty country tracks and sounds nowhere else.

When it plays, no rider is there. The meeting: all four instruments sound together in exactly three tracks, Four Riders, The Standoff and Four Roads Out, where the tune passes from rider to rider.
