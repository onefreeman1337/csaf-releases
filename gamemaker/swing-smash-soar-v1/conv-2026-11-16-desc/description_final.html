Swing, Smash and Soar is 3 action-adventure systems for GameMaker 2024.14 in pure GML, each with its own demo room: melee swings, breakables and a Mode 7 flight view. One commercial licence covers all three in unlimited games.

Drop one, two or all three into your game. Every smear, break, world and sky is drawn or cut in code; the only image files are font atlases. The raw GML ships beside each package, readable without GameMaker.

Brandish: melee swings

8 weapon classes in 3 input modes: press-to-swing combos, a weapon that orbits toward the cursor, and cursor-drag swings whose speed sets the damage. Every hit is tested along the whole arc the blade swept since the last step, so a fast swing never passes through a target. Each swing draws its own smear in 5 styles (crescent, ink brush, pixel, flame, frost): 40 presets, with ghosts and hit sparks.

Smithereens: breakables

Strike a pot and it bursts into sherds cut from the exact frame your instance is showing, so a break always matches your art. 7 materials break in 7 different structures; pieces bounce, settle, cast shadows and fade. Gathering nodes crack per strike and grow back.

Overflight: fly, dive and look down

A whole island world generated from a seed in 8 styles, rendered as a Mode 7 floor under a sky in 5 times of day. Two cloud layers you fly over and fall through, your own sprites billboarded with shadows, and a map.

Wired together, tested together

Each part keeps its own prefix (brd_, smr_, ovf_ scripts and BRD_, SMR_, OVF_ macros), so two parts never define the same name. All three packages were imported into one blank GameMaker project, built with Igor on runtime 2024.14.4.268, and each part's own buyer checks ran inside it. A fourth run wired them the way the Readme shows: a Brandish swing hit a pot instance, Smithereens broke it into pieces cut from its sprite, and Overflight drew a world in the same project. 93 assertions passed.
