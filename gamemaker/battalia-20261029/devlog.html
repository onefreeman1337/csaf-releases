Battalia is out: army battles for GameMaker, in pure GML.

You declare two armies of regiments with a line of text each ("foot:44:Iron Company, archer:34:Rivermarch Bows, horse:16:Queen's Own"), and the package draws the rest: every soldier, every regiment's banner (its arms struck from the regiment's name), and the battlefield itself, painted from a seed. The player picks a formation from four cards, and the battle plays out soldier by soldier, with volleys, spells, charges and routs, ending in a war record that charts the course of the battle and hands the survivors back to your code in soldiers.

Measured in the package's own suite on a real build: With the smaller army (32 foot against 40), a wedge against the enemy's line won 17 of 20 battles where copying the line won 0; pikes in a square held horse in a wedge 16 times in 16; identical armies split 14 to 16 over 30 battles.

It is a GameMaker edition of our RPG Maker MZ battle system Warhost, rebuilt in GML with a demo room of five battles. The code is raw and commented, everything is prefixed, and no image files ship.
