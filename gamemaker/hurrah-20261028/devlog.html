Hurrah is out for GameMaker: celebration effects where one call fires a whole moment, and every piece of it is drawn by the package.

A level up fires rays behind a red ribbon banner with your words on it, a flash, a starburst, sparkles and a burst of confetti. A win fires two confetti cannons from the bottom corners with streamers and drops ticker tape from the top. An opened chest throws coins and gems that bounce and stack on your floor, and a jackpot flies them one by one into your score counter, each reporting its value as it lands. There are eight moments like that, and the pieces they are made of fire on their own too.

The confetti is paper with two faces. Each piece tumbles about its own axis, so the face it shows decides its colour, how squarely it faces you decides its light, and foil catches a glint as it turns flat. It falls the way paper falls: it hangs while it lies flat, slips while it is edge-on, and drifts from side to side. Streamers are ropes of twelve points thrown head first with the tail following, balloons rise and sway with their strings tied to the knot, coins spin edge-on with a milled rim, and gems flash facet by facet as they turn. 43 forms in all, in six palettes or your own colours.

A world takes a budget, and pieces past it are thinned rather than queued, so a frame has a ceiling you choose. Paper is drawn from an atlas the package draws itself on first use, one batched sprite per piece, and everything else goes into vertex buffers, five submits a frame whatever the count.

Every test runs in a real exe built by GameMaker's own compiler (runtime 2024.14.4.268, VM, Windows): the suite's 339 assertions passed on two runs, including a forty coin shower that arrives whole under a budget of ten and every one of the sixteen kinds retiring cleanly, and the shipped package was installed into a blank project with the Readme's own code run verbatim. HTML5, mobile and YYC exports were not built. Made with AI assistance: the code and the graphics were written with AI, and every picture is drawn by that code.
