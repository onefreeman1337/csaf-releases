Table Service is out for GameMaker: a restaurant serving minigame for any room, with every customer and every dish drawn by the package.

Place tables, a kitchen pass and a door in your room and open for the night. Customers walk in from the door on a path round your tables, sit, ring a brass bell for service and wait while the candle on their table burns down. The player takes the order at the table, picks the finished dishes up at the pass two at a time and serves the right table before patience runs out. A party that waits too long walks out, and its dishes are wasted. At closing time the ledger page counts the takings and the tips and gives the night up to three stars.

The part we like most is the menu. Dishes are drawn from their names: write "Pumpkin Soup" and a bowl the colour of pumpkin comes out of the kitchen, "Pale Ale" is a wooden tankard with a head of foam, "Grilled Trout" a whole fish with lemon, "Mana Tonic" a corked bottle. About 170 words across 19 kinds of dish and drink are understood, colour words included, and an unknown name still gets a plated dish. The customers are generated from a seed too: skin, hair, hats, coats and one extra, seated or walking in four directions.

This is the GameMaker edition of our RPG Maker MZ plugin, and its rules are held to the MZ code number for number: 34 planned nights and 27 whole shifts played by a scripted server give exactly the same arrivals, orders, tips and stars in both. The GameMaker edition adds the clothed round tables and chairs (MZ used your map's tiles), customers that path round your furniture, and a drawn dining room for the demo.

Pure GML, GameMaker 2024.14 or later, tested on runtime 2024.14.4.268 (VM, Windows). Made with AI assistance.
