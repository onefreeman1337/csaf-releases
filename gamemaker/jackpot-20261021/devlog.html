Jackpot is out: generated slot machines for GameMaker, drawn by the package and honest about their maths.

Every machine is built from a seed and a theme: its reel strips, its paylines, its pay table, its name on the marquee and its cabinet. Eight themes of ten symbols are drawn in code (Fruit, Gems, Royals, Runes, Sea, Desert Relics, Candy and Space, each with its own wild and scatter), on three cabinets: an upright with a lever and chasing bulbs, a slant-top and a bar-top, in four liveries.

The part we cared most about is the payback. A slot machine's return to player is usually a number someone typed into a design document. Here it is computed exactly from each reel's symbol counts, and the package can re-tune a machine's pay table to whatever your game corner should pay: 92%, 96%, your call. The in-engine tests check the exact figure against every one of the 13,824 stop combinations of a 3-reel machine, and the core is held bit for bit to a reference implementation across 24 generated machines and 240 scripted spins.

The machine plays out of the box: reels with motion smear that stop one after another and settle with a bounce, paylines lighting up in turn, pulsing winners, LED meters rolling up, a coin fountain and a BIG WIN banner. Every press and result is posted as an event so your own sounds hook straight in. The demo room, THE GAME CORNER, puts eight machines on the floor with their pay tables and exact figures beside them, and one key re-tunes a machine live.

Play money only: nothing in the package takes or pays real money. Pure GML, GameMaker 2024.14 or later, tested on runtime 2024.14.4.268 (VM, Windows). Made with AI assistance.
