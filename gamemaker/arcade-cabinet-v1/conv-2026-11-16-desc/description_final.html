Arcade Cabinet is 3 complete minigames for GameMaker 2024.14 in pure GML, each with its own demo room: peg boards, downhill runs and a game-show quiz. One commercial licence covers all three in unlimited games.

Drop one, two or all three into your game. Every board, slope, stage and contestant is drawn in code; the only image files are font atlases. The raw GML ships beside each package, readable without GameMaker.

Pegfall: peg boards

A Peggle and pachinko style shooter. 10 layout grammars in 6 drawn themes generate boards from a seed, and every board is proven clearable before your player sees it. The aim guide runs the same physics as the shot. A moving bucket, three powers, a slow zoom on the last target and a fever finish.

Schuss: downhill runs

A SkiFree style run in 5 modes (ski, snowboard, sled, mine cart on rails, raft on a river) with 3 goals: slalom against the clock, collect coins, or escape a yeti, a boulder or a flood wave. Every course is ridden once by its own autopilot before anyone sees it, so every seed can be finished. 42 drawn course pieces across three worlds.

Quizmaster: a quiz written from your records

Register your game's records (items, monsters, heroes, any array of structs) and name the fields worth asking about. Quizmaster writes multiple choice questions whose answers are facts in that data, and asks one only when exactly one answer is right. A round against up to three rivals at three skill levels, or a solo exam with a pass mark, on 6 drawn stages.

Built to live in one project

Each part keeps its own prefix (pgf_, shs_, qzm_ scripts and PGF_, SHS_, QZM_ macros), so two parts never define the same name. All three packages were imported into one blank GameMaker project, built with Igor on runtime 2024.14.4.268, and each part's own buyer checks ran inside it: 101 assertions passed, and all 21 test renders matched each part tested alone, byte for byte.
