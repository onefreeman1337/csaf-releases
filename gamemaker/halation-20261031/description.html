Halation is a lens flare system for GameMaker 2024.14 with 80 designs: 8 lens kits by 10 light kinds, every ghost, spike and ring drawn in pure GML.

A light declares its kind and colour, and the package generates its flare through the lens you choose. One commercial licence covers all your games, and a free Windows demo of the whole thing is on this page.

What is inside

80 flare designs · 8 lens kits · 10 light kinds · 5 occlusion modes · glints · a demo room · raw GML source

  • Eight lenses: Vintage Prime, Anamorphic Cinema, Cheap Phone, Space Telescope, Fantasy Crystal, Retro Pixel, Night Vision and Dreamy Soft. Make your own from any of them.
  • Ten lights: sun, moon, torch, lantern, neon tube, muzzle flash, magic orb, eye glint, headlamp and explosion, each with its own colour temperature and flicker.
  • Real optics: ghosts string along the axis through your frame's centre and take the shape of the aperture. A 7-blade lens draws 14 spikes, as a real one does.
  • It knows what is in the way: objects, a tile layer, a mask surface, a line of sight or your own function. A sun half behind a column gives half its flare, and it fades.
  • Lens dirt and veiling glare that light up as a bright source crosses the frame.
  • Glints for gems and coins, and one that runs along a blade.

Three lines to a flare

// Create
my_lens = hal_world_create({ kit: "vintage", occluder: { mode: "objects", objects: obj_wall } });
my_sun = hal_light_add(my_lens, "sun", 200, 90);
// Step
hal_world_step(my_lens);
// Draw GUI
hal_world_draw(my_lens);

There is no Clean Up call. The flares draw with no sprite, surface, shader or vertex buffer, so a room change or a game_restart has nothing to leak.

Works with

GameMaker 2024.14 and later, built and tested on runtime 2024.14.4.268 (Windows, VM). HTML5, mobile and YYC were not built. Import with Tools > Import Local Package, then run rm_hal_demo. A world matrix, a camera or a GUI scale you have set simply applies, and your draw state is handed back. Read the full documentation.
