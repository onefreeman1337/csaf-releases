Halation is out: lens flares, glints and glare for GameMaker, in pure GML.

A light declares its kind (sun, moon, torch, lantern, neon, muzzle flash, magic orb, eye glint, headlamp or explosion) and looks through one of 8 lens kits, and the package generates the flare: ghosts along the axis through the frame's centre in the shape of the aperture, spikes, rainbow rings, anamorphic streaks, veiling glare and lens dirt. That is 80 designs, and you can define your own lens from any of them.

Flares fade when something is in the way, against objects, a tile layer, a mask surface, a line of sight or your own function. They draw in 1.5 ms to 1.6 ms for a full sun on the VM, with no sprite, surface, shader or vertex buffer, so there is nothing to clean up and nothing to leak on a game_restart. The fresh install restarts the game three times and reads the engine's own resource counts each time: they do not move.

A free Windows demo is on the page: one location from noon to night, with every lens on a key.
