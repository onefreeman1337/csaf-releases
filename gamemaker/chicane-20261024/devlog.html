Chicane is out for GameMaker: top-down racing with every circuit generated from a seed and drawn by the package, and the race layer done.

One call makes a circuit. It is a loop of straights and round corners, hairpins where the corner is sharp and sweepers where it is open, sometimes with a chicane pushed into a long straight, and it is checked before you see it: no corner tighter than the theme's car can drive, and no two parts of the track close enough for their run-offs to touch. A second call draws the whole thing once into a sprite: mown grass, grained asphalt with a darker rubbered line and braking streaks, kerbs on every apex, gravel traps, armco and tyre walls, a chequered line and numbered grid boxes, a pit lane with garages where the start straight has room, and grandstands full of spectators. There are eight themes, from a grand prix to a snow rally stage, a harbour and a night street circuit lit by its lamps.

The race itself is the part a racing project usually ends up writing by hand. Positions come from 24 checkpoint gates per lap, so cutting a corner gains nothing: a gate crossed on the inside of a corner beyond the kerb is either owed until the car comes back for it or credited with a time penalty, your choice, and running wide is never a cut. Gaps are timed at the gates, laps and sectors carry the purple, green and yellow colour code, and the HUD draws a position tower, a timing panel, a minimap and a five light start.

The AI drivers follow a racing line computed for each circuit, brake to a speed profile built from its corners, pull out of the tow to pass on the side with room, and cover the inside when someone sits behind them. In the test suite, one 3 lap race of 8 AI cars on each of the eight themes finished every car, with no car stuck against a barrier and 19 passes. The cars are drawn too: formula, kart, rally and saloon bodies with generated liveries, or your own sprite.

Every test runs in a real exe built by GameMaker's own compiler (runtime 2024.14.4.268, VM, Windows); the shipped package is installed into a blank project and the Readme's own code run. HTML5, mobile and YYC exports were not built. Made with AI assistance: the code and the graphics were written with AI, and every picture is drawn by that code.
