Fairway is out for GameMaker: mini golf with every hole generated from a seed and drawn by the package, and a par for every hole set by playing it first.

One call makes a course of 9 or 18 holes. Each hole is a lane walked across a grid, widened into greens at its ends, with its corners cut into 45 degree banks and walled by bevelled rails. Then a few features are placed and checked against the tee, the cup and each other: a windmill across the lane whose sails close its tunnel for part of every turn, a swing gate whose whole swing stays on the felt, a spinner, a ramp jump over a channel, a pipe that is a shortcut, bumpers, wedges, sand, a pond, hills and hollows. The felt is lit by its own slopes and striped by the mower, so a hill reads as a hill before the ball rolls over it.

Par comes from a shot planner. Before a hole is kept, the planner plays it from the tee, simulating shots straight at the cup, along the felt toward it and off the rails, and keeping the best each time. Par is its strokes plus one, because it knows the physics exactly, and a hole it cannot finish in six strokes is thrown away and the next one generated. The same planner plays the AI rivals, with its aim and pace scattered by a skill setting.

There are six themes, each with its own ground, rails, building, water, pipe, bumpers and scenery: a rose garden with a white windmill, a smugglers' cove with a lighthouse, a haunted manor, a sweetshop with a gingerbread mill and candy-cane rails, an orbit station with a reactor and the void, and sunken desert ruins with a water-wheel tower. The game keeps the strokes, penalties and turns for up to four players, and the scorecard is drawn as a paper card with pencilled figures.

Every test runs in a real exe built by GameMaker's own compiler (runtime 2024.14.4.268, VM, Windows): the suite's 410 assertions passed on two runs, including 720 full-power shots that hit the rails 4,353 times without one ball leaving the course, and the shipped package was installed into a blank project and the Readme's own code run through a full 9-hole round. HTML5, mobile and YYC exports were not built. Made with AI assistance: the code and the graphics were written with AI, and every picture is drawn by that code.
