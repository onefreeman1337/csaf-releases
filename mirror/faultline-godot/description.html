Faultline is a Godot 4.7 volume of four 2D destruction addons with 148 presets, working on your own TileMapLayers and sprites under Forward+ and Compatibility.

Seam makes the ground of your map diggable, with crack stages, chunks in your own tile colours, ore veins and drops. Smithereens breaks your own sprites the way their material would. Wrack turns your town into its aftermath without editing the map. The new layer, Faultline, joins the three: one damage call that breaks what stands on a cell, then the cell, then lays the scorch; one saved world state for all three; and 12 chain reactions.

One call, one save

Add a FaultlineWorld beside your layers and call damage(point, radius, power). It hits the props standing there first, digs the ground second, and lays the Wrack look last, so no smoke rises from a hole. get_world_state() returns one JSON safe record of what the player destroyed across all three packs; load_world_state() puts it back on a fresh scene. Faultline finds the parts by what they can do, so it works with any one of them missing.

The 12 chains and the Quarry

Powder keg, fault line, lantern fire, cellar collapse, meteor strike, root heave, blight bloom, flash flood, cave in, ore blast, crypt seal and gem resonance, each a resource you can duplicate and edit step by step. Press F5: the menu opens each part's own demo, the chains on loop, and the Quarry, a whole town where you set chains off with the mouse, save the world and load it back.
