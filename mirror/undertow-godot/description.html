Undertow is a Godot 4.7 volume of three 2D water addons with 144 presets, working on your own TileMapLayers under Forward+ and Compatibility.

Foreshore draws shoreline foam, surf and a tide along your coast. Creel puts fishing on your water tiles. Wake makes the water answer whatever moves in it: a wading foot, a swimmer, a boat, a splash, the rain. Every coast, catch and ring is drawn at runtime by shader and GDScript code, and no addon ships an image file.

Using them together

Put Foreshore2D, Wake2D and CreelWater2D beside your TileMapLayers. Foreshore and Wake read the same TileSet tag; Creel finds the water by its own tag, the layer name or the tile colour. Four signal lines connect the rod to the water, so every cast, bite and catch rings it. Give your player a WakeBody2D and each step through the shallows leaves rings.

The harbour

Press F5. The menu opens each part's own demo and the harbour, where the three run on one map: surf on the beach, an angler fishing from the pier by itself, a rowboat with its wake, and you wading with the arrow keys.
