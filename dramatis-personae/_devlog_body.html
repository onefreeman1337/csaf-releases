Dramatis Personae is a 50 track score built around people instead of places. 12 JRPG characters each own one five note theme, and each theme ships in the 4 forms a game cues for a character: the theme, a battle version, a music box memory and an entrance stinger. It is live now at $11.99, with 2 full tracks free to download from the page.

The idea. No two of the twelve cells are the same, and no inversion, retrograde or contraction of one is another's, so each character stays recognisable in every scene. Every one of a character's tracks is written from that character's cell and no other. The title quotes six of them; the finale states all twelve and twice sets two at once, the usurper's cell in the counter line under another character's.

How it was checked. 884 theory checks and 564 audio checks, 0 failed, with the reports in the download, plus a product check that finds each character's cell in the MIDI of all 4 of its tracks. 38 tracks loop on sample accurate LOOPSTART and LOOPLENGTH tags that RPG Maker reads directly. Those numbers say the music is correctly written, rendered and tagged; they are not a claim about taste, which is what the free tracks (The Hero and The Hero Draws Steel) are for.

The renders come from the MIDI through a General MIDI soundfont, not a console sound chip. The composition system is AI written code, and no generative audio model is used.
