Blackwater: 14 Animated PSX Zombies and Infected is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Blackwater is also sold on the Last Shift page, together with 13 PSX survivors on the same skeleton. Blackwater is 14 low poly PS1-style zombies and infected for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required. What you get 14 infected, each a different body and a different painted costume: the people of a small river town a week after the outbreak.

Shambler, infected nurse, infected officer, runner, bloated, infected janitor, quarantine soldier, burned, drowned, screamer, crawler, patient zero, infected priest and brute. 3 palette skins each: the clothes change colour, while the dead skin, hair and boots keep their paint.

45 animation clips plus a T-pose: idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, punches, pistol aim, shoot and reload, sword, spell, two hit reactions, a death, sit, swim, push, pick up, interact and more.
