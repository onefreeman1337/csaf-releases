Syndicate is 14 low poly PS1-style crime syndicate gangsters, hitmen and enforcers for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get A 1990s city crime syndicate from the boss to the street, 14 people: syndicate boss, club owner, hitman, getaway driver, enforcer, counsellor, loan shark, bodyguard, bookie, street runner, accountant, bouncer, safecracker and informant. Every one an adult, each a different body and outfit, in tailored suits, leather and street clothes.

The enemies a city shooter needs: bosses, muscle and street contacts share one skeleton and one set of clip names, so one animation script drives the whole syndicate.
