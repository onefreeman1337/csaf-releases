Ironvale, Oathfall, Highcourt: 42 PSX Fantasy Characters is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Ironvale, Oathfall and Highcourt are 42 low poly PS1-style fantasy characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 heroic adventurers, 14 dark knights and undead warriors, and 14 people of a royal court.

Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips and comes in 3 palette skins (126 to place), about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. One purchase downloads all three packs; if you bought this page before, Highcourt is already in your itch library at no extra cost.

Free samplers of all three (the knight, the black knight and the king, every skin, clip and format) are on this page.
