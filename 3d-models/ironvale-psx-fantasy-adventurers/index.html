Ironvale: 14 PSX Fantasy Characters (Rigged, Animated)

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Genre: Role Playing. Paid.

Ironvale: 14 PSX Fantasy Characters (Rigged, Animated) is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

A party of 14 fantasy adventurers for your PS1-style RPG: low poly, hand painted, rigged and animated, ready to walk out of the tavern and into the dungeon. What you get 14 characters, each a different body AND a different painted outfit: knight, ranger, rogue, cleric, wizard, barbarian, paladin, bard, monk, necromancer, druid, mercenary, squire, hedge witch. 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place.

On the armoured and leather characters only the main garment changes colour (a red surcoat becomes blue or green); steel, leather, trim, hair and skin keep their painted colours.

45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose: idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, 3 sword clips (idle, attack), 4 spell clips (enter, idle, cast, exit), punches, two hit reactions, a death, sit, swim, push, pick up, interact, kneel, torch idle and more. There is no bow clip: the ranger uses the sword and spell clips.

1495-1499 triangles per character, one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering. The PlayStation look, not a modern model with a filter on top.
