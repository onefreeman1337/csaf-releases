Saint Oswin: 14 Animated PSX Asylum Staff and Patients

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Saint Oswin: 14 Animated PSX Asylum Staff and Patients is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Saint Oswin is also sold on the Hollow Congregation page, together with 14 PSX cultists on the same skeleton. Saint Oswin is 14 low poly PS1-style asylum staff and patients for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get A whole institution, 14 people: the director, head nurse, orderly, psychiatrist, night nurse, night warden, chaplain, surgeon, maintenance man, investigator, ward patient, striped patient, long-stay patient and sleepless patient. Every one an adult, each a different body and outfit.

Caps you can see from every angle: the head nurse's starched cap, the night warden's peaked cap and the maintenance man's knit cap are modelled on the head, not painted. 3 palette skins each: scrubs, pyjamas and uniforms change colour as a set; cardigans and jackets change alone; faces, hair and shoes keep their paint.
