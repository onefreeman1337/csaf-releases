Camp Redfern: 14 Animated PSX Summer Camp Cast is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Camp Redfern is also sold on the Last Shift page, together with 13 PSX survival horror survivors on the same skeleton. Camp Redfern is 14 low poly PS1-style summer camp counsellors and staff for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get A whole 1980s camp in the woods, 14 people: head counsellor, sports counsellor, night counsellor, the stranger, lifeguard, activities lead, crafts counsellor, joker counsellor, nature counsellor, camp cook, camp nurse, camp owner, groundskeeper and park ranger. Every one an adult, each a different body and outfit, and no campers.

The stranger: a hulking figure in a burlap sack mask, no gore and no weapon, so he fits any rating your game needs. 3 palette skins each: the jacket, sweatshirt or uniform changes colour alone, or the whole set for the tracksuits; faces, hair and shoes keep their paint.
