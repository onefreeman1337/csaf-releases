Butcher's Night: 14 Animated PSX Slasher Villains

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Butcher's Night: 14 Animated PSX Slasher Villains is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Butcher's Night is 14 low poly PS1-style masked villains for a 1980s small town horror game, for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,495 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get 14 villains, every one a different mask or face: a farmhand in a burlap sack hood, a woman in a cracked porcelain doll mask, a butcher in a pig mask, a welder behind his helmet, a woman in a rabbit mask, a motel manager with no mask at all, a whiteface clown, a masked surgeon, a masquerade guest in a gold half mask, a hunter in a camouflage balaclava, a woman in a smiling plastic mask, a trucker behind mirrored glasses, a neighbour in a rubber old man mask and a biker in skull face paint.

Every one an adult, each a different body and painted outfit. Masks, not gore: there is no blood, wound or weapon anywhere, so they work as villains, cultists or party guests and sit beside your victims without a content warning.
