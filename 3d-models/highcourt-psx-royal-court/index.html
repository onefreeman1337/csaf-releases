Highcourt is 14 low poly PS1-style people of a royal court for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,495 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get The castle your heroes are summoned to, 14 characters: king, queen, prince, princess, chancellor, royal guard, jester, court mage, steward, lady in waiting, ambassador, bishop, spymaster and herald. Every one an adult (the prince and princess in their twenties), each a different body and painted outfit.
