Crosstown: 14 Animated PSX City Civilians

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Crosstown: 14 Animated PSX City Civilians is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Crosstown is also sold on the Fort Vale page, together with 14 PSX modern soldiers on the same skeleton. Crosstown is 14 low poly PS1-style city civilians for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required. What you get 14 civilians, each a different body and a different outfit: the everyday street crowd of a modern city.

Commuter, bike courier, barista, construction worker, jogger, businesswoman, university student, grandmother, taxi driver, street vendor, tourist, bouncer, delivery rider and busker. Every one an adult. Crowd clips on one skeleton: walk, formal walk, jog, talk, sit and sit talking, driving, dance, push, pick up and interact, among 45 clips plus a T-pose, so a street fills without new animation work.

3 palette skins each: the main garment changes colour; faces, hair and shoes keep their paint.
