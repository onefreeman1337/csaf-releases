Outpost: 14 Animated PSX Sci-Fi Marines and Crew

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Outpost: 14 Animated PSX Sci-Fi Marines and Crew is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Outpost is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style sci-fi characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1492-1498 triangles and a 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

The garrison and colony crew of a remote frontier outpost: hard armour marines beside the people they guard. What you get 14 characters, each a different body and a different painted costume: marine rifleman, android, marine sergeant, colony farmer, heavy trooper, dropship pilot, base commander, combat engineer, recon scout, field medic, smuggler, ore miner, xenobiologist, colony rebel.

3 palette skins each, 42 characters to place. The armour or garment changes colour (the rifleman's olive plates to desert sand or dark slate, the heavy trooper's dark red to gunmetal or sand, the pilot's sky blue flight suit to rust or sage); faces, undersuits and boots keep their paint.
