Deep Station, Black Site: 28 PSX Horror Characters is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Deep Station and Black Site are 28 low poly PS1-style horror characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 crew of a deep space mining station and 14 staff, guards and test subjects of an underground containment facility.

Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips and comes in 3 palette skins (84 to place), about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. One purchase downloads both packs; if you bought this page before, Black Site is already in your itch library at no extra cost.

Free samplers of both (the station engineer and the containment specialist, every skin, clip and format) are on this page.
