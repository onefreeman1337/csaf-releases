Deep Station: 14 PSX Space Horror Crew (Rigged, Animated) is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Deep Station is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style sci-fi horror characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips, comes in 3 palette skins (42 characters to place), and has 1495-1499 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

A free sampler (the station engineer, every skin and clip) is on this page. The crew of a remote deep space mining station, the night something got loose: the captain, the engineers in their work jumpsuits, the medic, security, the scientist, the pilot, the company's man, the android, a stowaway, and two crew who did not make it.

What you get 14 crew, each a different body AND a different painted costume: station engineer, captain, medic, security officer, eva technician, company liaison, android, shuttle pilot, research scientist, stowaway, cargo loader, infected crewman, hydroponics tech, infected deck officer. 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place.
