Fort Vale + Crosstown: 28 PSX Soldiers and City Civilians

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Fort Vale + Crosstown: 28 PSX Soldiers and City Civilians is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Fort Vale + Crosstown is 28 low poly PS1-style modern characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 soldiers and 14 city civilians. Free samplers of both are on this page. Every character is rigged to the same humanoid skeleton with the same 45 animation clips, about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

One purchase downloads both packs; if you bought Fort Vale before, Crosstown is already in your itch library at no extra cost. Crosstown: 14 city civilians (added November 2026) The everyday street crowd of a modern city: commuter, bike courier, barista, construction worker, jogger, businesswoman, university student, grandmother, taxi driver, street vendor, tourist, bouncer, delivery rider and busker.

Every one an adult, each a different body and outfit. Crowd clips on the same skeleton as the soldiers: walk, formal walk, jog, talk, sit and sit talking, driving, dance, push, pick up and interact, so a street fills without new animation work. 3 skins each: the main garment changes colour, faces, hair and shoes keep their paint (42 to place).
