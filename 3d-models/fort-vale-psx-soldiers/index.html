Fort Vale: 70 PSX Soldiers, Civilians, Raiders and Gangsters is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? 70 low poly PS1-style characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 modern soldiers, 14 Second World War soldiers, 14 city civilians, 14 wasteland raiders and 14 crime syndicate gangsters. Fort Vale, Foxhole, Crosstown, Ashfall and Syndicate share one page and one purchase, with free samplers of all five here.

Every character is rigged to the same humanoid skeleton with the same 45 animation clips, about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. If you bought this page before, Foxhole is already in your itch library at no extra cost.

Foxhole: 14 Second World War soldiers of four armies (added November 2026) Four armies: American, British, German and Soviet riflemen, a US sergeant, paratrooper and medic, a German machine gunner and squad leader, a British officer and commando, a Soviet sniper, tank crewman and field medic. All adults. Modelled helmets and caps, not painted ones, so the armies read apart by silhouette.

Plain uniforms: no insignia, badges, patches, flags or emblems of any kind, and the medics carry plain bags with no red cross.
