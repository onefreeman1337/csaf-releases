Bloom: 14 Animated PSX Space Horror Infected Characters

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Bloom: 14 Animated PSX Space Horror Infected Characters is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Bloom is 14 low poly PS1-style infected crew of a deep space station, the enemies for a science fiction survival horror game, for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page.

Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,496 to 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get 14 infected from one station: a rigger and an ore miner in orange jumpsuits, an EVA technician in a cracked pressure undersuit, a lab technician, a security officer in an armoured vest, the galley cook, the captain in a burgundy command jacket, the medic, a shuttle pilot, the comms officer, a reactor technician, the company executive in a charcoal suit, the hydroponics technician where it started, and the first host, gaunt and overgrown.

Every one an adult, each a different body and painted outfit.
