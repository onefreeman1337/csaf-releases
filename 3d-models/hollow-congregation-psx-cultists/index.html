Hollow Congregation: 14 PSX Cultist Characters (Rigged, Animated)

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Hollow Congregation: 14 PSX Cultist Characters (Rigged, Animated) is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Hollow Congregation is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style horror cultist characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips, comes in 3 palette skins (42 characters to place), and has 1494-1498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

A free sampler (the high priest, every skin and clip) is on this page. The faithful of a small rural cult, its priest, and the townsfolk it has already taken: the people your survivors are running from.

What you get 14 characters, each a different body AND a different painted outfit: high priest, the vessel, masked executioner, sister of the congregation, acolyte, the chosen, chanter, converted orderly, groundskeeper, converted deputy, penitent, matriarch, scribe, bell ringer. 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place.

The main garment changes colour (a black vestment becomes oxblood or dark green, the vessel's linen goes charcoal or slate); trim, bone charms, ribbons, leather, hair and skin keep their painted colours.
