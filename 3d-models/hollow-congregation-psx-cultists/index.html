Hollow Congregation + Saint Oswin: 28 PSX Horror Characters

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Hollow Congregation + Saint Oswin: 28 PSX Horror Characters is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Hollow Congregation + Saint Oswin is 28 low poly PS1-style horror characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal: 14 rural cultists and 14 staff and patients of a decaying asylum. Free samplers of both are on this page. Every character is rigged to the same humanoid skeleton with the same 45 animation clips, about 1,500 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

One purchase downloads both packs; if you bought Hollow Congregation before, Saint Oswin is already in your itch library at no extra cost.

Saint Oswin: 14 asylum staff and patients (added November 2026) A whole institution: the director, head nurse, orderly, psychiatrist, night nurse, night warden, chaplain, surgeon, maintenance man, investigator, ward patient, striped patient, long-stay patient and sleepless patient. Every one an adult, each a different body and outfit.

Caps you can see from every angle: the head nurse's starched cap, the night warden's peaked cap and the maintenance man's knit cap are modelled on the head, not painted. 3 skins each (42 to place): scrubs, pyjamas and uniforms change colour as a set; cardigans and jackets change alone; faces, hair and shoes keep their paint.
