Night Beat: 14 Animated PSX Police, Detectives and SWAT

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Night Beat: 14 Animated PSX Police, Detectives and SWAT is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Night Beat is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style police officers, detectives and SWAT for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 uniform palettes (42 to place), 1492-1498 triangles and a 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required. A whole precinct for a PlayStation era crime, cop or horror game: the beat, the detectives, the tactical team, the county and the station.

What you get 14 characters, each a different body and a different painted uniform: patrol officer, homicide detective, SWAT pointman, sergeant, SWAT breacher, motorcycle officer, state trooper, plainclothes detective, riot officer, county deputy, forensic technician, federal agent, dispatcher, prison guard. Real heights, 1.70 m to 1.94 m. The scale sits on the armature, so the clips scale with it.

3 palette skins each, 42 characters to place.
