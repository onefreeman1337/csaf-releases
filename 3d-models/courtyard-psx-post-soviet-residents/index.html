Courtyard: 14 Animated PSX Post-Soviet Residents

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Courtyard: 14 Animated PSX Post-Soviet Residents is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Courtyard is 14 low poly PS1-style residents of a 1990s post-Soviet apartment block courtyard, for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1,494 to 1,498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

What you get 14 neighbours for an Eastern Bloc housing estate: a babushka in a headscarf and quilted coat, an old chess player in a flat cap, a lad in a plain tracksuit, a student in a denim jacket, a kiosk vendor in a mohair cardigan, a neighbour in a white vest, a taxi driver in a leather jacket, a patrolman in a peaked cap, a postwoman, a plumber in a work coverall, a yard sweeper in an orange quilted jacket and knit cap, a schoolteacher, a night-shift nurse and a pensioner in a fur hat.

Every one an adult, each a different body and painted outfit.
