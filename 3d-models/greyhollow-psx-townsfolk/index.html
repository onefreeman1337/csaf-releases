Greyhollow: 14 PSX Medieval Townsfolk (Rigged, Animated)

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Greyhollow: 14 PSX Medieval Townsfolk (Rigged, Animated) is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Greyhollow is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style medieval townsfolk characters for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal. Every character is rigged to one humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips, comes in 3 palette skins (42 characters to place), and has 1494-1498 triangles and one 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats. Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required.

A free sampler (the innkeeper, every skin and clip) is on this page. The working people of a walled market town, for PlayStation era fantasy role playing games: the smith, the inn, the market, the mill, the church and the stables. The NPCs your hero walks past, talks to and buys from.

What you get 14 townsfolk, each a different body AND a different painted costume: blacksmith, washerwoman, town guard, innkeeper, village priest, miller, merchant, town crier, herbalist, beggar, noblewoman, farmer, fisherwoman, stable hand. 3 palette skins each = 42 characters to place.
