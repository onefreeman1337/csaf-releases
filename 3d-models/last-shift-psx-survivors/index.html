Last Shift: 13 PSX Survival Horror Characters (Rigged, Animated)

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Last Shift: 13 PSX Survival Horror Characters (Rigged, Animated) is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

13 small-town night shift survivors for your PS1-style horror game: low poly, hand painted, rigged and animated, ready to walk into your fog. What you get 13 characters, each a different body AND a different painted outfit: mechanic, night nurse, patrol officer, teacher, priest, trucker, student, hiker, diner cook, security guard, journalist, farmer, doctor.

4 palette skins each = 52 characters to place (clothes recoloured, skin tones kept). 45 animation clips on every character plus a T-pose: idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, roll, punches, pistol aim/shoot/reload, sword, spell, two hit reactions, a death, sit, swim, push, pick up, interact, kneel and fix, torch idle, driving and more.

1493-1500 triangles per character, one 256 x 256 px texture per skin, quantised to 15-bit colour for nearest filtering. The PlayStation look, not a modern model with a filter on top. Per character: GLB with every clip, GLB per extra skin, FBX with clips, OBJ + MTL, PNG textures, and the Blender source file.

A PSX shader for Godot 4 (godot_psx.gdshader): vertex snapping, affine texture warp, 15-bit colour with dithering, distance fog. Every setting is a slider.
