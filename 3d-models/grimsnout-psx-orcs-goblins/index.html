Grimsnout: 14 Animated PSX Orcs, Goblins and Brutes

A 3D model pack from Core Systems Asset Factory. Made with Blender. Paid.

Grimsnout: 14 Animated PSX Orcs, Goblins and Brutes is a 3D model pack for Blender, paid; its description points to a free sampler.

Want the whole set? Grimsnout is a pack of 14 low poly PS1-style orcs, goblins and an ogre for Godot 4, Unity and Unreal, rigged and animated, with a free sampler on this page. Every character has 45 animation clips on one humanoid skeleton, 3 palette skins (42 to place), 1493-1499 triangles and a 256 px texture per skin, in GLB, FBX, OBJ and Blender formats.

Use them in any number of commercial games, no attribution required. The enemy side of a dark fantasy RPG: an orc warband, goblin raiders and an ogre brute, at three real heights.

What you get 14 characters, each a different body and a different painted costume: orc warrior, goblin scout, orc shieldmaiden, goblin tinker, ogre brute, goblin cutpurse, orc shaman, orc warchief, goblin hexer, orc huntress, goblin warboss, orc berserker, orc camp cook, orc jailer. Real heights, 1.28 m to 2.10 m. The goblins stand well below the orcs and the ogre towers over them.

The scale sits on the armature, so the clips scale with it. 3 palette skins each, 42 characters to place.
