THEODOLITE - credits
====================

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory.

WHAT SHIPS
----------
  index.html, css/theodolite.css, js/cg_platform.js, js/sim.js, js/audio.js, js/game.js,
  art/plate_paper.png, art/plate_baize.png,
  audio/theodolite_theme.ogg, and these three text files.

PICTURES
--------
  Two plates ship: the map's cream paper with its fibres and foxing
  (plate_paper.png) and the survey office's green baize desk (plate_baize.png),
  each 128 px and seamless, generated for this game by the studio's own
  procedural pixel engine. No diffusion model and no stock texture was used.
  Everything else is drawn at runtime by js/game.js from the simulation in
  js/sim.js. Unsurveyed ground is a pencil sketch; each cell the plane-tables
  ink becomes an engraving: a wash by the kind of ground, hachures down every
  slope, trees in the woods, hedgerows on the low ground, water lining on the
  lakes and along the coast, contour lines traced over the heights, and the
  towns with their spires and names. Over it, the network: trig stations,
  observed sights in red ink, sights still to read in pencil, the observing
  parties at their theodolites and the heliotropes flashing from the far
  stations.

MUSIC
-----
  "Baseline", composed for this game (G major, a walking pace in 4/4 at 84 BPM,
  a 64-beat loop that joins end to start without a gap, with a high two-note
  bird call every other bar). Written as note data and rendered by the studio's
  own synthesiser; no generative audio model and no sample library.

SOUND
-----
  Every effect is synthesised live in the browser by js/audio.js with WebAudio
  oscillators and one fixed buffer of noise: a wooden knock when a station is
  raised, a small bell when a sight is read, two rising tones when a station is
  fixed, a quill scratching as the ground is inked, a clank for a tower or a new
  party, coins, a chime, a rubber stamp for a published sheet, a low swell when
  the fog comes in and a rising cadence when the county is done.

TYPE
----
  The browser's own Georgia and Trebuchet MS / Verdana stacks. No web font is
  downloaded.

THIS BUILD
----------
  The CrazyGames build. js/cg_platform.js connects the game to the CrazyGames
  SDK: your survey is kept by CrazyGames' own save, the site's mute setting
  is obeyed, and the game pauses and goes quiet while an advert plays.

AI DISCLOSURE
-------------
  This game was written with AI assistance: the code, the text, and the code
  that draws and generates every picture. No generative image or audio model was
  used in the game.
