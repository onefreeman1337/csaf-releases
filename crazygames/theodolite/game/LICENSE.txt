A NOTE ON THE SURVEY
--------------------
The survey follows the shape of a real triangulation (a measured baseline, trig
stations fixed from pairs of stations already known, error that grows with long
sights and badly shaped triangles, heliotropes flashing sunlight through haze,
plane-table parties filling in the detail), with the numbers compressed so a
season takes half a minute. It is a game, not a textbook.
