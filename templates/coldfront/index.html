questions

The ones everybody asks.

Do I need a whole new system? +

Usually not. Capacitors, contactors and low charge cause most no-cool calls and are a few hundred dollars. We will tell you when a replacement genuinely makes more sense than another repair.

How fast can you get here? +

Same day for no-cool calls, seven days a week. Maintenance-plan customers go to the front of the queue when it is 98 degrees and everyone is calling at once.

What does a service call cost? +

We quote the diagnostic fee on the phone before we drive out, and waive it if you go ahead with the repair. You will never get a surprise invoice from us.

Do you offer financing? +

Yes, on replacements. A system dying in August is not something most households have budgeted for, so we will show you monthly options before you decide anything.

Why is one room always hotter? +

Almost always airflow — a crushed duct, a leaking run in the attic, or a system that was never balanced. It is measurable, and it is fixable.
