good questions

The stuff you're probably wondering.

How much does a website cost? +

Right now I'm taking a few founding clients at intro rates — one-page sites start at $800. Standard pricing runs $1,500 to $7,500+ depending on size and features. For reference, agencies charge $15,000–$60,000+ for the same fully custom animated builds — you get that caliber without the agency invoice. The first chat is always free — by email or a quick call, whatever you prefer.

How long does it take? +

Spark and Bloom sites usually launch in 2–3 weeks. Full Signature 3D builds take 4–6. Either way, you'll get a clear timeline up front — no mystery.

I don't have photos or a logo. Can you still help? +

Absolutely. I can source beautiful imagery, generate custom visuals, and help shape your brand look so everything feels polished and pulled-together.

Do I own my website? +

100%. When we launch, the site and your domain are yours — no lock-in, no holding your website hostage. Ever.

What if I need changes later? +

Small tweaks are easy — grab a Care Plan and I'll handle updates for you, or reach out any time for a quick quote on bigger changes.

Do you only work with local businesses? +

Local is my love, but I build for anyone who wants a site that stands out. If you've got a vision, let's talk about it.
