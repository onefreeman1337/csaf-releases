good to know

Before you order.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake? +

One week for most celebration cakes, 4–6 weeks for tiered and wedding cakes. Rush? Ask — we love a challenge.

Do you have gluten-free options? +

Gluten-friendly bakes land every Friday, and most custom cakes can be made GF — just tell us when ordering.

Do you deliver? +

Custom cakes, yes — carefully, in a van with air conditioning and a very cautious driver.

Can we do a wedding tasting? +

Please do. Six flavors, two fillings each, unlimited opinions. Book a slot below.

What time do fresh bakes come out? +

7am on the dot. The croissant line forms at 6:55. You think we're joking.
