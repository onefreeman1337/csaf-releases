good to know

Lash questions, answered.

How long does a full set take? +

Plan for 2–2.5 hours of pure relaxation — most clients nap. Fills take 60–90 minutes.

How long do extensions last? +

With good aftercare, sets stay full for 2–3 weeks between fills, following your natural lash cycle.

Will extensions damage my natural lashes? +

Not when applied properly. We isolate every natural lash and choose weights your lashes can happily carry.

How should I prep for my appointment? +

Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes and skip caffeine right before — flutter-free eyes make for perfect sets.

What if I need to reschedule? +

Life happens! Just give us 24 hours' notice and we'll happily move your appointment.
