good to know

Before you sit down.

Do I need an appointment? +

Bookings guarantee your slot, but walk-ins are part of the culture — the wait's rarely more than one coffee.

How long does a cut take? +

30–45 minutes for a cut, about an hour for the full Works. We don't rush good work.

Do you sell product? +

Pomades, clays, beard oil — the exact stuff we use on you, so it looks the same on Tuesday.

Kids welcome? +

Absolutely. First-cut certificates and a steady hand for wiggly customers.

What if I don't know what I want? +

That's our favorite. Sit down, tell us about your week, and trust the process.
