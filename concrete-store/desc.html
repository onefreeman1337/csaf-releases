103 sounds. 17 modern places that feel empty. Car parks, subway platforms, laundromats, stairwells, a mall after hours, a server room, an office at night, a storm drain, a closed arcade, an indoor pool. Every one of them shipped in separable layers, not as one baked atmosphere.

A place comes apart

Every place gives you a seamless bed (30s), 2 layers you fade independently, and accents you fire on top. Start the bed, leave it running, and ride the layers underneath — one room covers empty, occupied and busy without a crossfade and without the loop restarting. The beds and layers are deliberately different lengths, so a room left running never repeats on a fixed cycle.

Every interior hums. The exteriors deliberately do not.

A 60 Hz mains hum — fluorescent ballast, transformer, the supply in a sleeping monitor — runs under all 12 interiors. That is what makes 17 rooms sound like one building instead of 17 unrelated atmospheres. Spraying it everywhere would just be one patch with reverb on it, so the 5 exteriors and water spaces are free of it.

That claim is measured, not asserted. The pack ships pack_proof.json with a harmonic-to-shoulder energy ratio at the mains frequencies for every room: the humming rooms measure 9.67× to 216.18×, the clear rooms 0.27× to 2.2×. The two groups do not overlap, and the build fails if any room crosses.

What you get

  • 103 sounds, each as 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono .wav and as .ogg
  • 51 seamless loops with sample-accurate loop points in both formats — a smpl chunk in the WAV and LOOPSTART/LOOPLENGTH comments in the OGG, which is what RPG Maker MV and MZ read. They were read back out of the encoded bytes after encoding, not assumed.
  • One loudness target — the inner range across all 103 files is 1.73 dB, so you can drop any two together without riding faders
  • pack_proof.json — 987 checks, 0 failed: clipping, silence, duration bands, loop seams two independent ways, level coherence, spectral and duration spread, and the hum spine
  • A plain-language LICENSE.txt, a readable manifest, and a copy-paste credit line if you want to use one

Try it first

The free demo is two complete places — one that hums and one that does not — with their beds, layers and accents, so you can test the layering, the loop points and the spine before paying. Both are the median of their group, not the best case.

Honest note

Every sound here is synthesised from code: hand-written modal materials, circularly-filtered noise beds, an additive mains-ballast oscillator and one shared room, rendered deterministically. No generative audio model was used at any point. The checks above measure the signal — they are not a claim that the sounds are beautiful. Download the free demo and listen.
