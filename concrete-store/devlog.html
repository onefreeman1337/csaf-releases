Concrete — Urban and Liminal Place Beds is out. 103 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The rooms that should hum, hum, and the rooms that should not, do not — measured rather than asserted. 12 places carry a declared 60 Hz electrical spine and 5 are declared clear, separated by the median per-harmonic ratio of the whole series. That is what makes the set sound like one building instead of unrelated atmospheres.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 987 checks, 0 failed. Loudness sits inside 1.73 dB across the pack, so nothing jumps when you swap one cue for another. 2 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
