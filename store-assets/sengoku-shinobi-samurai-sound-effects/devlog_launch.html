Sengoku: Shinobi and Samurai Sound Effects is out. 106 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack ships 3 escalation ladders: ordered ramps, not bags of cues. 9 rungs were resolved and 6 adjacent steps checked: every step moves in the declared direction by at least 8% in brightness or 12% in length, and every ladder spans at least 1.5x end to end. A ladder that stalls or reverses fails the build.

The pack ships 2 mechanisms as whole lifecycles rather than as single cues. 8 state files were resolved and 12 within-mechanism pairs were compared. 1 state is loopable and it carries real loop points, read out of the rendered file rather than out of the manifest. No state pair sits closer than 9% in brightness or 18% in length, which is what stops a lifecycle being one gesture under several names.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 970 checks, 0 failed. The inner 80 percent of the pack sits inside 1.2 dB of loudness, so swapping one cue for another does not jump. 25 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
