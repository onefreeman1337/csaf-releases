Threshold: Ability Gates, Unlocks and the Map Filling In is out. 104 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack ships 8 gates and every one of them carries exactly the same 5-stage chain (idle, refuse, hint, unlock and open), in the same order, under the same naming — resolved to 64 real files. That is the whole product: you read the stage list once and then wire it 8 times. It is checked mechanically rather than promised, and the build refuses the pack if any gate's chain differs from the canonical one by a single stage or a single position.

The pack ships 1 escalation ladder: ordered ramps, not bags of cues. 3 rungs were resolved and 2 adjacent steps checked: every step moves in the declared direction by at least 12% in length, and every ladder spans at least 1.5x end to end. A ladder that stalls or reverses fails the build.

The pack ships 8 gates as whole lifecycles rather than as single cues. 64 state files were resolved and 80 within-gate pairs were compared. 8 states are loopable and every one of them carries real loop points, read out of the rendered file rather than out of the manifest. No state pair sits closer than 9% in brightness or 18% in length, which is what stops a lifecycle being one gesture under several names.
