Clamber: Climbing Sound Effects is out. 660 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is a cube, not a list: 3 stacked grids, one per value of the third axis, and inside each grid every pair of cells that differ in exactly one thing was compared from the shipped files: 540 along the rows and 1188 down the columns, 648 files in all, each pair at least 9% apart in brightness or 18% in length. Failing grids: 0. The diagonal pairs are deliberately not compared, because no system ever asks for them.

The pack ships 72 escalation ladders: ordered ramps, not bags of cues. 216 rungs were resolved (648 files with their takes) and 144 adjacent steps checked: every step moves in the declared direction by at least 8% in brightness, and every ladder spans at least 1.5x end to end. A ladder that stalls or reverses fails the build.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 5962 checks, 0 failed. The inner 80 percent of the pack sits inside 2.99 dB of loudness, so swapping one cue for another does not jump. 216 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
