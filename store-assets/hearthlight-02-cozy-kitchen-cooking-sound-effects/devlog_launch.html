Hearthlight 02: Cozy Kitchen, From Pantry to Plate is out. 126 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack ships 8 dishes and every one of them carries exactly the same 6-stage cooking chain (prep, add, cook, done, plate and serve), in the same order, under the same naming — resolved to 56 real files. That is the whole product: you read the stage list once and then wire it 8 times, once per dish. It is checked mechanically rather than promised, and the build refuses the pack if any element's chain differs from the canonical one by a single stage or a single position.

The pack ships 2 escalation ladders: ordered ramps, not bags of cues. 8 rungs were resolved and 6 adjacent steps checked: every step moves in the declared direction by at least 8% in brightness, and every ladder spans at least 1.5x end to end. A ladder that stalls or reverses fails the build.

The pack ships 8 mechanisms as whole lifecycles rather than as single cues. 56 state files were resolved and 120 within-mechanism pairs were compared. 8 states are loopable and every one of them carries real loop points, read out of the rendered file rather than out of the manifest. No state pair sits closer than 9% in brightness or 18% in length, which is what stops a lifecycle being one gesture under several names.
