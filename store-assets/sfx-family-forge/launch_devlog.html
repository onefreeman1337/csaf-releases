SFX Family Forge is out. It is a small app that makes a whole family of game sound effects at once: pick what the sounds are made of (6 materials), set five knobs, press Forge, and get 116 sounds across 21 slots that belong to one game. Export them laid out for Godot 4, Unity, GameMaker or a plain folder.

The demo on the store page runs in your browser: whole families in two of the materials (Chip and Metal) and a 12 sound sampler to export. The paid download is a Windows app and a browser edition for Mac and Linux, with all 6 materials and every engine layout.

What we measured before shipping

We cannot listen, so we measure. The studio's own sound gate ran on 54 families forged by the shipped app (every material, the extremes of every knob, three seeds): 6264 sounds, 56916 checks, 0 failed. That covers clipping, silence, abrupt starts and ends, loop seams, and the family's loudness spread, which on the plate's sample family is 0.9 dB against 17.9 dB for unlevelled sounds.

An export imports into a blank Godot 4.7.2 project (7 of 7 checks) with both loops looping under the default import, and into a blank Unity 6 project as 116 clips at their folders (4 of 4). Then we broke the app on purpose 11 ways and checked that a test caught each one (11 of 11).

The sounds are synthesised by the app's own code: no recordings, no samples, no generative audio model. Royalty free, commercial use included, no credit required.
