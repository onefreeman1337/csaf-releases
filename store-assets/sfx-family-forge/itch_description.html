One vessel. A whole family of sound effects.

Free sound generators make one sound at a time from random settings, so the jump, the coin and the menu click rarely sound like the same game. SFX Family Forge makes the whole family at once: pick what the sounds are made of, press Forge, and get 116 sound effects that belong together, laid out for your engine.

Try it free, right here

The demo on this page runs in your browser. Forge and play whole families in two of the six materials (Chip and Metal), with every knob, slot and take, and export a 12 sound sampler. Buying unlocks the other four materials (Synth, Wood, Glass and Organic), the whole family on export and every engine layout.

Licence for what you forge, up front

Royalty free. Commercial use, client work included. No credit required. Every sound you forge can go in any game, app or video. The one thing you may not do is sell or give the sounds away as a sound pack or sound library. LICENSE.txt holds the plain language terms, with a copy and paste credit line if you want one. The app contains no recorded audio: every sound is synthesised by its own code.

Choose a vessel once

  • Six materials, each deciding what every sound in the family is made of: Chip (square and pulse waves with hard edges: the 8 bit console voice); Synth (detuned saws through a resonant filter: a modern synth voice); Metal (inharmonic struck bars and bells: steel, brass, armour); Wood (short hollow knocks and soft pitched blocks: wood, bone, clay); Glass (bright, clean partials with long shimmer: glass, crystal, ice); Organic (breathy, voiced and soft edged: creatures, cloth, nature).
  • Five knobs for the whole family: register, brightness, grit, space, punch.

21 slots, every one a game needs

Hover, click, confirm, cancel, error, coin, pickup, power up, jump, land, footstep, hit, hurt, shoot, explosion, magic, door, level up and death, plus a seamless ambience loop and a seamless alarm loop. 1 to 12 takes per slot (6 by default), so nothing has to repeat.

Levelled together, and measured

Every one shot is levelled toward one loudness target, so the family sits together in the mix. A report panel measures the family you just made: loudness spread, brightness range, shortest to longest sound.

Ready for your engine

One zip per family, laid out for Godot 4, Unity, GameMaker or a plain folder: 16 bit 44.1 kHz mono WAV, the two loops carrying sample accurate loop points, plus pack.json (every file, slot and setting) and FAMILY.txt. Checked in the engines: an export imports into a blank Godot 4.7.2 project with both loops looping on their own, and into Unity 6 as 116 clips at their folders.

The Windows app is not code signed, so SmartScreen may ask once: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Limits, honestly

  • WAV only, no OGG export in this version (RPG Maker MV and MZ need OGG: convert first).
  • Mono, 44.1 kHz. Slots are fixed; you choose the takes.
  • The app measures the sound; it does not listen. Audition the family before you ship it.
