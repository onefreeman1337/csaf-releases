Blip 03 — Dialogue Voices, a Third Cast is out. 104 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is a cast of 13 voices, each its own synthesis method rather than one patch re-pitched. Across the series the same check ran over 39 voices from 3 packs: 741 of 741 pairs separated, and 26 of 26 earlier voices planted back in as re-pitched copies were caught.
