Trail, Ranch and Fort: Western Sound Effects is out. 117 sounds in WAV and OGG for the western frontier beyond main street: the ranch and the cattle drive, the gold rush placer camp and the frontier fort. It extends Sixgun, our western town pack, into three places that pack never goes, in the same grammar: every source placed, and the relations a game asks for made measurable.

Every source placed. A cavalry carbine, a field cannon and a bullwhip are each heard on the open range, in a log stockade's yard and in a narrow rock gulch. The herd ships as a second grid: a small and a big herd at a walk, trot, lope and stampede (8 loops, each pace its own length so a game crossfades as the herd speeds up). Across both grids 34 pairs of cells that differ in one thing were compared from the shipped files, each at least 9% apart in brightness or 18% in length. Failing pairs: 0.

5 ladders: the cannon from near to distant (4 rungs, each darker), the two herds by pace, a drill squad at slow time, quick time and the double, and gold in the pan from a flake to a big nugget. 13 steps checked, every one moving the declared way by at least 8%, every ladder at least 1.5x end to end.

3 whole lifecycles (the stockade gate, the placer rocker and the flag halyard: start, loop, stop and fail) with 18 state pairs compared and none alike, and a fort under siege as three loops of one length that stack, summed and checked at all 3 levels. 28 loops in all, loop points written into the WAV and the OGG.
