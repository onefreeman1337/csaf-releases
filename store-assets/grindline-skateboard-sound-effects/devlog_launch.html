Grindline: 149 Skateboard Sound Effects is out. 149 sounds in WAV and OGG from one board: a maple deck, two aluminium trucks, four urethane wheels and griptape, and every place it rides.

The board rolling is a grid. a concrete bowl, smooth concrete, a wooden ramp, slab pavement, rough asphalt and a steel plate ramp, each at slow, cruising and fast speeds: 18 seamless loops. Each surface is its own recipe, and the three speeds of one surface share a length and a structure, so a game can crossfade by speed without a jump. 63 pairs of cells were compared and none came out the same.

The numbers a skate game already has pick the file. The ollie pop is a 5 step strength ladder for how long the jump was charged, the landing a 5 step drop height ladder from a curb to a big gap, the flip whoosh a ladder by spin speed, and every rolling surface a ladder by speed.

Grinds, slides and manuals are whole. A square rail, a round handrail, a waxed ledge, pipe coping and a painted curb, three board slides, two manuals and a wall ride: each one an enter, a seamless loop to hold for as long as the trick lasts, and an exit. 11 of them, 33 state pairs compared, none the same sound renamed. Bails come on concrete, wood and metal, with the board clattering and rolling away after.
