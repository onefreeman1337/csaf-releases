Hearthlight 03: Pet Care, From Greeting to Sleep is out. 126 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is a grid, 8 pet values by 8 stage values (cat, kitten, small dog, big dog, rabbit, ferret, parrot and hedgehog; greet, ask, eat, drink, petted, play, groom and sleep), so a game asks for the cell it needs and gets it. All 64 cells ship real audio (72 files), and along each axis every pair of cells was compared from the shipped files: 224 along the rows and 224 down the columns, each at least 9% apart in brightness or 18% in length. Indistinct pairs: 0. Change the pet and the sound changes; change the stage and it changes too.

The pack is a cast of 8 voices, each its own recipe rather than one patch re-pitched. All 28 pairs of voices were measured from the shipped files and every pair sits at least 12% apart in brightness or 25% in length; failing pairs: 0. The cast spans 12.16x in brightness.

The pack ships 8 pets and every one of them carries exactly the same 8-stage care chain (greet, ask, eat, drink, petted, play, groom and sleep), in the same order, under the same naming — resolved to 72 real files. That is the whole product: you read the stage list once and then wire it 8 times. It is checked mechanically rather than promised, and the build refuses the pack if any pet's chain differs from the canonical one by a single stage or a single position.

The pack ships 2 escalation ladders: ordered ramps, not bags of cues. 10 rungs were resolved and 8 adjacent steps checked: every step moves in the declared direction by at least 8% in brightness or 12% in length, and every ladder spans at least 1.5x end to end. A ladder that stalls or reverses fails the build.

The pack ships 8 pets as whole lifecycles rather than as single cues. 72 state files were resolved and 224 within-pet pairs were compared. 8 states are loopable and every one of them carries real loop points, read out of the rendered file rather than out of the manifest. No state pair sits closer than 9% in brightness or 18% in length, which is what stops a lifecycle being one gesture under several names.
