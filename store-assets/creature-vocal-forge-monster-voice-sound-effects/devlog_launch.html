Creature Vocal Forge: Monster Voice Sets is out. 120 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is a cast of 12 voices, each its own recipe rather than one patch re-pitched. All 66 pairs of voices were measured from the shipped files and every pair sits at least 12% apart in brightness or 25% in length; failing pairs: 0. The cast spans 15.72x in brightness.

The pack ships 12 creatures and every one of them carries exactly the same 6-stage state chain (idle, alert, attack, hurt, death and special), in the same order, under the same naming — resolved to 120 real files. That is the whole product: you read the stage list once and then wire it 12 times. It is checked mechanically rather than promised, and the build refuses the pack if any creature's chain differs from the canonical one by a single stage or a single position.

The pack ships 12 creatures as whole lifecycles rather than as single cues. 120 state files were resolved and 180 within-creature pairs were compared; 12 states are loopable and every one of them carries real loop points, read out of the rendered file rather than out of the manifest. No state pair sits closer than 9% in brightness or 18% in length, which is what stops a lifecycle being one gesture under several names.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 1103 checks, 0 failed. The inner 80 percent of the pack sits inside 0.29 dB of loudness, so swapping one cue for another does not jump. 36 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
