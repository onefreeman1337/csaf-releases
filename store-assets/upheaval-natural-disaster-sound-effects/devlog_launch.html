Upheaval — Natural Disasters, Layered and Rampable is out. 104 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is 8 systems you drive with one value: each ships loop layers that stack by intensity, all of one length, so a game fades layers in instead of swapping files. All 32 tier sums were re-summed from the shipped WAV files: each wraps without a click or a pump and stays under a 22 dB crest. Failing sums: 0.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 977 checks, 0 failed. Loudness sits inside 5.03 dB across the pack, so nothing jumps when you swap one cue for another. 0 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
