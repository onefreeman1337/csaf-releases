Leeward 02 — The Sea Fight is out. 124 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG with loop points on every loopable file, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is a grid, 3 projectile values by 3 perspective values (round shot, chain shot and grapeshot; on deck, below decks and from another ship), so a game asks for the cell it needs and gets it. All 9 cells ship real audio (9 files), and along each axis every pair of cells was compared from the shipped files: 9 along the rows and 9 down the columns, each at least 9% apart in brightness or 18% in length. Indistinct pairs: 0. Change the projectile and the sound changes; change the perspective and it changes too.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 1147 checks, 0 failed. Loudness sits inside 2.1 dB across the pack, so nothing jumps when you swap one cue for another. 23 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
