Bonbon: 185 Candy World Sound Effects is out. 185 sounds in WAV and OGG from one candy world: the candies, the places and the machines of the factory.

The candies are a grid. chocolate, marshmallow, licorice, gummy, jelly, cotton candy, hard candy and sugar glass, each answering the same 5 actions: collect, land on, bounce, bite and break. Two takes per cell, so a game wires one candy and swaps only the material. The brittle candies are struck bodies with cracks and crumbs, the elastic ones overshoot and settle, and the soft ones are air and fibres with nothing that rings. 220 pairs of cells were compared and none came out the same.

The numbers a candy game already has pick the file. A bubble blown at 5 sizes and popped at 5, bigger bubbles popping lower. A jawbreaker and a sugar glass bridge cracking in 4 stages. A 6 step collect streak that climbs by one fixed ratio, not a scale. Sweets poured into a jar, from a pinch to a bag.

The places and the factory loop. the chocolate river, the caramel bog, the soda spring, sprinkle rain, the rock candy cave, the jelly lake, wind over the sugar dunes, the frosting falls and the candy factory floor are seamless loops. 5 machines each have a start, a loop to hold for as long as they run, and a stop, and 19 state pairs were compared with none the same sound renamed.

There is a free demo on the page: 19 sounds, with gummy across all 5 actions, two more candies, both ends of the bubble and the streak, the taffy puller from start to stop and two places. The middle 80 percent of files sit within 1.41 dB of each other in loudness.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. The code and the cover were made with AI tools; the sounds were not: every one is procedural synthesis written in code, with no generative audio model anywhere in the pipeline. There is no music, no jingle and no voice. These measurements are signal checks, so please listen to the demo to judge the sound for yourself.
