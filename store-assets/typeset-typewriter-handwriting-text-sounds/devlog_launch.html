Typeset — Typewriter and Handwriting Text Sounds is out. 143 sounds, shipped as WAV and OGG, a naming manifest and per-engine import notes.

The pack is a cast of 11 voices, each its own synthesis method rather than one patch re-pitched. All 55 pairs of voices were measured from the shipped files and every pair sits at least 12% apart in brightness or 25% in length; failing pairs: 0. The cast spans 4.14x in brightness.

Royalty-free. Commercial use. No attribution required. Everything here is synthesised from scratch in code, so there are no samples and no upstream licence sitting behind it. The plain-language licence ships in the download. The only thing you may not do is resell the pack itself as a pack.

There is a free demo on the page — a real subset of the pack, not a teaser reel. Download it and listen before you decide.

What was measured, and it is in pack_proof.json inside the download so you can check it yourself: 1296 checks, 0 failed. Loudness sits inside 1.57 dB across the pack, so nothing jumps when you swap one cue for another. 11 variation families are declared and each was checked for real variation rather than the same render twice.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. Code and graphics are AI-assisted; the audio is not — every sample is procedural synthesis written in Node, with no generative-audio model anywhere in the pipeline.
