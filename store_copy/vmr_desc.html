Dawn is coming and you are extremely not a morning person.

Shut every window. Drape every mirror. Get the garlic out of the house. Then get in the coffin and hold the lid shut. You have about two minutes, you are fast and heavy and clumsy, and the light bends around whatever you knock over.

Free, plays in your browser, no account, nothing to install.

The sunlight is a real object, not a timer

Most vampire games treat daylight as a countdown, or as a zone painted on the floor. Here it is simulated:

  • The sun is one angle. It rises in the east and crosses to the west over the course of the night, so a window in the left-hand wall is only dangerous while the sun is on that side. Halfway through, the side windows go quiet and the skylights open up. The threat moves, on a schedule you can read off the floor.
  • Beams stop at things. A wall, a bookcase, a wardrobe you have shoved into place. The shadow behind an object is real cover, and it moves when the object does.
  • Mirrors redirect. A bare mirror turns a beam ninety degrees. Shoving a mirror does not trigger anything, it re-aims a shaft, because the shaft was always being cast off wherever that mirror happens to be standing.
  • The chore list is the light-management list. Shuttering a window deletes a beam at its source. Draping a mirror deletes a bounce. Every job you finish makes the room safer for the jobs that are left.

Eight nights, one new idea each

Windows. Then garlic you have to carry to a chute, which means crossing the room with your hands full. Then mirrors. Then a skylight nobody can shutter, so the only answer is to shove something under it. Then Mrs Ashgrove, who means well, and who opens the shutters again behind you.

You never lose progress

Burning up costs you that night and nothing else. Every upgrade, every night you have reached and all your banked GLOAM are kept, and the night starts again. There is no run to lose and no full restart anywhere in the game.

GLOAM is paid for chores done and for time to spare, and it buys nine upgrades in a tree with real prerequisites: faster boots, a longer cloak that buys you two more seconds in the sun, a grip that shoves furniture harder, a sense that shows unfinished chores through walls, a bell that slows the housekeeper down.

Controls

  • Move — arrow keys or WASD, or drag anywhere on the left of the screen
  • Do a chore — hold SPACE, or hold the DO IT button, while standing next to the thing
  • Mute — M. Back to the title — ESC

The game is fully playable with nothing but taps. Every button on every screen is a real hit-tested control, and a whole night can be finished without touching a keyboard. Your save lives in your own browser's local storage: nothing is sent anywhere, there is no account, no tracking, and no network request of any kind after the page has loaded.

Credits and disclosure

  • Code — yes. Every line.
