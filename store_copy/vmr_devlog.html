Vampire's Morning Routine is out. It is free, it plays in your browser, and it is about being extremely bad at mornings.

Dawn is coming. Shut every window, drape every mirror, get the garlic out of the house, then get in the coffin and hold the lid shut. You have about two minutes a night, and eight nights to survive.

The thing that makes it different

The sunlight is not a timer and it is not a zone painted on the floor. It is one angle that sweeps east to west across the night, cast off the live positions of everything in the room, and bounced by any mirror you have not covered yet.

That means the shadow behind a wardrobe is real cover, and it moves when you shove the wardrobe. It means a bare mirror ninety degrees away is a second sun. And it means the chore list and the danger are the same list: shuttering a window deletes a beam at its source, so every job you finish makes the next one safer.

You never lose progress

Burning up costs you that night and nothing else. Every upgrade, every night you have reached and all your banked GLOAM are kept. There is no run to lose and no full restart anywhere in the game. GLOAM buys nine upgrades in a tree with real prerequisites, and each one is drawn as its own generated sigil.

Eight nights, one new idea each

Windows. Then garlic you have to carry to a chute, which means crossing the room with your hands full. Then mirrors. Then a skylight nobody can shutter, so the only answer is to put something under it. Then Mrs Ashgrove, the housekeeper, who means well, and who opens the shutters again behind you.

How it was made

Hand-written HTML5 canvas and vanilla JavaScript, no engine. Every pixel is generated by our own code at build time: five tiling material plates, 23 character frames, 21 props, 22 effects and a 51-glyph typeface whose letter spacing is derived from each glyph's own ink. No image-generation model was used anywhere in this product, including the cover. Every sound cue and the music are rendered from authored note-by-note scores by our own synthesiser.

The game ships with 996 assertions across 12 gates, a 4,935-check layout and touch gate that finishes a whole night with pointer events only, and an adversarial pass that spams input, resizes to 320x240, reloads mid-run and feeds it ten hostile save files.

It is free. Go and not burn up.
