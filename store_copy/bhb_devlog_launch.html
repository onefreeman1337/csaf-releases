BULLET HELL BOTANIST is live and free to play in your browser.

You have no weapon. The garden shoots.

A plant in flower is two things at once: it is your income, and it is your covering fire. Harvesting it pays you and takes the gun away. So every harvest is a hole you chose to open, in the middle of a wave, in the place you chose to open it. Everything else in the game is pressure on that one decision.

Three systems, and they are wired to each other

The blight aims at you, and anything its fire crosses on the way is damaged. Where you stand decides which of your own beds gets hit, so dodging and farming are not two activities, they are one.

While anything is alive above the beds, the blight creeps down into them, a row at a time, permanently for that season. A taken row cannot be replanted. Clear the board and the line retreats. A fight you are dragging out is a fight you are losing, not merely a long one.

Eight species unlock over time and each one changes a rule rather than a number: a radial burst, a slowing field, a thorn wall, homing spores, a decoy bloom that pulls fire on purpose.

Sixty plant forms, and nobody drew any of them

Every plant is drawn by code from a parameter set: stem geometry, leaf shape and count, flower form, palette. Eight species across four growth stages is 32 forms. Graft two species and the hybrid is blended from both parents at the moment you make it, which is another 28. A hybrid fires both parents' patterns and yields more, and the plant you are about to make has never been drawn by anyone.

Death ends a season, not your progress

Being overrun wilts the beds and loses the harvest you were carrying. Your unlocked species, your discovered hybrids and your soil upgrades stay. You go to the greenhouse, spend, and plant again.

Playing it

Free, in the browser, no download. Keyboard, mouse or touch. About half an hour for a full run. It saves as you go.

Made by Core Systems Asset Factory. AI disclosure: code yes, graphics yes, sounds no (the score and effects are procedural synthesis, which is arithmetic rather than a generative model), text and dialog yes.
