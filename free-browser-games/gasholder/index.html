GASHOLDER is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

The day makes the gas. The night burns it. GASHOLDER is a free gasworks incremental that plays in your browser. Your retorts cook coal into gas around the clock, and you can watch the holder's bell rise as it fills. At dusk the town lights its lamps and draws on the holder until dawn. A summer night is 7 hours. A winter night is 14.

A works that coped in June runs dry in December, and when the holder is empty, whole streets go dark. Three things that pull against each other The works. Each retort makes 30 gas a day and burns a ton of coal. The parish sends one free ton every morning, so the works never stops for good; buy more coal, sell the coke it leaves, raise the holder lift by lift, and later build a second one. The mains.

Every street lies further down the main from the works, and gas loses pressure on the way. The far streets burn dim until you widen their main. The town. A street kept lit all night, brightly enough, builds up trade: its shops and inns stay open and its windows light up. Trade is paid every dawn, and it opens new streets, better burners and new contracts. A dark night loses it.
