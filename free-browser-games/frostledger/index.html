The frozen ground here yields impossibly well, and the price is not money. A smallholding in a valley the winter never leaves. You break new ground, take up tools, bring people up to help, and cut the frost deeper because the frost is what makes it grow. Everything compounds. Everything you own has WEIGHT, and weight costs you MEMORIES: the thirty-six things you can still be moved by.

The gate latch that catches on the third try. The tune you hum without knowing you are humming it. The way she stood at the end of a row, looking back. Every offer tells you what it will take from you before you press it. New in 1.3: it plays on a phone On a phone the page used to be the desktop board at full size, so more than half of it sat off the side of the screen.

Now a phone held upright gets its own layout in one column: the figures and the speed buttons stay at the top, and the page under them scrolls when you drag it. Held sideways, the fields sit two to a row. No text is drawn smaller than 10 CSS pixels and no button is smaller than 24. Tap a mark on the ledger to read the memory it stands for.
