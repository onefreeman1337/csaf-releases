FROSTLEDGER

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

FROSTLEDGER is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

FROSTLEDGER is a free incremental farming game for the browser with a horror at its centre: 42 things to buy, 36 memories to lose, and two endings. A smallholding in a valley the winter never leaves. You break new ground, take up tools, bring people up to help, and cut the frost deeper because the frost is what makes it grow. Everything compounds.

Everything you own has weight, and weight costs you memories: the thirty six things you can still be moved by. Every offer tells you what it will take from you before you press it. The horror is in the writing When a remembrance goes, the valley's own description of itself loses the sentence that remembrance wrote. The paragraph describing your own farm gets shorter, harvest by harvest, and then it stops.

The ledger is a wall of thirty six drawn marks that grey out one at a time, and you can still see what each one was. One currency, two ways out Sheaves are the money. The more weight you carry, the fewer memories the valley lets you keep, and they fade on their own too. Earning more cannot get you back. Owning less can: everything you have can be let go, and it refunds nothing except the room to feel something.
