AFFINEUR is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Milk in, wheels down, and time does the rest. AFFINEUR is a free cheese-cave incremental that plays in your browser. An affineur is the person who ages cheese. In most idle games you make a thing and sell it at once. Here, what you make is worth more the longer you keep it, up to a point, and a wheel on the shelf is money you cannot spend yet. So the question the game keeps asking is: sell now, or wait?

Three things that pull against each other The milk. Every morning the herd fills the vat: a flood in spring, a trickle in winter. What the vat cannot hold is lost, so a bigger herd means making more cheese. The cave. Every gallery you dig is colder and damper than the one above. Deep galleries age a wheel more slowly, but each recipe has galleries it likes: a blue wants the damp deep, a washed rind the warm mouth.

Quality rises where a wheel is happy and falls where it suffers. The market. Every seventh day buyers post orders for a cheese inside an age window, at a premium. The first is a regular who wants a wheel you already have; the others build reputation, and reputation unlocks washed rind, cave blue and the alpine wheel.
