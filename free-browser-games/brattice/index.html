BRATTICE is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

The coal is easy. The air is the game. BRATTICE is a free colliery incremental for your browser. A pit in the 1850s, drawn as a section through the seams. You cut roads and open coal faces, and the coal pays. But every face gives off firedamp, and only the air you bring past it keeps it working. How it plays A furnace at the foot of the upcast shaft draws fresh air down the downcast, along your roads and up again.

The chalk arrows are the air, and the bigger the arrow, the stronger the current. The air is solved as a real network. It splits between parallel roads by how hard they are to push through, so every road you cut changes where the air goes everywhere else in the pit. The air carries firedamp from one face to the next. Faces in a row foul each other's air, and a face over the limit is withdrawn until you fix its air.

Nobody is hurt; the coal just stops. Hang a door to send the air round another way. Run brattice cloth up a dead end heading to reach the face at its end. Cut a second road and you have a second current, so every face gets air of its own.
