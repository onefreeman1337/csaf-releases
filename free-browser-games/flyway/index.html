FLYWAY

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation, Strategy. Free.

FLYWAY is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Every autumn, millions of small birds cross the sea at night. Most nights they fly straight over your headland. When the wind comes off the sea and it rains at dawn, they come down in every bush: a fall. You run the observatory. Set mist nets beside cover, walk the net rounds, ring every bird and let it go.

Now and then a ring is found again, far down the coast or across the sea, and a line appears on your flyway map. The rule that matters: no bird waits. A bird left 40 minutes in a net, or 70 in a bag at the hut, is let go unringed, and your members hear about it. The strip under the map prints the oldest bird's real wait and the limit that applies to it. On a big fall, opening every net loses birds.

Closing nets until your ringers keep up rings more. The weather forecast tells you when a fall is coming, if you have bought the instruments to read it. A first game starts paused with a three step guide: a net beside a bush, the waiting bar under the map, and closing a net when the bar turns amber. The first morning opens with one net, and the first fall is a gentle one.
