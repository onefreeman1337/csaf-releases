IRONROAD

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Action. Free.

IRONROAD is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A free beat-em-up down an elevated railway, one station at a time. Plays in your browser, on a keyboard, a mouse or a phone. The Iron Road runs over a neon city, and every station on it has a gang waiting on the platform. Fight down the line to the TERMINUS and THE CONDUCTOR. Between stations you draft the moves your combo is made of, and where you set each one decides what it can do.

Your combo is a deck you build Your chain has six slots: STRIKE 1, STRIKE 2, STRIKE 3, FINISHER, THROW and DASH HIT. Clear a station and you draft one of three links (GUARD BREAK, BRACE, LAUNCHER, SWEEP, RUSH, ARC, SIPHON, STATIC, ECHO, HEAVY, SHATTER, RICOCHET) and set it in a slot.

The slot matters as much as the link: a WARDEN's block stops your chain dead, so GUARD BREAK on the FINISHER rarely gets there, while on STRIKE 1 it opens him up. The route map shows who waits at the next station before you choose, and one of the three links on offer always answers them.

The line Seven stations, from CANAL STREET to the TERMINUS, with one new kind of enemy joining at each: THUG, RUNNER, WARDEN, HACKER, BRUISER, AGENT, then THE CONDUCTOR, a boss in three phases.
