IRONROAD

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Action. Free.

IRONROAD is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

IRONROAD is a free beat 'em up for your browser: fight down 7 stations of an elevated railway and draft a 6 slot combo between them. It plays on a keyboard, a mouse or a phone. The Iron Road runs over a neon city, every station has a gang waiting on the platform, and THE CONDUCTOR waits at the TERMINUS. How it plays Your chain has six slots: STRIKE 1, STRIKE 2, STRIKE 3, FINISHER, THROW and DASH HIT.

Clear a station and you draft one of three links (there are 12, from GUARD BREAK to RICOCHET) and set it in a slot. The slot matters as much as the link: GUARD BREAK on STRIKE 1 opens a WARDEN up, while on the FINISHER his block stops your chain before it gets there. The route map shows who waits at the next station, and one of the three links on offer always answers them.

The line Seven stations from CANAL STREET to the TERMINUS, with a new kind of enemy at each: THUG, RUNNER, WARDEN, HACKER, BRUISER, AGENT, then THE CONDUCTOR, a boss in three phases. A red! is a swing on its way: hit first, or DASH straight through it. Three quick hits that are no finisher give an enemy GRIT, and it swings back.
