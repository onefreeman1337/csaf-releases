BULLET HELL BOTANIST

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Action, Survival. Free.

BULLET HELL BOTANIST is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

You have no weapon. The garden shoots. A plant in flower is both your income and your covering fire. Harvesting it pays you and takes the gun away. Every harvest is a hole you chose to open, in the middle of a wave, in the place you chose to open it. That is the whole game, and everything else is pressure on that one decision. Your body is the bait You cannot shoot.

The blight aims at you, and anything its fire crosses on the way is damaged — so where you stand decides which of your own beds gets hit. Stand below your plants and you drag a barrage through your garden. Stand wide and it lands in open ground. The blight takes the ground, not just your health While anything is alive above the beds, the blight creeps down into them — a row at a time, and permanently for that season.

A taken row cannot be replanted. Take the last one and the season is over. Clear the board and the line retreats. So a fight you are dragging out is a fight you are losing, not merely a long one. Sixty plant forms, and nobody drew any of them Eight species across four growth stages is 32 forms.
