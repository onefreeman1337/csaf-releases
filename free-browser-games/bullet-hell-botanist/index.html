You have no weapon. The garden shoots. A free browser bullet hell over 10 seasons, where 8 plant species and 28 hybrids do the shooting for you. New in 1.1: on a phone Held upright, the garden sits on top at the phone's full width, with the seed rack, a blight meter, a round walk pad and six buttons under it. Held sideways, the garden fills the screen's height and the buttons sit over the hedges.

Every word is at least 10 CSS pixels tall and every button at least 24 on every phone size we test, and on a computer it is the same game it always was. The trade A plant in flower is both your income and your covering fire. Harvesting it pays you pollen and takes the gun away, so every harvest is a hole you chose to open, in the middle of a wave.

Your body is the bait The blight aims at you, and anything its fire crosses on the way is damaged. Stand below your beds and you drag a barrage through your own garden; stand wide and it lands in open ground. The blight takes the ground While anything is alive above the beds, the blight creeps down into them a row at a time, and a taken row stays dead for the season.
