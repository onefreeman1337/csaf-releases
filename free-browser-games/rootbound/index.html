ROOTBOUND

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

ROOTBOUND is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A plant that thrives grows big, and a big plant shades, drains and sours the ground around it. The bed you laid out perfectly stops working because it worked. The shovel is the only answer, and it costs the plant real health to use. ROOTBOUND is a free garden simulation with twelve real plant species in it.

Every cell of the garden holds four invisible values, light, moisture, soil pH and nutrient, and every species wants a particular band on all four of them. You dig beds, you plant specimens where their niche is met, and they grow in real time through four stages: seedling, young, mature, flowering. Then they start editing the ground for everything around them. What you do Dig.

Rough ground becomes a workable bed, and digging is free. The scarce thing in this game is never ground, it is ground that suits the plant you want to put in it. Look. Four overlays paint light, water, soil pH and feed across the whole garden, so you can see the shade a tree is throwing before you plant anything into it. Plant.

A plant is scored on its worst axis rather than on an average of the four, so perfect light in bone dry soil is a dying plant and not a mediocre one.
