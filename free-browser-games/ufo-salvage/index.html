UFO SALVAGE is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

The aliens lost. The saucers are still in the fields. UFO SALVAGE is a free browser salvage incremental with 7 counties and a different generated saucer every time: tow them home before the Army gets there, strip them, and sell the parts. How it plays The invasion lasted a weekend. You run a farm-town salvage yard, and the sky is still falling. Pick a saucer, pick a road, and send a truck. Two roads.

Often there are two. FAST takes the blacktop, past the town and the diner. QUIET takes the gravel the long way round. The button says about how long and about how much talk it costs, and the map rings who will see you. The Army is the clock. A red line works across the county and takes every saucer it reaches. When it finishes its route it collects what is left, and the county closes.

Get half of what fell home and the next one opens. Talk costs you. A truck somebody sees starts talk. Enough talk and somebody phones it in: the Army hurries, turns round to look, and the third call in a county comes to your yard. A search takes what is out there, and a fine. At night hardly anyone is awake. Live tech. Some saucers still have something running.
