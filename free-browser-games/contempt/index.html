CONTEMPT

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Fighting. Free.

CONTEMPT is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A courtroom where the trial is the fight. Opposing counsel raises an argument. You have a fraction of a second to name the objection that answers it — and only the right one works. Land it and they lose composure, you gain a document, and the jury moves your way. Name the wrong class, or move too early, and you are overruled: silenced, standing there, while the case drifts away from you. You can also just hit them.

It always lands. It also costs a point of the judge's patience, and three points is a mistrial. Free, plays in your browser, no account, nothing to install. Two systems, and one of them is a loan The legal game is an economy: objections pay you evidence, evidence buys the points you press, points move the verdict.

The strike sits outside it — it costs nothing you own, which is exactly why it costs something you cannot buy. Object — 1 2 3 4, one key per class. Hearsay, Speculation, Relevance, Leading. Press — spend a document to make your own point. It has a wind-up, so starting one while an argument is landing means you eat the argument. Strike — always works, always +1 contempt.

Two clean legal actions pay one point back, so a strike you can afford is tempo you stole.
