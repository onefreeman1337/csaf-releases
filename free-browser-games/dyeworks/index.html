THE DYEWORKS

THE DYEWORKS is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Run a small dye house through the seasons. Grow the dyes, keep the vats alive, and dip bolts of cloth until they match the colour the town ordered. THE DYEWORKS is a free browser incremental where the colour on the cloth is what you sell.

Each dyestuff is modelled as the light it absorbs and every dip adds to it, so weld over woad comes out green, a mordant shifts the shade, and the same dips come out lighter on linen than on silk. The closer your bolt is to the ordered swatch, the more stars it earns and the more it pays. A working day The fields. Weld for yellow, woad for blue, madder for red.

Woad only grows in spring and summer, madder stays in the ground for three seasons, and nothing grows in winter, when the market sells dye dear. The vats. A bath weakens with every dip, so later bolts come out lighter. Feed a woad vat before it turns. Keep madder in its heat window or its dips come out browned. The orders.

Take an order, dip, let the bolt air (woad comes out of the vat green and turns blue in the air), and deliver when the stars look right. A wrong colour is refused, and any bolt sells back as seconds. The pattern book.
