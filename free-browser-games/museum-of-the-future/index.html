Museum of the Future is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Nobody remembers what any of this was. Make it up. Museum of the Future is a free tour guide game that plays in your browser. It is the year 3000 and you work at the Museum of the Ancient 21st Century. The exhibits are a toaster, a rubber duck, a traffic cone, a phone, one sneaker, and nobody has the faintest idea what any of them were for. Tour groups walk the gallery in real time.

When a group stops at an exhibit, three stories appear and a ring starts closing. Pick one, fast: click, tap, or press 1, 2 or 3. Tell them what they want, or tell the truth you made up yesterday Every group loves one theme. Romantics love Romance, Battle Buffs love Battle, Science Nerds love Science, Pilgrims love Sacred. A story in their theme earns triple wonder, and wonder becomes tickets in the gift shop.

Whatever you say becomes history. The first story about an exhibit goes on record. Three tellings to groups that love its theme make it a Legend. Changing your story costs you. A rewrite loses credibility, more for a story people loved and double if a Historian is listening. The official story is not always on offer, so sometimes the honest move is to say nothing.
