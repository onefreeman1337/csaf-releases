COMBWRIGHT

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation, Strategy. Free.

COMBWRIGHT is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Lay out the comb. Raise the brood. Keep the colony through the winter. A free beekeeping game in your browser. Each hive is one frame of comb, and you decide what every cell is for: brood, pollen or honey. The bees do the rest, by rules you can see.

New in 1.1: it plays on a phone On a phone the game used to draw its desktop or tablet layout at about two thirds of its size, so the smallest words came out 7 pixels tall. Now a phone held upright or sideways gets its own layout: four views picked at the top (HIVE, COLONY, FORAGE and MARKET), with the comb and your tools together on HIVE, so nothing scrolls and painting cells never fights a scroll.

No word is drawn smaller than 10 CSS pixels, and on any screen at least 360x560 upright or 568x320 sideways no button or comb cell is smaller than 24. In this page's player, and in any window with room for it, the game is the same as before, pixel for pixel. Enter or Space on the title now starts the game, or continues your saved apiary, which carries straight over. Three things that pull against each other The comb.
