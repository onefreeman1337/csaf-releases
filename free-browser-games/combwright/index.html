COMBWRIGHT

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation, Strategy. Free; pay what you want.

COMBWRIGHT is a free browser game, free to play in the browser, pay what you want.

Lay out the comb. Raise the brood. Keep the colony through the winter. A free beekeeping game in your browser. Each hive is one frame of comb, and you decide what every cell is for: brood, pollen or honey. The bees do the rest, by rules you can see. Three things that pull against each other The comb. Draw bare foundation into comb with wax and give each cell a job.

Brood only grows where the cluster keeps it warm, so the brood core has to be central. Turn on WARMTH to see it. The colony. Eggs, larvae that eat pollen, capped brood, nurses, foragers. A pollen shortage today is fewer bees a brood cycle from now. The year. Four seasons with their own blooms for your foragers. Winter has none, and the colony lives on the honey you left it.

In spring a crowded colony swarms and half the bees leave, unless a spare hive box catches them as a new hive. Wax draws comb and caps honey. Honey feeds the winter and sells. Take too much and the winter takes most of the colony: a hive that starves is robbed of everything it stored, so a careful keeper earns more than a greedy one. No restarts.

A colony that starves keeps its comb, and a small wild swarm moves in the next spring.
