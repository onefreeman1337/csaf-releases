ANAGAMA

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

ANAGAMA is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

An anagama is a kiln that is a tunnel up a hillside. You light a fire at the bottom and for the next several days the flame climbs, and everything it touches on the way is changed by it. What comes out at the other end is not what you put in. This is an incremental about running one. The numbers buy clay and wood. What you get back is not a bigger number — it is a shelf of pots that will never exist again.

The fire finishes what you start Throw greenware from clay. Clay is the cheap part; what a pot really costs you is a place in the kiln. Pack it into the chambers. Chamber one sits against the firebox: hottest, ashiest, most dangerous. Each chamber up the slope is cooler, cleaner and calmer. Inside a chamber, the front slot faces the flame and the back one hides from it. Light it, and you stop building.

From that moment you have three controls and no undo: Stoke — too little and the clay never matures. Too much, held too long, and the load slumps. Damper — close it and you starve the fire of air. That is reduction, and it pulls the iron in the ash glaze to green; open it and the same glaze goes amber.
