ANAGAMA is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

ANAGAMA is a free browser incremental about a wood-fired kiln: 10 vessel forms, 5 surface families, up to 6 chambers, and every pot drawn by its own firing. What you build is a shelf of pots that will never exist again. How it plays Throw. Clay is cheap; what a pot really costs is a place in the kiln. Pack. Chamber one sits against the firebox: hottest, ashiest, most dangerous.

Each chamber up the slope is cooler and calmer. Fire. Once lit, there is no undo. Too little stoke and the clay never matures; too much and the load slumps. Close the damper to pull the glaze green, at a cost in heat. Pine burns hot and ashy, oak steady. Heat climbs the slope with a delay, so you are always firing the past. Sell. Collectors want surfaces, such as a green ash celadon tea bowl, heavily ashed.

Two identical bowls packed a metre apart come out different, and the shelf keeps both. A ruined firing costs the load, never the shelf. A medieval stained glass workshop for one season, and twelve patrons with opinions. Sixty six nonogram plates; each solved grid develops into a mounted specimen.
