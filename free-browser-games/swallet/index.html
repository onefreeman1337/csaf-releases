SWALLET

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

SWALLET is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

You are the cave. Cut chambers into a hill, put things in them, and decide what happens to the people who come down looking for whatever they think is in there. It is an incremental — numbers go up, the board gets bigger, you can leave it running — but the thing you are optimising is a route, and the thing walking that route is somebody else's run.

Plays on a phone (new in 1.1) Held upright, the whole hill is on top and the chamber you picked is underneath, with a second tab for the hollow's numbers. Held sideways, the hill runs down the left at full height and everything else sits beside it. SOUND has its own button, and on a phone VEINS asks you to pick a vein and then take it, so a permanent upgrade is never one stray tap.

Measured on the shipped build in windows from 360 by 640 CSS px upright and 568 by 320 sideways (a phone held in fullscreen): every word is drawn at 10.0 CSS px or more, and every button and board cell at 24.1 CSS px or more. A browser that keeps its own bars on screen leaves the game a smaller window, and it draws smaller with it.

In 1.0 a phone held upright drew the text at under 5 CSS px, and a phone held sideways lost the bottom of the screen.
