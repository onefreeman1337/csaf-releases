SWALLET

SWALLET is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

SWALLET is a free browser incremental where you are the cave: 12 fixtures, 4 bands of rock and 5 kinds of delver coming down to rob you. You cut chambers into a hill and put things in them. Delvers walk in, take what they can, and walk out, and the route they walk is the thing you build.

New in 1.2: the first night Until your first sink stands, the line under the hill says what to do next in gold, the square it means is ringed, and the panel lists the steps: cut a chamber, set a SNARE, cut one more row so it bites going down and coming back, add a second ward, watch a delver die, then set a BONE PRESS beside the body. Following it, the first delver dies inside two minutes. How it plays Cut.

Any rock touching an open chamber can be cut, and every row costs more than the last. Furnish. One fixture per chamber: roots make spoil, wards bite whoever passes, lures pull delvers deeper, sinks render the dead into spoil and marrow. Kill or let go. A body feeds your sinks. A delver who gets out hurt raises your renown, which brings bigger groups and richer kinds; one nobody touched has little to tell. The water.

Every row you cut raises the water table.
