SCOURFAIR is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Keep the white horse white, then cut your own. SCOURFAIR is a free incremental about an old chalk hill figure, playable in your browser. A white horse is cut into the down above a vale, and every day the turf creeps back in. Travellers on the vale road pay tolls by how famous the figure is.

You scour it by hand, hire scourers, cart in fresh chalk, lay kerbs, bring back the scouring fair, and at the end you cut a figure of your own on the second hill. The rule at the heart of it Each cell of the figure greys by the share of its eight sides that are turf, so the corners grey fastest. Fame comes from detail, and detail is edge.

So the more intricate a figure, the more it draws the road and the more it costs to keep. Detail pays. A giant with arms and legs draws far more fame than a plain block of the same size. Edges cost. In a wet spring that giant greys more than twice as fast as the block. Kerbs answer the corners. Timber kerbs stop the creep for good, and on the corners they save more than twice what they save on a straight edge.

Each one costs a little more than the last, so you choose where they go. The fair crowd follows fame.
