STARCHART

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Strategy. Free.

STARCHART is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Every voyage you fly is inscribed forever. The route you took IS the constellation. You fly a route between stars on flux and hull. When the voyage ends — however it ends — the chart keeps what you drew: the stars you touched, in the order you touched them, become a permanent, named constellation in your atlas. There is no full restart, ever. Death is not a reset.

A voyage that ends badly still leaves a figure on the chart, and that figure is permanent power. The atlas IS the save file, and it only ever grows. Read the sky Every star is a generated form, and its shape tells you what it does — 48 distinct forms across six families, each drawn in eight palettes, so a sector holds 384 possible stars. There is not one image file in this game.

A forge repairs your hull, a shrine tithes flux, a nebula is a gamble, a twin wreck offers a choice. Learning to read the sky at a glance is the skill. Three decisions that matter Which gate you cast off from — three gates sit far apart in every sector, and each opens a different region. You are choosing where the next figure goes on your chart.
