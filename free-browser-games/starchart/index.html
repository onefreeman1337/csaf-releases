STARCHART

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Strategy. Free.

STARCHART is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

STARCHART is a free browser roguelite of 4 sectors and 48 generated star forms: every route you fly is inscribed as a constellation you keep. The stars you touched, in the order you touched them, become a named figure in your atlas, and that figure is permanent power. How it plays Cast off from one of three gates spread across the sky. Each opens a different region of your chart. Read the sky.

A star's shape tells you what it does: a forge repairs the hull, a haven gives flux, a surge makes the next jump free, a nebula is a gamble, a twin wreck offers a choice, a shrine pays flux. Jump. Every jump costs flux by distance, printed under each star you can reach. Stop when you choose. SET ANCHOR banks the figure. Close the route into a loop and it seals, which doubles its grant.

Every figure grants by its dominant family: Bulwark, Reserve, Farsight, Fortune, Ward, Covenant. Grants stack, so more range reaches stars you could not afford before. Fly a charted route again and the line deepens instead of copying itself. The Drift opens at 5 figures, the Hollow Crown at 12, the Long Dark at 21. There is no full restart: a voyage that ends badly still leaves its figure on the chart.
