SEEDLIFT

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

SEEDLIFT is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A cozy farm that grows while you are away, and the only way to get new seed is to raid the wilds for it. SEEDLIFT crosses a farming incremental with an extraction raid. At home you sow, harvest and cross-breed. Out in the wilds you walk a map that is different every time, pack what seed you can carry, and hold on in an open balloon lift to fly home before the storm rolls over you.

If it catches you, you lose the pack, never the farm. The two halves need each other. Raids are the only source of new strains. The farm is the only source of tools: sweetroot becomes LURES that pull a charging boar off its line, and smokeherb becomes SMOKE that scatters the crows trying to rob you. Four places to raid HEDGEROW. Field edges and hawthorn. Nothing here bites. FEN.

Bog that drags your boots, and crows that rob your pack. OLD ORCHARD. Walled rows of old trees, and boars that charge in straight lines. RIDGE GARDEN. A ruined botanic garden on the high ground. Everything at once, and a fast storm. Each opens with a permit that costs coin and a number of strains in your codex. Deep in the last of them grows the centrepiece. Bring it home, grow it under glass, and watch it flower.
