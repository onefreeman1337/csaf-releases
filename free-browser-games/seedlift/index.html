SEEDLIFT

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

SEEDLIFT is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

SEEDLIFT is a free browser farming incremental with 24 strains to collect, 4 zones to raid and 8 shop upgrades, where new seed only comes from raids. At home you sow, harvest and cross-breed. Out in the wilds you pack what seed you can carry and ride a balloon lift home before the storm closes in. If it catches you, you lose the pack, never the farm. How it plays The farm.

Sow from the seed bank and harvest ripe plots for coin and one or two seeds back. Two different strains ripe side by side can cross into a hybrid. The farm keeps growing for up to 8 hours while you are away. The raid. A new map every time, a pack with slots and a weight limit, and balloon lifts that open and close in turn while the storm crosses from the west. The tools.

Sweetroot grows into LURES that pull a charging boar off its line, and smokeherb into SMOKE that scatters the crows robbing your pack. The farm is the only place either comes from. The codex. 16 wild strains and 8 hybrids. Permits to the Fen, the Old Orchard and the Ridge Garden cost coin and strains found. Deep in the Ridge Garden grows the centrepiece. Bring it home, grow it under a cold frame, and watch it flower.
