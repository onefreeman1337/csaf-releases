KAIJU DISPOSAL is a free browser clean-up incremental with 12 contracts, and this page opens the CSAF Arcade: 37 free games in one window. The heroes won. Now somebody has to move it. Get the dead monster off the street before it rots, swells or glows. How it plays Click a part of the carcass to put it on the foreman's board.

Saw crews take the cuts in order, and trucks haul each material to its buyer: bone to the museum, hide to the tannery, the isotope core to the reactor. Fresher parts pay more. The body is the clock. Every part rots, the stench costs the city's patience, the core heats the meat around it, and the gut bursts across the street if nobody vents it.

Mid-job events (a buyer paying double, a news crew, a flaring core) change which cut should go next. Every carcass is generated from seven body plans and ten species, so no two jobs look the same. Between jobs, hire crews and buy nine upgrades. Twelve contracts make a campaign of about seventeen minutes. Forty generated carcasses.
