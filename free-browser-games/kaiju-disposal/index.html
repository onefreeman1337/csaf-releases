KAIJU DISPOSAL is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

The heroes won. Now somebody has to move it. KAIJU DISPOSAL is a free clean-up incremental for your browser. A giant monster has been killed in the middle of the city, and your small firm has the contract to get it off the street before it rots, swells or glows any further. How it plays Click a part of the carcass (a leg, the head, a stretch of tail, the glowing core, the gut) to put it on the foreman's board.

The board holds two next cuts, and a free saw crew takes the next one, so you choose every cut as the crews free up. Trucks fill up at the kerb and haul to the buyers: bone to the museum, hide to the tannery, glands to the lab, horn to the carvers, the isotope core to the reactor. Fresher parts pay more. The body is the clock. Every part rots, rot raises a stench, and the stench costs the city's patience.

The core heats the body around it so it rots faster, and the gut fills with gas and bursts across the street if nobody vents it or cuts it out first.
