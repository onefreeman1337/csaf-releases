RUSTBLOOM

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Adventure. Free.

RUSTBLOOM is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

You never find the key. You forge it. In most games like this, a locked thing asks what you are carrying. A bramble wall wants the fire rod, and there is exactly one fire rod. Here a locked thing asks what your tool CARRIES. That wall does not want an item. It wants HEAT 2+. An EMBERCUP head answers that — and so does a CLEAVER wrapped in a SPITE binding, and so do seventy-six other things you might have built.

Every barrier in the Ashfen has many solutions, so two players open the same world differently. Your tool is three parts HEAD — the verb. What it fundamentally does. HAFT — the shape of the swing. Reach, arc, speed. BINDING — the side effect, and the cost. 8 heads × 6 hafts × 6 bindings = 288 tools. Every one of them draws its own portrait from its own parts, at the moment you build it.

There is no sprite sheet in this game. And the same three parts fight for you A husk wears its weakness openly, in that property's own colour. The tool you forged to open a wall is the tool you then have to swing at whatever is behind it — which is the tension the whole thing turns on.
