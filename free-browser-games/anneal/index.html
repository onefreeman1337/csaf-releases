ANNEAL

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Puzzle. Free.

ANNEAL is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A free browser game in twelve channels: steer a stream of glowing motes up a foundry shaft, where every crucible changes how many you carry and what they are made of. The pour has a COUNT and a TEMPER: EMBER, FROST or IRON. A crucible reading ×4 EMBER multiplies you by four and makes you ember, and you pass exactly one crucible per row.

Each flaw in the shaft yields to one temper only (a SEAL to ember, a RACE to frost, a SLAGWALL to iron) and wants a minimum count. The crucible that gives you the count you need is often the one that gives you the wrong temper two rows later. Arriving big and wrong is still a loss.

New in 1.1: play it on a phone Hold a phone upright and the shaft sits above a thumb pad that names the crucibles and flaws of the row you are steering into; drag or tap anywhere across it. Where your phone lets the game turn, held sideways the readout sits beside the shaft, and turning keeps your pour. Either way the shaft shows exactly as far up as it does on a desktop, so it is the same game.

On these phone layouts every word is at least 10 CSS pixels and every button at least 24, measured from 360x560 up.
