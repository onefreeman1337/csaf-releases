PERIGEE is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

PERIGEE is a free browser tide game of 10 seasons with one control: the moon's orbit. Bring the moon close and the sea climbs the coast; a settlement of four kinds of building wants different water at different hours, and it will not listen to you. Free, plays in the browser on a computer or a phone, about fifteen minutes for ten seasons. Progress is saved after every season.

New in 1.1: it plays on a phone An upright phone gets its own layout: the coast across the top, the moon's dial and the season under it, and two big buttons, CLOSER and FARTHER. Held sideways, the coast sits on the left and the dial on the right. On a phone no text is drawn under 10 css px and no control is under 24 css px. Long screens turn pages.

Touch works: drag up or down on the coast to move the moon, tap a building to read what it wants. The theme loops cleanly now, and old snags are fixed for everyone: NEW COAST warns on the button and a double tap cannot abandon a save, a help screen could skip a season, the ending offered an eleventh season, and a yield just under the quota could read as met.
