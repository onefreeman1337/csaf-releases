THE WARM ROOM

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

THE WARM ROOM is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

THE WARM ROOM is a free browser management sim of 90 winter days: keep a derelict glasshouse alive, and the heat in the room is made of the people in it. They gave you the key at the town hall. The glass is broken in places, the stove is cold and the beds are bare.

How it plays Every visitor standing inside is a heat source in a real simulation, and the fire works to a thermostat, so a busy afternoon damps the stove and your wood lasts. Heat. Every floor cell has its own temperature. It leaks through each pane according to how sound that pane is, and water barrels bank the afternoon for the night. Growth.

Eight crops with temperature bands instead of timers, from corn salad that takes almost anything to a lemon tree that is a bet on how warm you can hold one corner until March. People. Sixteen visitors, each wanting one small thing: to warm up, to sit down, something hot to hold, a bit of quiet. Meet it and they leave something in the tin and tell somebody. Come back often enough and a person takes one of five jobs.

Nineteen fittings and about half an hour to spring, with the longest night of the winter as the test the first half prepares you for.
