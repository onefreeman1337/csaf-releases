THE WARM ROOM

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

THE WARM ROOM is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

They gave you the key at the town hall and did not ask for anything back. A derelict public glasshouse at the edge of town, in the first week of winter. The glass is broken in places, the stove is cold, and the beds are bare. Ninety days until spring. The heat in the room is made of the people in it That is a mechanic before it is a sentiment.

Every visitor standing inside is a heat source in a real simulation, so a full room is literally a warm room — and because the fire works to a thermostat, a busy afternoon means the stove damps down and your wood lasts. Measured in the game's own simulation, on the third image on this page: the same building, the same stove, the same target temperature.

With twelve people in it the fire runs at 68% instead of 100%, and the room is warmer anyway. Three systems, and they are the same problem HEAT. Every cell of the floor has its own temperature.

It spreads between neighbours, leaks out through each individual pane according to how sound that pane is, comes in through the glass while the sun is up, and is banked by water barrels that soak up the afternoon and hand it back after dark.
