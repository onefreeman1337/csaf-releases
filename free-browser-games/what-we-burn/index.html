WHAT WE BURN

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Survival. Free.

WHAT WE BURN is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A free horror game you play in the browser, about the cost of staying warm. The house is full of things that belonged to people you loved. The fire will ask for every one of them. One fire, 53 keepsakes. The fire burns down on its own and only a burn feeds it.

Carry each thing back to the Hearth and choose: burn it for warmth and fire, at the cost of your humanity and the ghost of whoever owned it, or keep it on the Mantel, where kept things resonate: the same owner sings, opposites clash. The Heart melts only when the Mantel resonates to 26 and the fire stands at 90, so it will cost you more than one thing you loved.

Burn too much and LET IT GO ends that fire instead: the other ending. New in 1.3: play it on a phone Hold a phone upright and the game gets its own layout, one view at a time: THE HOUSE, the map, or HERE, the room you stand in with doors to walk on, or the Mantel at the Hearth. Every word is at least 10 CSS pixels and every button at least 24 on the phones we measured, from 360x560 up.

Held sideways, a phone still gets the small desktop layout. The desktop game is unchanged, and a fire saved in 1.2 carries straight over. You cannot lose your progress.
