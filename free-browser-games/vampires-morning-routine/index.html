Vampire's Morning Routine

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Puzzle. Free.

Vampire's Morning Routine is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Dawn is coming and you are extremely not a morning person. Shut every window. Drape every mirror. Get the garlic out of the house. Then get in the coffin and hold the lid shut. You have about two minutes, you are fast and heavy and clumsy, and the light bends around whatever you knock over. Free, plays in your browser, no account, nothing to install.

The sunlight is a real object, not a timer Most vampire games treat daylight as a countdown, or as a zone painted on the floor. Here it is simulated: The sun is one angle. It rises in the east and crosses to the west over the course of the night, so a window in the left-hand wall is only dangerous while the sun is on that side. Halfway through, the side windows go quiet and the skylights open up.

The threat moves, on a schedule you can read off the floor. Beams stop at things. A wall, a bookcase, a wardrobe you have shoved into place. The shadow behind an object is real cover, and it moves when the object does. Mirrors redirect. A bare mirror turns a beam ninety degrees.

Shoving a mirror does not trigger anything, it re-aims a shaft, because the shaft was always being cast off wherever that mirror happens to be standing.
