THE LAST FLOCK

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Action. Free.

THE LAST FLOCK is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Your army is a crowd you shape with movement alone — and it dies. Stand still and they ring you. Run and they spear. There is not one order button in this game. Movement is the only order you give Most games about commanding a mob give you a mob that ignores you: the documented complaint about every minion in the genre is that they "run off and do their own thing".

So there are no unit orders here, no selection box and no formation hotkeys — because there is no formation system. There is one flocking rule, read at three player speeds: Stand still → the flock settles into a RING around you, guns facing out in every direction.

Sprint → it strings into a SPEAR: your light Ducklings and Teal ride your shoulder, your Geese and Swans string out behind, and the tail is the part that gets caught. Put your back to a wall → the ring flattens into a SHIELD WALL of bodies between you and the marsh. Nothing switches modes. Those are the same equation at three speeds, which is why the flock never stops feeling like part of your own body.

The army is the health bar Growth is not a number going up — it is more birds on screen, each one a body you can count and lose.
