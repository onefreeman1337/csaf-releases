TORCHWATER is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Light the water, draw the shoal, land the season. TORCHWATER is a free incremental for your browser about a cove's fire-fishing fleet. On dark summer nights your boats row out and light the fires at their bows. Shoals rise to the flame and leap at it, and the netters scoop them out of the air. The same flame that draws a shoal is the only light you can see it by, and the moon decides how far that light carries.

How it plays The night. Tap a boat, then tap the sea to row there. Its torch draws any shoal it reaches, and the fish swim up toward the light. Everywhere your fire does not fall, you see at most nervous water: the surface shivering over a shoal. The flare. A burst of fire pulls fish from much farther off (4.3 cells against a burn of 2.7) and makes a risen shoal leap hardest, but it burns fuel four times as fast.

Flare too early and the fish are still deep; wait too long under one steady light and the shoal grows used to it and sinks away. The light is a field. Two lights that pull one shoal about equally split it in two, so where each boat sits matters. A full boat has to row home to unload and refuel, and distance is time. The moon runs in eights.
