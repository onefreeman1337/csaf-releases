SIDINGS

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation, Strategy. Free.

SIDINGS is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A single line, a winter timetable, and one train at a time. Every other train game asks you to make the railway bigger. This one is about the thing that actually decides what a railway can carry: a single track holds one train at a time. You are the signalman on a branch that climbs from Lowgarth to High Fell. Trains come up the valley and down it on a timetable that does not care whether you are ready.

Two trains in one block section is not a setback. It is a wreck. Your capacity is not your mileage. It is your signalling — and you buy it one layer at a time, up the same ladder the real Victorian railways climbed for exactly the same reason. One Engine In Steam → Automatic Route Setting Seven rungs, in the real order.

Each one automates the job the last one made you do by hand, and opens a timetable the last one could not have carried. Staff & Ticket — a wooden staff guards each section, and it travels on the train. Until something comes back the other way, that section is one way only. You will feel this one. Electric Token — the staff stops having to ride home. Absolute Block — bells and instruments between every box.

Track Circuits — the rails themselves report.
