PROMPTBOX

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Puzzle. Free.

PROMPTBOX is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

PROMPTBOX is a free three-minute time-loop mystery for the browser: five of an opera company, nine rooms backstage, and a casebook that survives every reset. You are the prompter, hidden in the box at the lip of the stage. The overture runs 180 seconds, the curtain goes up on a corpse, and then it runs again. When the loop resets you keep nothing but what you worked out.

A company on real schedules Five of them move through the backstage on real schedules. Each has somewhere to be by a given bar, and each one reroutes when a door is bolted, a lamp is out or a prop has gone from where they left it. Leave everything alone and you see the same three minutes. Touch one thing and the night bends around it, so interfering is how the next loop shows you something new.

You see the room you stand in. Next door you hear that somebody is there, but not who. Everywhere else is dark. Earn your verbs You begin able only to WATCH. Facts you witness go into the casebook, and the casebook pays for the rest: FOLLOW, then BOLT a door, DOUSE a lamp, PALM what somebody is going to need, and CUE, a line whispered from the box that works only on the right bar.
