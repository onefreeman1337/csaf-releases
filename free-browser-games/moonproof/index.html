MOONPROOF is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Fit the wards before the full moon. Hold the night. Seal the town. MOONPROOF is a free incremental for your browser about the small firm that werewolf-proofs a village. Between full moons your crews fit shutters, iron latches and silver caps to the houses that hire you. On the full moon the wolves come down the lane and test every door, window and sheep pen, and you watch each test land.

How it plays The moon is a clock. A dial turns through six working days, and every crew gives a day of work to the wards you order. When the moon is up, the night begins. Fear is the market. Every door the wolves break frightens the houses around it, shown as a red haze. Only an afraid house asks for a contract, so the street you leave unguarded tonight is next month's work.

Sign only what your crews can ward before the moon: a signed house broken into before its ward is up walks away, and the town talks. A covered client that holds pays a bounty and lifts your reputation, and reputation sets every fee. One that breaks costs a repair, loses a ward and some trust. Broken twice, it leaves and will not hire you for two moons.
