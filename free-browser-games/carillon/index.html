CARILLON

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Strategy. Free.

CARILLON is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A tower defence whose towers never shoot. You do not place guns. You hang bells. Every bell sounds a ring on the metronome, and the ring spreads out across the valley. Where two rings touch, a spark is born. The spark is the only thing in this game that does damage. Nothing on this board has a range, a target, a cooldown or a projectile.

The whole weapon is the intersection of two circles, which means everything the genre normally tunes with numbers is expressed here as geometry you can see and plan against. Placement is aiming Two bells that ring together have rings of the same size, so every place they can touch lies on the straight line halfway between them. That faint gold line is drawn for you.

Hang your bells so it crosses the road, and the procession walks through it. Two bells that ring a beat apart have rings of different sizes, so their line bows into a curve instead. That is not a fault to avoid. A bowed chord reaches places a straight one cannot, and one of your three interventions exists to bow one on purpose.

The second system, and the part that surprises people A damper walks the road carrying wet felt, and every bell it passes close to slides off the beat.
