CHARGE BARGE is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Drive a storm-powered ironclad up an insane hostile coastline. Everything in this game is charge — the run, the money, the miracles, the ram. Built for AI Browser Game Jam 4, theme CHARGE! — and the theme is the resource system, the verb and the fantasy at once. New in 1.2.0: play it on a phone Upright or on its side, the coast fits the screen and the console stays big enough to read and press.

Upright, the sea fills the phone and you see further up the coast. A touch helm sits under your thumbs: hold LEFT and RIGHT to steer, CHARGE and EASE for the throttle, and FIRE to lay the pivot gun on the nearest threat, or touch the sea to aim it yourself. On a keyboard, SPACE now does the same assisted fire, P pauses, and the game holds itself when you click away. The two charges VOLTS (amber) — stored charge.

Stripped from wrecks, trickled in by the trade dynamo while you sail. Spent in coves on permanent upgrades: weapon, defence, speed, income-over-time, income-per-kill, salvage magnet, surge capacity, and more. SURGE (blue) — battle charge.
