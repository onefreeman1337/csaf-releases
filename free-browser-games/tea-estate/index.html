TEA ESTATE is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Pluck it fine, fire it right, and sell it by the grade. TEA ESTATE is a free incremental for your browser about a hill tea estate. Your pluckers go round the sections of bushes, the factory withers, rolls, oxidises and fires each day's leaf, and every six days the tea goes to auction by grade. Two decisions run the estate and they pull against each other: how fine to pluck, and how long to oxidise.

How it plays The field. Every day the bushes grow new shoots, slowly in the first flush and fastest in the monsoon. Bright tips on a section are leaf waiting to be plucked; they turn olive when the leaf grows coarse. The plucking standard. FINE takes two leaves and a bud and makes the best tea, but the round is slow. COARSE strips everything fast and makes poor tea.

The PLUCKING panel shows the race: how many days a round takes against how many days the leaf stays fine. The oxidation dial. Every flush wants a different time, and the weather moves it a little every day. The tea taster's green band always holds the right time, a copper pip shows what the last batch really wanted, and too long flattens the grade faster than too short. The factory takes a fixed weight of leaf a day.
