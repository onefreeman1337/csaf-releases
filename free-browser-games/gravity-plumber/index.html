Gravity Plumber is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Gravity is not a button here, and it is not yours. The room falls toward whichever of its four wall tanks is heaviest. All you control is where the water goes. Twenty-four sealed machine chambers. Turn a valve wheel, fit a length of pipe, open a drain cock, and the chamber tips a quarter turn under you. You fall with it, and when it settles, the wall you were standing beside is the floor you are standing on.

Reach the hatch and the next chamber is waiting. What is in the machine room Water as ballast. Every wall tank holds a real volume of water, and the heaviest wall is down. Moving water from one tank to another is how you turn the room. Four chapters, four fittings. The valve wheel, the elbow, the check valve and the accumulator.

Each chapter adds one piece to your kit and spends six chambers on what it does to the rest. The boiler. It builds pressure while you work, and the relief whistle tells you when that is about to matter. Your breath. A chamber can flood faster than you climb, and the air gauge is counting. Hot floors and scalding steam in the later chapters, where the fall you make is also the ground you land on.
