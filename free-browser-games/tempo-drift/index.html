TEMPO DRIFT

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Racing, Rhythm. Free.

TEMPO DRIFT is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

A racer whose road is the beat. Every dash on the lane lines is one beat, and it lights up as you cross it. Change lanes on the beat and the song speeds up, and so does your car. Miss and it slows down. Chain it cleanly and you drive yourself into the fastest, hardest version of the road on purpose. There is no note highway.

The rhythm is the tarmac: the cream stripe across the road is a bar line, the chequered strip is a checkpoint, and every four dashes make a bar. Six roads, six songs DAWN COAST, 104 BPM, 40 bars. A long road above the sea at first light, at the gentlest tempo of the six. PINE PASS, 112 BPM, 44 bars. Switchbacks in the mist, with hills rolling under the road. SALT FLATS, 120 BPM, 48 bars.

The flattest road there is, with gentle bends and the shortest clock of the six. NIGHT HARBOUR, 126 BPM, 48 bars. Wet quayside, lamps in the water, and a dock road that will not stay straight. AUTUMN VALE, 132 BPM, 52 bars. Down through the orchards, everything gold, everything bending. STORM RIDGE, 140 BPM, 56 bars. The last ridge in weather, at a tempo the first road could not have survived.

What you play with Gates. A pink diamond is a tap. A pink bar is a hold.
