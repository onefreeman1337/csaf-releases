PRISMHORN is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

You never cast a spell. You cast a rainbow. One shaft of dawn strikes your horn and leaves it as seven bands of light. Each band is a different verb. They fire together, always, in a rigid fan — so every shot is seven decisions at once, and you aim the whole spectrum or none of it. Plays in your browser. Free. The entire game is 13,312 bytes.

The seven verbs UNMAKE — dissolves the made FORGE — hardens into matter WAKE — animates the dormant GROW — accelerates living things CHILL — slows and stills SCRY — reveals what is hidden MEND — restores what it touches The trap is in that list, not in the difficulty. GROW is your entire economy — it ripens the blooms that pay you in Light.

It is also the fastest way to breed a monster, because the blight standing beside that bloom is a living thing too, and GROW does not know the difference. MEND heals your cairns and it heals whatever is eating them. FORGE hardens your ground and it armours the thing standing on it. You cannot fire one band. That is the game. What you actually control Two keys. Q narrows the fan to a lance — everything on one target.

E opens it into a curtain that touches the whole hillside thinly.
