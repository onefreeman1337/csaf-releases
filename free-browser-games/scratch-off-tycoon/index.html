SCRATCH-OFF TYCOON is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Every coin you win, you rub off the foil yourself. SCRATCH-OFF TYCOON is a free browser game about a night kiosk and a coin that wears down. Buy a scratch card and rub it with your coin. Every rub spends the coin: a plain coin clears only about half of a Gold or Platinum card, so every card is a small puzzle about where to spend it.

How a card works Rub a corner of a cell and it glints its colour: red is a cherry or a skull, gold is a bell or a crown, blue is a star or a gem, grey is a blank. Rub half the cell away and the symbol is revealed. Three of a kind pays. Four pays double. Every revealed skull halves the card. A cherry pays 4, a bell 7, a star 11, a gem 20 and a crown 34, times the card's multiplier.

So you peek, you read the colours, and you spend the rest of the coin on the cells most likely to pay. A careful player and a lucky one play the same card very differently. The kiosk Five tickets: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and the Golden Ticket, which wins the game.

A coin workshop with four upgrades: a harder coin that wears slower, earlier glints, a wider coin, and a charm that strikes off the first skull (two at level II). An album of twenty stamps.
