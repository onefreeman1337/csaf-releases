BANNERET: SUCCESSION

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Strategy. Free.

BANNERET: SUCCESSION is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

You never move a knight. You plant a banner — and every knight in the House charges it at once. A charge that never started is a death. Knights who fall are dead. At the next muster their heirs stand before the banner: seat the heir, or pass the lance and end the line. Blood carries — a trait from the sire, and sometimes a flaw.

A craven line will refuse a defended banner at the worst possible moment, and the chronicle will remember exactly when. New in v1.2 — THE DOWNS The ground is no longer flat, and the ground is now the puzzle. Two rules, and they rewrite every threshold in the game: Crest. A blow struck riding down off the high ground carries an extra tile of weight. One tile of run, taken downhill, smashes a pikewall that needs two.

Climb. Riding up needs a run. From a standing start the horse balks at the bank — and a knight stopped at the foot of a hill, beside a pike, is a funeral. High ground is drawn the way a period map draws it, in hachures. 126 of the 141 fields carry it; your first three deliberately do not. And the Orders — a stock you spend, replenished at every muster, never per-field.
