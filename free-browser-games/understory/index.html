UNDERSTORY

A free browser game from Core Systems Asset Factory. Genre: Simulation. Free.

UNDERSTORY is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

The forest floor eats everything. You are what does the eating. You are the mycelium under a forest floor. Spread hyphae across the litter, digest what you reach, and fruit where it counts. There is no run, no reset and no death screen. Ground can dry out and die back, but your spores, your upgrades, your fruiting bodies and the substrate you have opened are permanent. You never start over.

Two systems that fight SPREAD wants you far from home, because new ground is new food. DECAY punishes exactly that. Moisture falls away from springs and fruiting bodies, and a cell can only get as dense as its moisture allows, while density multiplies both your yield and the spores a fruiting pays. Push past your water and the frontier thins out behind you.

Sit still and you eat your ground down to nothing while rival mould creeps into what is left. Neither expanding nor holding is a strategy on its own. That is the game. Prestige that does not wipe the board Fruiting is how you make spores, and it costs you: biomass, plus density from the cell and the ring around it. What you get back is permanent.
