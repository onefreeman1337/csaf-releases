A fireworks yard, shell by shell. SHELLYARD is a free fireworks yard incremental that plays in your browser. Every shed in the yard holds powder, and the licence it holds draws a circle that no other shed may stand inside. A bigger licence works faster and forbids more ground. You mix, roll the stars one colour at a time, dry them while the weather lets you, fill the shells and stack them in the magazine.

Then you fire the town's commissions, and every show plays in the sky, built from the shells you picked. Four things that pull against each other The yard. A mixing house, star rooms, drying racks, a filling shed and a magazine. Every shed that holds powder carries a licence tier from 1 to 3.

Raise a tier and the shed works up to 3.5 times as fast, and its circle grows, so the yard needs more land, bought a strip at a time. An earth traverse takes a plot off a circle. If a shed goes up in an accident, the circles are why it is the only thing you lose. The line. Composition is rolled into stars of one colour each: red, then gold, green and blue (blue yields half).
