SPOILHEAP

SPOILHEAP is a free browser game, free to play in the browser.

Digging it destroys it. Your notes are all that is left. SPOILHEAP is a free browser archaeology incremental with 7 generated fields and 57 kinds of find: run a rescue dig before the machine arrives, and get it on paper. How it plays A field is about to be built on. The picture is that field cut through from the side: ploughsoil, pits, ditches, roundhouses, flint walls and, further on, a mosaic floor.

All of it happened, oldest at the bottom, and all of it goes when you dig it. The pace. A trowel records everything and takes for ever. A mattock goes through soil fast and writes almost nothing down. A find pays its worth times the share you recorded, and a context only counts at 70%, so ploughsoil gets the mattock and a dark pit fill gets the trowel. The order.

A layer can only be read once whatever lay on it has gone. Dig under something still standing and the finds lose their date and most of their worth. Red on the section shows what is in the way, and a wider trench takes it in. The heap. Every bucket goes on a heap you place. A far tip is safe and a long walk. A near one is a short walk, buries good ground, and runs back into your trench when its foot gets there.

The machine.
