Bracket is live. It reads the Shop Processing commands already in your events and hangs a sign over every shop: a wrought iron bracket, a painted board on two chains, a trade device, the shop's name in carved or gilt capitals and the trade beneath it. Every sign is drawn in code at run time. No image files ship with it and none are added to your project.

What decides a sign

  • The device comes from what the shop stocks: item kind, equipment slot, effect codes and your own price ladder first, then the words in the goods' names, then the shop's name. Twelve devices.
  • The board is cut to the name it has to carry. Ten outlines.
  • The bracket comes from where the shop stands on your project's price ladder. Eight wrought forms, from a plain hook to a crowned scroll with a brace.
  • The paint comes from the trade and from standing. Six schemes.
  • The weathering comes from the district's prosperity: paint thinning over the grain, gilt going off, rust creeping along the bar from the rings, a split corner, a missing link.

That is 5,760 sign forms, each in five states of repair. The demo town hangs 48 of them, and no two share the same device, board, bracket and scheme.

Where players see them

Over every shop door on the map, automatically. Full size in the Close Look, swinging on its chain over a dusk street the plugin draws. In the Signwright's Yard, a directory of every sign the player has met, grouped by district. And above the goods windows when the shop opens, with the windows moved down to make room.

Notetags overrule anything it reads, and an event with no shop can still carry a sign, so a guild hall, a temple or a locked door can be signed too.

How it was checked

The shipped zip was installed into a blank MZ project and used as a buyer would: two shops stocked with RPG Maker's own default items produced full signs with no notetags. The engine gate measured the Close Look and the Yard at 2 draw calls with no texture uploads per frame. The Close Look and the Yard draw no RPG Maker windows and do not use RPG Maker's font.

Bracket for RPG Maker MZ. If your shops already have prices that move, Bazaar Core runs the inside and Bracket hangs the outside.

Made with AI assistance: code and graphics yes, sounds and text no.
