3. AI DISCLOSURE

   The artwork in this pack is AI-generated. Every sprite was produced with
   PixelLab, a pixel-art diffusion model, on a paid plan that grants commercial
   rights to the output. The sprites were then palette-locked and cut to the grid

   source code was written with AI assistance.

   Every opaque pixel in the pack falls inside the palette listed in pack.json,
   and every frame is exactly the declared 64x64 cell size with strictly binary
   alpha. Those are mechanical assertions re-run against the decoded pixels of
   every file before release, not claims about how the art was made.
