Deepwarren Side is 15 pixel art side-view monster sprites for Godot 4 and any engine that reads PNG sheets: crypt undead, cavern beasts and wildwood monsters. Use them in commercial and free games, no credit needed. A free one foe demo is on this page.

What is inside 15 foes · 5 animations each · left and right · strips, sheets and single frames at 224 and 112 px The crypt (5): skeleton swordsman, bone archer, ghoul, wraith and armoured revenant. The cavern (5): cave slime, giant bat, rock crawler, mushroom fiend and crystal troll. The wildwood (5): carnivorous plant, dire wolf, tusked boar, treant and giant spider.

Every foe has an idle (4 frames), a move (6), an attack (6), a hurt (6) and a death (6): 840 frames at each size. foes.json gives every foe's pivot at each size and facing and the attack frame where the blow lands, so you can wire up damage and placement without measuring anything. One palette, one world scale and one ground row, so any two foes can share a scene at 1:1.

Every foe at 2x of its 224px master, on one ground row per set. Each foe at rest, then on the hit frame foes.json names.
