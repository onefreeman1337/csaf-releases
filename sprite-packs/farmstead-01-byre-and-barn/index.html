Six farm animals and the two hands who keep them. A dairy cow, a sow, a ewe, a billy goat, a rooster and a gander, with a milkmaid and a stablehand at the same world scale. Every one of them has an idle and a six-frame walk in all four directions, so a farmyard can have animals moving in it instead of standing in it. They are at true relative size. The cow towers over the ewe; the rooster comes up to the goat's knee.

That is bought by the canvas each species was drawn on, not by resizing a sprite afterwards, and every frame is then placed into one shared 144px cell by integer pad-and-translate — so they share a ground line and you can drop the whole cast into one scene without rescaling anything.
