29 medieval townsfolk sprites for side-scrolling games. A pixel art character pack for platformers, brawlers and side-view RPGs: the town trades, the keep household and its guards, and the folk of the road and the tavern. Every character has an idle, a walk and a work animation; 4 fighters also attack, flinch and fall.

1,072 frames facing left and right at 160x160 and 80x80, with sprite sheets, frames.json and Godot 4 SpriteFrames. Commercial use allowed, no credit required. A free Hearthhands sampler is linked below.

What is inside 29 characters in one style and one 128-colour palette: smith, baker, butcher, fishmonger, carpenter, washerwoman, apothecary, pieSeller, townCrier, townChild, king, steward, maid, cook, jester, scribe, priest, keepGuard, shieldGuard, merchant, pilgrim, bard, hunter, farmer, beggar, tavernKeeper, barmaid, brawler, bandit.

Every character: idle (4 frames, breathing), walk (6) and a job loop (6): the smith swings his hammer, the crier rings his bell, the king waves, the jester capers, the barmaid raises a toast. 4 fighters (keepGuard, shieldGuard, hunter, bandit): attack, hurt and death (6 frames each, one-shots), so they work as enemies.
