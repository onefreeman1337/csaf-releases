The Mourncrest Collection is 19 dark fantasy sprites for RPG Maker MZ, Godot or any engine, packs 01 to 04 in one download. Each has 4 directions and a 3 frame walk, drawn natively at 64x64 and 128x128. Commercial use in unlimited games, no royalty, no attribution required. Mourncrest 00: The First Rung is free. Four packs, one graveyard 01 The Restless Dead: skeleton, wraith, ghoul, wight, gravebound and bone hound.

02 The Pale Choir: the 5 cultists who serve them. 03 Beasts of the Barrow: dire bat, carrion crow, tomb spider, ossuary ooze and barrow wyrm. 04 The Iron Wardens: the 3 armoured wardens who hunt all of the above. One palette, provable Four packs from four sellers will not match, and the only reason to buy a series is that it does.

Every opaque pixel in all four packs falls inside one 70 colour master palette, shipped as palette_master.json, and each pack widened the palette without ever replacing a colour. That is why the pack 01 skeleton and the pack 04 knight read as the same world. What you get 19 characters in 4 directions with a contact, pass, contact walk. Two native grids, 64x64 and 128x128, neither a resize of the other.
