Bestiary is 12 top-down pixel art monster sprites for Godot 4, RPG Maker MZ and any engine that reads PNG sheets: dragons, yokai and giant bugs. Use them in commercial and free games, no credit needed.

What is inside 12 monsters · 5 animations each · 4 directions · sheets and single frames at 160 and 80 px Dragons and drakes (4): ember drake that rests sitting, jade dragon with its wings spread, great blue dragon and storm raptor with an orange-red crest. Oni and yokai (4): huge red oni, crow tengu with a long beak, squat river kappa with a white dish on its head and white kitsune with one great tail.

Giant bugs (4): mantis with two scythe forelegs, black spider, scorpion with its stinger raised and hornet on four pale wings. Every monster has an idle, a walk (6 frames), a strike (6), a hurt (6) and a death (6) facing south, west, east and north: 1,200 frames at each size. They fight with their own bodies: fists, jaws, scythes and a stinger. No weapons to lose, and no projectile sprites.

One 128 colour palette and one scale, so all 12 stand in one scene at 1:1, the kappa beside the dragon. One frame from every state, every monster, at 1x of the 160 px master.
