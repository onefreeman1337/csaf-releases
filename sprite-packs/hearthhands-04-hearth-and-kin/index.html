Hearthhands 04: Hearth and Kin — 200x200 & 100x100 village family sprites is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

The Hearthhands village at home. 10 villagers who live with the working village: five children (pinafore girl, cap boy, sun hat girl, satchel boy and gangle youth), two grandparents (grandmother and grandfather) and three grown-ups of the household (baby mother, father and laundry wife).

Every one of them has an idle, a 6-frame walk and a 6-frame small move of their own in all four directions, front, back and both profiles. Children drawn as children. Each child was generated at its own smaller size from the same reference figure as the grown-ups, so they are shorter and slighter by build, not shrunk adults, and they stand on the same ground line as everyone else in the series.

It drops straight into the Hearthhands village. Hearthhands 01: The Working Day, Hearthhands 02: Market and Craft, Hearthhands 03: Watch and Ward and Hearthhands Hallowtide: the Costumed Village hold 39 grown-up villagers.

This pack shares their 200 and 100 cells and their ground line (every foot on row 168 of the 200 cell, measured across all 49 villagers of the five packs), and the palette holds every one of the series' 43 colours unchanged, plus 53 more fitted to this cast.
