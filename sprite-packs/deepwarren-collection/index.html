The Deepwarren Collection - 23 dungeon monster sprites, 160 & 176 px is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

23 dungeon monsters on one locked palette, every one of them combat-complete. What is in it Deepwarren 01: The Living Dark (12, 160x160 and 80x80): warren rat, kobold skirmisher, kobold shaman, cave beetle, myconid, gelatinous cube, mimic, roper, troglodyte, eye tyrant, stone warden, sporefather. States: idle, walk, strike, hurt, death.

Deepwarren 02 — The Winged Dark (11, 176x176 and 88x88): giant bat, imp, cockatrice, wyrmling, harpy, gargoyle, dire vulture, peryton, nightmare, manticore, cave wyvern. States: idle, fly, strike, hurt, death, plus a matching ground shadow for every frame. 2,138 frames at each pack's two sizes, one sheet per animation and frames.json with every frame's rectangle.

Each pack folder is the pack exactly as it is sold separately, byte for byte, with its own Readme and licence. Neither pack ships engine importers; in our test a blank Godot 4.7.2 project built SpriteFrames from frames.json for every frame of both packs at their native size.

Two cell sizes, one floor line Deepwarren 01 is drawn in a 160x160 cell and Deepwarren 02 is drawn in a 176x176 cell, kept exactly as sold, never rescaled.
