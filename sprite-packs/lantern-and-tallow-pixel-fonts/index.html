Lantern & Tallow: JRPG Pixel Font Pair is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

Two original pixel fonts drawn as a pair for role-playing game screens. Tallow is the dialogue face for text boxes, menus, item names and numbers (native 14 px). Lantern is the display face for titles, chapter cards, headers and names (native 22 px). White ink on a transparent background, so your engine tints them to any colour.

What is in the download Tallow/ and Lantern/: the glyph sheet (.png), an AngelCode BMFont file (.fnt, kerning included), a TrueType file (.ttf) that draws the same pixels, and a metrics JSON for a custom renderer pack.json, Readme_for_Users.md (Godot steps and engine notes), LICENSE.txt Character set: 197 in each face Printable ASCII, the full Latin-1 Supplement (French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, the Nordic languages and Icelandic, plus symbols such as £ ¥ &copy; ° ± × ÷ ¿ ¡ « »), and six typographic characters: ‘ ’ “ ”... — Metrics Tallow: line 14 px, capitals 8 px, lowercase 6 px, 39 kerning pairs.

Every digit sits in one 5 px box with one 6 px advance, so HP, MP and prices line up; the zero is slashed. Lantern: line 22 px, capitals 12 px, lowercase 8 px, 53 kerning pairs.
