Lantern and Tallow is 2 original pixel fonts for JRPG screens, for Godot and any engine that reads.fnt or.ttf, with 197 characters each. Royalty free in your games. Tallow is the dialogue face for text boxes, menus, item names and numbers, native 14 px. Lantern is the display face for titles, chapter cards, headers and names, native 22 px.

White ink on a transparent background, so your engine tints them to any colour.

What is inside 2 fonts · 197 characters each · 92 kerning pairs · PNG glyph sheet,.fnt,.ttf and metrics JSON per face An AngelCode BMFont file with kerning included, and a TrueType file that draws the same pixels Printable ASCII, the full Latin-1 Supplement for French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, the Nordic languages and Icelandic, and six typographic characters Tallow's digits share one 5 px box and one 6 px advance, so HP, MP and prices line up, and its zero is slashed One ink colour and hard alpha, so no grey edge pixels.

Use them at native size or a whole multiple with smoothing off Both faces in a battle screen. The background, window frames and monsters were composed for this image and do not ship with the fonts.
