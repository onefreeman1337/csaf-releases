A top-down pixel-art sprite pack of 10 dog breeds, 6 cat breeds and 53 pet-shop props for Godot 4, RPG Maker MZ, Unity, GameMaker or any engine that reads PNGs. Every breed walks, lies down, sleeps and eats in all 4 directions, at 128x128 and 64x64. Commercial use in unlimited games, no attribution required. Free to try first: the Kithfell Sampler (https://csaf.itch.io/kithfell-sampler).

The dogs (10): Australian shepherd, chihuahua, dachshund, dalmatian, doberman, German shepherd, great dane, husky, pug, shiba inu. The cats (6): Maine Coon, Persian, Russian Blue, Siamese, Sphynx, tuxedo cat. 6 states per breed, 116 frames each: idle: the seated pose every other state starts from. walk: a 6-frame loop, a walk on all four legs. lie down: 5 frames, from sitting to lying down, head on the paws.

Plays once, then loop sleep; play it backwards to sit up again. sleep: a 6-frame loop, lying still with the eyes closed, breathing, a small twitch of the tail or ears. head down: 5 frames, from sitting, the head goes down to the floor. Plays once, then loop eat; play it backwards to sit up again. eat: a 6-frame loop, eating with the head down.
