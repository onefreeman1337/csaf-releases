Kithfell Companions is 16 animal side-view battlers for RPG Maker MZ, each with all 18 battle motions, ready for img/sv_actors with no plugin. Use them in commercial and free games, no credit needed. What is inside 16 animals · 18 motions each · 32 sheets · 800 frames at each of two sizes Cats and dogs: tabby, black and calico cats, a herd dog, a beagle and a corgi.

Small companions: a rabbit, a ferret and a hedgehog. Wild beasts: a red fox, a grey wolf, a wild boar, a brown bear and a mane lion. Hooved mounts: a red stag and a draught horse, which rear, stamp and charge. Every animal bites, swipes, pounces, howls, guards, flinches, hops, cheers and falls. Sheets at 720x480 (80 px cells) and 1440x960 (160 px cells).

One 224 colour palette and one ground line, so the hedgehog and the stag share a party at true size. The background is an illustration and is not in the pack. Works with RPG Maker MZ: copy a sheet to img/sv_actors, set it as the actor's [SV] Battler, turn on side view. We did exactly that in a blank project and ran a real battle at both sizes.
