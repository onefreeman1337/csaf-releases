Every effect tells you how to play it Most VFX packs ship you frames and stop.

To actually use one you still have to work out three things for yourself: how fast it runs, whether it loops, and where to put it. That last one is the awkward one. A character sprite lines up on a ground line you can see. An impact effect has to line up on its impact point — which is usually not the middle of the cell, and which you cannot work out without opening every frame.

So this pack measures it and writes it down, per effect, in effects.json: { "id": "impact_burst", "fps": 14, "loop": false, "anchorPx": { "x": 61, "y": 64 } } Draw the cell so that anchorPx sits on the point of impact. That is the whole integration.
