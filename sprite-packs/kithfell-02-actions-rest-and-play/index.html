Kithfell 02 Actions: Lie Down, Sleep and Eat (128x128 & 64x64 pet sprites) is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

A free sample from the same series: Kithfell Sampler: 3 Free Animal Sprites, Horse, Beagle and Fox [128x128].

Kithfell 02 gave the house pets their idle and their walk. This pack gives them their downtime. The beagle, black cat, calico cat, corgi, ferret, hedgehog, rabbit, tabby cat and westie now lie down, sleep, put their heads down and eat, in all 4 directions, at 128x128 and 64x64.

Try one free first: the Kithfell Sampler is free and includes the beagle lying down from this pack, in all four directions, so you can drop it into your engine before you buy. 5 states per pet, 92 frames each: lie down: 5 frames, from sitting to lying down at rest.

Plays once, then loop sleep; play it backwards to sit up again. sleep: a 6-frame loop, asleep, breathing. head down: 5 frames, from sitting, the head goes down to the floor. Plays once, then loop eat; play it backwards to sit up again. eat: a 6-frame loop, eating with the head down. idle: the seated pose from Kithfell 02 itself, so every transition starts from it. Made for Kithfell 02, not beside it.

Every cycle was generated starting from Kithfell 02's own shipped frames, so the cell, the ground line and the scale are pack 02's.
