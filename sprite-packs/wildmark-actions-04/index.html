A dodge roll and a block for the Wildmark 01 party, in every facing. The elf, warrior, mage and rogue get the two defensive moves an action game asks for: 64 new animation cycles, 432 frames per grid size, at 128×128 and 64×64. Every roll and every guard opens from that character's own shipped idle, so they play straight out of sprites you already own.

What is in the pack Dodge roll (12 frames): seen from the side, a dive into a low curl, a full tumble over the head and back, and up onto the feet (lowest point 29-48% of standing height). Seen head-on, a fast duck into a tight crouch and back up (down to 48-87%), because a roll straight toward or away from the camera hides its turn. Move the sprite along the ground while it plays.

Guard up (6 frames): feet set wide, knees bent, both fists come up to block. Guard hold (4 frames, looping): the held block, breathing. Guard down (5 frames): the block lowers back toward the idle. All four in all four facings (south, west, east, north) for all four characters. Roll, guard up and guard down play once; the hold loops. Who they are Elf — The ranger.
