The 12 creatures of Deepwarren 01: The Living Dark as RPG Maker MZ battlers.

Warren Rat, Kobold Skirmisher, Kobold Shaman, Cave Beetle, Myconid, Gelatinous Cube, Mimic, Roper, Troglodyte, Eye Tyrant, Stone Warden and Sporefather: each one with all 18 of MZ's side-view battle motions in MZ's own sheet layout, 12 sheets at 720×480 (80×80 cells) and 12 HD sheets at 1440×960 (160×160 cells), plus a still enemy image of every creature for side-view and front-view battles.

The monster a player meets on your map is the one they fight. Install (no plugin) As enemies: copy rpgmaker_mz/img/sv_enemies/ (side-view, facing right) or rpgmaker_mz/img/enemies/ (front-view, facing the camera) into the same folder of your project, then pick the image in Database, Enemies.

As animated party members: copy rpgmaker_mz/img/sv_actors/ into your project's img/sv_actors/, set each actor's [SV] Battler to its Deepwarren_... sheet, and turn on side-view battle in Database, System 1. Tested exactly that way in a blank MZ project with a real side-view battle: all 12 creatures in the party, every motion forced on every one of them and read back from the engine, and three creatures as enemies.
