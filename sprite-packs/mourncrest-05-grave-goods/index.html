Mourncrest 05: Grave Goods — 45 graveyard object sprites, 64x64 & 128x128 is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

45 graveyard props for top-down games — sarcophagi, coffins, headstones, braziers, urns, bone piles, altars, grave markers, tools and the small things a burial ground is actually full of. Two native grid sizes, one palette, one visual system. Every object is sized to every other object.

Each prop's height comes from the real-world size stated in its own description, measured against an adult human taken from the shipped Mourncrest cast, and every sprite is bottom-anchored in its cell. So you can drop an urn and a sarcophagus into one scene and they already stand on the same floor, in proportion, with no fiddling. At the 64px grid the pack runs from 14px to 62px.

It is one consistent relative scale, compressed at the small end so every object stays readable — 6 hand-sized props are lifted to a 14px floor, because at their strict size they would land at 12px and a 12px prop is a smudge rather than a sprite. The ordering still does the work: a sarcophagus is still far larger than an urn, and an urn than a skull. We would rather tell you this than let you find it.
