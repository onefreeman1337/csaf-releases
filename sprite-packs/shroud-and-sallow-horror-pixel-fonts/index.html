Shroud & Sallow: Horror Pixel Font Pair is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

Two original pixel fonts drawn as a pair for horror games. Sallow is the dialogue face: a narrow hand for notes, diary pages, dialogue, item text and menus that stays readable over a whole page (native 14 px). Shroud is the display face: tall condensed letters with cut tops and drips for title screens, chapter cards, location names and the word on the wall (native 22 px).

White ink on a transparent background, so your engine tints them to any colour.

What is in the download Sallow/ and Shroud/: the glyph sheet (.png), an AngelCode BMFont file (.fnt, kerning included), a TrueType file (.ttf) that draws the same pixels, and a metrics JSON for a custom renderer pack.json, Readme_for_Users.md (Godot steps and engine notes), LICENSE.txt Character set: 197 in each face Printable ASCII, the full Latin-1 Supplement (French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, the Nordic languages and Icelandic, plus symbols such as £ ¥ &copy; ° ± × ÷ ¿ ¡ « »), and six typographic characters: ‘ ’ “ ”... — The drips are a rule, not noise Every Shroud letter wears the same wear.

On each glyph's top row every stroke loses its left pixel (one brush angle across the face) and long bars end frayed.
