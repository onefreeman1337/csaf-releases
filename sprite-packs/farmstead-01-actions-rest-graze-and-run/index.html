Farmstead 01 Actions: Rest, Graze and Run (144x144 & 72x72 farm animal sprites) is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

Farmstead 01 gave the farmyard its animals standing and walking. This pack gives them everything else. The cow, sow, ewe, billy goat, rooster and gander now lie down, fall asleep, sleep, put their heads down to graze (the sow roots, the birds peck) and run, in all four directions, at 144x144 and 72x72. Seven states per animal, 148 frames each: lie down: 6 frames from standing to lying.

Play it backwards to stand up again. fall asleep: 6 frames, the head goes down and the eyes close. sleep: a 6-frame loop, breathing. head down: 6 frames from standing to grazing.

Play it backwards to lift the head. graze: a 6-frame loop: grazing for the cow, ewe and goat, rooting for the sow, pecking for the birds. run: a 6-frame loop: a trot for the four-legged stock, a run for the birds. idle: the standing pose itself, so every transition starts from it. Made for Farmstead 01, not beside it.

Every cycle was generated starting from Farmstead 01's own shipped frames, so the cell, the ground line and the scale are pack 01's. The palette is exactly Farmstead 01's 44 colours, and the 48 idle frames here are pixel-identical to the ones you already own, at both grids.
