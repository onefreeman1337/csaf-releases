Skyfoundling 01 is 10 flying bird and insect sprites with ground shadows for Godot 4 or any engine, at 128 and 64 px. Use them in commercial and non commercial games, unlimited projects and units, no credit required. Do not resell the art itself as an asset pack.

What is inside 10 creatures · 4 directions · 6 frame fly loop · 2 shadow styles · 720 frames per grid size · PNG sheets at 128 and 64 px 10 creatures: a house sparrow, raven, white dove, seagull, tawny owl, macaw, red tailed hawk, honeybee, monarch butterfly and dragonfly. A 6 frame fly loop in 4 directions. The first frame, wings spread wide, doubles as a glide pose.

A shadow for every frame: each is cut from its own sprite frame on the same pivot, so it flaps with the wings. One style is solid, to fade in your engine, and one is dithered 50%, to drop in with no engine opacity. Two grids: 128x128 and 64x64. The 64 is an exact 2:1 reduction of the 128 master, palette locked.

Scale is kept: the large birds fill more of the cell than the sparrow and dove, and the insects less again, so nothing needs rescaling. Draw the shadow on the ground, draw the creature above it, and change the gap to climb or dive over any tileset.
