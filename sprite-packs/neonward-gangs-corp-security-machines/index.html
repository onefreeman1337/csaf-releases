Nine top-down cyberpunk pixel art enemies for Godot 4, RPG Maker MZ or any engine that reads PNG sprite sheets. Three casts in one neon-and-grime style: two street gangers, three corporate security troopers and four machines (an android, a two-legged mech, a hover drone and a spider drone). Each ships idle, walk, attack, hurt and death in four directions, 900 frames at 160x160 plus the same 900 at 80x80.

Six fight in melee and three shoot. Commercial use allowed, no credit required. The enemy roster for Neonward 01: Street Cast. Same palette (all of its colours, unchanged) and the same human build, so these gangers and troopers belong on the same street as its netrunner, courier, street samurai and street kid.

Every character fights: 100 frames each, a 6-frame walk, a 6-frame attack, a 6-frame hurt and a 6-frame death in all four directions. The 160x160 frames are the native master (nothing upscaled); the 80x80 set is an exact 2:1 reduction, palette-locked so it stays crisp.
