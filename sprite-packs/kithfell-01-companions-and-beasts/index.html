Kithfell 01 Companions and Beasts is four animal sprites for RPG Maker MZ, Godot 4 and any top down game: herd dog, draught horse, brown bear and mane lion. Commercial use in unlimited games, no royalty and no attribution required. Each walks in 4 true directions with a 9 frame cycle and has its own standing pose, drawn natively at 128x128 and 64x64.

MZ character sheets and Godot SpriteFrames are included, plus PNG frames, packed sheets and frames.json for Unity, GameMaker or any engine. The free Kithfell Sampler includes the draught horse from this pack, so you can try it in your engine first. What is in it 4 animals on one locked 128 colour palette that the whole Kithfell series shares. 4 directions each, with true opposed side profiles and real back views.

A 9 frame walk in every direction, plus a separate standing pose, so a resting animal is not frozen mid stride. 160 frames per grid size, at 128x128 and 64x64. Each size is cut from the source at its own resolution; neither is a resize of the other. 360 PNGs, single frames and packed sheets, plus frames.json with every frame's exact x, y, w and h.
