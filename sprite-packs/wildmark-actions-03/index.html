The everyday actions for the Wildmark 01 party, in every facing. Pick up, interact, knocked down and get up for the elf, warrior, mage and rogue: 64 new animation cycles, 384 frames per grid size, at 128×128 and 64×64. Every pick-up, interact and knockdown opens from that character's own shipped idle, and every get-up opens from the pose its knockdown ends on, so they play straight out of sprites you already own.

What is in the pack Pick up (8 frames): crouches on bent knees, down to 54-71% of standing height, with one open hand reaching toward the ground, then rises. No item is drawn, so you place your own at the hand. Interact (5 frames): from the side, one arm reaches out to full length and comes back, for a door, a lever or a chest.

Head-on, one arm rises to an open-hand gesture and lowers, because a push straight into the screen does not read head-on. Knocked down (6 frames): off both feet and down. 14 of 16 cycles end lying on the ground. The rogue, seen from the side (west and east), lands sitting instead. Get up (5 frames): pushes up from the pose the knockdown ends on and rises back to standing.
