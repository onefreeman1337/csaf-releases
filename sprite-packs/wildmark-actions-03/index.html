64 top down action cycles for 4 RPG heroes at 128 and 64 px, ready for Godot 4 and RPG Maker Wildmark Actions 03 gives the four Wildmark 01 heroes their everyday verbs: pick up, interact, knocked down and get up, in all four facings. 64 animation cycles, 384 frames at each grid size, 128×128 and 64×64. Commercial use in any number of games, no attribution.

Every pick up, interact and knockdown opens from that character's own shipped idle, and every get up opens from the pose its knockdown ends on, so the new cycles play straight out of sprites you already own. What is in the pack Pick up (8 frames): crouches on bent knees with one open hand reaching to the ground, then rises. No item is drawn, so you place your own at the hand.

Interact (5 frames): from the side, one arm reaches out and comes back, for a door, a lever or a chest. Head on, one arm rises to an open hand gesture. Knocked down (6 frames): off both feet and down. 14 of 16 cycles end lying on the ground; the rogue, seen from the side, lands sitting. Get up (5 frames): pushes up from the knockdown's last pose and rises back to standing.
