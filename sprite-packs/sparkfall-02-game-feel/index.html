60 pixel-art game-feel effects for 2D games: the dust, hits, pickups and pop-ups that make jumping, fighting and collecting feel good. 51 one-shots and 9 loops, each 8 frames, at 64x64 and 32x32. Every effect names its anchor, the pixel it was drawn around (the feet, the base of a flame, the item, the point of a hit), so you place it without nudging it by hand.

Godot 4: drop-in scenes A ready.tscn per effect whose origin is the anchor: instance it, set its position, add it. One-shots remove themselves when they end; loops keep playing. Nearest filtering is set on the node, so pixels stay crisp without touching project settings. 3 effects (Power-Up Swirl, Level Up, Teleport Out) pass behind your character: a back sprite and a front sprite.

Plain SpriteFrames.tres too, if you use your own AnimatedSprite2D. Tested in Godot 4.7.2: all 60 scenes instanced in blank projects at both sizes and the drawn pixels compared with the shipped frames, 307,200 pixels, 0 different. After 1.5 seconds the one-shots had removed themselves and exactly the 9 loops were still running.
