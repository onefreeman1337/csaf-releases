Wildmark 03: The Far Kin — 128x128 & 64x64 character sprites is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

Four adventurers of other folk, for the same company. A dwarf smith, a halfling burglar, a monk of the cat folk and a warlock of the horned folk. Every one of them has a standing pose and a 6-frame walk in all four directions, front, back and both profiles, at 128x128 and 64x64. Drawn at the height of their own folk.

In the 128 cell the dwarf stands 84 pixels tall and the halfling 58, beside the 95 to 117 of the Wildmark 01 and 02 adventurers, so a dwarf in your party is genuinely short rather than a tall sprite shrunk. All four stand on one ground line (row 123 of the 128 cell). It joins the Wildmark party.

Wildmark 01: The Party and Wildmark 02: The Second Rank hold eight adventurers of the tall folk on the same 128 and 64 grids. This pack's 224-colour palette contains every one of pack 02's 192 colours unchanged (and pack 02's holds all 128 of pack 01's), plus 32 more fitted to this cast, so all twelve can share a screen without a colour clash.

The cast Dwarf: A broad dwarf of the mountain folk with a great copper beard and a leather smith's apron. Halfling: A small halfling woman with a mop of curls and big bare feet.
