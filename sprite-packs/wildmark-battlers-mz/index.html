The eight Wildmark adventurers as RPG Maker MZ side-view battlers. All 18 of MZ's battle motions for the elf, warrior, mage, rogue, priest, vanguard, alchemist and forester, in MZ's own sheet layout: 8 sheets at 576×384 (MZ's default battler size) and 8 HD sheets at 1152×768. 400 frames per size. The hero a player walks around your map is the same hero who fights.

Install (no plugin) Copy the eight PNGs from rpgmaker_mz/img/sv_actors/ into your project's img/sv_actors/. Database, Actors: set each actor's [SV] Battler image to its Wildmark_... sheet. Database, System 1: turn on side-view battle. Tested exactly that way in a blank MZ project with a real side-view battle: every motion forced on every hero and read back from the engine.

At 64×64: 144 hero-motion pairs, 432 engine reads, 0 wrong. At 128×128: 144 pairs, 432 reads, 0 wrong. The HD sheets need no plugin either: MZ cuts a battler sheet by its own size, so they draw at twice the default scale.
