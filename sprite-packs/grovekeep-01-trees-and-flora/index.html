Grovekeep 01: Trees and Flora - 47 nature sprites, 128x128 & 64x64 is a pixel art sprite pack, paid.

47 nature props in one style and one 64-colour palette: 12 trees and saplings, 7 shrubs and thickets, 7 ferns and grasses, 11 wildflowers, 4 fungi and 6 pieces of deadwood.

Every object stands on the bottom row of its cell, centred, so anything you place by its base sits on the ground, and the sizes are set against a 48px character: the trees stand about two and a half characters tall, shrubs, stumps and logs reach about waist to chest height, and flowers and fungi stay lower. Two grid sizes. At 128x128 the trees keep their generated pixel grid 1:1, with colours snapped to the palette.

Smaller objects are whole-number reductions of their own art (1/2 to 1/4) with the one-pixel dark outline redrawn, so a mushroom keeps the same hard edge as an oak. The 64x64 set is composed the same way from the same art, not resampled from the 128.
