Greenskin 01 is 10 top down goblin and orc sprites for RPG Maker MZ, Godot 4 or any engine: one warband, one palette, one true scale. Commercial use is allowed and no credit is needed. What is inside 10 characters · 4 directions · idle and 6 frame walk · PNG frames and sheets at 144 and 72 px 6 goblins: Scout, Archer, Shaman, Spearman, Clubber, Chieftain. 4 orcs: Brute, Axeman, Pikeman, Warchief.

28 frames per character, 280 per grid size, 660 PNGs in all, plus a frame map for every rectangle. 44 colours, one closed palette across the whole cast at both sizes. Ready to load: RPG Maker MZ character sheets and Godot 4 SpriteFrames resources. Nine of the ten at their true relative size. The Scout appears in the next plate.

One true scale The goblins and the orcs were generated separately, each at its own canvas, then placed into one shared 144 px cell with no pixel resampled. On the shipped south idles a goblin stands about 77 px and an orc about 112 px, a ratio of 1.00 to 1.45, so an orc beside a goblin looks right with no scaling in your engine.

Every character stands on the same ground line, and its idle stands where its walk stands, so switching animation does not make it hop.
