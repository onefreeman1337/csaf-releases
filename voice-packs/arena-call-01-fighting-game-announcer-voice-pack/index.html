One announcer for the whole fight: rounds, knockouts, combos, winners and the continue countdown 178 spoken lines by one invented arena announcer, The Crucible Herald, for fighting games, brawlers and arcade games. Every line ships as a 48 kHz WAV and an OGG, trimmed tight and loudness matched, with a manifest that doubles as your subtitle table.

AI voice, said up front These lines are AI generated: rendered with OpenAI text to speech (model gpt-4o-mini-tts, built in voice cedar). No human voice actor, and no cloned or imitated real voice. OpenAI's usage policies require that players are told a text to speech voice is AI generated, so the licence asks you to say so in your credits and gives you the line to paste.

What you get in place of an actor's name: one character recipe behind every line, every file measured, and a price below the human voiced packs.
