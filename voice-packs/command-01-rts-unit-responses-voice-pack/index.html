Command 01: RTS Unit Responses Voice Pack is a voice pack, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

Its store page states that commercial use is permitted; the exact licence terms are on the store page.

Five RTS units and an advisor, voiced for every click 140 spoken lines by five invented unit types and an advisor, every character on its own built in voice: a worker, a footsoldier, an archer, a rider and a mage with 20 lines each, and an advisor with 40 base and map alerts.

Every unit covers the same seven moments with the same number of lines (selected, move order, main action, cannot do that, under attack, unit ready and clicked again), so your unit code calls one set of ids and any unit can answer. Every line ships as a 48 kHz WAV and an OGG, trimmed tight and loudness matched, with a manifest that doubles as your subtitle table.

AI voices, said up front These lines are AI generated: rendered with OpenAI text to speech (model gpt-4o-mini-tts, built in voices ballad, onyx, nova, cedar, fable, marin). No human voice actors, and no cloned or imitated real voice. OpenAI's usage policies require that players are told a text to speech voice is AI generated, so the licence asks you to say so in your credits and gives you the line to paste.
