Squad Net 01: Fireteam Callouts Voice Pack is a voice pack, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

A fireteam, voiced: four soldiers, four voices, every callout a firefight needs 180 spoken callouts by four invented squad members, 45 each, every soldier on its own built in voice: contact, movement, cover and suppression, reloads and ammo, grenades and smoke, hit and man down, target down, orders and acknowledgements, objectives, radio and support, extraction and mission end.

Every squad member covers the same eleven situations with the same number of lines, so any voice can stand in for any squadmate. Every line ships as a 48 kHz WAV and an OGG, trimmed tight and loudness matched, with a manifest that doubles as your subtitle table. AI voices, said up front These lines are AI generated: rendered with OpenAI text to speech (model gpt-4o-mini-tts, built in voices ash, verse, alloy, nova).

No human voice actors, and no cloned or imitated real voice. OpenAI's usage policies require that players are told a text to speech voice is AI generated, so the licence asks you to say so in your credits and gives you the line to paste. Shouted, urgent delivery is asked for in every recipe; how intense it lands is the one thing a measurement cannot tell you, so hear the sampler first.
