Plunder 01: Pirate Crew Voice Pack is a voice pack, paid, with a free browser demo on the store page.

Its store page states that commercial use is permitted; the exact licence terms are on the store page.

A pirate captain and three crew, voiced for every event of a voyage 156 spoken lines by an invented pirate crew, each on its own built in voice: the captain, the first mate, the gunner and the lookout, with 39 lines each. Lines are grouped by the ten events of a voyage, from shore leave in port to the call to sail, the sighting, the cannon fire, the boarding and the treasure, plus storms, mutiny talk and defeat.

Every crew member covers the same thirteen moments with three lines each, so any of the four can speak any moment by changing one word of the id. Every line ships as a 48 kHz WAV and an OGG, trimmed tight and loudness matched, with a manifest that names the event of every line and doubles as your subtitle table. Import notes for Godot 4, Unity, Unreal Engine 5, GameMaker and RPG Maker MZ.

Royalty free in commercial games, AI voices disclosed, and a free 10 line sampler on this page. AI voices, said up front These lines are AI generated: rendered with OpenAI text to speech (model gpt-4o-mini-tts, built in voices onyx, ash, verse, coral). No human voice actors, and no cloned or imitated real voice.
