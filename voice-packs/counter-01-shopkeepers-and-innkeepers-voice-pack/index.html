Its store page states that commercial use is permitted; the exact licence terms are on the store page.

Four fantasy shopkeepers, voiced for your whole shop screen 120 spoken lines by four invented shopkeepers, 30 each, every shopkeeper on its own built in voice: a blacksmith, an apothecary, an innkeeper and a general store trader.

Every shopkeeper covers the same ten shop situations with the same number of lines (greeting, browsing, a sale, buying from the player, not enough gold, closed, the shop's own service, rumours, farewell and a returning customer), so your shop screen calls one set of ids and any merchant can answer.

Every line ships as a 48 kHz WAV and an OGG, trimmed tight and loudness matched, with a manifest that doubles as your subtitle table. AI voices, said up front These lines are AI generated: rendered with OpenAI text to speech (model gpt-4o-mini-tts, built in voices cedar, fable, coral, verse). No human voice actors, and no cloned or imitated real voice.

OpenAI's usage policies require that players are told a text to speech voice is AI generated, so the licence asks you to say so in your credits and gives you the line to paste.
