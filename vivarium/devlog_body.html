Vivarium is out: the small life of every map, drawn at runtime, for RPG Maker MZ.

A meadow in MZ is a painted floor: the pond is still, the grass is empty, and at night nothing glows. Vivarium fills each map with the life a real place has, and it places that life itself. It reads every tile once when the map loads: water gets fish under the surface, pond skaters on it and dragonflies over it; the bank gets frogs; the rest of the walkable ground gets butterflies and bees by day, and moths, fireflies and bats when the screen darkens. There are no events to place.

Every creature is drawn in code from a species record: 43 species in nine families, from the Cabbage White and the Peacock to the Banded Demoiselle, the Kohaku koi, the Poison Dart Frog and the Fruit Bat. Every animation frame is drawn too: four-frame wingbeats, a swaying tail, a frog that sits, croaks and hops. You can add species of your own in a parameter: pick a family, three colours and a size.

The life notices the party. Walk up to a frog and it dives with a ripple and surfaces further along the bank. Fish dart from the bank you stand on. At night, moths circle the party as if it carried a lamp, and each species of firefly falls into step and flashes together, so a meadow pulses in waves. Day and night follow the screen tone by default, so any day/night plugin that tints the screen already drives it.

It is built to cost nothing you would notice: every frame of every species is painted once into one image, and life lives only around the part of the map on screen. In the real engine it measured 0 texture uploads per frame and about 11 draw calls with 96 creatures on screen. Every core method it touches is extended, never replaced.

No birds, on purpose: bird plugins exist, and Vivarium fills everything under the sky beside them. The tiles and the character in the store images are our own Verdant 01 tileset and Wayfarer sprite pack, and are not included.

Vivarium is $12.99. The code and graphics were made with AI assistance; no sounds or story text ship with it.
