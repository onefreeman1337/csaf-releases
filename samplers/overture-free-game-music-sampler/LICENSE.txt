HOW IT WAS MADE
---------------
The compositions are original works generated by a purpose-built composition system: hand-authored
melodic themes and functional harmonic progressions, developed by code that understands them. There
is no randomness in the pipeline and no generative-audio model anywhere in it.

The audio was synthesized from those MIDI files with FluidSynth using the GeneralUser GS soundfont,
whose licence expressly permits commercial use of recordings made with it ("including the ability to
make profit from musical recordings created with GeneralUser GS"). The soundfont itself is not
included or redistributed in this package.
