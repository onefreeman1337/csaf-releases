Smithereens: 48 2D breakables for Godot 4 that break the way their material would A pot bursts into curved sherds. A crate splits into planks along its grain. A pane webs out from the blow and rains down.

Put a Breakable2D under any Sprite2D or AnimatedSprite2D and hit it: the pieces are cut from your own sprite at the moment it breaks, by a fracture made for its material, then they fly, bounce, slide and settle with dust, glints, splinters, petals, straw, spilt grain or a pool. var b:= Breakable2D.new() b.style = Smithereens.style("pot_burst") $Pot.add_child(b) # a Sprite2D or AnimatedSprite2D b.hit(player.global_position) # multi hit styles crack first Smithereens.break_node($Crate, "crate_planks", player.global_position) Nothing is a frame animation.

The cut, the cracks, the flight and what is left on the ground are computed at runtime from the sprite being broken, so the pieces always match your art style at any resolution, and every number is a parameter of a SmashStyle resource. 48 break styles in 8 materials Ceramic: Pot Burst, Grain Spill, Jug Splash, Vase Shatter, Urn Ash, Amphora Topple.
