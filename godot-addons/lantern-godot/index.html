Lantern: 48 2D lighting presets for Godot 4, from window light to a whole day and night Light that has a shape, flames that live, a sky that runs the day, and darkness that follows your player.

Drop a preset into any 2D scene and it lights it: a cottage window throws four warm panes across the street, a chapel's rose window lays red, blue and gold glass on the cobbles, an oil lantern's cage casts bars, a neon sign keeps cutting out, and when the sun goes down the windows come on by themselves. var sky:= LanternCatalog.instantiate("moonlit") as LanternSky add_child(sky) var window:= LanternCatalog.instantiate("cottage_window") as LanternLight window.follow_night = true # off by day, on at dusk add_child(window) player.add_child(LanternCatalog.instantiate("torch_radius")) # darkness that follows the player Nothing is a frame sheet or a painted light texture.

Every light shape is drawn by code when the game runs, every flicker is a curve, every sky is a colour grade and every darkness is a shader, so every colour, size and speed is a slider.
