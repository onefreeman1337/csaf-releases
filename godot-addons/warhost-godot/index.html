A free MIT lite with 12 battles is on this page. Give it two armies as plain data: regiments of foot, pikes, archers, horse and mages, each with a name and a soldier count.

Pick a formation and the battle plays out soldier by soldier on a generated battlefield. Volleys arc overhead, spells burst, riders charge, regiments break and run.

When it ends, a signal hands every regiment's survivors back to your game. var battle:= WarhostBattle.new() battle.player_army = [{"name": "Iron Company", "role": "foot", "count": 120}] battle.enemy_army = [{"name": "Grey Lances", "role": "horse", "count": 40}] battle.battle_finished.connect(func(result): print(result["winner"])) add_child(battle) Every soldier drawn No image files ship.
