Jigsaw for Godot 4: any picture becomes a puzzle your players solve, in 48 styles Drop any Texture2D in and get a playable jigsaw. The picture is cut from a seed into interlocking pieces, every shared side one curve so each tab meets its own socket, and laid out on a table beside a tray.

Your player picks pieces up with the mouse, a finger, the keyboard or a gamepad, turns them if you want rotation, peeks at the box lid, and clicks the last one in. One line opens it and hands back the result: var result:= await JigsawMinigame.open(self, preload("res://art/castle.png"), {"pieces": 48, "pause": true}) if result.solved: open_the_gate() Nothing is a sprite sheet.

Every cut, bevel, shadow, table, tray, plaque, numeral and ending is drawn by the addon while it runs, so it works on a CG, a map screenshot, a portrait or a ViewportTexture of your own level.
