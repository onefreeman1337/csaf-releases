Threshold is a Godot 4.7 addon with 48 door, gate and chest openings, each drawn at runtime from the pixels of your own tile. Royalty free in your games. A free MIT lite with 12 presets is on this page. Your door tile swings, your chest lid tips, your portcullis ratchets up on its chains. Threshold reads the tile once and rebuilds every frame from it, so there are no sprite sheets to draw.

Closed, the tile is exactly as you drew it. var door:= ThresholdDoor2D.new() door.texture = preload("res://art/my_door.png") door.style = ThresholdLibrary.get_style("plank-swing") add_child(door) door.open() Mark a tile and it opens Add a threshold custom data layer to your TileSet and type a word on the door tile: door, double, portcullis, hatch, chest.

ThresholdLayer2D turns every marked tile on your TileMapLayer into a door, and neighbours with the same word open as one. A door painted on a wall tile? door@4,4,8,11 moves only that rectangle. Set cast_light and an open door throws a real light under your night. What is inside Doors and windows (20): hinged, double, bifold, sliding, roller shutters, and doors that burn, shatter, dissolve or pour out as sand.
