48 material ageing shaders for Godot 4, each grown by one age slider Verdigris ages your own 2D art while the game runs: rust blooming out of the rivets, copper going green, frost creeping in from the edges, snow settling on top, moss and ivy taking a wall, paint peeling, soot rising, a statue turning to stone. Every state is one number, age, from 0 (your sprite, untouched) to 1 (fully taken).

Tween it over a campaign, tie it to days at sea, a curse meter or a burning building. The shaders read your sprite's own pixels, its silhouette edges, the faces that look up and its dark crevices, so the ageing lands where it would in life.
