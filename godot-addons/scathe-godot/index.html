Scathe: battle damage drawn into your own sprites, for Godot 4 Scathe is a Godot 4.7 addon that draws the damage every hit leaves on your own enemy and player sprites: 49 presets, 7 kinds of mark on 7 materials, and an HP band wear that grinds the whole sprite down as its health falls. One canvas shader and one Scathe node; nothing to paint, no damaged variants.

A free MIT lite (12 presets) is on this page. # add a Scathe node under your Sprite2D, then in your combat code: $Enemy/Scathe.take_damage(amount, max_hp, "Fire Breath") # lowers health, draws a SCORCH mark The element NAME picks the mark, so your own element list works: fire burns, ice frosts, thunder burns a branching scar, poison bruises, earth cracks, acid eats through, anything else cuts.

Each new mark lands on a part of the body thick enough to hold it, away from the marks already there, and never outside the sprite's outline. 7 kinds of mark Cut: a lens shaped gash with a broken lit lip; bleeds and runs on flesh and scale. Crack: a crack network and radial spokes in the material's own colour in shadow, bare material on dark surfaces.
