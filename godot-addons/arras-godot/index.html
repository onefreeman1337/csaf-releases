Arras for Godot 4: your playthrough, woven into a tapestry Arras is a Godot 4.7 addon that turns what a player did into a Bayeux style embroidered hanging they can open and scroll. Each act of the playthrough becomes one woven panel: figures, a border, a ground and a stitched Latin inscription that names the act in your own words.

It ships 48 scenes over 16 figures, 12 props (ships, halls, castles, trees, a feast table, a comet), 10 border registers, 5 grounds and 6 dye sets, and every figure, letter and stitch is drawn at runtime by code. No image file ships. Royalty free in your games.
