Vivarium for Godot 4: 48 species of small life drawn in code Vivarium is a Godot 4.7 addon that fills your 2D maps with the small life a real place has: 48 species in 10 families (butterflies, moths, fireflies, dragonflies, bees, fish, frogs, bats, pond skaters and lizards), placed by reading your own TileMapLayer and changing with the time of day.

Butterflies and bees work the meadow, dragonflies quarter the pond, koi move under the surface, frogs on the bank dive when your player walks up, lizards bask on the path, and at night moths circle the lamp, fireflies flash and bats hunt. No image file ships: every wing, fin and leg is drawn by GDScript into one texture when a level loads. A free MIT lite (12 species) is on this page.
