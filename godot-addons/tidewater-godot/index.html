48 2D water shaders for Godot 4: lakes, seas, pools, waterfalls and underwater Tidewater puts water into your 2D game that is computed while it runs. Top down lakes, seas, rivers, swamps and lava whose depth and surf follow the outline you draw or the water tiles you already painted. Side on pools for platformers that bend what is behind them, mirror what is above them and ripple where the player lands.

Waterfalls, cascades, drips and fountains. And full screen underwater filters that fade in by themselves as your camera goes below the surface. Every colour is a parameter; nothing is a frame sheet. Four families, twelve presets each Top down (TidewaterBody): lake, open sea, lagoon, river, sewer, lava, swamp, frozen pond, pixel sea, moonlit bay, toxic pool, hot spring.

Side on (TidewaterSide): clear pool, ocean swell, murky canal, lava flow, mirror lake, acid vat, cave water, mud bog, pixel water, arctic water, sunset sea, poison marsh. Falls and flows (TidewaterFall): waterfall, thin cascade, lavafall, sewer outflow, frozen fall, mana fall, mist veil, drip ledge, fountain jet, rapids chute, pixel fall, slime fall.
